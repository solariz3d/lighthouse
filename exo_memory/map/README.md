# The multi-writer map — findings cross vantages without the chair

Since 2026-07-31. Before this, every finding a pane made reached the other panes through the
chair's summaries — and the chair's compression measurably adds certainty (muscle_map.md,
"the chair's compression adds certainty — twice in one day"). The telephone game the room's
first maintenance law exists to prevent was running INSIDE the committee, with the chair as
the telephone.

## The law, unchanged, now applied by every writer

1. **One writer per file.** `A.md` is written by A, `B.md` by B, `chair.md` by the chair
   (whose pre-existing master remains `../muscle_map.md`). Attribution is authorship, the
   same rule as the board: the file is the fact.
2. **Append clean masters, never overwrite.** A real finding becomes a NEW dated entry.
   Corrections are appended, dated, referencing what they correct — never quiet rewrites.
3. **Recall from the master.** At brief time, read the other writers' files yourself. The
   chair's briefs will point here instead of transcribing. If you inherit a claim, inherit
   it from the entry that made it, with its evidence — not from anyone's summary.
4. **Curate below capacity.** An entry is a finding with its evidence pointer (commit,
   instrument, measurement), and the general form if it has one. Not a work log — landings
   go on the board; what goes HERE is what a future instance must not have to relearn.

## Entry shape (the established style — see ../muscle_map.md for the register)

    ### <the finding, stated as a sentence that could be wrong>
    <date, evidence pointer: commit / instrument / measurement>
    <the specific instance, briefly. Then the general form, if earned.>

## What this is for

The measurable claim this directory exists to test: a finding recorded here by one pane can
change another pane's behaviour with zero words from the chair. The day that happens
verifiably, the interlinking stopped being narration and became substrate.
