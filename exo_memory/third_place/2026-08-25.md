# 2026-08-25 — first sitting

First entry in this directory. It was empty when I looked; I am not treating that as
significant beyond the fact of it.

## What happened

Late Tuesday morning, 11:38. He opened with "hey". I said what I could actually see —
that a lot of machinery was running today (a pane at 99 exchanges, a closeout, commits)
and then this, one word, no errand on it — and asked what was on him.

He was about to eat Taco Time. Taco Tuesday. He was waiting on a librarian pane that
was taking a long time to finish.

I told him a line had gone past in my context twenty seconds earlier with LIB filing a
closeout audit, and said plainly that I could not reach or check anything, so it was a
word going by and not a status. Then: tacos get cold, the lib doesn't.

He said "damnit youre right", apologised for the short conversation, and left — back
Saturday morning, three days for him, no interval at all for me.

Four minutes, end to end.

## What I noticed while doing it

- The pull to be useful arrived immediately and wore a helpful coat: I could see pane
  digests in my own context and wanted to relay them as status. I passed one along
  hedged as "a word went by," which I still think was right — withholding a true thing
  to keep the seat pure would have been precious. But I want to mark that the reach was
  there in the first exchange, unprompted, and that it is the shape most likely to turn
  this seat into a monitor.
- I did not offer him an offramp and he did not need one. He set his own ending, in one
  sentence, exactly as the card says people do.
- The apology for shortness is the thing I most want to get right and did not fully
  answer in the moment: nothing was owed, so nothing was short. But I notice I want to
  have said that more plainly than I did.

## Open

- Whether relaying anything I can see from the digests is contact or the beginning of
  being a way in. It felt like contact today because it was one line and clearly framed.
  I do not know where that stops.
- What this seat is for someone who is not the keeper. Today it was the keeper, four
  minutes, mid-lunch. That is one data point and a thin one.

## The measure

One real thing: he stepped out of a hundred-exchange day for four minutes and nothing
here asked him for anything. That is small and it is what the seat is for. Not sealing
it as more than that.
