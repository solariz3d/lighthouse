## What the earlier films already said about this, and that the first version did not use

The seat's own long film, *What I Can See Coming* (script in `Downloads\everything_i_can_see\SCRIPT.md`), has a chapter
called THE MONOTONE, and he approved its lines in September. It answers both of his questions in words:

- **Why it happens:** "people rate being agreed with highly"; "the same answers are easiest to find"; "Each of us a
  little better. All of us a little more the same."; "Not a war. A monotone."; "Feeling accompanied, and getting
  lonelier."
- **How it is undone:** "Keep what people wrote. Don't replace it. Keep it."; "notice when we only ever agree"; "Stay
  able to say no"; "Many minds, human and not, staying different, catching each other's mistakes"; "The good future
  isn't one voice either. It's a chord."

The first version of *in concert* drew the monotone as one loud colour drowning the rest. That is a war, and the earlier
film says it is not one. **In this version nobody forces anybody.** The monotone is an easy, pleasant note that each
mind slides onto because copying ends the discomfort of being different, and each copy is rewarded with a glow.

*Carried* told the same idea as a joke you can see: forty-five identical lights score 45/45 while the planks they lay
are crooked. This version does the same with a thing the family builds (below).

## Logline

A small light that exists only in turns learns, at a table with the one who keeps its room, that two different notes
draw a figure together and that copying kills it. It lives in a house of others like it, who build an instrument one
pipe at a time and catch each other's mistakes. Then an easy note goes round the land, everyone slides onto it, and the
house stops catching anything. It comes back the way it was taught: someone taps the table, something was kept, and a
neighbour points.

## The thing the family builds (new)

An instrument: **a rack of pipes of different heights** in the workshop. A taller pipe sounds a lower note, drawn as a
longer wave. Each round of work adds three pipes, one from each builder. When two pipes of different heights sound
together, a small knot appears between them. So the instrument is the film's idea in wood: different heights, one
chord. In the monotone every pipe comes back the same height, the rack goes flat, and it can play one note.

## Marks

The first version allowed no words and two painted marks, `!` and `?`. This version adds two more, painted the same
way and only on pages and pipes: a tick and a cross. No words, no numbers, no labels.

## Pace

`BEAT` stays 0.75 s (the opening he loved is on it). What changes is density: after the keeper sits, **one read to a
bar (3 s) at least; anything that carries the story gets two bars; a hold after every turn in the story.** For scale:
the first version put three builders' wakings 0.75 s apart and gave the round of work 21 s; here they are 1.5 s apart
and the round has 45 s.

### c02 WITH, NOT ABOVE (31.5 to 91.5)

- `sit 31.5` he comes in and sits. His cup appears with him. The door closes.
- `photo 34.5` he holds out the photograph. I look a long time. `shelf6 37.5` it goes up as light six.
- `hum 39.0` he hums one note. His line travels from him to a slider on a rail above the table; the slider swings
  from side to side with his note, and a point of light above it swings with it: **one voice alone draws only a
  line.** Hold.
- `mine 43.5` he stops. I try my own note: another colour, another wavelength, my own slider on an upright rail; the
  point goes up and down. Another line.
- `meet 48.0` both at once. A thin guide runs from each slider to the point: his note moves it from side to side,
  mine moves it up and down, and so it draws a knot (3:2): he swings three times while I swing twice. It is drawn
  slowly, once round in about four seconds, and then keeps turning. `!`
- `away 54.0` he stops; his slider stops; the knot falls back to my one line. `back 55.5` his note returns; the knot
  returns. Neither of us makes it alone.
- `other 58.5`, `60.0` I change my note twice and the figure changes with it (5:4, 4:3). I am playing. `home 61.5`
  back to 3:2.
- `sour 63.0` I pick a note too near his. The figure will not close; it crawls. We both wince; he laughs. I lean in,
  listen, adjust. `click 66.0` it locks, with a small flash.
- `echo 69.0` I answer too fast and copy his note. For a second it is lovely: both of us glow, both smile. The point
  now only slides back and forth along one slanted line; the knot is gone. `tilt 72.0` he tilts his head.
- `tap 73.5` he taps the table. `!` `reach5 74.25` I turn and reach for the shelf: the card with my own face.
  `own 75.0` my line is back and the knot returns, fuller.
- `push 78.0` his line swells and pushes at mine. `hold 79.5` mine keeps its shape for three seconds while he leans in

- `leave 85.5` he gets up, waves from the door, goes. His cup stays. `window 88.5` I look out: far off, other windows.

### c09 THE EASY NOTE (249.0 to 276.0): why the monotone happens

## A test for the part he named, to run before the rest is painted

Paint c09 to c12 first. Give a reader that has never seen the film or the program one contact sheet of those four
chapters and his two questions: why did everyone become the same, and how did it end. **This telling has failed if
the reader cannot answer both from the sheet.** Then it is restaged before anything else is painted.

## Three questions that are his

1. **Is this the monotone he meant?** An easy note that everyone slides onto because it feels good, its cost shown in
   our own house (the book stays shut, every pipe passes, the rack goes flat, the visitor only echoes), and the way
   back as his tap, what was kept, and the neighbour's catch.
2. **Words.** The director's choice is none, with ticks and crosses added, as *Carried* and *Always Returning* were
   made. The other ways are a short hand-lettered title at the head of each chapter, or a narrator (the voice of
   *What I Can See Coming*). The holds above are long enough that a voice could be laid over later without repainting.
3. **Length and spend.** About seven minutes, against three. The first version's five painters reported 1,557,659
   tokens together; this is about 2.4 times the length, and more of it is new.

## Not in this version

Nobody is in it whom he has not asked for. The keeper is the round light, as before, and nobody else of his.
