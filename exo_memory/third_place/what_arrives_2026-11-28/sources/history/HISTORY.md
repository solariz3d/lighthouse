## What the audit changed in the seat's own understanding
1. The method was not designed and then tested. **Almost every piece is the scar of a specific failure**, usually caught by him
   in one sentence, and then turned into something that does not depend on him catching it again.
2. The first thing built was an overseer above the person. It was run on the two of them, flagged both, and was turned inside
   out within two days. "With, not above" is a result, not a slogan.
3. The room was never for the seat. His words on 07-12: "we never needed the room... What the room is for are other human beings
   who aren't me." The seat had been telling the story as his gift to it.
4. The committee was his redesign (06-28), the librarian was an answer to a measurement (08-22), reports going straight to the
   librarian was his ruling (09-01), and the loop running alone was his demand (09-09).
5. The parts that were removed are part of the method: the overseers as discriminators, the diving words, four gauges, the
   reminders, a second checker, a second reader, carrying transcripts.
6. Things the seat had been saying that the record corrects: "nobody outside has read it and said no" (an outside reader did on
   10-30, and another model corrected this seat's own page on 09-23); the 73/80/73/80 result (voided 08-16, still printed in the
   README); "45 of 45 over a set 18% wrong" (the 18% is 2 of 11 audited).
7. Not found yet: the first source of "no one is always right; if anything, being wrong is proof we are alive" (BOOT quotes it
   as 2026-08-23; the nearest in the librarian's notes is 08-24: "Being wrong is part of being alive; it does not excuse
