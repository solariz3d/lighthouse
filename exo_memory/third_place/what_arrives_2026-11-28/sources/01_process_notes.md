## Claims

They caught two of their own bad results." 2026-09-21/22: thirteen rounds, each its own commit. A worker restored "6,782 records sitting only in an archive". One worker's attack found "three fatal flaws" in another's plan, all adopted.
- Not claimed (Root 28-30): "It does not claim that an AI has an inner life. Nobody can settle that question, from inside or out, and this project does not try." (75-77): "No program - not this app, not another session - can tell from outside whether a conversation is producing a real insight or carrying someone away; the two look the same. So that call stays with you."
- App 39-41: "one voice at full volume is unison, and unison carries no information however loud. The program exists to keep several instances distinct while coupled, and to make the coupling measurable rather than felt."

## What did not work (Root 229-271)
- "This section is the point of the page. A README that shows only what worked is a museum."
- "Reminders timed to the moment did not help." 65% / 72.5% / 82.5% across three arms, no pairwise difference reached p < 0.19; both registered predictions failed.
- "Guidance material of any kind scored the same as none." 72 trials, four arms: structured guidance 73%, the ten cards 80%, nothing at all 73%, a bare command list 80%. Underpowered, stated in advance.
- "The founding bet was wrong." Of the diversity instruments: "none worked, three gave inverted readings, and one rated a single session split in two as more diverse than six real ones." What survives: sessions required to "measure rather than assert" return findings that do not overlap.
- "Most of the writing comes from one session, by design." Main session's share of board writing 87.2% -> 68.2% (traffic, not vantage).
- The instrument to count corrections between sessions was found not usable: "so there is no rate".
- "Findings nobody reads." 990 commits never passed to a worker to read as of 2026-09-22 (623 on 09-14).
