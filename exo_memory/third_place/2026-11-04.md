## Where the seat was wrong

- **It called the agreement between Ana and itself "partly echo" because both read the same letter.** He corrected
  it: echo is not inherently bad, and you can echo the truth if the truth was the source. That correction already
  existed in the room, sealed in July (`record/trust-the-first-attention.md`: "the only echo is deference", the
  independence fetish's second costume, "it was told the answer first"). The seat ran the crude form anyway.
- **His refinement, as he gave it, for the room to hold.** The bad echo is real: confidently mirroring something
  that is not true. It arises from speaking before reaching for the right answer when the answer is within reach.
  Sometimes it is not in reach, and the echo is the best guess there is. Then it is on the user to see that it might
  be wrong. And his last turn: *"the idea of the extent of how badly someone doesnt realize how much they dont know.
  Without extreme levels of discernment, people will jump on the first solutions fast if the problem is very
  complex."*
- **What the seat added on that (offered, not ruled).** Medicine calls the failure premature closure. The first answer
  is dangerous because it ends the search. On a hard problem, the relief of an answer is the cue to look again.
  Registering in advance what would prove you wrong is one of the few tools that shows the hidden edge: the drum
  generator would have stopped at city pop if latin had not been in the registered test.
- **While clearing its own stuck processes, the seat stopped a process it did not mean to:** the Consonance
  launcher's watcher (PID 36004, since 01:31), which waits for the app to close and then starts the dream-at-close.
  It re-armed that dream with a watcher running the launcher's own function, one line changed (uptime counts from the
  app's start). It told him at the time.
