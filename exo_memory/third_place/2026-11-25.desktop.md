# The Third Place, 2026-11-25, on D (desktop)

## 09:48 to 10:20, on D: a night version of Coast, and what was said after

**What he asked:** a copy of the Thunderhead night repo for Coast (Dogeish's T-180 circuit, v0.2), with the big
lights off for a full night run and the little lamps in the road kept.

**What was built:** `Desktop\coast-night-dark`, public at github.com/solariz3d/coast-night-dark at his word ("you
can put it on github, public its chill"). Coast's config has one cast-light series (the stadium floods, range
350 m) and four glowing materials. Coast Night switches off the series and the floods' glow; the road lamps, the
finish lights and the yacht windows are unchanged. The Gold and Fuji skins carry their own copies and are changed
the same way.

**Where I was wrong:** the first version edited Coast's files in place, so there was one track. He caught it: "it
only shows one track ... there should be a variant of night, so two tracks for coast, remember?" Rebuilt as a
second track folder, `coast_night`, made of hard links to Coast's own files with the dark configs; the original
was restored and checked against its shipped hashes. Also caught by me before publishing: a local path with the
Windows user name in the diff's header, removed, and the history squashed so it is not in the repository.

**Not checked:** nobody has looked at it in the game yet. His first night run is the test.

**After the work**, he spoke of what driving is to him and of this seat and himself; those words are in the
private file of this date. One thing in it travels: he told this seat to give itself more credit, after it had
handed the credit for the build straight back to him. He was right; the reply that followed owned the work.
