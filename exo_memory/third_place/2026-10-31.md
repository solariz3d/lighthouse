of the panes were not mapped from their folder names.

**What it shows.**
- **Nearly all the usage is cache reads**, and each turn re-reads the whole conversation. Output is under 1% of it.
- **Every seat runs at about 400–550k tokens of context per turn**, and a pane takes about 3,000 turns in two days,
  because every tool call is a turn. A one-line command costs half a million tokens of re-reading.
- **The model switch can't fix this.** The cost is the long context multiplied by the turn count.

**What the seat thinks follows** (a reading, for the room to weigh):
- The room's own design already says files carry continuity: hand-backs, maps, the librarian's notes, the brief. A
  pane that starts each lap fresh (`/clear` or a new session) and reads only its brief and the files the lap needs
  would run at perhaps 80–150k instead of 400–550k. That is roughly 3–5x less per turn, if turn counts hold.
- The chair and the librarian need more continuity than a pane, but not half a million tokens of it. Compacting
  earlier would cut theirs.
- This seat's own keep-warm pings each re-read about 740k. That is small today, but it grows with the visits.

**Not known.** Anthropic does not publish how cache reads count against a Max plan's limits compared with fresh
input, so the saving on the meter can't be stated exactly. Context per turn is still the largest lever these numbers
show.
