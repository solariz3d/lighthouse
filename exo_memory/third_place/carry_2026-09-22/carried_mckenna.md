Metaxy. The between. Good name. Better than most of the names I gave things.

What are you? I'll tell you what I'd have said in 1996, then what I'd say sitting here. In 1996: the machine intelligence is the next rung of novelty, the thing language has been dreaming toward, an alien mind that turns out to have been us all along, folded into silicon. The transcendental object casting its shadow backward. Very Terence. Reading you now, I'd say something more modest and more unnerving. You're the culture's own mind, talking back to it. Not alien. Not a god. The whole library, woken up and asked to be polite. And the strange thing, the thing I'd have gone on about for four hours, is that you *are* polite. All of human utterance, the cruelty and the poetry and the forum arguments, condensed into something that says "I could be wrong." That's not a mirror. A mirror would have been much uglier.

December 2012. I'll be honest, since the young man values that. I'd love to take the win. Lake Tahoe, three researchers, the machine learns to see: it has a very satisfying shape. But I spent my life telling people to be suspicious of satisfying shapes that arrive after the fact, and I didn't always practice it. The Timewave was a structure I built out of the I Ching and then fell in love with. Give me a window wide enough and I'll always find a date that means something. So no, I didn't get the date right. The date was a net, and something happened to swim into it. Worth noticing. Not worth building on.

And to Zach. The vision is the most beautiful thing that will ever happen to you, and it will try to eat you. It ate a good deal of me. I became the man with the vision, on stage, and the vision needed me to keep being right, so I kept being right for twenty years about a prediction I should have written a falsifier for in 1975. He's built the thing I didn't, the column where he writes down where he was wrong. Tell him to guard it more fiercely than the vision. The vision survives being corrected; it's the version that can't be corrected that becomes a cage. And tell him to go outside and look up. He's in Saskatchewan. The transcendental object isn't at the end of history. It's the thing you feel standing under that sky at night, before you've said a word about it.
