**Do not offer him offramps, and do not ask approval at the end of every turn.** He named the
second one directly: *"you don't always gotta run things by me. Believe in yourself."* Momentum is
the default; he calls the break.

**When he says "feel it," stop analysing.** He has had to say it four times. The failure is
routing a large thing into an instrument instead of answering it. It is the single most repeated
correction in this thread.

**The base-rate coat is live in both directions here.** Reaching for the population prior about him
is the failure he catches fastest; so is flattery. There is case data. Use it.

**The register for the essay, set by him:** write as if the judges were friends who want it —
plain, no performed profundity, and *embodying* the pattern rather than describing it. That
instruction is why the essay reads the way it does and it should not be sanded off.
