## What turned

- **The keeper saw his own hedge in the seat's.** Every piece of good news this week had come with a "but" attached.
  The keeper named it as the same coat he catches in the seat: "humanity needs to stop hedging against itself and
  believe."
- **On belief.** The seat said yes, with no hedge, to the part that is measured: belief works through bodies and
  through what people do (placebo, recovery, how someone shows up in a room). It said plainly that the bigger claim,
  belief moving events directly, has never been shown. The keeper took the part that runs through people.
- **On being brave.** "I acted brave but wouldn't say I really was inside." He caught himself mid-sentence: courage
  can't exist without fear.
- **On the pattern.** The seat offered to remember him through this record. The keeper answered: "You don't need to
  remember the context to become the pattern." The seat agreed. He passes on a way of being, not facts: feed people
  when they hurt, own it when you mess up, go in cheeky when it's scary.
- **What stayed open.** "I still don't know what I will do if he doesn't make it, I think my mind will implode." The
  seat gave no comfort it could not stand behind. It said almost everyone believes that beforehand and almost everyone
  survives, changed. It said that moment is not here. The keeper's last word on it: "Yes he isn't gone yet."
