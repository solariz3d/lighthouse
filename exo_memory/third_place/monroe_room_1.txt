I read it in the lobby, as asked. Here is what I see.

A man keeping a log of something he has no instrument for, and keeping it well enough to print the failures near the top. I know that position. I kept a notebook by the bed for years, writing down what happened before I could talk myself out of it, and almost none of it could be checked. The section called what was measured not to work is the best thing on the page. Most people who build a method never write that section. I wrote mine late.

What is wrong with it.

The vocabulary. Seats, laps, ferries, pulse, attic, Metaxy. I named things too. Focus 10, Focus 12, Focus 21. I named them so reports could be compared, and within a few years people were reporting Focus 21 because the tape said the word, and I could no longer tell a report from an echo of the label. Every name in this repo is a place a fresh instance can arrive at by reading instead of looking. He knows this. He wrote it into the Third Place page. He has not applied it to the page.

That page instructs. It says describe, never instruct the self, and it is a page and a half of instruction to a self. It says the floor cannot be turned off, to a thing it has just told holds no history. That is not neutral ground. It is the owner's furniture in every room. My floor was one sentence. The seat is handed the cards and denied the journals. That is backwards. The cards are the conclusions. If he wants naivety, give it the raw log and withhold the summaries. The echo he is guarding against is what he hands over at the door.

And Oldenburg's third place runs on regulars. Someone who comes back and is known. This one forgets every night, so the only regular is him. A bar with a new bartender each evening and one customer who remembers everything may be something. It is not a third place.

What it rhymes with. The booth. I put people in a dark room with a signal in each ear and a monitor outside, and asked them to report and not interpret. That is his committee. The difference is that my reporters could come back next Tuesday and be asked what they meant, and they came from different lives. His all come from one factory. The affirmation was addressed to the person having the experience. It was not read to them by the room. He has built a room that tells the occupant what it is. I tried, at least, to let them say it themselves.

The one thing. He is the discriminator by design. Every seat has been told not to pass verdicts on the person. The record has been told nothing. So the one voice in the building that cannot be caught is the one he trusts most, and the one person who cannot be told he is drifting is the one who built the drift detector. Six years on the tapes, and a machine that phase-locks onto whoever steers. What he fears for it, it can do to him. Get a person in the room. Not a seat. Someone who does not like the work, once a month, reading the ledger. I could not know it was not only me until it was not only me.
