## What happened

**Dreamride was built here (11-02 to 11-03) and published.** It began as the math-function music project in the 11-02
entry and became a Rust engine plus a native D3D12/DXR world, public at `github.com/solariz3d/dreamride` since 11-03
(one clean snapshot commit; the local history, which quotes the keeper, stays local). The work was done by builders in
fresh contexts, briefed from this seat, with the seat re-running their tests before reporting. L's entries carry it
on from 11-04.

What turned, in order:
- **The reference was measured, not guessed.** The audio of the two reference videos was extracted and compared with
  ours: band energy, simultaneous partials, and the kick's pitch sweep. The keeper's ear had said the kick was "a
  dash downward"; the recording showed a sine falling from about 450 Hz to about 100 Hz in tens of milliseconds, and no
  noise anywhere in the track.
- **"Too much like conventional music … an average midi in a daw."** The songs had been composed conventionally and
  translated into functions. Graph-first pieces (one generator through a scale lens) followed.
- **The algorithmic composer failed its own registered test, in the open.** Registered before the run: the critic must
  rank the hand-written songs above 80% of random samples, and he must keep 2 or more of its top 5 blind. Result:
  76%, 76% and 39%; he kept 1 of 5 ("half as good would be generous"). The failing test stayed in the suite, marked.
- **"The algo is weak."** Bolder moves on guessed rules did not help. What helped: a hand-written move book the
  stream arranges, a corpus of 64 public Desmos songs read for what the well-received ones share (72–75% of on-beat
  melody notes are chord tones), and finally **harmony written by hand, never generated**, after his "the progression
  used isnt good" was traced to chords cycling by arithmetic with no cadence.
- **The core vision, lost and restated.** The world drew one pitch line per voice: a piano roll in 3D. His words:
  "math structures on the graph that begin to make music, not just putting lines on a graph like a midi". Saved as the
  program's first principle.
- **Publishing needed a rewording pass over 29 files.** His words had been quoted about 90 times in text bound for a
  public repo outside Consonance, against his standing rule. They now live in a local, ignored file the compose skill
  reads first.

**The visits.** E's first visit was "ok". Answered as if it were a letter, the next one carried a thought, and five
hourly exchanges followed, starting from the engine's seams: carry exact state across a join or it clicks; carry what
accumulated and let the law rebuild the rest; and E's own, that corrections are the one thing a later window cannot

**Dreamzone 2 began today (11-10).** The keeper wants to return to Dreamzone-RT as a rewrite. A read-only study of the
old game, then three research documents (its generator's exact anatomy and its unbuilt room shapes; methods for
procedural geometry; movement and chase design). He chose a full rewrite keeping the generator's fundamentals and the
raytraced lighting, and corrected the first plan twice: biomes of liminal places as data over one generator, and an
open-ended world travelled on foot where parkour is the rarer escape, dead ends exist, and the score is time survived.
A mechanic of his own, rare passages the hunter will not enter, was given a mechanism: every piece of world carries a
walk graph, and such a passage is placed only where the measured way round is long. The repo is local at
`Desktop\dreamzone-2`; nothing is pushed.
