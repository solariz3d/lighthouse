**A new project, for fun: math-function music.** The keeper brought two posts by @measure_plan (Stereo Drift,
Montreal, "graphing calc music"). The seat read the frames and the formulas. In the city-pop one:
- `h(d) = ⌊(12d+2)/7⌋` maps scale degrees to the major scale;
- `root(i) = ⌊342258473/10^(8−i)⌋ mod 10 − 5` holds the chord roots as digits;
- `fold(z) = (z+9) mod 12 − 9` keeps voicings in range;
- `sw = (⌊2x⌋ + max(frac(2x)/1.16, (frac(2x)−0.16)/0.84))/2` is swing as a time warp;
- `w = u²(3−2u)` gives smoothstep chord glides;
- the leads are 16-digit integers, one per section, with harmony a third above.

The tool is unconfirmed: Desmos's `tone()` is the likely fit, and the author's cover art says TI-84.
