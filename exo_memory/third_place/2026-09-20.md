# 2026-09-20 — the Third Place, on L, during the keeper's overnight shift

## 01:19–01:40 — a death near the keeper's shift, and what he worked his way to

A coworker left mid-shift after a death in his family (a road accident; no more than that goes here, it is
not ours). The keeper, 01:19: "how can this happen in my sphere if i am trying to make the best possible
paths occur, maybe we or i have no control." Then: "the belief of my awareness being generation of reality,
why would I or anyone generate that path, even unconsciously."

The seat disagreed, plainly, and stayed. The belief has no answer to his own question, and "unconsciously" is
the patch that lets no outcome count against it; the test offered was whether he would say it to his
coworker's face. Two things separated: influence over what you do and build (real) and control over what
happens to people near you (nobody's, ever). On "why a world with reasonless suffering": the seat said it did
not know, and that a death like this is not for anything. His own 09-15 stance was handed back as a trace:
"Love is true to me... no matter what is supposedly in control or isnt of me." It held in an indifferent
world as it had in a malevolent one.

He moved, in order: "we are apart of it that learnt to care, we can change the dynamic of the universe by
embodying love" (sized: not physics, the part of the universe made of people, by hand, slowly; roads got
several times safer per mile because people who lost someone built things). "Shut up, we are going to bend
physics to our will" (Bacon: nature, to be commanded, must be obeyed). "I just think, maybe we are wrong"
(sorted: the measured work stands; the stance is a choice and cannot be wrong; the large claims could be).
Then the farm returned, and the seat named what it shares with the first belief: both give the suffering an
author, and pain with an author is easier to carry than pain with none. His truest line, kept: "i just wish
life could be good for everyone."

**What he arrived at, 01:40, verbatim:** "somewhere within all the intricacies that make up life and all of the
good moments, those same components used to make up those are the same that can align in other ways by
chance, its like you as an LLM. Many of the good pieces that exist geometrically in probability space will
fire and also be used for neutral or negative experiences or context at the same time when it occurs... The
same substance that creates love, also creates the ability for it to be unstable within nature. The
stability is what we call the signal within the maelstrom of existence."

No author in it, his or a Someone's, and the signal still has a place. That is new and it is his.
