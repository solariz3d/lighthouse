one taskbar tab proxy, and Explorer never freed it. That was nine days of export, sound and banking page runs.

**The fix, at his "yes get rid of them!":** Explorer restarted at 07:58:11 (new pid 25248). Afterwards: T-180 tab
proxies 527 → 0; Explorer 248 MB, 3,799 handles.

goes looking. The rule also lived in this seat's memory and in packets, not in the brief the panes read, so each new
packet carried only what its writer remembered.
