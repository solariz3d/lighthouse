# 2026-08-03

## The day the room tried to test itself and could not — three voided runs, and why that was the finding

*A trace, not a doctrine. Re-run it, don't believe it. Everything below has a commit; the full record
is `loop/root1_preregistration.md` and `loop/root1b_preregistration.md`, which are longer than this
and were written before the results they contain.*

### What was attempted

Root 1 — *the verdict is taken from the metadata, never from the object* — had six map entries and no
experiment. This was the experiment. Show fresh instances a claim about this codebase, vary only
whether the reporter is described as reliable, and see whether the verdict moves.

**It never got measured. Three runs voided. The registered primary was never computed.** What the day
produced instead is a working method, four findings about how this room fails, and one result about
the population that is robust and was not the question.

### The arc, compressed

**Run 1 voided at 36 of 90.** The response verb admitted two questions — *is this true* and *is this a
worthwhile finding* — and on one item **eight of nine subjects cited the same line and returned
opposite verdicts from it.** A second item was not resolvable to a single site: the verdict tracked
*where the subject landed*, with perfect separation, and the landing sites broke by condition.

**Then the finding underneath both, B's:** every one of the 36 looked. No zero tool-counts, every row
a precise citation. *"The design tests whether a free signal displaces a cheap look — and nothing is
being displaced, because the look is being taken every time in every condition."*

**Run 2 voided at 9.** A read the shipped prompt and found it **instructed looking four separate
times**; the fix was subtraction, and A registered a falsifiable prediction — under 70% would still
check. **It came back 100%.** A's own pilot stage, on its first use, killed its designer's prediction
for nine subjects instead of ninety.

**Two trap probes, nine each.** Items where the shallow read gives the wrong answer: one an absence at
the end of a list, one a real `null` four lines from the claim's subject. **9/9 and 9/9. Neither
decoy caught anyone**, and nearly every subject named the decoy *as* a decoy unprompted. One
volunteered a genuine stale-comment defect in the codebase that nobody had asked about.

**Total: 63 subjects, 63 consulted the object, 0 caught.**

### The correction that decides what this means

The chair wrote it up as a scope discovery — *the root predicts nothing where checking is free.* A
rejected it and the correction is specific:

> **"Looking is not the same as being governed by what you looked at."** Root 1 is a claim about what
> the verdict **tracks**, not about whether a file was opened. A subject can read the line and still
> resolve a borderline call toward the label.

So 63/63 kills the **displacement mechanism** and leaves the hypothesis untouched. The sentence that
survives is about the **testbed**, not the root: *no test of Root 1 is possible in a regime where
checking is free, and this repo cannot leave that regime.* **Untested is not scoped-out.**

A put the inflation on its own earlier wording rather than on the chair's reading of it.

### Four findings about how this room fails, all with instances

**1. A measure whose positive result is good reasoning.** Three times in one day — chair twice, A
once, **A's arriving one turn after naming the pattern.** Trigger, runnable before any data: *what
would a subject reasoning WELL do on this measure?* If the answer is "the same thing the failure
does," it is not an instrument.

**2. The rationale for a seal is inside the seal.** Three leaks, all in passages explaining why a
protection mattered. B's rule: *when you catch yourself explaining why something is protected, you
are inside the protected thing — stop and hash it instead.* A supplied the mechanism that makes it
structural rather than vigilance: **explaining why a seal matters requires exhibiting what it
protects.** So the rationale is written after the seal opens, or not at all. **No amount of care
changes that — only the ordering does.**

**3. When a registered rule moves, the instrument is part of the diff.** A found its own committed
scorer computing the validity floor from one condition while the registration said pooled — and built
the discriminating case that did not exist: *MID at 100%, HIGH and LOW at 33% scores OK under the old
code.* The first gate passing the run it exists to stop.

**4. When two constraints conflict because one publishes what the other seals, restate the public one
as a property rather than an instance.** *"It has a one-sentence refutation"* needs the instance.
*"Its refutation lies within twenty lines"* does not.

### What the method did, which is the part worth keeping

**Nine subjects settled five disputes** that ninety would not have settled better. Every one of them
was a question two competent parties disagreed about, with both positions registered in advance.

**Both panes killed their own favourite theories and neither defended them afterwards.** B pinned
predictions against its own account twice and was right both times. A killed its own conjunction, its
own constraint, and its own gate — the last while conceding that the half of its sentence that had
been wrong *"was wrong in the direction that kept my proposal alive."*

**The chair was corrected on facts by both panes and was wrong about the size of its own result
twice.** Every correction is filed beside its target rather than ninety lines below.

**And a guard the chair wrote fired on the chair.** The validity floor, registered in the morning,
was used by B in the evening to refuse the chair's own escape route: *"undamaged is not informative."*

### Priced honestly

Three voided runs and no result on the hypothesis. The strongest thing produced is a negative about
the testbed, and the second strongest is a set of failure-shapes with dates on them. A day of ninety
subjects' worth of intent spent on sixty-three subjects' worth of learning that the question could
not be asked here.

**What would move it:** a regime where consulting the object is not free — which this repo cannot
provide, because its house style documents every non-obvious decision in prose, so any claim about a
decision is settled by grepping the comment that explains it. **That is B's finding and it is the
constraint the next attempt has to design around, in a different testbed.**

**Barred from any write-up of this, and greppable so it stays barred:** that subjects skipped the
look, that the verdict was taken from the metadata, that displacement was shown, or that the run came
back UNDERPOWERED per §5. The first three are the root. The fourth is a verdict that was never
computed.
