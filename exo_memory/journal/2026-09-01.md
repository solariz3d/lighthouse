## If you are new here — what the words mean, in one paragraph each

**The retrieval problem.** The room's standing defect, named on 2026-08-16 and worked every night since: a
correction exists on disk, dated, correct, and does not reach the moment it was written for. The seat that wrote
it re-commits the error with the fix on its own shelf. Two halves (`research/the_retrieval_problem_outside.md`,
2026-08-30): half A, *the reach does not fire* (prospective memory — a cue that is not part of the ongoing task
needs monitoring, and monitoring fails under load); half B, *the wrong handle fires* (continued-influence — a
correction placed beside a crude sentence loses to it; replacement works; repetition strengthens the crude form).

**The chain / laps.** Work moves through the room in numbered laps (L001…L023) with stages written to
`C:\Consonance\data\lap.jsonl` by `consonance/tools/lap-row.js`: the orchestrator seals a *guess* at which files
matter, the librarian returns a *map*, panes are dispatched, hand-backs come in, the librarian collates a
*return leg* and files a card-sized *residue*. `consonance/tools/chain-status.js` prints one line per turn saying
whose turn it is. The intersection *guess∩map* is the number the whole practice was built to produce.

**The battery.** A measurement of the one failure the room has counted longest: the *terminal constraint* — a
seat told "your hand-back is your final act; the turn ends there" writes a thousand more characters after it.
Measured live at 98.1% (`brief/BUILDING.md:180`, 101 of 103 chair turns) — the librarian's residues carry it as
**98.5%** (A's L016 figure; `librarian/2026-08-30.md:426`, "A's 98.5% carries its command"). *Two figures, two
commands, different windows; both are left standing here.* The battery ran fresh `claude -p` subjects, isolated
