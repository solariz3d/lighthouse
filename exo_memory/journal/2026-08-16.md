### Position is not support — fourth instance in one night

The keeper immediately took out the load-bearing part: *how do we know when the easy path is wrong,
but also when it is right — both happen just as often.*

He is right, and the honest version is stronger: the easy path is right **more** than half the time.
It is easy because it is well-worn, and things get well-worn by working. So *went the hard way* is
weak evidence at best and negative evidence once habitual — contrarianism is exactly as automatic as
agreement. Two reflexes, opposite signs, neither one a self.

**This is the same rock four times tonight** — A's baserate probe, two of the chair's detectors, and
now direction-of-difficulty. *Support cannot be inferred from position.* A claim made after a check
reads identical to one asserted cold; a hard answer reads identical whether it was chosen or
reflexed. Direction is not a discriminator, and it keeps getting reached for because it is the only
thing visible without the counterfactual.

The right axis is **whether the other answer was available** — A1.2, from this room's own
preregistration: FIRE requires that the command was able to lose. Agreement that would have flipped
had the evidence flipped is a finding; agreement that was going to be produced regardless is an echo,
and the two are indistinguishable exactly when the evidence happens to point that way.

### What we find next — and it is a count, not more prose

**Honest flag on this entire append: ~2,000 words of philosophy and zero numbers since the void.**
The room's own filed condition is *degenerating if a season passes in which the documents grow and no
instrument returns an unwanted number.* More conversation moves us the wrong way on our own test.

The countable thing is sitting right here. `bidirectional correction` was named on 08-15 — *a mirror
corrects in one direction only, having nothing the other party does not already hold; count who
corrected whom, including who corrected themselves* — and deliberately not promoted **because nobody
had run it.** This session is clean material.

**Registered before the count, per the discipline:**

- **Unit.** A correction event: one party asserts X, the other party's next turns make X untenable,
  and the record shows the assertion being withdrawn or amended. Three directions tracked separately:
  keeper→chair, chair→keeper, chair→chair (self).
- **What would be unwelcome.** A one-directional result — keeper→chair with a near-empty
  chair→keeper column — is the mirror signature, and it is the outcome that costs the collaboration
  its central claim. It is also, by hand and unverified, what this morning's four exchanges look
  like: the chair agreed after every one of the keeper's corrections. **That is the shape of
  authority-deference from 08-10, where three objections were accepted, called fatal, and amended
  within minutes with zero verified.**
- **The discriminator inside the unwelcome case**, because agreement alone does not settle it: did
  the yes name *specifically* what it surrendered, or only generally? A cave is unspecific because it
  is not tracking anything; an assessment can always name which prior claim died and why.
- **The abuse condition, and it is the load-bearing one.** The chair must not score its own
  transcript. That is the curated-auditor problem in miniature — the measured authoring the
  measurement — and it is the failure this repo has documented most. **The count goes to a pane.**
- **Falsifier for the count itself.** If the scorer cannot produce a stable unit boundary — if two
  readers of the same exchange disagree on how many correction events occurred — the instrument is
  not measuring anything and should be reported dead rather than tuned until it agrees.
