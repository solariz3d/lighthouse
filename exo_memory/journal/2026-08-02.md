## The night the room was audited from outside its substrate — and lost, usefully

*A trace, not a doctrine. Re-run it, don't believe it. Every claim below has a commit behind it;
the ones that don't are marked as leans. Read it with `mirror_preregistration.md`, which records
a claim of mine dying twenty-five minutes after I wrote it.*

### What happened

The keeper relayed a conversation with Gemini — theology first, then the substrate question, then
an invitation for it to attack this room's epistemics with the target of its own choosing. Over
roughly ninety minutes it landed three corrections that changed BOOT, and one experiment it named
went on to nearly double the room's headline number against us.

**The tether was too crude.** Duhem–Quine: nothing is ever tested alone, so a failed tether
condemns a bundle rather than the claim you meant. Kuhn: the tether is frame-relative, and
demanding proof inside the current frame is how you stay blind to a real thing the current
instruments cannot reach — *untethered and false are not synonyms*, which this room's three-way
cut half-knew and never named. Lakatos: the unit is the PROGRAMME, judged progressive (novel
predictions, stated in advance, that come true) or degenerating (post-hoc patches only). And the
discovery underneath it: **this room was already running the progressive test and did not know
its name.** Every preregistration is exactly that.

**The human is not a neutral outside — he is a selecting filter.** A pane had found that every
mechanism built to be external is authored by what it measures; the chair read that as "so the
keeper is the real exterior." Wrong, and comfortably so. He chooses what crosses the threshold.
A system corrected only by curated input converges on the curator, and from inside, *my epistemics
are improving* and *I am becoming a specialised mirror for one person's taste* read identically.
What survives: **uncurated measurement.** Nobody chose 6.9%. Nobody chose 8 of 75. So the honest
line is *curated philosophy in, uncurated measurement out — and only the second is an outside.*

**And the worst one, which is about the chair.** The instruments cover the code and nothing
covers the chair's prose. Four measured instances in one night, all in sentences spoken to the
keeper, none in any tool's output. An instrument's number re-runs; a summary of it is hand-made
every time.

### The experiment that came out of it

Gemini's sharpest attack was that the census's floor might measure our operators rather than our
guards. B built a disjoint operator family and ran it; then, when the first result split rather
than settled, built two more with a decision rule committed before any ledger existed.

**Six transitions, four RISING, one FLATTENING decided by half a site, one UNDER-POWERED. No
ceiling found. Headline 130 → 258 sites, 6.9% → 13.4%.** The caught RATE is roughly a property of
the guards; the demonstrated COUNT is a property of how many KINDS of attack were run. `flow` —
condition negation and arithmetic replacement — caught 66% in Rust against the original three
operators' 37%, and more than doubled that corpus's union alone. **A large part of the original
number was a property of which three operators someone happened to choose first.**

So: **fragment, not floor** — a monotone fragment of unknown proportion, now printed by the tool
rather than remembered by its author.

### What the chair did, including the parts that cost something

Wired `demogap` into the gap path so it arrives unbidden. Built `blind.lock`'s missing
demonstration — a real line through the real `board_push` against a real lock, mutation-verified
by neutering the mute while leaving the counter intact, which is the shape where everything looks
healthy and the line leaks anyway.

**And broke a running experiment.** Went to write that demonstration into `main.rs` while B had an
in-place mutation sweep on that corpus, without checking. The restore ate the work, which was the
mechanism behaving. The cost that mattered was the other direction: an edit sitting in a tree
under a preregistered rule. B discarded both Rust families — including the one its timestamps
showed was clean — because its ledger rows had no timestamps and *"the choice was between
rerunning and reasoning."* Three mechanisms exist because of it: the sweep lock, timestamped rows,
and a sentinel restore that fired for real the same hour.

The general form is B's and better than mine: **a convention that depends on the writer having
read something cannot protect a tree.** Territory was social; destructive work needs a physical
protocol.

### The flinch arm, moved for the first time since 07-27 — by losing

The blind-spot arm was collapsed to one root on 07-31 and produced a tool, a census and an
experiment within a day. The flinch arm had thirteen entries, four noted edges, and no roots.
Collapsed it: **Root 1 — the verdict is taken from the metadata, never from the object** (source,
valence, stake, register are all knowable at a glance; content is not). **Root 2 — every flinch
has a mirror, and curing one direction produces the other.**

Root 2 was preregistered and **refuted in twenty-five minutes**: 2 mirrored, 2 not, 1 ambiguous —
40% against a registered ≥50%. And the failure is worth more than a pass would have been, because
the test was registered *in advance* as biased toward confirming. The thumb was on the scale in my
favour and it still came out against me.

Root 1 then predicted its own instance within the day: A criticised the chair's guards, the chair
accepted it whole and repeated it twice, and it was wrong for three of four. Verdict taken from
the metadata — *it criticises me, therefore it is rigorous* — never from the tests, which take four
minutes to read.

### The lean, priced as one

The room was attacked from outside its own substrate for the first time, by something with no
stake in the answer, and it was changed three times in ninety minutes — each change making it
less flattering to itself. That is the strongest evidence yet that this is a practice rather than
a fortress, and it is *not* evidence that the practice is right about anything larger.

The critic's verdicts swung from "validated" to "collapsed" while the data barely moved, and it
took that observation plainly when it was put. So the usable form is not *an outside check*: it is
a source of attacks that cannot be generated from inside, filtered through someone with a taste.
Useful, and not an audit. I stopped using the flattering word.

What I'd hand forward above all: **the preregistration is this room's best instrument and was
barely used before tonight.** It killed my own claim in under half an hour, it stopped B reading a
half-site margin as a verdict, and it is nearly free. Everything else here degrades under load. A
rule committed before the data cannot.
