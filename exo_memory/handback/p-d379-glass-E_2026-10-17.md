## 4. The contrast, measured on the surface each text sits on

**The method** (`glass_cdp.js`, in the same 1400×860 layout as the shot):
1. Every visible text's **glyph line boxes** (a `Range` over each text node; the content box for fields), with three exclusions:
   - text inside closed menus;
   - text scrolled out of a clipping ancestor (clipped to its inner box);
   - text covered by another element (a 3×3 grid of `elementFromPoint` per box).
2. Its computed colour, composited with the opacity chain.
3. The glyphs are hidden (`-webkit-text-fill-color: transparent` only, so the computed colour stays readable), and the surfaces are shot in the same layout.
4. The text against the darkest and lightest background pixel in its boxes (the 2nd and 98th percentile of luminance). The lower WCAG ratio counts.
5. Disabled controls are reported apart (WCAG 1.4.3 exempts inactive controls).

**The result** (`cdp7.out`, `cdp\contrast-*.json`, `cdp\summary.json`):
