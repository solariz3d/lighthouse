## 2 · What it makes
- **The layers** (§5b's list):
  - `grain` (asphalt);
  - `noise` (fractal, tileable);
  - `gradient` (`bands`, Rainbow's hard bands, or `smooth`; any number of repeats);
  - `stripes` (runs along or across the road);
  - `lines` (a lane line: position, width, dashes, duty);
  - `panels` (a cols × rows grid, seams, a per-panel tone);
  - `glow` (an emissive strip with a soft falloff, also written into an emissive map);
  - `decal` (rect, circle, diamond or chevron; quarter turns; placed once, or `count` times along the road).
- **Blend modes:** over, multiply, add. The base is a colour. At most 32 layers.
- **A texture is text.** The same texture is always the same bytes: `serialize(parse(serialize(t))) === serialize(t)`. A hand-edited text (keys in any order, defaults left out, `#rrggbb`, float noise such as `0.30000000000000004`) loads and saves canonical.
- **Determinism:** the arithmetic is + − × ÷, floor, round, sqrt and `Math.imul` hashing only. No sin, exp or pow, which the language does not pin to the last bit. Quarter turns only, for that reason.
- **Resolution independence:** each pixel is the texture averaged over its footprint.
  - **Grain, stripes, lines, bands and panels** are integrated EXACTLY: a 1D periodic box integral, and area-weighted cells.
  - **Noise, smooth gradients and glow** are sampled at the pixel's centre. Noise octaves finer than the render can hold fade out (full up to 4 px a cell, gone at 2 px).
  - **Decals** are supersampled 4×4.
- **Contact sheet** (all four presets at 256²; scratchpad `tex\presets.png`, our own renders, not committed):
  - asphalt: grainy grey;
  - rainbow: six bands, red through amber, green, steel blue and blue to white, with grain and white edge lines;
  - lanes: white edges, a yellow dashed centre line with 4 dashes, chevrons;
  - neon-night: cyan and magenta glow edges over panels.
  - I first had neon-night's dark cross-stripes drawn over its glows, and moved them under.
- **Speed** (single runs, wall time, `inferred:` a shared machine): 256² takes 55–247 ms, 512² 96–469 ms, 1024² 511–1,641 ms. "lanes" is the slowest, from its decal's 4×4 supersampling. Fine per edit, not per animation frame.

## 3 · The seam for A (src/texture, part 1), CHECKED
- **`makeTexture(params, w, h) → { width, height, rgba }`** is exactly the shape A's `decodeImage` returns and A's `encodeDds` takes. `params` is the texture's text or object. Test "seam" (in `test/texmaker.test.js`): A's `encodeDds(makeTexture(rainbow, 128, 64))` gives a `DDS ` file with 8 mip levels whose level 0 reads back BYTE-IDENTICAL to the maker's pixels. At 512² it is 1,398,228 B with 10 levels (probe run).
- **`emissive`:** an RGBA map of only the glow layers marked `emissive`, or `null`. It is there for whichever slot A wants it in, since AC's emissive is a material property.
- **The slot stores the TEXT** (`serialize(texture)`), not the pixels, so it regenerates at the export's resolution. `TexmakerError.code` names every refusal, for A's messages.
- **Where A mounts the panel (proposed; A's files):** in `app/texture/panel.js`, each slot gets a "Make…" button. It calls `require('../texmaker/index.js').mount(root, { start: slotText || 'asphalt', onChange: (text) => slot.setMakerText(text) })`. The panel also sends `t180-texmaker-change` `{ detail: { text } }` on every accepted edit, if A prefers an event. At export, A renders `makeTexture(text, size, size)` and takes it down its PNG/JPG path to DDS. Nothing crosses IPC.

## 4 · Tests (`node --test --test-concurrency=4 test/texmaker.test.js app/test/texmaker-panel.test.js` → 23/23)
- **Determinism:**
  - the same bytes twice, text and object alike, for every preset;
  - **the same bytes in a fresh process**;
  - a pinned sha256 of "lanes" at 64² (`b9af9b9f…`).
- **Resolution** (the 512² render averaged 2×2 against the 256² render, per channel, in 8-bit levels, stated in the header before the first run):
  - **each exactly-integrated layer alone:** max ≤ 1, mean ≤ 0.5;
  - **composites and the point-sampled or supersampled layers:** mean ≤ 0.5, max ≤ 32.
  - Measured on the presets: rainbow max 0.50, asphalt 1.00, neon-night 4.75, and lanes 24 (its decal edges).
  - **CORRECTED, said plainly:** I first claimed the ≤ 1 bound for a COMPOSITE of the exact layers too. It measured max 22.75. Compositing (over, multiply) is not linear, so two layers that both vary inside one pixel break exactness. The header now says so, and the composite case is tested under the looser bound. That was my claim being wrong, not the code.
- **Each layer's parameters, in pixels:**
  - 8 stripes at width 0.25 are 8 runs of 16 px at 512 wide, and the offset is honoured;
  - across-stripes are 32 px runs;
  - a line at 0.5, 0.02 wide, is px 245–254 of 500; 4 dashes at duty 0.5 are 64 px on and 64 off at 512 long;
  - grain is one cell per pixel at `cells` pixels, 2×2 blocks at double, and the exact 2×2 average at half;
  - bands hold each stop (a 100 px / 100 px split), smooth mode interpolates (within 1 level), and repeat 4 gives 8 bands;
  - panel seams sit on the boundaries (named pixels), and the tone varies within ±30%;
  - the glow core is exact, nothing past the falloff, the falloff fades, and the emissive map holds only emissive glows;
  - a decal's area is within 1%, a quarter turn swaps its sides (named pixels), and "along" gives 2 copies; every shape draws;
  - noise amount 0 changes nothing, the deviation scales with the amount, and it tiles (the wrap is as smooth as the inside).
- **The text:**
  - every preset round-trips byte-exact;
  - a hand-edited text saves canonical;
  - `fmt(0.1 + 0.2) === '0.3'`.
- **Refusals by code:** BAD_TEXT (twice), BAD_VERSION, UNKNOWN_KEY (top level, and in a layer), UNKNOWN_LAYER, BAD_PARAM (range, kind, colour, stops out of order, zero size, a bad name), TOO_MANY_LAYERS, and BAD_SIZE (0, 8193, 1.5, '8'). The message names the layer, the key and the range.
- **The panel:**
  - an accepted edit changes the text;
  - a bad edit changes nothing and names its code;
  - add, move and remove layers, and load text;
  - the swatch is `makeTexture` byte-for-byte;
  - it draws one list item per layer with one control per parameter, and the swatch;
  - an accepted edit calls `onChange` and sends `t180-texmaker-change`, and a refused one sends nothing.
