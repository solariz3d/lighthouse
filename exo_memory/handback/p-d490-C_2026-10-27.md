## 4. How it is built (branch d490-c 9225530)

- **`app/preview/sky/arcs.js`** holds the shared GLSL, written here from E's description; nothing is copied, and no CC BY-NC-SA source was used.
  - A curtain is a thin sheet hung along a field dipping 77°. In field-line coordinates (u along the arc, v across it, h height) it is the sheet v = D + fold(u, t).
  - The fold is a long bend, plus domain-warped fbm folds drifting at about 0.1 km/s, plus curls whose positions are warped by a slower noise, so they roll rather than slide.
  - The hem is a sharp lower edge near 100 km. Above it the green fades, with a tail as long as the ray is strong, then red tops above about 200 km. A magenta lower border appears only under bright rays.
  - The rays are 2–3 km columns running along the hem at about 1 km/s. They fade to their mean where finer than about two pixels, so they don't shimmer. Brightness varies along the arc, with a patch that brightens over about 9 s.
  - **Sheet:** one hit (a Newton step for the fold), weighted by 1/|cos| to the sheet, so edge-on is brighter.
  - **Volume:** the ray is marched through the slab the folded sheet can be in. In each step the sheet's Gaussian thickness is integrated exactly, as an erf difference with the fold taken at both ends of the step. The light is taken where the ray crosses the sheet, or at the step end nearest it. That is alias-free and banding-free at any step count; the first version took the light at each step's middle, which left seams like fish scales (fixed, §6).
- **`aurora1.js`:** four sheets. **`aurora2.js`:** the near curtain a volume at 12 steps, full resolution, plus three sheets. **`aurora3.js`:** four volumes at 20 steps, `scale: 0.5`, with the stars in `skyOverlay`.
  - The four curtains sit at 45 km south and 35 km north (the corona pair), then 210 and 420 km north.
- **`app/preview/sky/index.js`:** the three styles are registered, plus `scaleOf(style)`.
- **`app/preview/renderer.js`:**
  - A style with a scale under 1 is drawn into a texture that size, then scaled up (linear) by a composite pass that adds its `skyOverlay`, so stars stay a device pixel (D416).
  - `setTheme({ skyCycle: 'a' | 'b' | 'none' })` picks a colour cycle. `sky/cycle.js` works it out once a frame and sends it through the existing uSkyA/uSkyB (D489 §10 way 2).
- **`app/test/sky.test.js:85`:** the style-list pin now names the three prototypes, with a comment. **The rule changed:** they are registered on this branch. It goes back to seven when the pick lands as `aurora.js`.
- **No skin uses them.** `aurora.css` still names `--sky-style: aurora`. The prototypes are reached by `setTheme` only, as the harness does. So the app on this branch looks as it does on main.
