## THE RULE (src/compose/stream.rs)
- `Ctx::keep_mode` (`:580`), set to `self.version >= 4` where a stage is composed (`:2239`).
- **A progression pick** (`book_progression`, `:1371`): the draw is v3's own.
  - A pick in another mode is replaced by one in the mode, drawn from a **side generator**: `Rng::for_candidate(r.clone().next_u64(), "MODE")`, so `r` advances exactly as at v3.
  - "In another mode" means the module writes a `c` that differs from the one playing (`other_modes`, `:1395`).
  - When the mode has no other progression, the pick fails and the progression stays (the dorian case).
- **The `Mode` move:** `Move::Mode if ctx.keep_mode => None` (`:1194`).
- **A species turn** (`:2176`): if the new species opens in another mode, it installs a progression of the home mode (stage 1's `c`), or the stream does not turn. Not in the packet's letter, but the "c never changes" row needs it: 971140 itself turns species at stage 9.
- `tests/book.rs` `the_grammar_remains_the_fallback_when_the_book_has_no_lead_left` is **amended by name**, with a comment: it builds a `Ctx`, so it gains `keep_mode: false`.

**Why a side generator, and not filtering the draw (my first build, replaced):** filtering the candidate list shifts every weighted pick.
- checked: under the filter, 971140 at v4 got `prog.deceptive` at stage 4 where v3 has `prog.axis`. The v3/v4 pair would have differed from stage 4, in more than the mode.
- A second try, rejecting and drawing again from `r`, still moved `r`. At stage 2 v4 then chose a candidate in a different key (below).
- The side generator gives identity through stage 6, which is what makes the WAV pair a test of stage 7.
