## How f33783f does it (for the rework to keep or replace)
- **The signal:** `turn_ahead` (game/world/world_app.cpp, game/ only, chase/ read, never changed). It sums the signed turns at the chaser's own path points over the next `kLeadTime` 0.8 s of running, weighted `1 − distance/reach`. That sum becomes a turn rate over `kCornerArc` 2.5 m and then a lean at the chaser's speed.
- **Straights:** a dead zone of 0.3 rad/s. The plan doesn't sway, so straights stay upright without L173's 0.5 rad/s.
- **The springs:** the lean builds at the old spring (6/s) and lets go at **10/s**. Without the faster letting-go, the bot's straights flipped 0.51 a second: a corner's lean spilled into the next corner's straight.
- **Fallback:** where there's no plan to read (a link, a drop, off its feet), it uses L173's measured turn.
- **Its weak point, inferred:** a path point turning a little is counted as corner. The plan is re-made every 0.25 s, so the lean can step at a replan; only the spring smooths it.
- **Toward the rework:** L176's lessons point the same way (`handback/p-l176-openanim-C_2026-11-12.md`). A lean from the eased velocity's acceleration (Overgrowth) reads the turn as it builds, and a direction damper (Holden) eases the yaw. I did not use them here.
