| 1 | **Aurora's dot matrix does not stay a dot matrix at Windows' fractional display scales (125%, 150%, 175%).** The tile is 3x3 CSS px with one dot, drawn at 3 px. At 125% a 3 px tile is 3.75 device pixels, and the browser resamples it: the dots smear across two device pixels, get fainter and the spacing goes irregular. Aurora's strength was chosen at 0.10 on the assumption of one crisp 26-level dot every 3 px. Small and a look issue (it cannot push a text under the bar: it gets fainter, not stronger) but 125% and 150% are the common Windows scales | **real (small, look)** | probe `scratchpad/tex/dots.js`: the real `aurora.png` as `background: url() 0 0 / 3px 3px repeat` on black, headless Edge with `--force-device-scale-factor`, one screenshot row read back (E's `app/test/png.js` decoder). Gaps between lit pixels (device px: count) and a lit pixel's brightness: **scale 1: all 3 (99 of 99), brightness 26**; **1.25: gaps 1 and 3 only (75 and 99), brightness 7 to 21, pattern 1,3,1,3,3,1,3,1…**; **1.5: gaps 1, 3, 4 (100, 49, 50), brightness 10 to 18**; **1.75: gaps 1, 3, 4 (150, 24, 75), brightness 6 to 18**; **2: gaps 1 and 5 (a 2 px dot every 6), brightness 14**.
