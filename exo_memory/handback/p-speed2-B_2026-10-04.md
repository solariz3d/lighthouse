**By reading,** reuseMesh keeps a piece only when its flag says same, its start pitch and bank equal the old ones (1e-12), and (for a

placed"), so a change upstream cannot shift a kept piece's samples. The loop's joint is always remade.

## 3. reuseMesh's same[] contract: **yes, a stale same[] gives a wrong mesh, silently.** No caller does that today.

Row C: reuseMesh, given an all-true `same[]` for a document whose widths changed (the start and the segment count unchanged), **returns
a mesh that differs from the truth, with no error.** That's by design (A says so): the flags are the caller's word.

The only caller is the track model. It flags a piece as same only when the **content hash of the segment actually meshed** (the coarse
segment at detail 6) equals the slot's, AND the lift key equals, AND the slot has the same number of segments. The slot is kept per
detail, and cleared on an open track. Everything else a piece's mesh depends on is checked inside reuseMesh: the start pitch and
bank, and the previous piece's font. So a stale flag needs a hash collision (D235: none in 2,471 real segments; A has since added my
P6 high-word assertions). **A future second caller would have to keep that discipline.** A one-line warning in the reuseMesh doc
already says "the caller's word".

## 5. Memory (two meshes kept): **bounded.**

- `slots` is a Map keyed by detail. The preview only ever sets 1 and 6 (`DRAG_DETAIL`), so at most 2 entries. An open track clears it,
  and each build replaces its detail's entry.
- **Measured** (`scratchpad/speed2/mem.js`, `--expose-gc`, closed tube lap, one model, **40 cycles** of two coarse drag steps plus the
  release to full): heapUsed after forced GCs, every 5 cycles: **177.6 → 212.7 after the first cycles (the coarse slot), then 212.7,
  212.7, 212.8 … 212.9: +0.2 MB over the last 35 cycles.** There's no growing chain of reused meshes. The last release reused 699 of 748
  pieces.
- It scales with track length (E: +19–24% heap on the keeper's two tracks), and that is the price of the 6.4× release.
