## 3. The Park: shapes, lengths, luck and erosion

**(a) Today** (`gen.h` `ReprieveRecord`: a vertical ladder duct between storeys, or a one-storey passage through the mass; B's
measures):
- 8 of 9 Parks he met were the same 9.1 m ladder duct; 1 was a 21 m passage;
- median time bought +1.3 s;
- the generator rates them by a detour about twice the hunter's real one (V5), so it keeps Parks that do not pay.

**(b) A kit of curated pieces** (hand-designed, his "currated designed"):
- **Joints:** every piece meets the next at a standard joint (the 3 m grid, a 0.9 m clear doorway at floor level), so any piece links
  to any other and any piece can be **mirrored**.
- **The pieces:**

  | piece | what it is |
  |---|---|
  | **entry** | a doorway with the Park's frame (`kMaterialReprieveFrame`), a hatch, or a vent |
  | **run** | straight, short 9 m or long 18 m |
  | **turn** | left or right (one piece, mirrored) |
  | **drop** | a ladder down one storey (today's duct, kept as one piece), or a chute |
  | **rise** | a ladder up |
  | **room** | the Park's heart: a small lit room, bench and lamp, where the golden light of item 26 can sit |
  | **exit** | the far doorway |

- **Linking:** a passage is entry + n middle pieces + exit, placed by the seed through the mass.
- The generator checks each passage like a cut: no clash, both ends on walk cells.

**(c) Length (item 94: "less chance after like 4 pieces plus"):**
- From the seed: after each middle piece, the chance of another is **0.7 up to 3 pieces, then 0.35**.
- Most Parks are 1-3 pieces; some reach 5-7; rarely more. "Able to scale big" is the tail.

**(d) Joined to the long hallways** (item 93; "not every time"):
- A proposed 30 % of passages enter from a corridor with 60 m or more straight and come out in a **parallel** corridor.
- The hunter must go round the block, so the gap is real.

**(e) Rated truly:** the generator measures each Park's detour with the **hunter's own travel model** (the mesh's speed at corners, A's
numbers), not its current graph estimate. It keeps a passage only if it buys at least `reprieve_min_detour_s` by that measure (V5).

**(f) Luck and upgrades without changing the world** (item 94; the librarian's proposal, here for his yes):
- **The seed lays out each passage at its full possible length.**
- The player's **luck decides how many pieces are open.** Pieces past that count are behind a **closed Park door**: drawn and there
  in every run of that seed, opening only at higher luck.
- So luck changes what is *reachable*, never the world (V7).
- **The chest chance and the erosion delay** (his other two upgrades) act *inside* the Park and do not touch its layout.

**(g) Erosion in a long passage** (items 54-56: 10 s after first entry, whether he stays or not; then ordinary dream space):
- **The problem:** a 5-piece Park takes longer than 10 s to cross at a walk. A flat 10 s would erode it under him, and a long Park
  would be worse than a short one.
- **Proposed:** erosion begins **at the entry, 10 s after first entry, and creeps along the passage** at a fixed speed.
  - Proposed **3 m/s**: faster than a walk, slower than his sprint.
  - Visible and audible as item 54's "hell red and screaming", following him.
  - A player who runs through is safe; one who lingers is caught by the red; the exit erodes last.
  - The erosion upgrade slows the creep, or delays its start.
  - Chasers may enter the eroded part only.
  - Deterministic: the clock is the run's tick.
