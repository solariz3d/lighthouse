## 1. Who he is in 2004-05

- **Age 16.** It is spring 2005 when the game opens (item 70's period, still his to decide).
- **Height 1.80 m, as `BodyBuild` is built** (`game/body.h:67-77`: a 1.8 m figure, eye at 1.65 m). This keeps the camera exactly
  where it is.
- **Tall for 16 and not grown into it.** Long limbs, narrow shoulders, no bulk. He shot up in the last year.
  - [INV] The lore reads this as the year it happened. It is never said, only seen (item 46's rule).
- **Persona: quiet, watchful, more capable than he shows.**
  - He says little to his mother (item 31's knocks go unanswered sometimes).
  - He is good at getting out of places: a skater's habit of scanning for routes, which is why the dreamzone fits him.
  - He is not a fighter. He runs.

## 2. The face (item 79: detailed, distinct, seen rarely)

**Where it shows:** the pick screen, waking in bed (item 34), the chair's out-of-body camera (item 49), a camera turned round.
In play the camera *is* his eye, so the face is never seen in first person.

**Key reads,** in order of importance, for the MPFB face targets:
1. **Tired eyes.** Mid-brown, slightly hooded, faint shadows under them. Not dramatic, just underslept.
2. **A long, narrow face that still has a boy's softness at the jaw.** Cheekbones not yet sharp, a small chin.
3. **Ears that stand out a little from the head.** One small, distinct silhouette read, even under hair.
4. **Brows:** dark, straight, the left one a few millimetres higher than the right, so his resting look is half a question.
5. **Nose:** straight, with a slight bump at the bridge. **Mouth:** thin lips, closed, corners neutral.
6. **Skin:** light olive (MPFB skin, tuned toward the sheet's `#c9a07e`). A few faint freckles across the nose; nothing else.

**Not:** a scar, a tattoo, piercings, or any "tragic hero" mark. Grief is in the eyes and the posture, not in a feature.

## 3. Hair

- **Dark brown, grown out.** Past the ears and into the eyes, with no side parting any more and a cowlick at the crown.
  - [INV] Nobody reminded him to get it cut. That is never said; the length says it.
- **Build:** opaque mesh clumps, not cards. The renderer has no alpha (every ray `FORCE_OPAQUE`, `dz.hlsl:108-109`), so hair
  needs real volume in 8-12 shaped locks.
- **First person:** the hair is in the head's own ray group (0x04, C's step 4), so it never hangs in front of the camera, but it
  casts its shadow.

## 6. Posture and movement character

**At rest (the pick screen, the room, waiting):**
- head about 4 cm forward of the shoulders;
- shoulders slightly rounded;
- weight on one leg;
- hands in the hoodie's front pocket, or thumbs hooked through the cuff holes.

**Running:**
- loose and long-limbed, more scramble than form;
- the arms a little wide;
- the hood bouncing.

**[INV] Proposed, a progression read:** as his physical abilities are trained (items 29, 33), the body's procedural pose gets a
touch more upright and the arm swing tighter. Posture is the visible record of growing up. These are pose numbers in `Body`, not a
new model.

**One thing for A to reconcile.** Pelvis 0.92 minus thigh and shin (0.91) puts a straight-legged ankle at 0.01, against
`ankle_height 0.07`.
- inferred: the procedural pose stands with the knees slightly bent, or the hip joint sits below the pelvis point.
- Fit the bone lengths to `BodyBuild` exactly. Read the standing hip height from the game's own pose (`Body::pose` at rest), not
  from this table.

**MPFB settings (targets, to tune by eye on the renders):**
- male, age ~16 (MPFB's age slider in its teen range);
- weight and muscle low (lean, not thin);
- height set so the eye lands at 1.65 m; limb proportions long;
- shoulders narrow, hands and feet slightly large (still growing).

**Skeleton:** MPFB's game-engine skeleton. Bone map to the 21 `Joint`s (Eye…ToeR) in `joint_map.json` (D659 models §2 step 2).
The Eye joint is a bone at the eye midpoint, parented to the head.

**Layer order for A's passes:**
1. **Pass 1:** L0 body fitted and rigged, driven in-game by the joints. The first-person check is the arms and hands.
2. **Pass 2:** L1-L3 clothes as separate meshes, the hoodie last (it overlaps everything). Then the baked maps (normal, AO) at 2k.
   The cuffs and hands get the most texel density.
3. **Pass 3:** the face, from §2's key reads, and the hair, from §3, for the pick screen and waking.

**Renders A looks at between passes:**
- front, side, three-quarter at eye height, in neutral light (D659 models §2 step 6);
