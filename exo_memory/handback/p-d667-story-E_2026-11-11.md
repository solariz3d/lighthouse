## 1. The premise, in one paragraph [HIS 103, 105, 106]

- **The purchase.** Spring 2005. A sixteen-year-old buys a used PC off a sketchy forum.
- **What is on it.** Rips of old Gateway tapes and a homemade program that plays them.
- **The first run.** One night, out of curiosity, he runs it. Something answers. After his first run, a cheque comes under the door.
- **The owner.** Whoever owned the PC before him went somewhere and did not come back, and the dreamzone is full of his traces.
- **The sale was not chance.** The owner was a stranger connected to the institute, an agency or some party, and he knew the boy's
  affinity. The PC found him.
- **At home.** He lives with his mother, who works nights. He talks every night to an online best friend. The seller will not let go.
- **The heart.** The PC room is the heart of the story.
