## 2. The same from the export's own flow, in memory (`S/d543/measure.js`, the Part 0 instrument, CRAZY TEST s 3096–3140 on p6; `crazy-p6-tile.txt`)

- **The floor u is 0 throughout.**
- **At each of 23 cell boundaries u jumps**: wall up to 2.03e-2, roof up to 4.04e-2 of a tile (20 cm and 40 cm at the 10 m tile). The jumps grow as the ring narrows faster.
- **Within each cell: 0.**
- The plan's formula at the roof, n·Δw/(2w) with n = 6, Δw ≈ 0.8 m a cell and w ≈ 50 m, gives 0.048; measured 0.040.
- **Why both rows of a cell carry one w:** `mesh.js` lays `Us` once per 2 m segment, and that w is the segment's END circumference. Example: 59.9669 at both rows, against the geometry's 59.9994 at the first and 59.9663 at the second. So inside each cell the texture is **held fixed while the surface narrows under it (a shear)**, then **snaps** at the next boundary.
- That is what the crop of his screenshot shows (`S/d543/shot-wall-stripe.png`, 2×): the cyan stripe and its thin white side lines cut into short pieces, each **shifted sideways and slanted**, with a seam between them.
- The floor near the car is smooth (u = 0 there), and the steps grow toward the roof.

## 3. The image (`S/d543/tex.js`, `elysium-512.png`)

**`t180b_elysium.dds`:** 2048 × 2048, uncompressed 32-bit BGRA, 12 mips. It is a **road-marking texture for one flat road**:
- a bright cyan centre stripe, with a thin white line either side;
- dashed white lane lines at about x = 1/6 and 5/6, each dash about 40% of the tile;
- blue chevrons at both edges;
- faint tile-seam lines.

All of these run along the image's y. Column means vary strongly across x (variance 982.5); row means hardly vary down y (1.1).

In the kn5, uv[0] is constant down each column and uv[1] runs along the rows: **dir 'along', as intended, 10 m × 10 m a tile** (v = −s/10).
- Round the 60 m ring, u runs from −3 to +3 (n = 6), so the marking repeats **six times round the tube**: six cyan stripes and twelve dashed lane lines over the floor, walls and roof. That is why there are stripes on the walls at all.
- **Could the staircase come from the lines meeting the 2 m row spacing?** No. On p5 (constant width) the same lines are continuous to the bit in the file. The steps appear only where u jumps.

**The asphalt preset** (`src/texmaker/presets.js`) is noise and grain only (blotches ~60 cm, flecks ~10 cm at the 10 m tile), with **no lines**. On asphalt the same u jumps would hardly show. This image shows them at their worst.

## 4. The lighting

The normals do **not** step at any cell boundary I read: 0.0000° on p5 and on p6, at the floor, wall and roof. They turn smoothly within cells; ksPerPixel interpolates them.

inferred: the "shading steps" in the shot are the texture's own segments (the bright cyan stripe and its glow cut into pieces, and the dark seam lines of the shifted image), not the lighting. The tube's 0.02 ambient makes the inside very dependent on the sun's angle, which is smooth here.

## 5. What this means for Part 1

The plan's Part 1 is the right fix, aimed at the right pieces: **closed rings whose circumference changes along the run** (his p3 and p6; any tube that widens, narrows or opens):
- one scale for the run's u (`u = n · arc / W`), so u at a fixed point on the wall is the same in every cell;
- u laid per ROW from that row's own arc, not the segment's end arc held for both rows.

That needs the mesh's per-row arc (today `Us` is per segment); flow.js already reads per row.

**The trade the plan names:** where w differs from W, the roof seam closes only to within n·(w − W)/(2W) of a repeat. On a 60 → 32 m narrowing that is large (n = 6: up to about 1.4 repeats at the 32 m end if W = 60). So **the run should probably re-choose n (and W) where the width changes a lot**: for example one W per run of rings within ±5% of each other, with a seam only at those few places. The plan's anchors in Part 1 should include his p6 (60 → 32 m) as well as the gentle 31 → 32 m case.
