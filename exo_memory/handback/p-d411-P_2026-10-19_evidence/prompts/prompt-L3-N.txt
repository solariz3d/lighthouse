# Task

In src/core/extend.js the edge clamp bounds each side to [max(lo, floor), cap]. With a numeric transition shorter than the piece, a side brought from −45.5° back to 0 rings above 0 (a control at +8.277°) and ends at +0.670° with slope 0.585, so the next piece is not plain and a lap is refused at the seam. A side asked back to 0 must land at exactly 0, from below as it does from above.
