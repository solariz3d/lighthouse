## 1. The test was wrong, not the layout

Row 17 loads the page in a frame a fraction of a pixel wider at 125%. It asks that the title fold there as it does at the whole width below, and it compared the title's widths after rounding them.

    checked: node --test --test-name-pattern="row 17" app/test/menubar-page.test.js at a6af8cf → "at 1099.20 px the title folds as at 1099 px (h1 59 px, not 60)"
    checked: the same row with the unrounded widths printed (a local edit, put back before the fix) →
      979: 15.6 vs 16 · 999: 15.6 vs 16 · 1099: 59.125 vs 59.53125

**Why it is verifiably wrong:**
- **Both widths at 1099 and 1099.2 fall under the same fold rule.** That is `@media (width < 1100)` (`app/index.html:149`); the neighbouring folds are at 1000 and 1320. So the same spans show at both widths.
- **The page at 125% lays the title's glyphs on whole device pixels, so within one fold it reads up to 0.4 px narrower.** At 979 and 999 that already happens, and the row passes only because both widths round to 16.
- **My 27116e0 (D547 Part 3) set the title in 12 px type instead of 13.** That put its width near a half pixel (59.53), where the two roundings split into 59 and 60.
- **A real fold change moves the title by tens of pixels.** It shows or hides a whole span ("T-180" below 1000, "Track Builder" below 1320).

**The fix** (`app/test/menubar-page.test.js:376`, AMENDED with a comment saying why): the two widths must be **under 1 px apart**. A fold still fails it, and sub-pixel snapping no longer does. The layout is unchanged.
