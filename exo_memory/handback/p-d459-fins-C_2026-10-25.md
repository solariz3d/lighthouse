## 8. The root cause, written down: t180 `docs/SEAMS.md`

The note covers the class of fault, not the instance:
- **Why two pieces' rows differ:** each piece picks its vertex count across the road from its own cross-section (`maxSeamDeg` 1°, `maxAcross` 1 m), densely where the
  profile curves. So two rows at one station agree in shape but not in count or spacing. That is normal and correct.
- **Why index-fraction pairing breaks:** with non-uniform spacing, the same index fraction of two rows lands metres apart along the curve. Both rows lie in one station
  plane, so the triangle between them stands in that plane above the surface.
- **The rule now, three parts:**
  1. Zip by lateral position.
  2. Drop a seam triangle that stands. "Up" is the road's own normal, never the world's: a loop or a tube is vertical and is fine.
  3. The export checks the mesh it is about to write and refuses a fin.
- **Why the checks missed it:** every surface check cast rays down, and a vertical sheet is invisible to that. The note also names the tests that hold the rule.
- **Its one claim about the sweep** ("12 of 30 seeds red on 0.4.19") is `E/sweep-base30.tap`.
