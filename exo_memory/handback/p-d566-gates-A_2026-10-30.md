**`src/markers/layout.js`.** `lapSectors` puts each gate at the nearest station to its third, searching both ways 2 m at a time up to a quarter lap, where `gateFlatAt` holds:
- the gate's L–R line is level within 15°, which is `FLOOR_MAX_DEG`, the grid's rule;
- it is at least half the road's width seen from above;
- the road climbs under 20°;
- the road faces up, so not the top of a loop;
- neither end is past vertical, so not a tube's roof.
