## 1. The scaling as it stands (cab3530)

**One clock drives every dial** [code]
- Each dial is `start + per_minute × minutes`, stopped at its limit (`chase/dials.cpp:41-48`). Minutes are `start_minutes + pace × t / 60` (`dials.h:86-89`).
- So **a difficulty level is a head start on the same curve, run at a different speed** (`dials.cpp:17-38`):

| level | start_minutes | pace |
|---|---|---|
| gentle | 0 | 0.6 |
| normal (default) | 0 | 1.0 |
| hard | 2 | 1.3 |
| brutal | 5 | 1.6 |

**The lesser chasers' dials** [code, `dials.h`]
- `lesser_max` 1.0 +0.5/min, limit 8 (:39).
- `lesser_spawn_s` 24 s −1.5/min, floor 8 (:40).
- `relax_s` 25 −1/min, floor 12 (:53).
- `peak_hold_s` 4 +0.2/min, to 7 (:52).
- The mix (:47-49): `mix_sprinter` 1.0; `mix_interceptor` 0 +0.2/min; `mix_pack` −0.3 +0.15/min. Below zero means not yet.

**How they are read** [code]
- A spawn happens only in **Build Up**, when the spawn tick is due and `lesser_alive() < (int) lesser_max` (`run.cpp:1141-1143`). **The cast floors**, so 3.5 allows 3.
- A **pack spawns 3 bodies** (`archetype.cpp:89`), clipped to the room left (`run.cpp:1093-1094`).
- **Placement** (`run.cpp:1101-1104`, `find_site` at `:1052`):
  - a "Direct" body (the sprinter, or a pack's first) prefers **behind** the player's run direction;
  - an "Ahead" body (the interceptor, a pack's flankers) prefers **ahead** (the `prefer` logic at find_site's line 22: ahead means `dot(site − player, run) > 0`);
  - **no site the player can see is used** (line 20: `see(eye, …)` → skip);
  - sites lie at 0.5–2.2× its reach, and never closer than `spawn_min_travel_s` (6 s of its travel).

**What that gives over a run** (computed from the dial rows; `node` over the formulas above, the floor applied by hand):

| level | at 0 min | at 2 min | at 5 min | at 10 min |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| gentle | 1 alive, every 24 s | 1, 22 s | 2, 19.5 s, packs start | 4, 15 s |
| normal | 1, 24 s | 2, 21 s, packs at 0 | 3, 16.5 s | 6, 9 s |
| hard | 2, 21 s | 3, 17 s | 5, 11 s | 8, 8 s |
| brutal | **3, 16.5 s, packs on, interceptors at full** | 5, 11.7 s | 7, 8 s | 8, 8 s |

- Spawning **does** prefer behind for the followers and ahead for the cutters, out of sight. I had not traced `prefer`.
- Corrected there too (its Corrections section).

**What is not there** [code]
- **Nothing reads progress.** No runs played, chapter or save is read (grep of `chase/` for run count, chapter, save: none). The dials depend on "time survived and nothing else" (`dials.h:7`).
- **The forgiving start does not exist:** brutal's first run is brutal's hundredth.
- A persistent run count lives nowhere yet. C's run record keeps the last 20 runs (`game\runs\`, D673), not a total.

## 2. The shape, from L4D (my L169 §1) [source: Booth GDC 2009]

- Mobs of **20–30**, every **90–180 s on Normal**, growing "from minimum just after spawn to maximum after a duration".
- "**75% of Mobs come from behind**", out of sight, by **flow distance**. Slow kinds spawn ahead ("Boomers spawn ahead").
- **Relax** 30–45 s with no mobs.
- [inferred] Dreamzone already has the out-of-sight rule and the behind/ahead split by role. It lacks two things: **groups larger than a pack of 3**, and **mob events** (a burst of several at once at a Build Up's height, with a long interval between). It is "behind the run direction", not flow distance, which is right for an open procedural building with no exit to measure flow toward.

## 3. Options

### Axis (a), the chosen difficulty: keep the clock as it is
- [option] Keep `start_minutes` and `pace`. They already make brutal start where normal is at 5 minutes.
- [inferred] His "putting it to brutal right at the start will be harder" is **already true** on the hunter, the mix and the counts.

### Axis (b), progress: three ways to make the first few runs forgiving

| option | how | what it touches | trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G1. Grace minutes** | Effective `start_minutes = level.start − grace(runs)`, with `grace = G × max(0, 1 − runs/N)`. E.g. G = 3 min, N = 5 runs: brutal's first run starts at 2 min (hard's start), its sixth at 5. | one number into `dials_at`; every dial follows | **Everything** eases: the hunter's speed, interceptors, packs. Simple and coherent, but the first brutal run is not brutal in feel, which his "harder" may not want. |
| **G2. A count ceiling by runs** | `lesser_max` capped at `cap(runs)` = 1 on runs 1–2, 2 on 3–4, 3 on 5–6, then the dial alone. The hunter untouched. | only the cap on line `run.cpp:1141` (and `:1093`) | Brutal stays brutal in the hunter and the mix, but **fewer bodies at once** early. Closest to "forgiving" without softening the hunter. My lean. |
| **G3. A count ceiling by chapter** | `lesser_max`'s limit (8) becomes per chapter: ch. 1 → 3, ch. 2 → 5, ch. 3+ → 8. It ties to item 28 ("the seeds get more chaotic", "further biomes become closer"). | the limit, per chapter | It needs the story's chapters, which are not built, and does not ease run 1 of chapter 1 on brutal by itself. **Pairs with G2:** G2 for the first runs, G3 for the long arc. |

### Group size and mob events (the L4D shape)
- [option] **M1, keep single spawns + packs of 3** (today). Cheap; no mob moment.
- [option] **M2, a mob event at Build Up's height:** when intensity reaches 0.7 in Build Up, spawn a **group of k** at once:
  - k = 2–3 on gentle and normal, 3–4 on hard, 4–6 on brutal;
  - **75 % placed behind** the run direction, the rest ahead;
  - **one mob per Build Up**, with its own timer of 90–180 s on normal (L4D's), shortened per level.
  - It needs `lesser_max` headroom, so the mob may exceed the cap by k for its own life.
  - **Trade:** the most L4D-like and the scariest on the look-behind (item 130: several sloshing round one corner). It is the biggest change to catch rates, and its bots-measured price must be registered first (A's paired method).
- [option] **M3, mobs only after the grace:** M2 off while G2's cap is below 3. The first runs never see a mob.

## 4. What the bots could measure, and what only his play can

**The bots can** [inferred, using A's paired 150-seed method from L169 and L170]:
- the flee bot's catch rate and survival time **per level × per grace step**, so run 1 on brutal can be placed between hard and brutal by number;
- **simultaneous chasers** over time (the max and the mean alive), and that the cap holds;
- **the share spawned behind against ahead**, measured against the 75 % target;
- that **no spawn is in sight** (the `see` rule), counted;
- **time to first contact** after a mob, and whether a mob ends runs at a registered rate (the falsifier to register: "M2 raises the flee bot's catch rate by more than X in both blocks, or it has failed its purpose", and its cutback price);
- that the director still reaches Relax (phase durations).

**Only his play can say:**
- whether brutal's first run feels **forgiving, or patronising**. "Harder but forgiving" is a feel;
- whether a mob **reads** as alive on the look-behind (item 130), which depends on C's lean and feet;
- whether the count of bodies is **scary or cluttered** in a 3 m corridor;
- whether progress-based easing feels **earned or rubber-banded**. `dials.h:7-8` rules out rubber-banding by distance; easing by runs played is a different thing, and a player can still feel it as one.

## 5. What it needs that does not exist [inferred]

- **A persistent count of runs played** (and, later, the chapter). It belongs to the save/progression system (D673's frame has no total).
  - Without it, G1 and G2 have nothing to read. A `runs_played` field in the run's parameters, passed into `chase::RunParams` like `run_length_s`, would carry it.
  - The replay record must store it, or old runs would replay with a different cap. As with `run_length_s`, "0 = today's behaviour" keeps every old record byte-identical.
