## 1. What the middle delete does today

- **Where:** `app/core/coreshell.js:826-870` (the shell), `src/core/piece.js:252-271` (`deleteRun`), `app/core/piecesui.js:59-99` (the selection box and the preview).
- **End vs middle** (`coreshell.js:834-843`):
  - When the selection includes the last piece, the pieces simply go, as one undo step (`:838-841`).
  - Otherwise it becomes a **preview** (`proposeDelete`, `:845-858`).
- **A closed track refuses** (`:837`): "CLOSED: a closed track has no open end to delete from; Ctrl+Backspace removes the last piece and opens the loop". `deleteRun` refuses it too (`piece.js:253`).
- **What moves** (`piece.js:256-270`):
  - It first tries the two sides joined as they are (`:259`).
  - Failing that, it rewrites the **first two control points of every channel of the first far-side piece** to the near side's end value and slope, so the joint is C1 (`:261-268`).
  - The far side keeps its own shape. Its **position moves as one rigid piece**, because every pose is integrated from the start through the pieces before it (the document stores no positions; `src/core/document.js:332-334` holds only `start` and `pieces`).
  - If neither works, it refuses with `DELETE_REJOIN` (`:270`).
- **The preview** (`coreshell.js:849-856`) shows:
  - the result as a ghost;
  - how far each later piece moves (`displacementAfterDelete`);
  - an overlap check, run off the thread when there's a worker.
  - **Apply waits** until that check is done (`piecesui.js:83`).
- **Apply** (`coreshell.js:859-869`):
  - writes the track to its backups first (`pre-delete`) and refuses by name if that copy fails (`:865`);
  - then commits **one undo step** (`:868`).
  - The preview belongs to the document it was made on; any edit drops it (`:213-215`).
- **Cancel** (`:870`) drops the preview; nothing changes. The buttons are `piecesui.js:63` (Delete selected) and `:72-73` (Apply delete, Cancel delete).

## 2. The document model, and what a layout needs from it

**The model** (`src/core/document.js`):
- **The document:** `{ schema, generator, name, closed, start: { pos, heading, pitch }, nextId, pieces[], spawns?, pitLane? }` (`:332-334`, `:447-454`).
- **One chain of pieces.** `checkDoc` requires every road joint to be C1 in every channel (`:392-445`, the JOINT error at `:432`; the rule is `jointProblem`, `:345-359`).
- **Closed** is a flag. The lap seam (last piece → start) is made by Close, not checked as a joint here. `appendPiece` refuses a closed track (`:336-338`).
- **Undo:** one history per document, `{ past, present, future }`, whole documents (`:514`, `:518`, `:526`). The undo history is saved beside the track (D272, `coreshell.js:91`).
- **The file:** one document per `.t180track` (`coreshell.js:942-946`: `storage.saveDoc(PREFIX + name, D.serialize(doc()))`).
- **Spawns** (`:211-220`): the start line is `line.along`, **metres into the FIRST piece**. The hotlap is a piece id plus metres; the grid is spacing only. The export anchors the line to the first piece's id (`coreshell.js:1112`).
- **Pit lane** (`:248-252`): anchored to piece ids plus metres (`leave`, `rejoin`). Neither block requires its pieces to exist; the export says so (`:219`, `:250`).

**A cut that leaves two open ends:**
- Not representable as "a chain with a gap": `checkDoc` makes every joint C1.
- **Representable as a rotation:** cut pieces `a..b` out of a closed lap of `n` pieces. The open track is:
  - `start` = the pose where piece `b+1` begins;
  - `pieces` = `[b+1 … n-1, 0 … a-1]`, the same piece objects and ids;
  - `closed: false`.
- Its start is the far end of the cut and its open end is the near end. Nothing built moves, and no format change is needed.
- What has to hold:
  - the old lap seam (piece `n-1` → piece `0`) becomes an ordinary joint, so it must pass `jointProblem`'s quantisation tolerance (`:359`);
  - `start` needs the pose at piece `b+1`'s start, read off the resolved path (`src/core/adapter.js`); bank, and the h/l offsets, come from the channels themselves (inferred: the start holds only pos, heading and pitch, `:397`).
  - inferred, not checked: Close leaves the seam C1 within that tolerance (`src/core/close.js:6-9` says the seam is "a joint like any other: C1 in every channel"). The check is one call: `checkDoc` on a rotated copy of a closed keeper track. I didn't run it (no probes this lap).
- **Edge cases the cut must refuse or handle:**
  - a cut whose far side starts with a landing (the flight before it was cut away);
  - the first or last piece being a flight (Close refuses a lap that ends in a jump, `close.js:246-247`);
  - a pit lane or hotlap anchored on a cut piece.

**Connecting the two ends with Extend and Close:**
- Extend works from the open end as always.
- **Close's routes** (`app/core/closejob.js:66-75`, `runCloseBest`): the near bend, the last piece, the last ~20% and ~40%, the whole lap, and **Finish to start** (`src/core/finish.js:1-12`: "ADDS road instead and moves nothing the user built", a Dubins turn–straight–turn of Turn by pieces).
  - **Only Finish to start and a local close whose window is just the connector's pieces** leave the kept road exactly as it was (`close.js:250-255`: outside its window, columns are fixed and pieces are returned as the same objects).
  - The bend routes move kept road: harmless for a copy, fatal for sharing (§3, Option 3).
- **The checker** reads the current document only (`app/validate-ui/index.js:131`, `history.present`). So it checks one layout at a time, whichever is shown.

## 3. The options

### Option 1: a layout is a whole copy, made by "Cut here…" (recommended first)

- **What the user does:**
  - selects a run on a closed track and picks Edit ▸ Cut here… (new);
  - the preview shows the two ends;
  - Apply gives a new **open** document (the rotation above), named "<track> <layout>";
  - he connects it with Extend / Close and saves it as its own track.
- **Undo, the checker, spawns and pit are per layout, for free:** each is its own document with its own history, check, `spawns` and `pitLane`.
- **Code touched:**
  - `src/core/piece.js` (a `cutRun(doc, from, to)` beside `deleteRun`, `:252`);
  - `app/core/coreshell.js` (a cut preview and Apply, modelled on `:845-869`, with the same pre-cut backup);
  - `app/core/piecesui.js` (one item beside Delete selected, `:49`, `:63`);
  - tests.
  - The start-line `along` must be re-expressed: the old first piece is no longer first. Either reset `spawns.line` with a message, or carry it when piece 0 survives. This is the one awkward bit (`document.js:213`, `coreshell.js:1112`).
- **File format:** none.
- **Export:** one AC track with N layouts needs a way to say which saved tracks are layouts of which. That's an export-side list (E's part), not a document change.
- **Risk to existing saves:** none; old files are untouched, and a cut never edits the source track.
- **Cost:** an edit to road shared by two layouts must be made twice. Drift is the price.

### Option 2: one file, several whole copies

- **What it is:** a container `t180b.layouts/1` = `{ name, layouts: [{ name, doc }] }`, each `doc` a full core document as today. The shell holds one history per layout and a layout switcher.
- **Code touched:**
  - a new parse/serialize wrapper beside `document.js:447-480`;
  - `coreshell.js` state (history, name, the undo sidecar of `:91`, recovery `:161`) becomes per layout;
  - open/save, plus a View ▸ Layout menu item.
- **File format:** new. An old builder must refuse it by name, the seal-V3 rule `document.js:26-27`, `:40`, as /4 and /5 do. Single-layout files stay `t180b.core/*` byte for byte.
- **Risk to existing saves:** low if single-layout files are never rewritten as containers. Medium for the undo sidecar and autosave recovery, which assume one document (`coreshell.js:118-121`, `:161-165`).
- **Same drift cost as Option 1.** It adds only one file.

### Option 3: shared pieces plus per-layout cuts and connectors (a bad idea now)

- **What it is:** a base lap plus per-layout `{ cut: [fromId, toId], connector: pieces[], spawns, pitLane }`; each layout derived as rotate(base, cut) + connector.
- **Why not:**
  1. **Any edit to a shared piece moves the gap.** Every pose is integrated through the pieces before it (§1), so editing a piece before a cut changes where every other layout's connector must end. **Every layout's connector would have to re-close after every edit to the base, unseen**, and any that fails leaves a layout open.
  2. **Close's bend routes and the brush's re-close move shared road.** A rate brush on a closed lap re-closes (`coreshell.js:506-507`), and the whole-lap close spreads its correction everywhere (`:297-298`). Each would change the other layouts. They would all have to be barred, or limited to Finish to start and connector-only windows.
  3. **Undo across layouts:** one base edit is an undo step in every layout at once. Today's history is one chain of whole documents (`document.js:514-526`), with nothing to say which layout a step belongs to.
  4. **The checker would have to prove every layout after every base edit,** not just the one shown (`validate-ui/index.js:131`).
  5. **The saving may be illusory.** If AC needs a whole road kn5 per layout (Aurora: `plan :1655-1656`; E's part decides), sharing saves nothing at export.
- **Code touched:** the document model, the history, the shell, the checker, Close, the brush and the export. **File format:** new, and every edit path changes. **Risk:** high.
- **What would change my mind:** E finding that AC can share road in one kn5 with only the differing pieces per layout, and the keeper wanting edits to flow to every layout. Even then I'd build Option 1 first and add sharing as "rebase this layout onto the edited base", run on demand, not on every edit.
