## 4. The fix — PLANNED, not built (it needs a ruling; and P is reviewing d434-o)

Target: every closer joint and the seam within his own joints' range at the outer wheels (≤ 21.7 °/100 m, his 90th percentile; at worst ≤ 24.3, his worst),
and the cup changing no faster than a value to be ruled (his own road does 0.021 °/m; the closer is 3.9 km, so his rate would fit a 27° change in 1.3 km).

1. **Ease the bank's roll RATE, not only its size and rate** (finish.js `bankPlan`): limit the change of the control-polygon slope between neighbouring spans
   too (a second limiter, the roll acceleration), so the ramp at a pinned joint starts from 0 and builds up instead of starting at the cap. Measured target:
   the u ±8.4 step at p41|p42, p42|p43, p43|p44 ≤ 21.7.
2. **Keep the bank's ramp off a laid roll:** limit the TOTAL bank (laid plus added), not only the added part, so where the roll straight is still rolling the
   bank does not add to it (p41|p42).
3. **Bring the cup home over the closer, not at the seam** (finish.js `lay`): the new road eases the cup from the end's value to the start's over its length
   (an extend target on the straight or across the route), so the seam's cup row has nothing left to do. Ruling owed: the rate (his 0.021 °/m, or faster).
4. **And separate them at the seam:** with (3) the bank's return in the last metres no longer coincides with the cup's drop.

**Cost:** one lap. finish.js (~40 lines: the second limiter, the total-bank limit, the cup target in lay), test rows red-first on TEST TRACK 10 and TEST 10 (the
u ±8.4 step at each closer joint and the seam against a bar ruled from his own joints; the cup's rate on the closer), the close and core harnesses whole,
