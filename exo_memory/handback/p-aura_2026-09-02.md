## 13 · THE TWO GOLDS, AND THE KEEPER'S TEST — MEASURED, NOT ASSERTED

*"if the two colours cannot be told apart from across the room, they failed their only job."* Hue
could not do this work — they are both gold, which is the ask — so the separation is on **three
channels**, computed rather than eyeballed:

| channel | WORKING | COMPLETE |
|---|---|---|
| shape | glow, no fill | filled block, no glow, rounded top |
| motion | breathes, 2.4s | dead still |
| brightness | `#FFC24A`, rel. luminance **.603** | `#9C8A52`, rel. luminance **.259** |

**Contrast ratio between the two: 2.12:1.** The chill gold against the page is **5.6:1**, so it
reads as *lit and settled*, not as dim. `prefers-reduced-motion` drops the middle channel
deliberately; shape and brightness still carry it, **which is why there are three and not one.**

test it is amber-versus-radiant, not the two golds.** That is the prediction; the glance settles it.

## 18 · THE SCROLLBAR — the chair's starting point is right about the cause and wrong about whose scrollbar it is

Verified at the CSS before changing anything, and the correction matters because it decides where the
fix goes:

**`#streambar` has no `overflow` rule, so it is `visible` — and a visible overflow draws no scrollbar
of its own.** It enlarges the scrollable area of the **viewport** instead. `html, body` are
`height: 100%`, so the document's horizontal scrollbar takes ~15px off the bottom of a full-height
layout; `main { flex: 1 }` absorbs the loss and the terminals get shorter. **That is the "unneeded
space": it is the document's scrollbar, sitting directly under the bar, and the bar itself never
grows — `height: 28px` is fixed.** Intermittent because it depends on content width.

*Checked and rejected as the place to fix it:* `#stream { overflow: hidden }` would clip
`#streambody`, which is `position: absolute` inside it (`app.css:439-441`) — the expanded stream log
would be cut off. `body { overflow-x: hidden }` would work and is global; it would mask a real
horizontal overflow anywhere else in the app. The rule went on `#streambar`.

**Why the row cannot shrink today:** a flex item's automatic minimum size is its min-content width,
and every chip in `.statuscluster` is `white-space: nowrap` with visible overflow — so each one
refuses to give up a pixel. The cluster itself is `min-width: auto`, so it cannot shrink even where
its children could. `#tapticker` was the only guarded item on the row. **At the 840px default window
the cluster's own min-content already exceeds the row**, which is why this was waiting to happen.

## 19 · WHAT THE KEEPER TRADES — the degradation order, and it drops nothing

Nothing is hidden and nothing vanishes. The least load-bearing readouts yield first, **every one of
them ellipsizes, and the `…` is the signal that there is more.** A chip that silently disappeared
would be the same defect class as tonight's placeholder — two states, one appearance.

| order | element | what it costs |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `#tapticker` | floor lowered 120px → 60px. It is `flex: 1`, so its flex-basis is 0 and **flex-shrink cannot reach it** — under overflow it just sits on its min-width. Lowering the floor is what frees the space; a shrink weight here would have been decoration that read like a fix. |
| 2 | `#status` | already ellipsized, but had no `min-width: 0`, so its automatic minimum was its content and it never actually gave anything back. Now it does. |
| 3 | `#chainchip` | ellipsizes, **keeps a 7ch stub**. It yields third and not first because after tonight the tab aura at the TOP is the primary display for holder and next hop; this row is the detail line. Its full text stays in the `title` on hover. |
| 4 | `#cost`, `#hud` | last resort. **The honest cost, and it is the only real one: neither carries a `title`, so a truncated value is not recoverable on hover** until the window is widened. They are written by `term.js`/`app.js`, not my files; adding titles there is a one-line packet for whoever owns them. |
| — | gate chip, `auto`, **⟳ summarize**, chevron | `flex-shrink: 0`, never shrink. They sit at the RIGHT end, which is where a clip would land, so they are the one group that must not be in the shrink chain at all. |

**`#streambar { overflow: hidden }` is a BACKSTOP and should be unreachable** — the chain above gives
back roughly 700px and the window's own `minWidth` is 700. It exists so an unforeseen future chip
cannot reintroduce the scrollbar. **Its one cost, stated because it is a real regression however
small:** the lit-pane glow (`#tappanes .tp.lit`, a 7px `text-shadow` in a 28px row) may clip by a
pixel or two at the very top and bottom. `overflow-x: hidden; overflow-y: visible` is not an option —
CSS forces the other axis to `auto`, which would add a vertical scrollbar to a 28px row.

**The aura is not regressed.** The four tab looks are on `.tabs button` in the top bar and are
untouched by any of this; `#chainchip` stays exactly where it was as the detail line.
