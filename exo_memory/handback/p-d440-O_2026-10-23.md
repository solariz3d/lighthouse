## 1. What was built

- **`closerLoad(doc, from)`** replaces `sideLoad`. In one pass over the closer it gives:
  - the peak lateral load at the design speed (as before);
  - how many curved stations (|κ| > 1/600 m) lean OUT by more than LEAN_OUT_DEG = 5°. That is core_finish_bank's `lean()`, the bar D434 ruled.
