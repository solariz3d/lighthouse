## Implications for E (§4, labelled as my inference)

- **Wrong-rate:** about 5–20% for unchecked state, higher for unchecked conclusions.
- **UNVERIFIABLE:** 10–35%, and **likely uneven across kinds**. Volatile state (panes, processes, board phase) has no
  commit to `git show`.
  - Report it per kind, with a degenerate clause.
  - This is a design risk, **not a mis-framing.**
- **Sample size to separate 10% from 30%:** about 25 verdict-bearing claims **per kind**, from an exact binomial test
  (α 0.05 one-sided, power 0.807). Clopper–Pearson intervals separate from n = 22.
  - Comparing two kinds needs about 62 per kind (normal approximation).
  - Then inflate for UNVERIFIABLE and clustering.

- **Overlap:** at least 40 claims. At 85% agreement that gives a CP95 of 0.70–0.94 (`node <scratchpad>/l119/cp.js`).
