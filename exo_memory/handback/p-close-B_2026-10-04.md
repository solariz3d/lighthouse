## 1. Items 1–3: the LOCAL close, the preview, and its overlap check

**`src/core/close.js`: `close(doc, { window })`.**
- Only the window's control points are columns (`parameters(doc, edited, free)` marks the rest `fixed`, weight 0).
- Every piece no free column reaches is returned **as the same object**. That is what makes "bit-identical" literal.
- **Refusals, by name (`CLOSE_WINDOW`), naming the window** ("can't close using only the last 200 m (p6): …. Widen the window or reshape the end"):
  - it does not converge ("gets no closer than X m (tangent T rad) in N steps");
  - the closing system is singular for that window (C's CLOSE_SINGULAR, re-worded);
  - the closed document breaks a `checkDoc` limit;
  - the window's roll rate goes past `ROLL_RED_DEG_M` 1.2144 °/m **and** above what it was before.
- **`closeWindow(doc, { fraction = 0.2, last })`:** the trailing road pieces covering ≥ 20% of the lap, always at least the last piece.
- **The whole-lap mode (`close(doc, { edited })`) is untouched**, because the sealed fixtures, `sculpt.js` and the core tests use it.

**`app/core/coreshell.js`.**
- `proposeClose({ fraction | last | whole })` commits nothing. It returns
  `closeProposal = { base, doc, resolved, window, report, displacement, check }`:
  - `displacement`: each piece's maximum centreline move;
  - `check`: the export's own checks on the closed track, meaning `buildMesh` selfCheck, then validate with `folds` and the built `roadMesh`. Its overlaps are self-intersection, stacked-within-2m and downforce-ray-gap.
- `applyClose()` is **one** undo step, and Undo returns the very same open document. `cancelClose()` drops the proposal.
- Any change of document drops a stale proposal.
- Panel: a "using" select (last ~20% / last piece / last ~40% / whole lap), then Close the loop, Apply and Cancel. The preview box shows the window, the pieces that move (top 8) and the reds as buttons.
- The preview draws the proposal as a ghost (`trackmodel.proposalGhost`).

## 2. Item 4: every red in plain words, and why the live panel was silent

**`app/validate-ui/redgroups.js`:**
- `groupReds` puts the overlap first, as ONE group, "the road overlaps itself". A downforce-ray red counts as an overlap only within 30 m of a self-intersection or stacked red; otherwise it keeps its own words (a gap).
- Roll-rate, load and lap-proof get plain titles.
- Each item carries s in km ("5.82–5.86 km") and its piece ("p6", or "p2–p8" for a range spanning pieces).
- `groupsText` lists each place once per group. The count is every red.

**Where it is used:**
- the export refusal message: `not exported: the road overlaps itself (32): at 1.40–1.43 km (p3), …; the road rolls too fast (1): …`;
- the red box (`app/index.html`);
- the preview box.

A click on a place sends `t180-camera-focus`. The preview's `focus(s)` switches to free mode, puts the eye above and behind the station, and looks at it.

**Why the keeper's live panel did not show TEST 1's reds** (checked: `why.js` on the TEST 1 copy):
- The live panel reports **14 reds: 13 stacked, 1 roll-rate.** The export reports **34: 13 self-intersection, 6 downforce-ray-gap, 13 stacked, 1 roll-rate, 1 lap-proof.**
- The cause: the live controller validates **without** the mesh. `app/validate-ui/panel.js` passes no `folds` and no `roadMesh`, so self-intersection and the downforce ray are export-only by construction.
- **Not changed** in the live panel, because building the mesh on every live revalidation is the cost it exists to avoid. The close preview runs that check once, and the export still refuses.

- after Undo the fields read X: 120, turn 30, bank 20, the bank tick on;

- Each Extend from the panel (`extendHere`, used by Extend and by Straight) remembers the fields it was made with, in a WeakMap keyed by the document it made.
