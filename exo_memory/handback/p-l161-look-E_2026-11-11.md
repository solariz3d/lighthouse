## What the renderer gives us to work with (read from `render/`, so the costs are grounded)

- **What it is:** one primary ray per pixel, traced inline in a compute shader (`TraceCS`, `dz.hlsl:299`). Then direct light with soft shadows, AO, one indirect bounce and fog, followed by TAA (`TaaCS`), bloom (`BloomDownCS` / `BloomUpCS`), and tone and grade (`renderer.h:3-6`).
- **Measured, RTX 5080, 2560×1440, average by pass** (`render/CHANGES.md:153`): trace and light **1.15 ms**, anti-aliasing **0.09**, bloom **0.07**, tone and grade **0.03**.
- **The budget is 2.78 ms p99** (`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:57`). The costliest views of the generated building already take **2.8–2.9 ms**, all of it in the trace (`CHANGES.md:179`).
- **So the rule for every effect below:**
  - **no new rays.** Anything that traces more is over budget in the worst rooms;
  - **a full-screen post pass costs about what tone and grade costs, ~0.03 ms.** inferred: by analogy to the measured passes; not timed.
- **The raytracer's gift.** Each pixel's ray direction is computed in the shader. So **any projection is free**: a fisheye, a stretch, a slow breathing of the view, slices of the world offset from each other. There is no rasteriser to fight. The one catch is TAA: it reprojects last frame by the camera, so a warp it is not told about smears. That smear is itself a usable tracer (below).
- **Already in the grade** (`renderer.h:84-96`): vignette, contrast, saturation, warmth, grain, chromatic aberration (`chroma 0.28`), split tone and black lift. Fog is `1 − exp(−(d·density)²)`, toward the void (`:78-81`). Several effects below are only these existing numbers, driven.

### "Out of body, lucid": what the reports describe, and which effect carries it

- **PsychonautWiki's "3rd person" perspective hallucination** describes an out-of-body view that floats above, below, behind or in front of the body (source L1, Perspective hallucinations).
- **Monroe's "Locale I"** is the out-of-body view of the ordinary physical world: "the material world… containing no strange beings, events or places" (source L5, quoting *Journeys Out of the Body*, 1971).
- **Which effects carry it:**
  - mainly **depth and perspective distortion together with haze**: the familiar world, seen slightly too wide and too far, through air that glows;
  - and, for a rare beat, the camera **lifting a little above and behind the body**. C's body this lap makes that visible. It is a camera offset, so free.
- inferred, from the reports above: "lucid" reads as clarity, not blur. The PsychonautWiki effect for that is visual acuity enhancement ("a heightening of the clearness and clarity of vision"). A lucid beat could be the one moment the haze clears.

## The lore: the documents first, later retellings marked

### The 1983 paper (the document itself)

- **"Analysis and Assessment of Gateway Process"**, a memorandum by **LTC Wayne M. McDonnell**, Commander, Det O, US Army Operational Group, US Army Intelligence and Security Command, **9 June 1983**.
- **Text read from two copies:**
  - Wikisource (L2);
  - the Internet Archive's OCR of the scan, a 28-page item (L3). It was checked against the Wikisource wording for ¶18, ¶19 and ¶21: **the same words, no OCR differences** in those sentences.
  - The CIA reading-room link returned only the reading room's front page, so the scan was read on the Archive.
- **The Absolute (¶18):** "energy in its absolute state, or simply 'the Absolute.'"
- **The in-between, in the paper's own words:**
  - ¶18: "various intervening dimensions to which human consciousness in altered states of being may gain access";
  - ¶18: "Between the Absolute and the 'material' universe in which we experience our physical existence…";
  - ¶21: "the intermediate dimensions beyond time-space";
  - ¶21: "those dimensions between time-space and the Absolute".
- **The way out (¶19):** consciousness "'clicks out' of time-space and joins infinity". ¶20 calls it the "click out phase".
- **Shapes:**
  - the **"cosmic egg"**, "an ovoid or egg shape" with the Absolute "layered over" it (¶24–25);
  - the **"energy balloon"** (¶29), "very reminiscent of the cosmic egg", as protection.
  - **"Bubble" is not in the text** (the librarian found this, and I confirm it).
- **Focus levels in the paper:**
  - Focus 3 (¶5, the basic tapes);
  - Focus 10, after which the participant tries "a state of sufficiently expanded awareness" (¶30);
  - Focus 12, which "involves conscious efforts on his part" (¶30);
  - Focus 15, "a very advanced state and… extremely difficult to achieve" (¶30G);
  - Focus 21, "the last and most advanced of all the Focus states associated with the Gateway training program", involving "movement outside of the boundaries of time-space" (¶30H).
  - **Not in the paper:** "Mind awake, body asleep" for Focus 10 and "no time" for Focus 15. Those are Monroe Institute usage, so later.
- **Other intelligences:**
  - "interact with other intelligences in other dimensions" (¶27);
  - "possible encounters with intelligent, non-corporal energy forms" (¶38 J; Wikisource; in the OCR copy that paragraph falls in the unread last 4k);
  - the energy balloon as "protection against conscious entities possessing lower energy levels" (¶29, as the librarian quotes it).
  - **The paper does not sort them into good and bad.** That is his (item 25).
- **Sound in the paper:** ¶7 describes beat frequencies heard when different tones reach each ear. ¶31 quotes Beta "around 2877.3 CPS"; ¶32 "resonance at approximately 7 Hertz". The paper never uses the word "binaural".

### Monroe's own terms (his books, through secondary sources)

- **The Locales,** from *Journeys Out of the Body* (1971), quoted by the SPR Psi Encyclopedia (L5):
  - **Locale I**, the material world;
  - **Locale II**, "a non-material environment with unknown bounds, which contains all aspects of what are known as heaven and hell";
  - **Locale III**, "an apparently parallel material world, different from ours in technology and social customs".
- **"Local traffic" and "interstate":** his later simplification, where the interstate means places more distant and immaterial (L5; no book named there). Wikipedia also gives his road image of Hemi-Sync as a ramp from the "local road" to the "interstate" (L4; uncited there).
- **Hemi-Sync:** binaural beats. Wikipedia's example is 170 Hz in one ear and 174 Hz in the other for a 4 Hz beat (L6).
  - **Its "frequency-following" claim is contested.** Stone et al. 2002 found the EEG change did not occur with air-conduction headphones (L6). That matters only if the game ever claims an effect; as a sound motif it is just a sound.

### Later retellings (marked; not in the 1983 paper)

- **Focus 22–27** come from Monroe's later books (*Far Journeys* 1985, *Ultimate Journey* 1994) and later Monroe Institute programs:
  - **Focus 23:** the recently dead, confused;
  - **Focus 24–26:** the **"Belief System Territories"**, where people with strong beliefs go after death;
  - **Focus 27:** **"the Park"**, the hub of a **"Reception Center"**, a way-station.
  - Sources are secondary and uneven (L4 uncited, L5 citing Russell 2007, plus search results). Not checked in the books.
- **"Mind awake, body asleep" (Focus 10):** Monroe Institute usage (an external-link title on Wikipedia, L4). Not in the paper.
- **"Page 25 was missing" from the CIA release:** a well-known later story. The Archive item does carry page 25 as a separate file (L3), which fits the story, but the story itself is not in the document.

### "Intermediary zones": the real term

- **There is no single coined term "intermediary zones"** in the 1983 paper, and I found none in Monroe either.
- **The paper's own words are "intervening dimensions" (¶18) and "intermediate dimensions" (¶21).**
- **Monroe's nearest named places** are **Locale II** (early) and the later **Focus 22–27 territories**, most of all the **Belief System Territories** (a retelling via his later books).

## The menu (ideas to choose from, not a design)

**Names of zones or biomes, drawn from the documents:**
1. **Depth as Focus levels:** a run descends 3 → 10 → 12 → 15 → 21, and each tier shifts the dream dial. It is the paper's own ladder, and Focus 21 is "the last and most advanced".
2. **"Intervening dimensions"**, the run's world itself: the places "between time-space and the Absolute". The Absolute is the unreachable end, never a level.
3. **Monroe's Locales**, three biome families: Locale I (the familiar world, slightly wrong), Locale II (thought-shaped, unbounded), Locale III (a parallel built world with other customs).
4. **The Belief System Territories** (later, Monroe): biomes shaped by one fixed belief each, a place where some entities are stuck.
5. **"The Park"** (Focus 27, later Monroe) as the name of a reprieve: the one place nothing feeds.

**Signs in the world, quiet:**
- a Focus number painted small at a tier's threshold;
- the egg shape in architecture, the cosmic egg;
- an ovoid light around the player when the good entity's "holy aura" fires, which is the paper's own energy balloon, ¶29, as protection.

**A sound motif (`audio/`):**
- a low binaural beat under the score (a 4 Hz difference, the Wikipedia example), deepening with the Focus tier;
- a tape-hiss bed;
- at a death or a deep descent, a click: the paper's "click out".

**Two easter-egg shapes:**
1. **The memo with a missing page.** Typed pages of an in-world report found one at a time across runs, paragraph-numbered like the real one. One page is never found. The real paper's §-numbers could set where each sits.
2. **The tapes.** A set of found cassettes, each unlocking the next Focus tier's name in the HUD. The last tape is blank except for the beat, and playing it in a reprieve gives a lucid moment: the haze clears, and the view lifts out of the body for a breath.
