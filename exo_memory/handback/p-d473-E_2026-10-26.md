## 1. What changed

- **`app/core/coreshell.js` `bankLap()`**
  - Runs `bankLapWith` (src/core/finish.js) on the closed lap.
  - The result is ONE undo step (`D.commit`), `lastStep.op 'bankLap'`.
  - Refusals, each with nothing changed:
    - An open lap says "not banked: the lap is not closed …".
    - A brush drag in progress says "finish the brush drag first".
    - A CoreError says "not banked: <reason>".
  - A guard compares every piece's `kh`, `kv` and length before and after. If any differs, it refuses with "not banked: the bank would have moved the route; nothing changed".
- **Status line**
  - The form is "banked the lap: N pieces (ids), up to X° into their turns; the route did not move (Ctrl+Z takes the bank back)".
  - It is followed by what was held back, in plain words:
    - the level rule: "no bank on p4: it climbs or dives too steeply to bank (a loop or a steep ramp)";
    - a withheld piece: "would lean out of its turn";
    - a flip: Close's own words;
    - the plan's sweep budget: `budgetNote`.
  - A lap with nothing to bank says "nothing to bank: every curve on this lap already has its bank; nothing changed".
- **`src/core/finish.js`**
  - The level rule used to drop loops and steep ramps silently, so nothing could say so.
  - `bankLapRun(doc, info = {})` now records `info.level`: the upright curves that rule kept out. `bankLapWith` returns it as `level`.
  - This is an added field only. Its other caller, `closejob.js`, reads none of it, and its output is unchanged.
- **`app/core/trackmenu.js`**
  - New item "Bank the lap", after Smooth ramps. Its `disabled: !closed` is read each time the bar calls `items(ctx)`, which happens on every open (menubar.js renderItems).
  - On an open lap its title says "close the loop first".
- **`app/index.html`**: the Track ▾ hover title also names "bank the lap". This is "just do it" text.
- **`CHANGELOG.md`**: under Unreleased ▸ Added, "Track ▸ Bank the lap, for a lap that is already closed".
