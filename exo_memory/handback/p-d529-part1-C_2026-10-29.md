## 1. The change

- **In `refresh`:** a state with no drag open runs the model's first step at once (`startBuild`).
  - A build that does not yield (a placement, a sculpt, an undo, the same document) is whole at once and shown that frame, as before.
  - A build that yields is a full rebuild. It goes through the existing sliced path, 3 ms a frame, which also makes the road lines and tangents ahead of the first draw.
  - Meanwhile the stage shows what it showed, and the HUD reads "… · building the road…".
- **The state the preview is mounted on is still built at once** (`refreshNow`).
  - **Correction:** my first version routed the mount through the sliced path too. Its `clockNow` is defined further down, so a preview mounted on a placed track failed to start ("Cannot access 'clockNow' before initialization").
  - It was 7 reds in `core-pieces-ui`, `preview-speed` and `sculpt-release`. My own new test began from an empty shell and could not see it.
  - Fixed by keeping the mount as it was; the 91 above are after the fix.
