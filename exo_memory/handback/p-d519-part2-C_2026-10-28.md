## 1. What was built, by O's sections

- **§2, the pick across** (`app/preview/preview.js` `pickAcross`, beside `pickAt`; the preview's `t180-pick` answers with it):
  - candidates are every sample's centreline and its two rims;
  - the 4 nearest within 40 px are refined on 41 points of their own section, then once at the half step;
  - it returns pickAt's `{ s, pos, px }` plus `u`, `at`, `axes { U, L }` and `side`.
  - The surface point (`acrossAt`) lives in `app/core/zonegrab.js`, which the preview and the panel both read.
- **§1, the zones** (`app/core/zonegrab.js`, pure):
  - `zoneAt(u, piece, mods)` follows O's table: 0.2 / the piece's own `s` / 0.9 / 1.25.
  - `axisOf(motion, axes, zone)`: the axis closer to the motion wins; lift wins a tie; under 4 px a metre of lift the drag reads out-and-in only; a zone with no L channel is always lift.
  - The Grab key is the zone grab. The other keys are unchanged.
- **The piece's kind** comes from the shell, through two read-only methods in `app/core/coreshell.js`:
  - `zoneInfo(s)` returns `{ id, cup, edge, tube, family, s, walls }`;
  - `zoneRefusal(channel, s0, r)` gives sculpt.js checkReach's rule, in the hover's words.
- **§3, the dots:**
  - `dotStations` (zonegrab.js), `placeDots`, `dotHit` and the overlay's `dotAt` / `dots()` (handles.js), and the panel's `dotsOf`.
  - Rows: the centre, the two rims, and ±0.5 on a cup piece. They are hollow, colours as O's §3.
  - Stations are at least max(100 m, the radius) and 24 px apart, 40 a row at most, the run's ends kept.
  - They are placed once per pose and run (the overlay's frame key), and left out while a brush is open.
  - A press on a dot opens the zone grab at the dot.
- **§4, the hover cue:**
  - the glyph per zone (`glyphOf`), at the hit's u;
  - the first line in the zone's words;
  - the second line is the family's line, or a refused channel's reason ("no cup on piece p1: extend with a cup target first").
  - During the drag, the value line is D425's own for the locked channel.

## 2. Where this differs from the design, and why

- **The tube's cup zone shows `brush-sweep`**, not `brush-twist`: its channel there is the sweep (`t`), so the glyph names that.
- **The dot stations are a knot span (20 m) apart from the run's start**, not the pieces' own control points. The panel cannot read the document's knots
  (the shell does not hand them out). KNOT_M is the documents' spacing, and placeDots thins to 100 m either way.
- **The Grab hint is shorter than O's §4 words would make it.** The hint line has no fixed height, so a longer one wrapped and pushed the stage down (§4's
  control). The new hint is 145 characters against the old 147, and the skin test shows nothing moves.
- **Shift and Ctrl are not wired to the zone grab's drag.** Today's Sculpt drags have neither, and adding them is beyond "as written". inferred: they would come
  from handles.js targetFor's rules if wanted.
- **The overlay's `handles()` still lists handles only**, and the dots come from a new `dots()`. Without that split, core-pieces-ui row 12 (which pins the
  selected piece's handles) went red. I changed the code, not the row.
