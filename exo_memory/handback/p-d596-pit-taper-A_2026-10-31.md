## 1. The change

**The lane (src/geom/pitlane.js).**
- **Width.** It is born at the road's edge NARROW_M (1 mm) wide and widens to its full width **over the existing diverge**, while it moves out as it always did. The merge mirrors it.
- **Mechanism.** The mesh's own profile blend: 'in' from the 1 mm section to the full one, 'out' the 1 mm section blended from the full one. The centre is set from the same width, by the lane's own arc length (three passes), so the mesh's inner edge is exactly where the lane says it is.
- **Unchanged:** the diverge, the run and the merge keep their lengths; along the run the lane is offsetM out, its full width and level; the parts are still in, body, out, so the boxes do not move. No tilt.

**The underside (src/export/pitlane.js `withLaneSkin`, one call in src/export/fromwords.js after `withUnderskin`).**
- Before, the road's underside skin stopped at the road's edge all along the lane, and the lane and infill had none.
- Now the lane cells get the skin by underskin.js's own rule (`skinOf`).
- The infill's skin takes, for each of its vertices, the skin vertex of the road or lane vertex it is. On the road side that is the road skin's own edge vertex, worked out the way the road skin works it out. So the road's, the infill's and the lane's skins share their joins.

**The infill** takes only the lane's inner edge, now that the lane is a sliver where it is born.
