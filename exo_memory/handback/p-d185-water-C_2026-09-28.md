## 2 · The water, as GO §5 says, and where it deviates
**The surface** is the adapter's shape: `surfaceFrom({ samples, closed, lengthM, profileAt })`. The samples are the
`{ s, pos, T, L, U, kvec, … }` that `src/geom` builds, and `profileAt(sample)` returns that station's cross-section. The road
is S(s,u) = pos + L·X(u) + U·Y(u), with X and Y exact from `profile.js`. Between samples:
- pos is a quintic Hermite through pos, T and kvec (C2);
- L and U are cubic Hermite, with three-point slopes for uneven spacing;
- two different profiles are blended by smoothstep (C1).

**The water:**
- RK4 in 3-D Cartesian, then projection after every step: position by Gauss–Newton in (s, u), velocity into the tangent plane.
- a = g + (N/m)·n, with N/m = κn·v² + g·(n·up) (references/06 §8.1).
- The energy is measured, never restored.

**The reds:**
- **LIFT-OFF:** N < 0.
- **SPILL:** u beyond the profile's edge.
- **SHOCK:** neighbouring streams' u(s) cross.
- Each red is located inside its step by linear interpolation.

**Deviations, with why:**
1. **A's adapter is not on main**, so `test/core_water.test.js` builds its surfaces with `src/geom` `buildPath`.
   - A's `toPath` is `buildPath` over ≤ 2 m segments (A's hand-back §6), so the sample shape is the same.
   - Test 5 through A's real document and adapter is `test/core_water_adapter.test.js`. It is SKIPPED on main, and **passes 2/2**
     on a scratch copy of A's worktree's `src/` + `tools/` with `water.js` added (§4).
2. **The energy base.**
   - The spec says "½v² + gz"; this repo's up is +y, so it is ½|v|² + g·y.
   - `energyTol` is held against the stream's starting ½v². The test scores B's way, against |E0|. Both are reported.
3. **A lift-off or spill STOPS that stream.** Its flight and any landing are not modelled.
4. **A flight's gap is refused** by name ("no road under it"). Pour each road run on its own. Jumps in the water are not done.
5. **A stream that runs backwards along s** has no u(s), so it is not compared for shocks.

## 8 · What this does NOT establish
- **The water on A's adapter with a VARYING width or rise-rate is untested.** A emits one profile per ≤ 2 m segment, and I blend
  it across the one 0.5 m sample span at each boundary. That is C1, but with a sharp second derivative there. A fast-changing
  w or r may therefore read as small bumps in N.
  - The "deepening dish" check shows a per-SAMPLE profile rides cleanly (drift 1.5e-6).
  - **The recommendation for A,** not done by me: let the adapter evaluate the profile per sample, from the channels at that s.
- **A cross-section that STEPS between two samples reads as a lift-off,** which is correct for that surface. The water shows
  the step; it never smooths it away.
- **Jumps:** the flight and the landing are not in the water (§2 deviation 4).
- **Tested only in these regimes:**
  - the textbook ring, a crest, straights, a twist, and dished rings;
  - no loop or inversion, and no wall past vertical.

  The surface form supports them (S(s,u) is not a graph), but no test puts water through one.
- **E's earlier `tools/water.cjs`** (R2, the mesh-triangle water, uncommitted) is a separate instrument. It is not reconciled
  with this one, and neither replaces the other.
- **Speed (spec test 6)** is extend and sculpt only. The water's cost above (0.63 s per 5-stream lap on 3.1 km) is a reading,
  not a bar.
