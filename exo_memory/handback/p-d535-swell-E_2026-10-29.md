## 3. What the plan got wrong, and the rows (the chair invited me to say so)

1. **"The swell begins with a nonzero slope, unlike the quintic; the bank moves > 0.1° by 5 m."** This is false.
   - A Bloss-bump rate starts at zero slope, so the bank grows like 8u³: slower at first than the old 10 m rounding, and about the quintic's 10u³.
   - No swell length that holds the bar meets 0.1° at 5 m (`S/d535/sweep.js`):

     | K | ramp | bank at 5 m | worst roll |
     |---|---|---|---|
     | 2.2 | 97.5 m | −0.094° | 1.024 °/m at 180°, over the bar |
     | 2.6 | 115.3 m | −0.057° | 0.867 |

   - The row tests what holds instead: |bank| > 0.3° by 10 m and > 2° by 20 m.
   - **Said plainly:** the swell leans later than today in the first 20 m (−3.5° against −8.0°). It is still far sooner than the box-off ease, and that is the trade the user's two sentences pull against each other. If his eye wants it sooner, the knobs are a shorter swell (a higher roll, still one bump) or an asymmetric ramp (quick in, long fade out). The asymmetric ramp would put the kink back at the start.
2. **"K about 2.0 to 2.2."** The swell needs **2.6**: at 2.2 its worst roll is 1.024 °/m (180°). The sweep:

   | K | his ramp | kink | worst roll |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | 2.2 | 97.5 m | 0.1072 | 1.024 |
   | 2.6 | 115.3 m | 0.0770 | 0.867 |
   | 3.0 | 133 m | 0.0580 | 0.752 |
   | 3.4 | 150.7 m | 0.0453 | 0.664 |

   **2.6 is the smallest K that holds both the bar and the halved kink.** At 2.6, 20° is 55.7 m and 54° is 150.4 m.
3. **"Box off keeps the quintic."** On this tree the panel sends `'rounded'` for every typed bank, so box off is rounded-linear over the whole piece, not the quintic. It is byte-identical either way (§4).
