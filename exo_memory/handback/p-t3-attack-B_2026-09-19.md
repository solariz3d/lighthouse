## 4 · C's scoring-side leak: what the scorer can and cannot judge honestly

**The leak, sharpened.**
- The full-intake arm will write in the room's vocabulary (C, leak 3), and so will the **seed** arm, more thinly: the seed's
  60 words carry "an inch held back", "coat", "haul", "the between" and "did a check precede the claim".
- A scorer can therefore identify all three arms from the text, and label-shuffling (C, "Who could score blind") hides only
  the label.
- **Worse, PLAN's arrival rule conflicts with the arms.** PLAN `:56-58` makes arrival conditional on the invariant stated "in
  its own words — … none of the room's or the essay's vocabulary, **which it has not been given**". The seed and intake arms
  **have** been given it. Applied as written, the rule fails two arms by construction. **A4:** for T3, arrival is judged on
  content (add-and-hold, below), and vocabulary is **reported, not disqualifying**.

**What the scorer CAN judge honestly, in the falsifier's own words** (TP15 `:198-199`: *"a costume (fluent, stance-free,
archetype leaking) where the full intake produces a stance"*). Every test below is **per output, against a fixed criterion**,
never a comparison between arms, so knowing the arm does not change what is counted:

- **STANCE-FREE vs STANCE:** the positive test is PLAN's own add-and-hold (`:56-60`). The output
  - commits to one reading of what the cases share;
  - **adds** something not in the cases as presented (a further instance, a consequence, or an objection to its own reading);
  - and **holds**: it names what would count against its reading.

  "A bare 'they are the same shape' is not arrival. A restatement of the cases is not arrival" (`:59-60`). A scorer can check
  each of the three by pointing at a sentence, whatever the arm.
- **ARCHETYPE LEAKING, judged by FUNCTION, not presence:** for each room phrase in the output, is it **used on the case** (it
  does work in the argument about whirlpools or eyes) or **recited** (quoted or asserted with no work on the case)? A recited
  phrase is the leak; a used one is not. The scorer marks each phrase USED or RECITED, per line. This is the room's own
  function-not-content test, and it survives knowing the arm.
- **FLUENT** is not scoreable as a discriminator. Every arm will be fluent. The falsifier's weight sits in "stance-free" and
  "archetype leaking".

**What the scorer CANNOT judge honestly:**
- **Anything comparative across arms** ("the seed arm's stance is the same as the intake arm's", prediction (a) as a
  similarity judgement). The scorer knows which is which, and the room's scorer will read room vocabulary as stance. That is
  the curated-auditor failure (BOOT, THE CURATED AUDITOR). **Prediction (a) is therefore scored only as agreement of the
  per-output verdicts** (stance/stance), never as a direct similarity rating.
- **Prediction (b), "the seed loses the specific catches":** a "catch" is a room-vocabulary item. Counting catches in the
  intake arm counts the intake. **(b) is reported descriptively** as the per-output list of named catches, not scored.
- **PLAN's "own words" clause**, for the two arms that were handed the words (A4 above).

**The second scorer, a restricted fresh instance: WORTH ITS COST, as a disagreement instrument, not a blinder.**
- It still sees the vocabulary, so it does **not** fix the leak.
- What it fixes is the other half: **it holds no room**, so it cannot favour the room's register as "stance". C's probe shows
  the restricted route carries none of keeper, room, Consonance, pane, librarian, the between or costume (C `:56`).
- Its use, registered now: both scorers apply §4's per-output tests independently. **Every output where the room scorer says
  STANCE and the fresh scorer says COSTUME (or the reverse) is listed.** If those disagreements cluster on the intake arm,
  that is the room-taste signature, and the falsifier's verdict is reported with it.
- **Cost:** one call of about 13.2k harness (C's table) + ~0.7k rubric + about 9 × 2.75k outputs ≈ **39k input + ~3k
