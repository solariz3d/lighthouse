## For his eye

- **Strafing at a jog** (from 2.7 m/s) the trailing foot now crosses **in front of** the leading one and comes back each
  stride, instead of both feet sweeping sideways along one line.
- **Strafing at a walk** the trailing foot closes up to the leading one and never passes it, a shuffle.
- **Walking backwards** each foot comes down **toe first**.
- **Seven dials, all OURS,** in `game/body.h`, set by your eye:
  - `kCrossoverSpeed` 2.7 m/s: where the crossover starts;
  - `kCrossoverBlend` 0.4 m/s;
  - `kCrossoverOffset` 0.12 m: how far in front the foot passes;
  - `kCrossoverReach` 0.12 m;
  - `kShuffleGap` 0.14 m;
  - `kBackToeDown` 15 degrees;
  - `kBackLift` 0.7.
- **Honest about the pictures:** in first person, looking down, your chest hides the feet most of the time, so the clips
  show little of this. The proof is the rows' numbers below. What the clips can show you is whether anything looks wrong
  when you look down while strafing or backing up.

## What changed

`game/body.cpp` (the run block) and `game/body.h`; rows in `game/tests/body_tests.cpp`; three clips in
`game/world/offscreen_fpbody.cpp` (`dz-world --capture-fpbody`). Sources:
- Kuntze, Sellers & Mansfield 2009, J Sports Sci Med 8(1):1-8, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0: the crossover is "movement of the trailing
  limb beyond the midline of the body".

These two short phrases, with their citations, are the only quotes in dreamzone-3. None of his words are there.
