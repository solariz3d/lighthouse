- **Sized from the CLOSED route:** bank by the laid route's curvature, close; swap that bank for the closed route's, close again (the bank row).
- **Every joint is the laid joint:** each piece's first two and last two bank points keep the laid bank, so a straight between turns gets none and a joint is C1.
- **Eases in, rolls under the amber:** between the pins the bank is rate-limited, forward and backward, to 0.9 × the amber (0.934°/m) per metre of the control
  points' Greville spacing; a cubic B-spline's slope is bounded by its control polygon's, so the bank's own roll is. A backstop scales the share down if the
  window still rolls past the amber; on these laps it never fires.
- **Only upright-laid turns are banked:** a turn laid on the user's own bank (a roll laid LAST, after the turns) keeps it; finish.js never rolls the road upright
  inside a turn. So Finish's choice of route is the one it made before on TEST TRACK 10 (roll first).
