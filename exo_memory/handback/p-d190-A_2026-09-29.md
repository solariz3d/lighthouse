## 1. What was built (contract for C: unchanged names)
- **Channel `c`** (degrees, 0 to 150; quantum `DEC.c = 6` decimals), in `CHANNELS`. **Schema `t180b.core/3`**; `/1` and `/2` files load and save as /3.
- **Cup piece vs legacy (V1/V6):** `P.cup === true` and a `c` array in its canonical text mark a cup piece; every other road piece, including everything read from /1 or /2, is legacy and renders through `profileAt(family, w, r)` UNCHANGED (its `c` is zeros in memory, absent in the text). A piece is a cup piece when Extend gets a `c` target, when the first piece is given `first: { c }`, or when it follows a cup piece.
- **Shape (V3):** `cupProfile(family, w, c)`: ψ at the four quarters is c·Fᵢ/F_edge on both sides. The r cap never applies.
- **Guard (V2/V5):** every c control point in [0, 150] else `BAD_CUP` (checkDoc, parse, Extend, brush); Extend clamps the fit's ringing to [0, 150] and makes a cup that ends AT a limit end flat (so the next piece can continue C1); the guard tolerates 1e-9 (close.js leaves float noise).
- **Joints (row 3):** cup ↔ cup C1 like every channel; carried through a flight; legacy ↔ cup: the RENDERED edge must be continuous to 0.05° (`D.legacyEdgeDeg`, a legacy piece's c for that purpose), so a cup after a legacy piece starts there; a lap closes c (value and slope) when its first and last road pieces are both cup pieces, and reports (`cupSeamStepDeg`) a cup meeting a legacy piece at the seam.
- **Readout (row 7):** `cupFromDeg`, `cupToDeg` on `pieceReadout`/`candidateReadout` (cup piece: c(0), c(L); legacy: the edge its road renders; flight: carried).
- **Brush:** `brush({ mode: 'value', channel: 'c' })` on cup pieces only; a window that reaches a legacy piece is `NOT_CUP`.
- **Additions C may use:** `first: { c }` on Extend, `toSegments(doc, { cupRuns })`, `D.legacyEdgeDeg`, `D.CUP_MAX`, `close`'s `cupSeamStepDeg`. Not changed: the channel name `c` and the readout fields.

## 5. Measured facts C, B and E should know
- **The Extend fit rings** (`checked`): a 90° target over 40 m of a 200 m piece gives control points up to 99.130° and a curve that peaks at **92.122°** before settling at **89.978°**; so "cup 90" ends within 0.03° of vertical, after a swing of 2°. The seal's V5 174.65° case is clamped: it can leave the curve SHORT of a high target: **150° over 20 m of a 60 m piece ends at 144.499°.**
- **Where the grid goes:** the layout reads `segment.profile`, so a lap needs an uncupped (or shallow) straight to put the grid on; the export test builds one by returning the cup to the start's edge in the last turn.
- **A cup piece brushed** keeps its road (lines, bank, lengths) outside the window; the blend expression of its segments is re-derived under `cupRuns`.
