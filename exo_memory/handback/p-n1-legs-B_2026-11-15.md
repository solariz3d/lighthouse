### 5.2 A third fault the look found, and its row: the hips between the feet

On the cut with the acceleration dropped (`course-after-v3`, since archived), backwards `runner_0140` still read tilted: both feet
together on one side of the body. The cause was in the plan:
- the trailing rule (half the reach) fired in an ordinary walk, and lifted feet ahead of the clock;
- a foot let go early then landed out of step, so the two feet fell in together on one side of the hips.

**The row, red first:** `the_hips_of_the_new_systems_body_stay_between_its_feet`. On every tick the body moves with both feet held, the
hips lie between the feet along the travel, to within 5 cm.

    checked: G:\consonance-scratch\b-n1e-red\rows.txt → "the runner's row: 8 ticks …; the hips at most 0.322 m outside the feet"; "the hunter's
             row: 50 ticks …; 0.390 m"; FAIL
    checked: G:\consonance-scratch\b-n1e-green\rows.txt, its print at the worst tick, after the first two changes → "speed 3.95, duty 0.37 …
             clock 0.514 …; along the travel from the hips: left +0.46, right +0.42" (the left foot landed early, both feet ahead)

**The fix:**
- the trailing threshold rises to 0.7 of the reach, above the landing's 0.6;
- a stance's half is capped at the landing reach;
- a foot lifted early is not lifted again until its stance window begins;
- **a moving foot's swing ends as its own stance window begins by the clock,** however early it lifted.

All four are ours, in `body_row.h` and `gait_plan.cpp`.
