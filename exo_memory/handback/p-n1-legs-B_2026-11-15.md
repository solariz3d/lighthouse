- **The bar:** the DRAWN ankle (the pose the models are posed from) holds within 1 cm from landing to lift. Non-vacuity: at least 30
  landings each.

**RED FIRST, on my own first build, and the cause fixed in the implementation:**

    checked: G:\consonance-scratch\b-n1-out-try1\row.txt → "the runner's row: 43 landings; a held foot's drawn ankle moved at most 0.4081 m
             (in the start and run)"; "the hunter's row: 38 landings; … 0.5456 m (in the start and run)"; FAIL

- **The cause:** a held foot was left behind as the body set off, and the leg, clamped at its reach, dragged its drawn ankle.
  - My first plan let a foot lift only on the clock's schedule or past the unlock radius.
  - Walking, it lifted no foot while the other was in the air.
- **The fix:** a held foot that trails its hip, along the travel, by more than half the leg's reach lifts, whatever the other foot is
  doing (`body_row.h` `trail_reach`, ours).
