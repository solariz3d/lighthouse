### 17.2 What the running leg is built from, each with its source

- **The angles:** the dataset's means, linear in the cycle and in speed (`run_curves.cpp`).

- **The plants:** heel, ball, or both (Kovar, Schreiner & Gleicher 2002, §3: "A footplant constraint consists of requiring either the

  a planted leg's bend (Johansen, 7.4.2, eq. 7.12-7.17).
