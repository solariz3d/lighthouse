## 1 · THE METHOD, fixed before any spectrum was computed (written 23:5x; nothing below this section existed then)

**The tracks:**
- **T-180: one layout per track, 13 tracks.** Rainbow Road, Centrifuge, Hazen Loop, Onuris (Long), Sakura Speedway,
  Coast, Nordic, Thunderhead (normal), Eagleton (full), T-180 Test Track, The Bowltrack, T-180 Bowl Track and Serpents
  Spiral.
- These are the corpus reads (`reads/*.read.json`, p-d182-corpus-E).
- **One layout per track, because** Onuris Medium and Short and Eagleton (short) are 34–85% the same road as their
  long layouts. Near-duplicates would make the T-180 group look closer to itself than it is: a bias toward PASS.
- Miandros is out (its read does not close), as are Aurora and the rest, as in the corpus.
- **The two normal circuits:** chosen below by what the reader can read, and named with the reason, BEFORE any
  spectrum is computed.

**The signals,** from each read's stations (road stations only; jump flights and junction stations dropped):
- **κ_h(s):** the reader's in-plane curvature `k` (1/m).
- **κ_v(s):** the change of the grade angle along s, d(atan(grade/100))/ds (1/m). It is taken on the uniform grid
  below, as a central difference.
- **bank(s):** the centre normal's tilt from vertical, `up` (degrees).

**The resampling:** each signal is linearly interpolated onto a uniform s grid with **Δs = 4 m** (the reader's own
step), from the first to the last station with a value. The mean is removed.

**The estimator: a Welch PSD.**
- Segments of **256 samples (1,024 m)**, a **Hann window**, **50% overlap**, one-sided, averaged over the segments.
- A track shorter than one segment would be excluded, but none is (the shortest is 2 km).

**The band:** frequencies from **1/512 to 1/16 cycles per metre** (wavelengths 16–512 m). Below it the 1,024 m
segment is too short to resolve; above it the reader's ±20 m smoothing flattens the signal.

**The summary numbers per track and signal:**
- **the falloff slope β:** the least-squares slope of log10 PSD against log10 f over the band;
- **the dominant period:** the wavelength (m) at the PSD's maximum in the band.

**The distance between two tracks' spectra:**
- For each signal, the PSD is restricted to the band and normalised to unit total power, so it is the SHAPE, not the
  level. A T-180 banks far more than a circuit, and a level difference would decide the question by itself.
- The distance is the RMS difference of log10 of the normalised PSDs over the band's frequency bins.
- A pair's distance is the **mean of the three signals'** (κ_h, κ_v, bank).

**"Closer", as a number:**
- **W** = the mean distance over all pairs of T-180 tracks (78 pairs).
- **B** = the mean distance over all T-180 × circuit pairs (26 pairs).
- **PASS iff W < B. FAILS if W ≥ B,** which is the falsifier as quoted.
- **Reported beside it, NOT part of the verdict:**
  - the ratio B/W;
  - for each T-180 track, whether its nearest other track is a T-180 track;
  - a permutation check. The group labels are shuffled 10,000 times (seed 1), counting how often a shuffle gives
    B − W at least as large as the observed. With only 2 circuits it is weak, and it is said to be.

## 3 · What the PASS does and does not show

- **The falsifier did not fire:** the T-180 spectra are closer to each other than to these circuits, in all three
  signals, for every circuit pair.
- **The "load-rhythm period" half has no peak to show.** The dominant period is 512 m, the band's longest wavelength,
  for nearly every track and signal. The spectra rise toward long wavelengths; there is no rhythm INSIDE 16–512 m.
  The prediction's "period" is not supported by this measurement, and not refuted either: the band cannot see a
  rhythm longer than 512 m.
- **Most of the separation is vertical, and partly because the circuits are flat.** Their κ_v RMS is 1.2×10⁻⁴ and
  3.9×10⁻⁵ 1/m against about 1.7×10⁻³ on the T-180 tracks (10–50× smaller), so their κ_v spectrum is closer to the
  reader's noise.
- **In turning alone (κ_h) the separation is weak, B/W 1.13.**
- **The strongest honest statement:** T-180 tracks share a steep, long-wavelength-dominated spectrum in cresting and
  banking, which these circuits lack. Their turning spectra are only slightly more alike than a circuit's.
- **Two circuits are two points,** read by a reader built for T-180 meshes. Fourteen of the
  seventeen circuits tried could not be used: twelve lost the road, reached the walk limit or crashed the reader, and
  two closed far short.
