**What is tried:**
- **(a) Stepping off.** The real controller (the walk proof's world: `sim::World` from the generated chunks) stands

  as a void: `CellKind::Void` (an atrium's air), `Abyss` (a void corridor), `Shaft` (a ladder shaft) or `Well` (a
  light well). The kinds are read from `Generator::cell`, never by hand.
