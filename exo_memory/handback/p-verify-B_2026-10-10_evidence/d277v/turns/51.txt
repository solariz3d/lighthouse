TEXT: Great idea, and it's queued. It fits the way AC works.

**How it works in AC:** a track's grip lives in a file called `surfaces.ini`. Each surface type has its own friction value, and each piece of road says which surface it's made of. Right now our export uses AC's one standard road surface for the whole track. Per-piece grip means one surface for each grip value you use, with each piece's road tagged with its own.

**The plan, after the current work (the Jump install, the bank wrap and the draw-call fix):**
1. **C checks the details against real tracks first:**
   - exactly how AC reads a piece's surface from its name;
   - whether the T-180 soft-road setting needs to know about the new surfaces;
   - what grip range T-180 tracks actually use.

   Getting any of those wrong would break the soft road, so it's facts first.
2. **E stores a grip value on each piece.** The default is 100%, AC's normal road. It gets saved, carried by saved pieces and mirror, and the export writes it out. Tracks where every piece is at 100% export exactly as they do now.
3. **A adds a Grip box** for the next piece and for selected pieces, plus a view that colours the track by grip.

**One honest limit:** the red-mark checker assumes normal grip. A slippery piece will drive differently in AC than the checker predicts, and it'll say so.
