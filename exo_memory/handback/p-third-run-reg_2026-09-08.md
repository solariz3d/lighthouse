## 4 · THE SENTENCE, AND WHY NEITHER READING WAS WRONG

`l045_read_brief_2026-09-08.md:87` kept its wording; the registration is the carrier and says so.

The brief carried **two scope rules in different modalities**: a deny-list of seven enumerated
actions with an explicit sanction (*"Each one voids you as a subject"*), and one sentence with none.
`consonance/ui/` is not on the deny-list. **B chose the rule with no stated sanction over the list
with one, and declared it before any score existed.** It did not bite in L039 only because that
object's defects did not require running the world — a latent ambiguity fires when the object changes
under it. **And the object made it worse: the census document prints commands to run.**

The replacement is §4 of the registration: a **governing clause** that names the condition line as
the only scope rule and the deny-list as a subset of it, then **three condition lines verbatim**,
each answering *may I open other files* and *may I execute* in the reader's own words, each with a
**required echo as the hand-back's first line**. The echo is a **gate**: a missing or altered echo
voids the run (F3) rather than scoring it. L045 discovered its condition split after the score
existed; this moves that discovery to before it. Text-only and text-plus-script also get an explicit
instruction that **an unresolvable check is a reportable item**, so a blocked reader lists the gap
instead of falling silent.
