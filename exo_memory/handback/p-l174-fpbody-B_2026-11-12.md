## RESULT
- **The look-back clip is fixed from its cause, and measured.**
  - **The cause (checked, read from the code):** `Shell::view` turned the view 2.9 rad (about 166°) **about the eye**, while the neck is 9 cm behind it (`BodyBuild::neck_back`). `glanced()` turned the chest only 50°. So the look-back looked from the front of the face back through the neck. Only the triangles that are at least half head or neck are put away for the eye (`stage.cpp`, `add_model`), so the collar, the shoulders and the upper back filled the picture.
  - **Before (checked, `dz-world --capture-fpbody`):** at the full glance the body covered **11.3 % (standing) and 12.1 % (sprinting)** of the picture, and **34–35 % of its bottom quarter**. At ¾ glance it covered 8.8–9.1 %. The neck was 53° off the view's axis, 16 cm away.
  - **The fix:** the eye goes round the neck as the view turns, as a head's does. `glance_eye_offset` (`game/body.h`) = `neck_back × (right sin a + forward (cos a − 1))`. At the full glance that is 17.7 cm back and 2.2 cm toward the turn. `Shell::view` and `glanced()` both use it, so the kid's locked eye stays on the camera. The view and the picture only: the rig, the run and the sim are untouched.
  - **After (checked, the same capture):** the body covers **0.0 %** at every glance stage (0, ¼, ½, ¾, full), standing and sprinting. The nearest joint, a shoulder, is 66–69° off the axis.
  - Pictures: `glance_before\sprint_glance100.png` (the shoulder and collar fill the lower right) against `glance_after\sprint_glance100.png` (the corridor behind, clear), and `stand_glance075` the same.
- **The first-person animation:** one clear defect, found on his run and fixed. The rest goes to him below.
  - **The arm snap (checked, `--capture-fpburst` on his run `run-20261112-080152`, 54 s, 682fb36).** At a sprint the forward hand held at the top of its swing, then fell 5.0, 6.6, 9.6 and 12.1 cm in successive 1/144 s frames, against a median of 0.9 cm (`sprint`, shots 21–24). In the 81° turn the right hand fell 9.0, 13.1 and 15.8 cm a frame against a median of 2.6 cm (`turn81`, shots 34–36). `sprint_20.png` shows the open hand held up mid-view; `sprint_23.png` shows it smeared on its way down.
  - **The cause (checked, read):** the arm took the other leg's `reach` (`hand(-1, reach_r)`). That runs linearly from +1 to −1 only while the foot is on the ground, a few hundredths of a second at a sprint, and eases slowly back through the flight. The hand copied it: hold, then snap.
  - **The fix:** each arm swings as a pendulum of the stride, `cos(leg phase − mid-stance + π·duty)`. It is forward as the other foot lands and back half a stride later, evenly in time. The feet keep their own contact profile.
