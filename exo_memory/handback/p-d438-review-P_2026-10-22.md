## 1. The checklist

1. **ROLL_ACCEL_FIT = 1.5: a rule, not a fit, because of how the roll straight is laid.** `extend` lays the roll's phi as `channelFn` (extend.js:34-39): the end's continuation blended to the target by `bloss(u) = u²(3 − 2u)`, which is a cubic from rest to rest when the end's roll rate is 0, so the laid roll's acceleration peaks at both ends at 6θ/L². With L = ROLL_ACCEL_FIT·2·√(θ/A) = 3√(θ/A) (finish.js:314) that peak is (2/3)·A for EVERY θ, and the outer-wheel step at the straight's two joints is 8.4 m·(2/3)·A = 1.12e-3 /m = 6.4 °/100 m, which is what O measured at p40|p41 (6.3/6.4). So 1.5 gives a fixed margin of 1.5 on the acceleration for every roll, not a one-track fit; at 2·√(θ/A) alone the laid roll's peak would be 1.5·A, over the bound (the rest-to-rest minimum 2·√(θ/A) is for a bang-bang profile, which the bloss blend is not), which is why 87 m failed and why O's tries at 2.0 and 2.5 changed the route and not the step. The margin is a property of the blend's shape, so a change to `channelFn` would move it; worth one line of comment at finish.js:312 saying so. **checked** across the sweep: the roll straight's own joints read 3.6–6.4 °/100 m at every roll from 10° to 150° at f5e1994 (the worst joints of the table are elsewhere, 14–18.4 at the turns' starts). **The one exception, inferred:** a lap whose last piece is still rolling at its end (a phi slope m ≠ 0 there; no `extend` makes one, a sculpted or hand-edited piece could) blends from that slope and the far end's acceleration becomes 6(θ − mL)/L²; `midroll438b.js` on such a doc (m = 0.00238 rad/m, 40°): Finish lays 178 m and the roll's control points overshoot to 41.7° before rolling down; its step I did not measure.

## 2. This is wrong, on laps built here (not on his two)

His two tracks are under the bar only because their closers are long (3.9 km, 2.6 km). Fix: ease linearly when 1.875·Δ/L > 0.021 (or always, J6: O measured the joints within 0.2 °/100 m of smootherstep's), and put the rate in the plan's report; one row on a short closer (turn 150, tail 300, roll 54: 0.0217 now). O's worktree carries exactly that linear ease uncommitted as I write (§4).
- **(b) The roll straight is bent by the close and leans OUT.** The roll straight (laid first, in the window) is closed with the turns; the windowed close gives it curvature, and it is not in the upright set (its phi runs θ → 0, outside ROLL_FREE), so it gets no bank: its own roll is then a lean the wrong way through a bend. `leanout438.js` at f5e1994, three laps: turn 210 tail 300 roll 30: 41 stations on the 154 m roll piece leaning out, worst **−26.3°** at R 595 m, side load up to **5.07 g** (its kh after the close reaches 3.07e-3, R 326 m, as tight as the R 300 turns); turn 210 tail 300 roll 54: 29 stations, −24.3°, 3.05 g; turn 210 tail 700 roll 30: 37 stations, −24.5°, 4.33 g. Fix, for the solver (not D438's scope as ruled, but the lap that lands it): keep the roll straight out of the close window when the roll is laid first (`finishToStart` :361, the window starts at `doc.pieces.length`; with `first` it should start one later), or hold its kh at 0 in the close; then a row with a built lap of this shape.

Fix, next lap: when a roll is owed, search `'first'` before `'last'`, or take the mode with the lower side load; a row from this pair.
