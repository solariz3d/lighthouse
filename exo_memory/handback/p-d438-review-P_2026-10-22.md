## 1. The checklist

1. **ROLL_ACCEL_FIT = 1.5: a rule, not a fit, because of how the roll straight is laid.** `extend` lays the roll's phi as `channelFn` (extend.js:34-39): the end's continuation blended to the target by `bloss(u) = u²(3 − 2u)`, which is a cubic from rest to rest when the end's roll rate is 0, so the laid roll's acceleration peaks at both ends at 6θ/L². With L = ROLL_ACCEL_FIT·2·√(θ/A) = 3√(θ/A) (finish.js:314) that peak is (2/3)·A for EVERY θ, and the outer-wheel step at the straight's two joints is 8.4 m·(2/3)·A = 1.12e-3 /m = 6.4 °/100 m, which is what O measured at p40|p41 (6.3/6.4). So 1.5 gives a fixed margin of 1.5 on the acceleration for every roll, not a one-track fit; at 2·√(θ/A) alone the laid roll's peak would be 1.5·A, over the bound (the rest-to-rest minimum 2·√(θ/A) is for a bang-bang profile, which the bloss blend is not), which is why 87 m failed and why O's tries at 2.0 and 2.5 changed the route and not the step. The margin is a property of the blend's shape, so a change to `channelFn` would move it; worth one line of comment at finish.js:312 saying so. **checked** across the sweep: the roll straight's own joints read 3.6–6.4 °/100 m at every roll from 10° to 150° at f5e1994 (the worst joints of the table are elsewhere, 14–18.4 at the turns' starts). **The one exception, inferred:** a lap whose last piece is still rolling at its end (a phi slope m ≠ 0 there; no `extend` makes one, a sculpted or hand-edited piece could) blends from that slope and the far end's acceleration becomes 6(θ − mL)/L²; `midroll438b.js` on such a doc (m = 0.00238 rad/m, 40°): Finish lays 178 m and the roll's control points overshoot to 41.7° before rolling down; its step I did not measure.

## 2. This is wrong, on laps built here (not on his two)

- **(a) The cup ease breaks the ruled rate on short closers, and the ruling's fallback is not there.** Ruled: "at no more than 0.021 °/m; where that doesn't fit, take the slowest rate the closer allows, and report it." `cupHome` (finish.js:248-259) uses smootherstep, whose peak is 1.875·Δ/L, with no fallback and no report. On 14 of the 45 built laps (Δ = 25°, closers 2.0–2.3 km) the peak is **0.0204–0.0377 °/m**, over the bar; the slowest rate those closers allow is linear, Δ/L = 0.011–0.020, under it. His two tracks are under the bar only because their closers are long (3.9 km, 2.6 km). Fix: ease linearly when 1.875·Δ/L > 0.021 (or always, J6: O measured the joints within 0.2 °/100 m of smootherstep's), and put the rate in the plan's report; one row on a short closer (turn 150, tail 300, roll 54: 0.0217 now). O's worktree carries exactly that linear ease uncommitted as I write (§4).
- **(b) The roll straight is bent by the close and leans OUT.** The roll straight (laid first, in the window) is closed with the turns; the windowed close gives it curvature, and it is not in the upright set (its phi runs θ → 0, outside ROLL_FREE), so it gets no bank: its own roll is then a lean the wrong way through a bend. `leanout438.js` at f5e1994, three laps: turn 210 tail 300 roll 30: 41 stations on the 154 m roll piece leaning out, worst **−26.3°** at R 595 m, side load up to **5.07 g** (its kh after the close reaches 3.07e-3, R 326 m, as tight as the R 300 turns); turn 210 tail 300 roll 54: 29 stations, −24.3°, 3.05 g; turn 210 tail 700 roll 30: 37 stations, −24.5°, 4.33 g. At 681727f the same bend is there (kh 2.03e-3 and 2.70e-3 on the 49 m and 87 m roll pieces) and one of the three already leaned out (17 stations, −32.5°); the longer straight gives the close more of it to bend. D434's "0 stations out" fails on 3 of 45 built laps at f5e1994 (0 of 45... no: 1 of 45 at 681727f). Fix, for the solver (not D438's scope as ruled, but the lap that lands it): keep the roll straight out of the close window when the roll is laid first (`finishToStart` :361, the window starts at `doc.pieces.length`; with `first` it should start one later), or hold its kh at 0 in the close; then a row with a built lap of this shape.
- **(c) Two built laps flip to a roll-LAST close and lose their bank.** turn 180 tail 1,200 roll 10°: 681727f roll-first, 4.71 g → f5e1994 roll-LAST, **10.89 g**; roll 30°: 4.75 → **9.16 g**. Cause: `finishToStart` tries `'last'` before `'first'` (:384) and takes the first route the car stays on; a roll-last route lays its turns at the end's bank, which the upright rule (D434) never banks. The longer roll straight moves the roll-last aim 89/154 m further back, and on these two laps that route now closes and holds, so it is taken. At 681727f 12 of the 45 built laps are roll-last and unbanked (4.4–11.2 g); at f5e1994 11 are. So this is D434's design (bank only on roll-first routes, shortest road first) that D438 moves two laps across; relative to what ships today (no bank anywhere) neither lap is worse. Fix, next lap: when a roll is owed, search `'first'` before `'last'`, or take the mode with the lower side load; a row from this pair.
