## 2. What was built

**The panel (`app/core/panel.js`):**
- **The list.** Each of turn, climb, bank, width, cup, left edge and right edge has a list of later changes right after its own row: "then" (in the field's unit), "from m" and a remove.
  - A field's rows are off, and not sent, while it has no target.
  - Rows are numbered for screen readers (`then bank 1`, `bank from 1`, `remove bank change 1`) and renumbered on remove.
- **No "+ change" button.** Per the third amendment, a change is made by dragging an offer on the ghost; the rows mirror the changes.
- **The options carry B's names:** `changes: { kh|kv|phi|w|c|e|er: [{ from, target }, …] }`, and as corners `changes.turnDeg` / `climbDeg: [{ from, deg }, …]` after the first corner.
  - With no change typed there is no `changes` key, so the options are as before.
  - Nothing is clamped in the panel. The core refuses out-of-range marks and short segments by name.
- **A missing `from` is laid** by `changeFroms`:
  - the first change at 85% of the piece;
  - a later one right after the previous mark plus that segment's minimum;
  - never before an early first ramp ends, nor past the room the later segments need (L − the sum of their minimums);
  - with one change this is L135's clamp;
  - corners split the piece evenly.
- **Marks.** A dragged mark keeps its share of the piece, so lengthening spreads the marks apart (his "inflated in between each other", the ruling). A typed `from` stays in metres; a cleared one is laid by the rule again.
- **Offers** (`offersAt`, form (ii)): for each field with a target, one offer where the stretch after its last change has room.
  - The stretch is R = L − (the last mark + that segment's minimum); a corner segment uses the core's `cornerMinM`.
  - The offer shows once R ≥ 2 × the core's `SECOND_FLOOR_M`, at the middle of R.
  - Dragging it (`secondOffer`) makes exactly one change on its first movement, then follows the drag.
- **Undo.** One Ctrl+Z (the existing undo guard) undoes the whole last drag on the ghost: it removes an offer's change, or puts a moved mark back. After Extend, Undo gives back every list (`fieldsNow` / `putBack`). A new document clears them.
- **Every minimum and the floor are the core's, read through the shell.** No number is copied, and L135's copy of the bank's 15 is gone.

**`app/core/secondtick.js` (rewritten):** marks and offers on the ghost.
- **A mark** is short strokes across the road in its field's handle colour (`handles.js` KINDS), at the field's own place across the road:
  - bank on both edges (lat 1);
  - climb at the centre;
  - cup at ±0.3;
  - turn just OUTSIDE the edge and width just INSIDE it;
  - left and right edge at 0.65, each on its own side.
- **Dragged along the piece,** a mark gives that change the nearest station.
- **An offer** is a faint ring (alpha 0.45) in the field's colour. It is dragged with the field's own handle maths (`handles.js` `screenAxis`, `dragPixels`, `targetFor`, imported, not copied).
- **Edges** have no handle kind, so they borrow the cup's maths (degrees across) and a colour of my own, `#3fd0c9`.
- **Presses.** A press on a mark or an offer is taken in the capture phase; any other press goes on to the handles and picking. It reads only the preview's own `t180:ghost-request` and `t180:view`.

**`app/core/coreshell.js` (three pass-throughs, at B's word "C carries the coreshell.js pass-throughs"):** `secondMinM(ch, a, b)`, `secondFloorM()` and `cornerMinM(doc, opts)`. Each returns null where the core lacks the export.

## 3. Design calls I made, and where I followed the newer source

- **Amendment D: form (ii)**, which the librarian's ruling also chose. An offer appears at R ≥ 2·floor, at R's middle.
- **The three edge-sharing fields.** Bank, turn and width all have `lat: 1`, so "at that field's handle position" alone would stack their marks. I nudged them apart in screen pixels:
  - turn +10 px outside the edge;
  - bank on it;
  - width 10 px inside.
- **Held dispatches.** Two dispatches reached me 700–880 s after they were sent: "+ change" at 06:50Z, and `turnDegs: [90, −90, 90]`. Both were superseded before delivery: by the third amendment (offers replace "+ change"), and by B's confirmed `changes.turnDeg: [{ from, deg }]`. **I built to the newer.**
- **"One undo step" for a drag on the GHOST** is the panel's undo guard. The next piece is not in the document until Extend, so a drag cannot be a document commit.
  - E's O3 words ("`D.serialize` sha256 equals the one before the drag") are met trivially: the document is the same object before and after a drag (row O3 checks this).
  - Extend is one document undo step, as before.
- **Shortening.** A shortened piece that breaks a used change shows the core's refusal by name in the ghost line, and Extend places nothing.
  - The panel does not move or drop a mark.
  - Dragged marks scale with the piece (their share), so a shortening can push a segment under its minimum. Then the core refuses it.
- **Offers reveal at the 20 m floor,** but a bank change's real minimum grows with Δ (172.5 m for 30°). So an offer dragged to a large change can be refused on release by name, as the plan says ("refuse by name on release"). O4 shows a 10° change from an offer being laid.
