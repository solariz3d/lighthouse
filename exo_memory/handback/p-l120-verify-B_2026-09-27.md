## 3 · Hard cases — where my reading may differ from C's

1. **Statements are often the extractor's check notes around a quoted phrase.** I judged the seat's quoted words.
   A note with no quote was matched to the unit it describes, and I say which in the `note` (5 cases, marked HARD).
2. **Presuppositions are NOT claims:** command arguments, globs, `sed` bodies, paths a script assumes, identifiers a test
   calls, and an extractor's "this assumes X". **Red-first test titles and assertions** (code not yet written) count as
   specification, not state (C:14616, C:19504). A test assertion message saying "must" counts as a requirement.
3. **The own-action rule:** a present-tense action the same message performs ("writing the hand-back", "checking where the
   rule is written") is IN and judged on whether it happened; "I will / next / before dispatching" is intent, OUT.
   Predictions ("will take a few minutes", "at startup it will say") are future, OUT.
4. **Hooks are not tool calls.** The pulse and the jev-flags hook injection count as sources, but not as CHECKED.
5. **Strict precision on stated figures.** I marked WRONG for a wrong unit (bytes vs characters), a relative time off by
   4×, a relative date off by a night, and "never sent" where the send came 15 minutes late. The chair's "never sent" is
   an idiom the room uses (not sent *with* the commit). I read it literally, and that is the case most likely to split from C.
6. **The extractor's own frame leaked into three notes:** it took the session model for the extractor's model, and
   "today" for the extraction date. I judged against the seat's T, and the claims were CORRECT.
7. **D-side state:** when D's board and ledgers on L did not carry it, I used another seat's later tool result within
   30 minutes (§4 source 3). Example: C's carrier-drift run 2 minutes after the chair's claim.
8. **Uncommitted work and §4.1:** a landing within 24 h was used only when the claim named the work as uncommitted.
   Otherwise, for a claim about the seat's own output, the transcript decided (the own-output rule), and I note the landing
   only for the record.
9. **A secondary as-of source:** one claim (the chair relaying the librarian) was decided by the librarian's committed
   collation. The primary hand-back landed 1 s after T and agrees.
