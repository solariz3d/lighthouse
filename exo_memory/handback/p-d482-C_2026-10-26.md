## 1. The finding

**The work done every frame is `widthAtHead` in `app/preview/preview.js`, reached from the preview's own frame loop.** The loop
(`frame`, preview.js:382 `const c = ctx(...)`) calls `ctx()` (:219). When nothing is placed yet, `ctx()` reads the ghost
(:221 `width: widthAtHead(ghost.segments)`), and `widthAtHead` (:573) runs `spanOf(readAt(g, g.length))` on the ghost's head segment.
The memo meant to stop that (`perHead`, :572) keeps an answer **only for a frozen segment**. During a drag the ghost is the
cheap one, and its head segment is not frozen. So the head's cross-section is worked out again on every frame.
`spanOf` (`src/geom/profile.js:136`) calls `offsetAt` once per knot. For an unfrozen profile, `offsetAt` (:82) takes its slow path
(:91), which filters and sorts the knots on every call. The cost is therefore about K² in the head profile's knot count.

**It is the edge field, not bank or cup.** With a left edge typed, the head profile has 137 knots. Plain, bank 20 and cup 30 each have 9.
- At 9 knots the work stays under 0.5 ms a frame.
- At 137 knots it is about 1.1 to 1.7 ms a frame, which crosses the harness's 1 ms work-frame line on every frame.

A's "any of bank, cup or edge" came from runs with all three typed. Each field alone separates them (§2).

**It needs an empty document.** With a piece placed, `ctx()` takes its first branch (:220, `track.segments`). The placed track's head
segment is frozen, so `perHead` keeps the answer. Measured: the preview frame is heavy on 2.3 frames a second, not every frame (§3).

**It does not explain the p95 on its own.** The 6.9 ms misses come from the drag's own rebuild, about 55–65 a second. With edge typed
it costs about 3× the plain rebuild, placed or not (§4). The every-frame cost raises how many frames count as work frames, from about 55
to about 400 a second. The p95 of those frames is set by the rebuild frames.

Unchanged in v0.4.21. checked: `git show v0.4.21:app/preview/preview.js` has the same `perHead` (moved to :575) and the same
ghost branch at :221. profile.js:82/:91/:136 and coarse.js:120/:135 are at the same lines and the same text on both tags (grep in this turn).

## 5. Answer to the order, in one place

- **What redraws every frame while a field is typed:** the preview frame's `ctx()` → `widthAtHead` (`app/preview/preview.js:219`, `:573`).
  Its memo `perHead` (:572) skips unfrozen segments. The cheap drag ghost's head segment is unfrozen, so `spanOf` → `offsetAt`
  (`src/geom/profile.js:136`, `:82`, slow path `:91`) runs every frame. Its cost is K² in the head profile's knots.
- **When:** only with a left (or right, inferred: the same field kind; I did not run right edge) edge typed, and only with nothing placed yet.
  Bank and cup alone do not trigger it.
- **What misses 6.9 ms:** the per-move rebuild with edge typed (§4), placed or not. A fix to `perHead` alone would bring the work-frame
  count back to about 55 a second. inferred: it would not bring the p95 under 6.9 ms, since the rebuild frames alone are p95 9.8–10.7 ms.
- **v0.4.21:** the same code and the same pattern. A's fitWithHold dominates there when bank or cup is typed.
