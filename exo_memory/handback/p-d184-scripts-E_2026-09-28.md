### 2.1 · The method, against the spec, step by step

| spec | what the code does | reference (C's `references/`) |
|---|---|---|
| 1. the true centreline by cross-rays | Walks across the road in each station's cross-plane, in 0.5 m steps. At each step a 1.2 m ray is cast from 0.6 m above the surface, along the local normal (Möller–Trumbore). Each edge is bisected six times. Reader stations give ORDER only; nothing is smoothed. | 01 §1 |
| a cross-ray that hits nothing is a jump gap | Yes, taken from the read's jump stations and confirmed by the mesh | 01 §1, 05 |
| width jump > 20% flagged, excluded, listed | Yes: `|w − w_prev| > 0.2·max(w)`. Listed by station index in each report. | 01 §3 |
| 2. split at jumps; on long laps also mid-straight (|κ| < 1/1500 for ≥ 200 m); never inside a corner | Yes. "Long" is > 10 km (**deviation D2**). | 03 §3 |
| 3. adaptive cubic B-spline in s for x, y, z and bank | A knot every 200 m to start. A midpoint knot goes into every span whose worst FITTED station is past 5 m / 5°. Cap 500 per piece, reported. | 03 §1–§3 |
| 4. G2 joints by KKT; a jump joint has no position constraint; ballistic check | Equality rows (value, slope, curvature) solved by a Schur complement (**D4**). The flight goes through `src/validate/jumps.js` `checkJump` at the builder's design speed. | 04 §2, 05 |
| 5. closure inside the same KKT | Yes: the wrap joint is one more G2 equality row. The bank's wrap row carries the lap's net frame turn (**D6**). | 04 §2 |
| 6. the report | pieces, control points per piece and total, line p95/max/% within 5 m, bank p95/% within 5°, the curve at 10/5/2/1 m, the flagged stations, plus a hold-out and the DOF | |
| 7a. overfitting reader glitches | Flagged stations are excluded. Only fitted stations steer knots. A span is split only while it holds ≥ 4 fitted stations (**D3**). Hold-out reported. | |
| 7b. crossover roads | Short rays along the LOCAL normal from just above the surface, so a road 5 m above is never reached (test). **D8** | 01 §2 |
| 7c. inverted sections | The cross-plane is the station's own frame (tangent × the station normal), never world-up | |
| 7d. like-for-like with M4 in DOF | M4 = 3(2N + 1) (C's 03 §3: "M4 stores 2N+1 per function", three functions). Piecewise = 4 channels × control points + knots. Both are reported, and so is CP against N. | 03 §3 |

### 2.2 · Declared deviations (each with its reason)

- **D1 · The centre is the SURFACE midpoint of the section**, not the 3-D midpoint of the two edge hits (01 §3). On a
  half-pipe the 3-D midpoint of the rims is in the air above the floor. The test *"a half-pipe … its centre sits on the
  floor"* holds it: 32 m read along the surface, and the centre height within 1e-6.
- **D2 · "Long lap" = over 10 km.** M4 passed every lap up to 10 km without splits, so straights are split only beyond that.
  The spec gives no number.
- **D3 · A span is split only while it holds ≥ 4 fitted stations.** Without this, spans shorter than the station spacing
  had no data, the ridge left them free, and the hold-out fit ran away to 498 of 500 control points on Serpents.
- **D4 · KKT by Schur complement on a banded Cholesky** (bandwidth 3). This is the same solution as 04 §2's system. The
  banded Cholesky has **no shelf source** (the Band-matrix article gives none). It is held by the test *"the banded
  Cholesky solves … exactly as a dense solve does"* (1e-10).
- **D5 · A second-difference ridge λ = 1e-6.** This keeps a span with too little data solvable. It is not on the shelf.
- **D6 · The bank uses a rotation-minimising frame (double reflection, WANG08) along the fitted line.** The bank is the angle
  from that frame to the measured normal, unwrapped, with the lap's net frame turn (netPhi) carried by the wrap joint.
  C's 04 §4 says "the RMF need not close; NOT tested here". This is where it gets used.
- **D7 · The evals judge against the mesh centreline** (spec step 2), where M4 judged against the read line. Said again,
  because it matters for the comparison.
- **D8 · Trap (b) is realised by short normal rays, not by "nearest the previous height"** (01 §2). A ray that never
  reaches the other road cannot pick it. The synthetic test holds it; no real crossover track is in tonight's set.

### 2.3 · Bugs found in my own fitter, and fixed (each before the real runs)

1. **The hold-out's refinement used held-out stations.** Now only fitted stations steer the knots.
2. **The hold-out fit ran away to 498 of 500 control points.** Spans were split below the data spacing. Fixed by D3.
3. **`fitSystem` treated rows with no value as zero data**, so the bank fell to about half its data (15.2° fitted against
   29.8° measured). Rows with no value are now skipped. The test *"a row with no value moves nothing"* was **proven to fail
   on the unfixed code** (`scratchpad/d184/mut.txt`).
4. **The bank closure's sign was negated twice, and wrap-joint detection was convoluted.** It is now one rule: the wrap joint
   carries `[netPhi, 0, 0]`.
5. **A self-closing piece's repeated first station was counted twice** in the frame sequence.
6. **After the runs:** a jump's `atS` was reported in the piece's continued coordinate, so it could exceed the lap. It is now
   modulo the lap. The per-track JSONs in §4 were written **before** this fix, so take their `atS` modulo `lapM`.

## 5 · The Test Track: why it caps (diagnosed, not fixed)

**Diagnosis** (`scratchpad/d184/ttdbg.js`, under the lock):
- 214 of 1,929 stations are more than 5° off in bank.
- They are scattered one to six at a time across 4,400–8,700 m, never in one block.
- **The measured bank itself jumps between stations 4 m apart:** −15.0°, −23.6°, −11.2° at 5,039, 5,043 and 5,047 m.
- The second-difference jitter of the measured bank has **median 2.43° and p95 9.82°** over that stretch (1.43° / 9.32°
  elsewhere).
- The line is fine (p95 0.23 m).

**The cause:** the bank is measured from the mesh normal AT the centre point, and this mesh's normals are faceted. The spline
spends control points chasing station-to-station noise until it hits the cap. That is trap (a) on the bank channel.

M4 passes this layout because the reader keeps its normal continuous and spread over the section. That is a smoother
measurement, not a better fit.

**Proposed fix, registered before it is tried:** measure the bank from the section's surface over its central ±2 m (the mean
of the hit normals), not from one triangle.
- **Prediction:** the Test Track passes under the cap, and no layout in §4 moves by more than 0.5 percentage points on either
  share.
- **Falsifier:** if the Test Track still caps, the jitter is in the geometry, not the measurement. The answer is then a bank
  smoothness term, and it is a finding.

**NOT done this packet:** it changes the measurement, so every figure above would need re-running. It is the chair's call
whether it rides in D184.
