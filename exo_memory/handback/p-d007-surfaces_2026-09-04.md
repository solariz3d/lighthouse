`he sleeps → they sleep`, `he wakes → they wake`, `He's → They're`. A blind `s/he/they/` produces a
shipped brief that reads as broken English, which is its own way of telling a stranger the room was
not written for them. (My D005 hand-back measured exactly that failure in `gen-consumer.js`'s
substitution pass: 2 of 5 occurrences left ungrammatical.)

**The replacements are not the guard.** The guard is a new clause in the generator's self-check:

    $pron = [regex]::Matches($t, '\b(?:He|he|Him|him|His|his|She|she|Her|her|hers)\b')

If the master grows a fourteenth site tomorrow, or one of my eleven anchors drifts, the build
**REFUSES and deletes the output** rather than shipping. Proven in §4, M3.
