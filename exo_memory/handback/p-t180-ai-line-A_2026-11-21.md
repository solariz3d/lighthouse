## R2: the bound, its units, what it costs
**What `CLEAR_UP` measures.** For a step between two points, `h = sqrt(dx²+dz²) / |step|` = |tangent x up| = cos(pitch): 1 on the flat, 0 on a vertical. AC recomputes a point's side vector from its neighbours' tangent crossed with up and divides by that length (the code's own comment; AC's source is not ours to read, so this is `inferred:`). The exporter reads it three ways per point (to the next point, from the previous, across both), on the float32 positions AC reads.

**Three lines, measured** (the bound is justified by these, and by geometry, not by AC's source):
| line | least h | AC |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-01 lap | 1e-6 | crashed |
| TEST TRACK 100 (his, 633 points, 6 m) | **1.424e-4** (two points; then 2.11e-4, 3.15e-4) | **crashed** |
| ELYSIUM (4,600 points, 2 m) | **1.933e-4** (two points; then 2.2e-3) | loads |
His line also has 88 steps over 80° and a run of 44 consecutive ones; ELYSIUM's has 78 points within 6° of vertical and loads: so steepness alone does not separate them, the least h does, in the window **(1.424e-4, 1.933e-4]**, `inferred:` if AC's threshold is a function of h alone.

**The bound chosen: 1.93e-4.** Every line written is then at least as clear as one AC is known to load, and ELYSIUM's own line is byte-identical (its pinned row still passes). His line's 1.424e-4 is below it, so it is refused or moved. **The room to spare is small, 1.36x, and I say so:** it is the tightest bound the evidence allows without moving ELYSIUM's line, and a higher bound would move it (the pinned row would need amending by name). **If he wants more margin, the dial is the one constant, and ELYSIUM's AI line is what it costs.**

**The cost, as a list** (`survey_before.txt` = `survey_after.txt`, line for line: checked, the same survey before and after): every track the repo's own tests export, and the platform test's two lines, floor and wall, read their least h: F1, F2 and the other core fixtures 0.98 to 1.0; `stadium-100km` 1.0; `platform_test` floor 0.998 and wall 0.974; `light-hull` 1.9e-2; `tube-cases`, `level-end`, `extra-bank`, `extra-turn` 0.99 to 1.0; **ELYSIUM 1.933e-4**. **No track in the repo's tests or samples loses its AI line under 1.93e-4.** ELYSIUM sits 0.2% above it; under any bound above 1.933e-4 it alone would change. `light-hull` (1.9e-2) is the next nearest and is 100x clear.

## Step 4: the extra bytes
`encodeAiLine` writes: int32 version, n, lapTime, sampleCount (16) · n x 20 (x,y,z,length,id) · int32 n again (4) · n x 18 float32 (72 each) · **int32 hasGrid (4)**. With n = 633: 16 + 12,660 + 4 + 45,576 + 4 = **58,260**. The brief's 58,256 omitted the last 4 bytes (`ailine.js` header comment; `readAiLine` refuses a byte left over, and reads this file, checked).
