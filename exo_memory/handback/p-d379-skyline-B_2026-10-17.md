## The design: open sky, a light skin

- **The window is a bright sky.** The top bar is a glossy white-to-sky band, with the upper half catching the light. The 3D view behind the track is sky: bright and sunlit at the top, a deeper blue toward the ground.
- **Glossy pills:** every button and menu name is a white-to-sky gloss pill. An open menu, the pressed camera buttons and Export to Assetto Corsa are filled deep sky-blue pills.
- **Frosted glass:** the left panel is a frosted white pane (16 px radius, blur and saturate, a light sheen at the top, sky-blue shadow). The menus, the camera dock, the guide card, the values overlay and the ask card are frosted white panes too.
- **Colour:** headings in deep sky blue with a fading rule, navy text.
- **Fonts:** the page's own (Segoe UI Variable, Bahnschrift, Cascadia Mono), nothing fetched. No size, weight, letter-spacing, padding, margin or border width is changed anywhere, which is why the layout holds.
- **Meaning colours:** the canvas hues (handles, spawns, grip, selection) are untouched; they are drawn by canvases this CSS cannot reach. The warning tokens keep their hue and are only deepened so they read on a light surface: `--bad #a32b19`, `--warn #735300`, `--ok #1f6643`, `--mine #7a4f08`.
- **Inline colours overridden** (the same colour role, now on light surfaces):
  - the Extend readout's near-white (`panel.js:245`), with `!important`;
  - the dark backing of the values overlay (`valuesoverlay.js:26`);
  - the guide card;
  - the guide-target outline, which takes the accent (the librarian's ruling on the unclear six).

### The one compromise: the 3D sky is a stand-in until part 2

The WebGL view clears to near-black (`renderer.js:143`), and CSS cannot change that. So the sky is a `#preview::after` gradient blended with `mix-blend-mode: screen`. Black becomes sky, and the road and its lines stay lighter than the sky behind them. Sorted by z-index, the overlay canvases (handles, selection, spawns, labels, z 3–6) sit **above** it and keep their true colours.

The cost: what WebGL itself draws is washed toward sky. The orange marker lines (`renderer.js:135`, a MEANING colour in my inventory §3) come out pale pink, and the road reads pale blue (see `cdp\shot-skyline-file.png`). Part 2's token reader removes this: CLEAR, fog and the road lines become Skyline tokens and the `::after` goes.
