## What is ours, and the falsifier

- **Ours:** the 10° hip tilt (it keeps the stance foot's depth under the hip within 1.5 cm through stance, computed from
  the runners' means); the growth past 4.5 m/s to 7 m/s, then held (the stand-in's stop at 6 m/s replaced on purpose);
  the shrink toward a stand below 2.5 m/s; the least-squares fit itself.
- **The falsifier tripped, by a little, and is said here:** the registration allowed about 20 numbers (five landmarks a
  speed plus the timing of each peak). The shapes use 24 in all, shared across the speeds: the four bump widths and the
  hip's second harmonic are beyond the count. The bar passes with room (every joint at half the runners' spread or
  less), so I do not read this as fitting noise; the next reader may.
- **A landed row amended by name:** `the_stand_in_gait_keeps_every_limb_its_own_length_and_stands_still_at_rest`. Its

  green attempt put the standing ankle 1.4 mm below rest height; the pelvis now takes its standing depth from the rest
  skeleton, so a stand puts the ankles exactly there.
