**Why the plan's mechanism cannot act here.** It needs w to change from cell to cell (Δw ≠ 0).
- All three of his closed-tube bends have a **constant width and a constant 360° sweep**: TUBE OVAL 40 m, TUBE V2 45 m, CRAZY TEST's bend 60 m. I read every piece of the three files; the only width changes are on CRAZY TEST's straight tube pieces p3 and p6 (32 → 60 m), which have no bend.
- So w is the same in every row to 4 decimals, and `n · arc / w` gives the same u in every cell.

**Two corrections to the plan's picture, both measured:**
- The mesh's "cells" here are the adapter's **2 m segments**: 3,000 segments on TUBE OVAL's 6 km, each a mesh piece with one cell. `buildMesh`'s cellLength is 150 m, but the adapter's chords set the 2 m. The "per 2 m" in the plan is right in effect.
- `mesh.js` lays `Us` **once per segment**, and flow.js reads it per row. Within a segment u is constant; across segments it would step only if w changed, and it does not.
