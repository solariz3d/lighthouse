## 1 · The fonts, from measured cross-sections
**Instrument:** `node tools/fontshape.cjs reads > src/geom/fontshape.json` (new; sha256 7f1b80d50f4a081b; json 535b81624864e12e).
- It samples words exactly as `tools/corpus.cjs` does: the same layouts, one word = one sample, shared road counted once, and
  inside/outside of the turn (the low/high side on a straight).
- **Checked first:** recomputed per CLASS, its ψ bands equal `corpus.json`'s, 48 of 48 bands, over 4,026 words
  (`check()`; a `node -e` run on 2026-09-28).
- It then groups the road-class words (straight, sweep, turn, tight: 3,543) by the reader's shape, as vocabgen's
  FONT_OF_SHAPE does.
- It adds the no-lip measure. For each station and side, it takes the rise of ψ over each quarter of that side's width, in
  °/m. A station's rate is the largest of these; a word's rate is the median over its stations.

### 1a · The families (fontshape.json)
| family | words | share | width p10/p50/p90 m | ψ p50 inside, ¼ / ½ / ¾ / edge | steepest rise p10/p50/p90 °/m |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| bowl | 1,872 | 52.8% | 28 / 31 / 41 | 2.8 / 8.8 / 15.6 / 7.7 | 0.58 / 2.99 / 6.12 |
| half-pipe (Sakura mode) | 349 | 9.9% | 24 / 31.5 / 34 | 5.9 / 10.8 / 14.9 / 30.6 | 3.83 / 4.55 / 4.77 |
| half-pipe-deep (measured, NOT a font) | 499 | 14.1% | 29 / 30.5 / 34 | 6.5 / 17.6 / 28.7 / 29.3 | 3.55 / 4.30 / 5.14 |
| flat | 823 | 23.2% | 35 / 45 / 91 | 0.7 / 1.45 / 2.1 / 2.7 | 0.05 / 0.20 / 0.71 |

- **The half-pipe is TWO shapes.** The pipe words' ψ at ¾ (the mean of both sides) peak at 12–16° and at 28–32°, with few
  between.
  - The median of the two is a profile no track has, and it rises more gently than every real half-pipe (2.9°/m, against
    a p10 of 3.7).
  - So they are split at ψ¾ = 20°:
    - **`half-pipe` = the gentle floor with a steep last quarter:** 289 of Sakura's 291 pipe words, and 53 of
      Thunderhead's 71.
    - **`half-pipe-deep` = the progressive rise that flattens at the rim:** all 396 of Centrifuge's, both bowl tracks',
      and Nordic's.
  - The `half-pipe` font is therefore 83% one track's shape (289 of 349): numbers only, and said plainly.
  - `half-pipe-deep` is a candidate font. **Adding a font id is A's and the keeper's call; I did not add one.**
- **The bowl is one mode, and SYMMETRIC:** inside and outside differ by ≤ 0.4° at ¼, ½ and ¾. The measured bowl is a dish
  across the whole width, not "a flat floor with the outside wall up". A corner tilts the WHOLE section by the word's roll:
  A's bank, −27.5° on a turn and −79.8° on a wall-ride.

### 1b · The font floors (`src/geom/fonts.js`, sha256 ec2a5f0ebc504c7d)
ψ at ¼, ½, ¾ and the edge of each side's half-width, linear between, ψ(0) = 0:
- **bowl** 2.7 / 8.6 / 15.5 / 15.5°, default width 31 m;
- **half-pipe** 5.6 / 10.8 / 14.1 / 30.6°, default width 31.5 m;
- **flat** 0.7 / 1.5 / 2.3 / 3.0°, default width 45 m. The flat banked ribbon is this font plus the roll.

How the numbers are set:
- Each fraction's value is the mean of the inside and outside medians.
- **The edge** is the larger of the edge median and the ¾ median, per side. The reader's edge sample is its last point before a
  fold sharper than 35° in a metre (FINDINGS §7f), so on the bowl it reads BELOW ¾ (7.7° against 15.6°): a fold, not the shape
  turning back down.

### 1c · A rim rises at a rate, not to an angle (found by the registered test, fixed in the model)
- **Found:** the first model scaled the angles with the width. The registered no-lip bound (≤ p90 at the family's p10 width)
  **FAILED**: the half-pipe at 24 m rose 5.50°/m, against a real p90 of 4.77.
- **Measured** (half-pipe words binned by width):

  | width | real steepest rise | real edge ψ |
  |---|---|---|
  | < 26 m | 4.7°/m | 25.7° (Thunderhead) |
  | 30–33 m | 4.6°/m | 30.7° (Sakura) |
  | 33–36 m | 4.2°/m | 30.9° |
  | > 36 m | 3.6°/m | 31.2° |

  **So the rate is near constant, and a narrow rim is lower.**
- **Fixed in the model, not the test:** each quarter's rise is capped at the family's median rate (RATES: bowl 2.993,
  half-pipe 4.55, flat 0.2 °/m; a test pins them to fontshape.json). At each family's median width no cap binds, so the fractions above ARE the font there.
- **At 24 m the half-pipe's edge is 27.75°** (`floorAt('half-pipe', 24)`), against Thunderhead's measured 25.7°.
- **Corrected by me, 08:29Z:** RATES first held 4.45 for the half-pipe. That was the median of ALL pipe words, from before the
  split, while its comment claimed fontshape.json's p50 for the family (4.55). Now 4.55, with a test that pins every RATE to
  the json. At the median width nothing changes (4.19°/m); at 24 m the edge moves from 27.5° to 27.75°.
  - The tree's full suite (§6) started at 08:27:38Z, before this edit landed at about 08:29Z, so it ran across the change.
  - The font tests are re-run afterwards, under the lock.

### 1d · No wall by default
- `DEFAULTS[font]` = `{ width: WIDTHS[font], wall: 0, psiL = psiR = the edge value }`.
- The corpus measures no wall (the reader stops at the lip). A wall a user then sculpts starts as the floor's own continuation
  and rises linearly to ψL / ψR over `wall` metres, as before.
- In the library a wall-ride is the whole road banked (80°), not a font wall.
