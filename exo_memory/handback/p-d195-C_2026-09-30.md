## 1. What was built (`app/core/panel.js`)

- **One small "at start" box after each channel field:** turn, climb, bank, cup and width, each with the aria-label `<field> at the start`.
  All are **off by default**, which is today's blend over the whole piece. The title says what it does: "reach this … within the first 20 m
  of the piece and hold it (off: ease to it over the whole piece)".
- **What is sent:**
  - `extendOptions(…, atStart)` adds `transition: { <channel>: min(20, length) }` for each ticked field that HAS a target. The fields
    map to channels as turn → `kh`, climb → `kv`, bank → `phi`, width → `w`, cup → `c`.
  - 20 m is `AT_START_M`: one knot span, the map's "≈ one knot span", `KNOT_M` in `src/core/document.js`. It is capped at the piece's
    length because the core requires a transition no longer than the piece.
  - With nothing ticked, or ticked only on untouched fields, there is **no `transition` key**, and the options are exactly the old ones.
- **D193 kept:** an untouched field has no target, so its box sends nothing. **D194a kept:** on an empty track `first` and `transition`
  both go.
- **The box is sticky,** kept from piece to piece like a preference. After Extend the fields show the head again (untouched), so a ticked
  box does nothing until a value is typed.
- **Ticking redraws the ghost and the readout inside its own handler,** like the fields.
- **Per-field boxes, not one switch.** The keeper's case is width alone ("the whole piece the same width"). One switch would force bank,
  turn and cup to snap in 20 m too whenever width did, which is the coupling he is separating. Five boxes cost one short line each.

## 2. THE CONTRACT with A, coded against

`extend(doc, { length, targets, transition })` accepts `transition` as a NUMBER (as now) or a per-channel MAP in metres, e.g.
`{ w: 20, phi: 20 }`. A channel not in the map blends over the whole piece. A picks the exact span and the ringing clamp in the core.
The panel only ever sends `min(20, length)` per ticked channel.

## 3. What I STUBBED, exactly (only in `app/test/core-readout-display.test.js`)

2. It REMOVES `transition` before the real core sees it, because `4ccdd58` refuses a map with `BAD_TRANSITION`.
