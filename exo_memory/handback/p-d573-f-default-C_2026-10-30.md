## What F is, as laid

- **The panel** (`app/core/panel.js`): a ticked bank, cup or edge asks a ramp of `F_RAMP_M` = 300 m. Where the roll bar wants more
  (3.4 × |change| / the amber, past about 82°), it takes that instead. The caps are as before: half the piece, and the bank's alone bends
  to the piece where half would pass the amber.
  - The shape is `startShapeOf(R)`: a rise of `F_RISE_M` = 20 m as laid (`20 / laidOf(R)`), the rest the fade.
  - A ramp laid in 40 m or less cannot hold a 20 m rise, so it gets the swell's halves.
  - The bank's goes as `bankShape`; the cup's and edge's as the new `startShapes`.
- **The core** (`src/core/extend.js`): `opts.startShapes { c, e, er }` lays a cup or edge at start with `rampShape`, as a shaped bank at
  start already was. It is applied only where that channel's ramp is short; a whole-piece ramp keeps the quintic.
  - The shaped knots (`shapeKnots`) of every shaped short ramp go in one insertion, then each channel is read again and its ends pinned.
  - A right edge follows the edge's shape, as its ramp does.
  - A bad map is refused by name (`BAD_SHAPE`).
  - **Left out, nothing moves** (below).
