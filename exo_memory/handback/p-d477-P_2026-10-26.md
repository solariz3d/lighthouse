## 1. The fix, in the core (finish.js)

### 1.1 The cause, as C found it, and why a relative plan alone is not enough

C's finding 1 holds as written: `flat` (:486) reads a piece whose bank is within ROLL_FREE (5°) of the start's as upright, and bankPlan's targets (:233) are the whole share bank, which `banked()` adds to what the piece carries. On the looped lap p3 (a 100 m extend after a Turn by, stored kh 2.66e-4, held under 5° by the pinned joint into the loop) went 1.06 → 1.92 → 2.78 → 3.65°.

The first half of the fix is the one C inferred: the plan is the difference between the total the bounds allow and the bank the piece carries. In `bankPlan` (`carried`, bankLap only): each seq point carries `e` (its phi less `upright`); the variable `t` is the TOTAL bank, started at the target as before; the rate and acceleration bounds and the box are on the total (the road's roll is the total); a pinned point's total is what it carries (its added bank 0, as before); the curvature rows are on the ADDED bank, `t − e` (the window's edges meet road whose added bank is 0, and a straight's third point keeps the roll-off tail an earlier press left it); the plan is `t − e`. With `e` 0 everywhere this is byte for byte the old plan.

    checked: probe.js on d477-p with that alone (`probe-public.out`): press 1 byte-equal to a4bfe52 on the flat, looped and t10-shaped laps; press 2 on the looped lap still moved p3 by 0.0311° (a4bfe52: 1.582°), on the t10-shaped lap p6 and p10 by under 5e-5° (a4bfe52: 3.820°)

Not 0: the second press solves a different system. The pieces the first press banked past 5° read as his own bank on the second, so the joint into them is a window edge instead of a linked joint, and the sweeps, which land on *a* point of the bounds, not the nearest one, land a little elsewhere. (Dykstra's projection, which lands on the one nearest point, would make the relative plan exact on its own; it is noted for after 0.4.21, not built.)
