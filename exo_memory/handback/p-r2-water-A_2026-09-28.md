## 0. The method is E's, registered before any track run

`exo_memory/handback/p-r2-water-E_2026-09-28.md` §1 was written before any track surface or replay went through the
tool. **I keep it as the registration, unchanged in every rule that produces the verdict:**
- the design speed, 460 km/h;
- the centre particle of a 21-particle sheet as the predicted line;
- off-line > 25 m on more than 10% of the road stations = the line FAILS;
- the p50/p99 load miss > 25% = FAILS;
- the replays chosen by §1.9's rule, each needing ≥ 90% coverage of its read.

The falsifier is quoted from design §9 in E's file.

**One change to the tool before any track was run, and it is outside the verdict.** E flagged as unchecked: "`spreadMap`
pairs samples by index, which assumes neighbours stay abreast". It did. It paired two neighbouring particles at the same
SAMPLE (the same time). On a curve the outer particle travels farther at the same speed and falls behind in progress, so
the along-track lag was counted as spread. **Now it pairs them at the same STATION** (every 4 m of progress, the first
sample past it, as `lineScore` takes them).
- Written with a temp file and a rename, so a queued run could not load a half-written tool.
- `node --test test/water.test.js` → 4/4 after, as before.
- **The spread map has no test** (it is inside the run function, not exported). The spread ratios are therefore
  reported as a map, with that limit.
- The verdict (`lineScore`, the loads) is untouched. The tool before my edit: `r2/water.before.cjs`.

**E's other two unchecked points, read in the code:**
- **A forward take-off:** past the edge moving forwards (|t̂·v̂| ≥ 0.7), the particle flies, and lands on contact
  within 5 s. **One risk the code does not guard:** in flight within 2 m under another road whose normal is within 45°
  of the last contact (an overpass), `near` could snap the particle UP onto it. Watched for in the output as a
  progress jump.
- **Seams:** a seam crossed sideways and regained within 0.25 s is a `lip` event, not a spill; crossed forwards, it is a
  short `flight`. Checked on Sakura's first release in §2, before any spill count is trusted.
