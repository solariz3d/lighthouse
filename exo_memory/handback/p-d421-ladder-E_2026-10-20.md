## 1. What the ladder does (design §4.3)

- **The drag frame** (`trackmodel.js` `update(resolved, { drag })`, `dragFrame`) rebuilds nothing placed. The path and mesh of the document the drag began on stay as they were, at full detail. The preview hands the shell's open brush (`state.brush`) to the model; while it is open, the preview never goes coarse.
- **The window** is what the step changed:
  - the segments not identical to the base's, trimmed at both ends by value (the shell re-converts a brushed piece whole, and its unreached chords come back equal but new);
  - and, for a hill or swerve, the span `[s0 − r, s0 + r]` its lift reaches.
  - It is hidden, with its two seams, and drawn as a **chord ghost**: its chords merged into at most about 24 (`mergeChords`, D404's 0.03 rad heading cap, roll ≤ 0.05 rad, none shorter than 8 m unless a cap ends it), the cheap ghost's row grid (`coarsenRun`), and a path grown from the base path's own frame at the window's start, lifted by the document's own lift.
- **A route step** (bend, climb): the road after the window is moved as one rigid body, from the exact window end. Each of its batches keeps its placed matrix and carries the move (`xform`). The renderer draws the chunk it baked before under that one matrix.
  - On a closed lap (marked closed, or opened for the drag by O's route tools), the joint's seam is hidden and the gap from the moved end to the start is drawn as a line.
- **The release**: the exact road is built with `update()` in the next animation frame, never in the state change that ended the drag. The drag's last frame stays on show until then, so nothing blinks.
- **Never during a drag:** `close()`, the overlap check, a full `toSegments`, a coarse rebuild, or `relift` over the lap.
- **Exactness, measured in node on A's fixtures:**
  - the moved road lies 1.15e-13 m (open twelve) and 1.42e-14 m (closed10) from an exact build of the same step;
  - every release equals a fresh build (`sameAsFresh: true` in all six cases).
  - checked: `probe3b.out`.
- **No `detail` field is added** to the `t180:track-request` reply. During a drag the shared track is the placed road, so A's `roadNow` reads it as whole, as before.
