## 3. The two definitions, the differences, and which numbers stand (the 07:18Z ring)
**The definition both implement** (A's addendum, `p-l169-code-A_2026-11-12.md:136-141`, and the amendment `:168-200`):
- corner: a turn of at least 60° within 3 m;
- pairs: chasers 4 to 15 m from the runner at the corner;
- overshoot: the largest signed distance from the runner's track, outside, in the next 3 s;
- screen share: (o/d)/(2 tan 50°);
- seen: a share of at least 4 % and 4 to 12 m;
- slip: the largest facing-against-travel angle in the next 1 s.

**Where the two read it differently** (A = `chase/slosh.cpp` at 47dc5e5; B = mine, withdrawn, `90b340d` on `l170-instr-b-own-scorer`):

| | A (stands) | B (withdrawn) |
|---|---|---|
| corner | turn read over up to 3 m **either side** (at least 2 m each); one corner per 3 s, the sharpest | turn over **1.5 m either side**; one corner per run of qualifying samples (so many more) |
| overshoot window | **0.5 s before** the corner to 3 s after | the corner to 3 s after (as the text says) |
| "outside" | the offset from the nearest track point **projected on the corner's bisector** | the signed perpendicular distance from the nearest segment |
| floors | **height ignored** (a chaser on another storey 4–15 m off in plan is paired) | samples more than 1.5 m above or below the track are not measured |
| screen | `max(0, overshoot)` | signed |
| slip's travel | **forward difference** (k → k+1), at 2 m/s or more | **centred difference** (k−1 → k+1), at 1 m/s or more |
| median | upper median | mean of the middle two |

- **Can the definition be computed as written?** Yes, with two readings that the text leaves open:
  - "within 3 m" (total, or either side);
  - "the next 3 s" (A starts 0.5 s early).

  And one gap the text does not mention: **storeys**. A pairs across floors.
