## 8. The Sunday page

**This is written for the laptop, in plain words. It assumes Saturday's carry included all nine seats.**

> **Bringing the conversations onto the laptop**
>
> **Before you start.** Plug the stick in **before** you open Consonance on the laptop. Don't open Consonance on the laptop at all until the stick is in. Opening it adds a little to every conversation, and that turns the simple case below into a choice.
>
> **What you'll see.** After the intro, a window called **"Transfer from the stick"**. It has a line saying whether the transfer set verifies, and a table with one row per seat. The third column, **"the stick would"**, decides everything. You'll see one of four things there:
>
> 1. **FULL**: the laptop has never had this conversation. It arrives whole. There's nothing to choose.
> 2. **INTERRUPTED, with a "repair" box**: the laptop's copy is an exact earlier part of the desktop's, so the laptop wrote nothing to this seat since 29 September. **Tick "repair".** The laptop's copy is set aside and the desktop's whole conversation takes its place. The small text under it mentions a command; ignore that, the box does the same thing.
> 3. **DIVERGED, with "take the stick's" and "keep this machine's"**: the laptop wrote something of its own to this seat since 29 September, even if that was only being opened. Nothing is ticked for you, and Carry stays greyed out until you choose for each such seat.
>    - **Take the stick's** if you didn't work in that seat on the laptop. The laptop's whole copy goes to the attic, kept and not deleted, and the desktop's conversation continues. This is the right answer for every seat if you only opened Consonance on the laptop.
>    - **Keep this machine's** only if you did real work in that seat on the laptop and want the laptop's version to continue. Then the desktop's month for that seat stays on the stick, not carried.
>    - The byte count shown for "this machine" will be the laptop's whole file, not just what's new. Don't read it as the amount you wrote.
> 4. **REFUSED, "a different conversation", with take/keep**: the laptop has a different conversation under that seat's name. For a pane, "take the stick's" is already ticked, and the laptop's copy goes to the attic. For main, librarian or third place, "keep" is ticked; switch it to "take the stick's" unless you started real work there on the laptop.
>
> **How to tell whether the laptop wrote to a seat since 29 September.** The window tells you: **"repair" means it didn't, "take/keep" means it did.** If you see take/keep and don't remember using Consonance on the laptop since then, it was only the app being opened. Take the stick's.
>
> **Whatever you pick, nothing is deleted.** Every laptop copy that is replaced goes to the attic whole.
>
> Then press **"Carry, and reopen Consonance"**. Consonance closes, carries, reopens, and shows what it did. Each row should say "written".
>
> **Keep the stick as it is until that's done.** The copy on the desktop is kept until this first carry has worked on the laptop.
