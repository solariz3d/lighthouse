## 3. Two things the figures say that the plan did not expect (said plainly, before he looks)

- **Every at-start ramp is laid compressed to three quarters of the length asked** (`extend.js` rampControl, `Math.max(R − 2R/sub, R/sub)` with sub = 8, RAMP_KNOTS_C2). This is the existing rule; START_SPREAD's comment already says so. So A's "151 m" finishes banking by about 113 m, and C's and D's "500 m" by about 375 m. The peak positions show it: A's swell peaks at 56.5 m = 0.375 × 150.7.
- **C does not lean in sooner than today.** Its "quick" rise is 0.1 of a 375 m laid ramp, i.e. 37.5 m. At 20 m C has banked 0.57°, against A's 1.63°. On these numbers **only B leans in sooner** (4.45° at 20 m), and B rolls fastest of the four. The plan's reading, "a visible lean within the first 20 to 50 m, then a slow fade", sits between B and C. If his eye says the same, a rise given in metres (about 15 m) with a long fall would be the shape to try. That is a fifth shape, so it is not laid here; the lap's falsifier points at a live length handle instead.
- **The laid B is not quite the asked B.** Its 11 m rise is narrower than the ramp's knot spacing (150.7/8 ≈ 19 m), so the fitted bank is up to **6.0° off** the ideal 0.1 / 0.9 curve, at 21 m. For C the gap is 1.0°; the existing swell, A, is 1.08° off its own curve. The renders and figures are of the laid pieces, which is what he would get. If B is picked, its rise needs more knots before it is the shape it claims to be.
