## 2. The new and edited files.

My own probe on `guidePlan` (`scratchpad/grid3d/probe.js`, `probe2.js`), with synthetic centrelines, independent of A's tests:

**Flat is ground-only:** "flat" is the CENTRELINE's height span under 0.5 m (cup walls do not count), by reading
(`heightRange`/`effectiveMode`) and A's 0.49/0.6 m test. **The lattice is bounded:** corners are capped at 400 (the spacing multiplies
until it fits), levels at 12, drop lines at 150. **Measured:** grid plus lattice is **690** line pairs on a 14 km loop in a 6 km box,
**353** on a 14 km or a 40 km track coiled into a 300 m box.

**The mirror gap is honest:**

| planted case | left/right | front/back | both |
|---|---|---|---|
| exact ellipse 200 × 100 m, 4,000 points | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| **a 5 m outward bump on ONE side** | 4.785 max (mean 0.145) | 4.785 | 4.785 |
| **the x > 0 half 3 m higher** | **3.0** (mean 2.95) | **0** | 3.0 |
| centre 7 m off in x | 14.0 | 0 | — |

The bump reads 4.785, not 5: the mirrored peak's distance to the NEAREST point of the real curve, which is not radially opposite.
The height case shows the gap is 3D and tells the mirrors apart (a front/back flip keeps each half on its own side). **Measuring one
direction is enough:** a mirror is an isometry and its own inverse, so d(t, M(T)) = d(M(t), T), and "mirrored to real" is the full
symmetric (Hausdorff) gap.

**FINDING 1 (owner A): the gap is slow on ordinary asymmetric tracks with Mirror on, and it runs on every brush step.**
`mirrorGap` searches 12 rings of cells (about 24 m on a keeper-sized track: cell = max(2 m, …)), then measures against EVERY segment.
On an asymmetric track most mirrored points are farther than that. Per plan rebuild, node, the default centre (the box's middle):

| track (3,000 points, ~1.5 km) | mirror off | left/right | front/back | both |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| an egg (one end fatter) | 0.3 ms | **219 ms** | 6.7 | **209 ms** |
| an open L | 0.1 | 111 | 180 | **282 ms** |
| the egg with a 60 m hill | 0.1 | **370 ms** | 6.1 | **355 ms** |

With the centre dragged 2 km away: **750 ms** (7,000 points) and **2.5 s** (20,000). A's "22 ms for 7,000 samples" was a near-symmetric
loop. The plan is rebuilt on every track change (every brush step) and every centre-ring move, so **with Mirror on, an edit on an
asymmetric track gains about 0.1 to 0.4 s per step** in node (more in the WebView). That is on top of D236's 120 ms cup step, and an
asymmetric track is exactly when the mirror is used. Fix candidates: an early exit from the box distance, a cell sized to the box, or
fewer gap queries while dragging. The grid alone (Auto) costs 0.1–0.3 ms: unaffected.

**FINDING 2 (owner A): the mirror ghost's line count is not bounded, unlike the rest.** The ghost's POINTS are capped at 900, but its
dashes are at least 2 m, so its line count grows with track length: **3,249 ghost lines on a 14 km track coiled into a 300 m box, 8,316
on 40 km** (A's cap test, 2,500 with guides, used a big-box loop). That's cheap to draw and not a correctness issue, but the file's own
header says "EVERY COUNT IS BOUNDED". Clamp the dash to the ghost's length over a maximum count.
