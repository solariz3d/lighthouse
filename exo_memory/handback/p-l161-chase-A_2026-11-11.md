## 1. What the model is (`chase/speed.h`, the account in `chase/SPEED_MODEL.md`)
A chaser's top speed on the flat = `ref_speed` (7 m/s, the chasers' own) x its row x its dial at the time survived (the base difficulty) x (1 + distance gain: 5% a km the player has covered, capped at 30%) x (1 + run-up: 5% a second at top speed on a straight, capped at 20%, lost in proportion to the radians turned). A turn also takes `turn_drag` (20% per radian) of its speed. A chaser brakes for every corner in full (`corner_floor` 1) and a head-on wall hit no longer stands it (`wall_stand` 0). The interceptor row is the nimble one: top 0.90 (was 1.12), turn rate at top speed 250 degrees a second (was 220). Every field is set in code, in `RunParams::speed`, or from `dz-chase ... --speed "ref_speed=6.5 dist_per_km=0.04"`; `--speed legacy` is the old chase. `--move "sprint_speed=6 sprint_peak_speed=9 sprint_peak_time=4"` sets the player's numbers for a sweep.

- **Own reference speed.** `make_body(limits, ref_speed, scale, model)`; the four callers read `params_.speed.ref_speed`, not the player's sprint. `checked:` `grep -n sprint_speed chase/run.cpp` → no reader left in `chase/` (the field in `RunParams` stays so callers compile; its comment says nothing reads it).
- **The amendment's item 1, read carefully.** The interceptor's lead already used the player's observed speed (`vlen`, `run.cpp` about line 983 at `d11ce9a`). The reader of `sprint_speed` at 993-994 was the **pack's** cut-off. It now reads the speed the player was seen to have, at least 3 m/s. `inferred:` that this is what the chair meant.
- **Lessers follow the model.** A lesser chaser's top is reset every tick from its row x the dial x the distance gain (before, it was fixed at its spawn). This changes legacy slightly, so `--speed legacy` is not bit-identical to `d11ce9a`.
