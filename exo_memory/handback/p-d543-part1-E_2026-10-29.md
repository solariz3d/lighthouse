## 1. The change (`src/texture/flow.js` only; mesh.js untouched)

1. **Each row takes its own section's across arc. I chose flow.js, the smaller correct change.**
   - mesh.js lays one arc per 2 m segment (its end section), and that arc is also the mesh's own uvs, which untextured exports write. Changing it there would have moved every untextured export.
   - In flow.js, a segment's first row takes the segment before's end section (the coincident row), read exactly as the mesh stores it: `Math.fround(Us / 10) * 10`. The last row takes its own. A middle row (a cell of three) takes the blend by its distance along.
   - This applies only where the section moved by more than `SAME_ARC_M` = 0.1 mm somewhere across the row. On a plain road, consecutive segments' arcs differ by up to 5e-14 m, which float32 can round either way, so below the threshold the bytes are kept.
2. **One n per run of closed rings, from the run's MEDIAN circumference** (`ringRunN`), and never per row.
   - The roof seam closes at every row: u_end − u_start = n.
   - I chose the median over "the first full ring row" because the first ring row would change his constant tubes: on CRAZY TEST it is p3's 32 m, which gives n = 3 for the 60 m p4/p5, 20 m tiles.
   - The value is kept per mesh state and segments, rebuilt when either is replaced or grows.
