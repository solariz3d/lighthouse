## What was built
**The body** (`game/body.h`, `game/body.cpp`), pure and ticked once per sim tick after the rig.
- **Attachment:**
  - `game/session.cpp:39,48` (gym) and the 9 rig sites in `game/world/world_app.cpp` (`:109`, `:118` and on).
- **Feet, tied to the stride the camera bobs to:**
  - both feet run off `rig.stride_phase()`;
  - the right foot's mid-stance sits at a quarter turn, where the rig sways the view over it (`camera.cpp`: `bob_side = sin(phase)`);
  - while a foot is down its world position is fixed, so it does not skate;
  - the hips sink, at most 12 cm, when a planted foot could not otherwise reach the ground (the top of a sprint's bob).
- **Arms:** they swing against the legs and pump higher with pace.
- **Moves:**
  - jump: knees up, arms up;
  - slide: leading leg out, a hand down behind;
  - vault: both hands planted on the obstacle, legs swung to the side;
  - climb: the hands grip where the climb starts;
  - wall-run: a hand to the wall;
  - ladder: hand over hand;
  - dive: reaching ahead;
  - roll: a tuck.
- **Smoothness:**
  - moves blend in about 0.05 s;
  - the hand and foot targets, and the knees and elbows, are inertialized in the body's own frame: a jump in a target is carried and eased out over about 0.06 s;
  - knees and elbows bend as hinges about the body's right, so a limb pointing along its old hint can't flip.

**The boxes** (`game/body_figure.h`, `game/body_figure.cpp`): one unit box per part.
- They are uploaded once and placed every frame: in the gym (`game/gym/stage.cpp:95`) and in the world, beside the chasers' figures (`game/world/stage.cpp:171`).
- The head and neck are put away in first person, since the camera is inside them.
- Materials are kit 1, roles 13 and 14.
