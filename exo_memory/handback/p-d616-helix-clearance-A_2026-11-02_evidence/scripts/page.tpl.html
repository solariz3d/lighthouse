How this was measured

Both kn5 files were read in place; nothing was written to the track folders. Their drivable road meshes are byte-equal, and so are p38's road and apron. The exports differ in the drawn underside, and in the edge aprons of other pieces, which moved by 11 mm at most.

Three passes over the whole track found this one place. Front-face rays from every road and apron triangle, 3 m out, hit something only here, and only from the apron: nearest drawn hull 0.92 m (old) and 0.30 m (new) along the apron's normal. Drawn vertices more than 2 cm above a road or apron are only here. Triangle against triangle, p38's hull meets p41's apron in 33 pairs in the new export and 3 in the old.
