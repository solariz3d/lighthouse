**The unit question: answered by the chair at 00:05, and built that way.** One corpus word is a reader FRAGMENT (a new
one starts at every change of shape, slope or roll). So:
- **A piece's SIZE** (a curved word's total turn, the straight's length) is the median **run**: `corpus.runs`,
  consecutive words of one class joined. The tempos pick the run's p10, p50 or p90.
- **Its SHAPE** (radius, width, bank, font) is the median **word**.

## 1. The vocabulary (`src/doc/vocabgen.js`, new; `src/doc/vocab.js`, generated from it)

- **`vocabgen.js generate(corpus)`**: pure and deterministic.
  - It reads E's schema `t180b.corpus/1`: `classes` for shape, `runs` for size, and `transitions` (their `counts`).
  - It refuses a malformed corpus by name (a missing quantity, bands out of order, an unknown shape or class).
  - A corpus without transitions gives a null grammar, not a guessed one.
- **Font:** the class's largest shape share, with bowl and bowl+ counted as the bowl and pipe and pipe+ as the
  half-pipe; a tie goes to a fixed order. **Every road class is a bowl** (0.48–0.57): straights and sweeps are not flat.
- **Bank leans INTO the turn** (`document.js`): positive roll raises the left edge (measured: +30° gives L.y = +0.5), so
  a left turn ends at −bank. **It is reached in the head's own revolution.** My first version was absolute, and after
  an inversion (head at 2π) it unwound a whole roll inside one turn. That was red first
  (`roll 6.283 → -0.419`), then fixed.
- **Width** is the class median whichever font the word wears. **Tempos:** compact, standard, grand (size bands), plus
  the old aurora and serpents, kept so documents naming them load.
- **The jump is not a median, and why (measured):** the builder's jump leaves at the ROAD's pitch (no kicker;
  `resolve.js solveJump`). At 460 km/h off a level road, the median jump (125 m, 14 m down) **misses its landing at both
  falls** (red: `landing-misses-zone`, 3.2 g MISS, 6.3 g MISS). The p10 gap, 81 m, is **caught at both**. The rule from
  D179/D181 is that a user's first jump is clean at the default speed, so the default gap is the p10 and the drop the
  median. The chair's note says "jumps a 125 m gap and a 14 m drop". **This conflicts with that note, and is for the
  keeper:** real T-180s take jumps off a kicker (median 7.2° up, 560 km/h, the 15 flights in FINDINGS §7d/§8). **A
  take-off-pitch handle would let the median jump be the default. That is a document-schema change, so I did not
  make it.**
