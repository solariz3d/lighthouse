## Step 1: light and air (checked: captured; free in cost)
- **Lamp colour:**
  - The apartment's rooms are warm tungsten (2700 K) and its corridors, the Lane and Cut cells, cool fluorescent (4000 K, green lifted 4 %).
  - `gen::lamp_kelvin` uses Tanner Helland's fit to the Planckian locus, taken to linear RGB, as a camera balanced at 3800 K sees it, scaled to the old lamps' luminance (0.872). The results are rooms (1.24, 0.82, 0.35) and corridors (0.81, 0.89, 0.92).
  - Each fixture glows in its own lamp's colour.
- **Dead lamps:** one in five (`LayoutRules::lamp_dead` 0.2, a new salt placed after the last so no other draw moves). The fixture is there, a dull white, and gives no light. Reprieves' and wells' lamps never die.
  - checked: the new gen row reads 1,174 apartment fixtures with 926 lit (21.1 % dead), and in the hotel 969 of 969 lit.
- **Air:**
  - fog 0.012 → 0.016 (`world.txt`);
  - its colour, and the void's, a dim warm (0.016, 0.012, 0.008) instead of a blue night;
  - a tight warm halation: the bloom's first, half-size level added once more, tinted (1.0, 0.72, 0.45), with `world.txt` `halation` 0.35.

## Step 2: surfaces (checked: captured; +0.05 ms)
- **What:** `dz.hlsl` `SurfaceAlbedo` makes each hit's colour from its world position per material role, at every hit, so the bounce carries it too:
  - low-pile carpet on floors (a fine pile and a faint diamond weave; beige-brown in the apartment);
  - popcorn ceilings in the apartment (kit 0);
  - painted walls with a roller's faint stripes and a darker band near the floor;
  - wood grain on frames and door leaves;
  - a slow grime field over all of it.
- **What stays flat:** lamps, the shared fixtures, the figures and the body keep their flat colours.
- **The switch:** it is `Settings::surfaces`, on in the world and off in the gym.
- **By eye at full size the patterns are faint**, as E's spec asked ("faint roller variation"). If he wants them stronger, the strengths are plain numbers in `SurfaceAlbedo`.
- **Door onto a wall** (the look page's "a door too many"):
  - Now and then an apartment room has a frame and a closed leaf, 3 to 4.5 cm proud of a wall, with nothing behind them.
  - It is drawn only (`kSurfaceDetail`, its own `SolidKind::FalseDoor`): nothing collides with it, and the walk graph, the chase and the sound never know it.
  - It is left out wherever it would overlap anything (a split room's steps), and it never sits on the next chunk's side of a chunk edge.
  - **I chose the drawn door over a real extra doorway.** A real one changes collision and the walk graph, so it changes the chase.
  - **Correction to myself:** the share was 0.025 a wall strip until the count read 46 in 36 chunks (about 1.3 a chunk, not "rare"). It is now 0.01: checked, 13 in 36 chunks, about one in three.

## Step 3: the soft picture (checked: captured; −0.74 ms)
- **Render scale:**
  - The trace runs at `Settings::renderScale` of the screen each way (`world.txt` `render_scale` 0.75).
  - The anti-aliasing, kept at the output's size, takes for each output pixel the traced sample nearest its centre (behind the jitter). Its history fills in between as the jitter walks the samples.
  - The trace's colour is now a ping-pong, which carries each pixel's frames of lighting to the next trace (that was read from the resolved picture, which is at the output's size).
  - At a scale of 1 nothing differs.
  - inferred: the history converges, so settled shots look much like step 2's. The softening shows in motion (the sprint view).
- **Dither:** interleaved gradient noise (Jimenez 2014), walked each frame.
  - **This is not true blue noise.** It is the texture-free stand-in with a similar spectrum, chosen because the renderer loads no textures. Real blue noise would need a texture.
- **Speed haze:** the fog and the bloom thicken with speed, up to `speed_haze` 0.6 at a full sprint, eased over 0.3 s.
