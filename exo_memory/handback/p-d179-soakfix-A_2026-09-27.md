  the pitch from 120.41° to −2° **through vertical in 15.9 m** (kp0 −0.509, kp1 +0.241). The flight chord then runs
  about 180° from the lip's horizontal heading. No car flies that.

**Fixed, red first** (`test/doc-jump.test.js`, 4 new tests, the first red before the fix: it resolved):
- `solveJump` refuses a take-off with |pitch| ≥ 90° (**JUMP_PAST_VERTICAL**, naming the pitch);
- and, after solving, a flight whose pitch reaches ±90° anywhere. p(s) is quadratic, so it checks both ends and the
  vertex: "the only flight from X that lands … turns its pitch to Y, through vertical".
- Tests: the soak's own 120.41° take-off is refused and 80° still resolves; every flown jump keeps |pitch| < 90° along
  the whole flight (at 28.6°, 80.2°, −57.3° and −85.9°); a 126° take-off is PAST VERTICAL, not "unsolvable"; and a
  take-off BELOW vertical whose only flight peaks at 99° mid-air is refused (from 51.6°, 3 m across, 8 m up, landing at
  −0.5 rad, **found by a search**, not guessed).
- `node --test --test-concurrency=4 test/doc-jump.test.js` → 16/16; `test/doc*.test.js` → 137/137 (with those).
- By D178's rule, a jump the resolver refuses makes the edit that would create it a refused edit, so the document never
