- **The first quantity that differs where it should not: the lights the light grid hands the point.** The record, material, normal,
  AO and albedo all match.
- **Why:** `LightsAt(P)` takes `floor((P - gridMin) / cell)`, and the grid's cells sit on whole multiples of their size
  (`render/src/renderer.cpp` BuildLightSet: "Cells sit on whole multiples of their size"; 3.5 m high, a storey). So **every storey's
  floor lies exactly on a boundary between two cells.**
  - A traced point micrometres under the floor is looked up in the storey below's cell.
  - That cell lists the lower storey's lamps. A room's lamp is listed from 2 cm above its floor (`game/world/stage.cpp`: below = y -
    floor - 0.02), so not there.
  - The floor itself blocks the lower lamps from the point, so it goes dark.
- **checked** that the patch's hits are under the floor and the bright ones on it: the P.y row above.
- **inferred, not traced:** why the far end of a long triangle tips under.
  - IN's error is about 100 times OUT's, and pane B's cut and reversed tops both cleared the patch. That fits an intersection error
    growing with distance from the triangle's first corner.
  - I did not trace the hardware's arithmetic.
  - The streaks are the cell flipping along lines of equal distance.
- **My own afternoon reading (two coplanar surfaces)** stands refuted, as pane B found. The "streaks along iso-depth lines" I saw were
  this flipping.

## R2, the fix at the cause

**`render/shaders/dz.hlsl` `Direct()`: `LightsAt(O)` where it was `LightsAt(P)`.** `O = P + Ng * 0.01` is the point its shadow rays
already start from: a centimetre off the surface, on the surface's own side, the side the ray came from.

- **The generator is not changed:** no world's solids or drawn tops move.
- **Not a change that only moves where the fault begins** (the packet's registered "wrong"):
  - the lookup no longer depends on the hit's sub-millimetre error at all;
  - it would return only if a hit's error passed a centimetre, about 3,500 times what R0 measured.
  - The row holds at 5 mm under.
