## The rows

1. The fixture is the cases it names (sweeps checked from the document), and each case has road and skin in the export, plus apron in the open cases. This is the control.
2. **THE JAG.** Across the road (edges whose direction is less than 60° off lateral), the skin may bend no more than the road it hangs under, plus 10°, in each case.
3. No open edge in the drawn hull. A boundary edge is open unless another boundary edge covers it within 2 mm (a T-junction). The track's two ends (4 m) are excluded.
4. No sliver: no drawn triangle thinner than `acready.js` `SLIVER` (4 mm).
5. Nothing clips: no drawn triangle passes through another (road, skin, apron), outside pairs that share a vertex.
6. Collision inside, read on the physics meshes (`1ROAD`/`1GRIP`):
   - every 2 m along the track, a ray down from 1 m above the centreline finds a surface;
   - in the closed cases, rays from the ring's centre find the wall every 15° round.
7. The AI line has points in every case, no step over 10 m, and none more than 1 m off the centreline.
