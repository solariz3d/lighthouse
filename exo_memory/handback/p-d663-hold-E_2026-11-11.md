## 1. What changed

**`consonance/tools/heavy-run.js`** (+105 −1; the old behaviour unchanged when nobody holds):
- **`hold({ cmd, exclusive: true })`** (`acquire`'s new `exclusive` option):
  - writes `<data>/heavy-run.hold` with `wx`: `{pid, seat, cmd, exclusive, tree, started, token, image, holder_start}`, plus
    `took_over` when it replaced a stale hold;
  - then waits until EVERY slot is free of other runs, any tree and any seat (`blocksMe`);
  - then takes its slot under the same token.
- **Every other runner waits** while the hold stands. It is the lock's own wait style, with the 30-minute max and exit 3. It says
  once: `heavy-run: waiting on an exclusive hold by <seat> pid <pid> (<cmd>, since <t>)`.
- **The holder's children pass:** a runner carrying the hold's token, through `CONSONANCE_HEAVY_RUN_TOKEN`.
- **A runner that claimed a slot looks at the hold again after its claim and backs off if one appeared.** It is the same two-step as
  the slot rival check, so a claim racing the hold's write never runs inside it.
- **A stale hold** is taken over by the lock's own `takeIfStale`:
  - kill(0); D283's second witness when it is young; PID reuse;
  - logged as `took over a stale lock — … (slot hold)`;
  - recorded in the new hold, not in the slot lock.
- **A hold older than `HOLD_MAX_MS` (90 min) is IGNORED,** with a loud line once per hold:
  `heavy-run: IGNORING the exclusive hold by …: it is Nm old, past the 90m maximum, so it no longer parks the room. If its run is
  over, remove <file>`.
  - An ordinary runner then runs.
  - An exclusive one moves the over-age hold aside (putting back a newer one a race may have moved) and writes its own.
- **Release** removes the hold only with its own token. **A holder that gives up** (the max wait) removes its hold before failing.
- **The CLI:** `node heavy-run.js --run [--exclusive] --cmd "<what>" -- <program> [args...]` runs one program under the lock (and the
  hold) and exits with its code. Usage errors exit 2. `windowsHide: true` on the spawn.

**`consonance/GATES.md`:** a new subsection "The exclusive hold (D663)" under "The heavy-run lock" (+29). It covers:
- what the hold is, the CLI, the waiting line, children, stale and over-age holds;
- why (the relayed hold that could not bind);
- the rule: **a timing session and a landing's quiet window take the exclusive hold; Blender bakes and any game run over 10 s go
  through heavy-run** (`--run`, or `hold({ cmd })`);
- that a run which never takes heavy-run is not stopped by it (no process watcher, out of scope).
- `git diff 637e533f -- consonance/GATES.md` gives the 29 lines.
