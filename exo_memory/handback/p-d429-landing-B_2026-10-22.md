## 2. A D428 regression, found by A's row and fixed (27d6dbf)

**Found.** After the merge, `sculpt-subscribers.test.js` row 4 was red: "the frame after the release asks once, for the let-go track: 1 !== 2" (`SC/lab3.tap`).

**The cause is D428's, not the merge's.** `sharedPick` cached one pick per pointer event. With the pointer still, the labels' D242 rule asks again when the track changes (`pickedFor = undefined` on new segments), but the event key had not changed, so the cache handed back the old track's pick. So since c1841ca, a still pointer kept its old piece after any edit. My D428 hand-back missed it, and D428 had no test of its own.

**Fixed.** The cache is now keyed by the event and by the track's `segments`:
- the labels pass `track.segments`;
- Sculpt's hover passes `askOf('t180:track-request').segments`.

Row 4 was red at ce96573 and is green at 27d6dbf. The three files `sculpt-subscribers`, `core-pieces-ui` and `sculpt-cues` give 63 / 63 (`SC/lab3b.tap`). That is 3 files, so no lock.
