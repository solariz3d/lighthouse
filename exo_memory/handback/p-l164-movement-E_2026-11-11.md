## THE SHORT ANSWER

- **Bhop can exist in our controller almost as it stands.**
  - [code] It already uses Quake's accelerate rule, capped only along the wished direction, in the air too (`sim/world.cpp:179-184`, `196-212`).
  - [code] A parameter decides how much of the speed gained by air-strafing is kept: `air_strafe_gain`, "0 steers only, 1 is Quake" (`sim/params.h`, the air-control block). It is 0 by default (`sim/tests/params_tests.cpp:19`).
  - [code] An existing test already shows speed growing with the Quake settings, `air_strafing_gains_speed_with_the_quake_settings` (`sim/tests/jump_tests.cpp:282-287`): 7 → 9–12 m/s in a second.
- **The landing side is already friction-free when a jump is buffered.**
  - [code] A jump pressed up to 0.12 s before landing (`jump_buffer_time`) fires on the first grounded tick, in the ground transitions (`sim/world.cpp:910-915`). That runs **before** the velocity switch, which is where `ground_friction()` would run (`:963-964`). The state is already Air by then.
  - [inferred] So today a well-timed hop loses **no** speed to friction. It gains none only because `air_strafe_gain` is 0.
  - That is the opposite of Source and Quake, where the friction-free moment is one frame [source, below].
- **"Hard at base, loosened by a late perk"** is mostly a parameter change, plus one new rule: a landing-friction window. Options are below.

## 2. Source and Quake air movement: the formulas, from the source code

**Quake III Arena** [source: `id-Software/Quake-III-Arena`, `code/game/bg_pmove.c`, GPL; quoted]
- **Accelerate**, ground and air alike: `currentspeed = DotProduct(velocity, wishdir); addspeed = wishspeed - currentspeed; if (addspeed <= 0) return; accelspeed = accel*frametime*wishspeed; if (accelspeed > addspeed) accelspeed = addspeed; velocity += accelspeed*wishdir;`
- **Constants:** `pm_accelerate = 10`, `pm_airaccelerate = 1`, `pm_friction = 6`, `pm_stopspeed = 100`.
- **Friction:** `control = speed < pm_stopspeed ? pm_stopspeed : speed; drop += control*pm_friction*frametime; newspeed = (speed - drop)/speed`.
- **Air:** `PM_AirMove` calls `PM_Accelerate(wishdir, wishspeed, pm_airaccelerate)`, with the comment "not on ground, so little effect on velocity".
- **No auto-hop:** `PM_CheckJump`: "must wait for jump to be released"; while `PMF_JUMP_HELD` is set, no jump.

**Source (Half-Life 2, CS:S)** [source: `ValveSoftware/source-sdk-2013`, `src/game/shared/gamemovement.cpp`; quoted]
- `AirAccelerate`: `if (wishspd > GetAirSpeedCap()) wishspd = GetAirSpeedCap();`. The cap value is not in that file's visible text.
  - [source, secondary: a gist on Source air speed] the cap is 30 units/s, `sv_air_max_wishspeed`.
  - [inferred] 30 u/s is about 0.57 m/s at 1.905 cm a unit.
- `AirMove` calls `AirAccelerate(wishdir, wishspeed, sv_airaccelerate.GetFloat())`.
- Friction has the same shape as Quake's, with `sv_stopspeed` and `sv_friction`.
- **No auto-hop:** `CheckJumpButton`: `if (mv->m_nOldButtons & IN_JUMP) return false; // don't pogo stick`.

**Why air-strafing grows speed** [source: Biagioli, "Bunnyhopping from the Programmer's Perspective", 2015, adrianb.io]
- "Instead of limiting velocity directly, only the projection of the current velocity onto acceleration is limited."
- Keep the wish direction nearly perpendicular to the velocity (strafe key plus mouse turn), and the projection stays under the cap, so acceleration keeps adding sideways and the total grows. Per tick, |v|² rises by about 2·proj·a + a².
- [code] Our test's own comment says the same (`jump_tests.cpp:281`).

**Friction on landing** [source: Biagioli]
- "there is a 1-frame window where friction is not applied when the player hits the ground"; "single frame to input the jump command without losing speed".
- [inferred] On Source's 66 Hz that frame is 15 ms. **At our 240 Hz, one tick is 4.2 ms.** A one-tick window would be much harder than Source; a few ticks match it.

**Auto-hop against manual timing**
- [source] Both engines refuse a held jump; the button must be released and pressed again.
- [source: repo research doc :38-41] Quake hopping gains up to about 500 u/s before friction between jumps cancels it.
- [code] **Ours is also edge-triggered**: `jump_press_age` counts from a press, so holding the button does not re-jump.
- [code] **But the 0.12 s buffer turns a manual press into a forgiving one** (`sim/params.h`, `jump_buffer_time 0.12`; `world.cpp:897`).
- [code] The first game deliberately killed bhop (`dreamzone-rt/src/main.cpp:188-191`, `[BHOPLOCK]`): a landing friction pulse (×2.2 for 0.12 s) and 50 ms on the ground before another jump (`:7602`, `:7657`).

## 3. Our controller against it (dreamzone-3 631b6a7)

**Can speed grow by strafing?**
- [code] Yes, mechanically. `air_accelerate` (`world.cpp:196-212`) is Quake's rule with `air_wish_speed` 0.8 m/s as the projection cap.
- [code] Then the gain over the previous speed is scaled by `air_strafe_gain` (0 by default).
- [code] `air_accel` is 2.0 (the parameter's own note: "Quake feel: about 12").

**What caps it:**
- [code] `soft_cap_speed` 14 m/s, with drag 3/s on the excess; `hard_cap_speed` 20 (`apply_speed_caps`, `:214-224`);
- [code] the slide boost's fatigue;
- [code] on foot, "turning never makes the player faster than the legs or the speed already carried" (`:966-973`).

**Friction on the landing tick:**
- [code] The tick that touches ground has no friction: `land()` runs in the move, after that tick's velocity.
- [code] The next tick, a buffered jump fires before friction (`:910-915` before `:963`).
- [code] Without a jump, friction applies from that tick on. A hard landing (a fall over `safe_fall_height` 6 m) staggers (`:275-280`).

**A chain detector already exists:**
- [code] The **momentum tier** (`flow_enabled` 0, off by default): "chained moves raise the top speed above sprint speed".
- [code] `flow_transition()` is called on a roll from a landing (`:274`), at the end of a vault or mantle (`:429`), on a wall-kick (`:519`) and on a slide start (`:1128`). The chain window is `flow_chain_window` 2 s. A hard stop is speed under `flow_break_speed` 3.5, and a stagger resets it (`:279`).

**What "bhop in the base, hard; a late perk loosens it" would touch:**

| piece | base (hard) | perk (loosened) | where |
|---|---|---|---|
| speed kept from strafing | `air_strafe_gain` small, e.g. 0.1–0.25 | 1.0 (Quake) | `params.h`; `world.cpp:205-211` |
| air accel | 2.0 today; perhaps 4–6 | 10–12 | `params.h` |
| friction-free landing window | **new rule**: friction applies on the first grounded tick unless the jump press fell within N ticks of landing. N small (1–3 ticks = 4–12 ms) | N larger (~15 ticks, 60 ms), or no friction on any buffered hop (today's behaviour) | new parameter; `world.cpp:910-915` and `:963` |
| buffer | `jump_buffer_time` 0.12 s still lets the press come early, but only a press inside N avoids friction | as today | `params.h` |
| top speed | `soft_cap_speed` 14 | raised, e.g. 18–20, so the evil god can be survived, not outrun | `params.h` |
| an optional landing tax | the first game's pulse (×2.2 for 0.12 s) as a base-only toll | removed | new; precedent `dreamzone-rt/src/main.cpp:188-191` |

## 5. What counts as a movement chain: options, not a design

Each option names its precedent. [inferred] for the fit.

- **A. The momentum tier's events, as they are.** A chain is the moves that call `flow_transition` (roll, vault or mantle finish, wall-kick, slide) inside a 2 s window; it breaks on a hard stop (< 3.5 m/s) or a stagger. XP = base × chain length.
  - Precedent: THPS's multiplier. **The cheapest option**, because the detector exists (`world.cpp:163-167`).
  - Jumps and hops are not in it today.
- **B. A, with variety.** The same move repeated in a chain scores less each time.
  - Precedent: THPS ("the same trick repeatedly… causes that trick's score to drop").
  - It keeps a slide-spam from paying.
- **C. Speed held.** XP per second above a speed threshold, such as above `sprint_peak_speed`, with a bonus for a straight held while something closes in (his "sprinting in a straight line… for letting it catch up").
  - Precedent: Titanfall's speed as its own reward; Catalyst's focus from running.
- **D. Risk-gated chains.** Chain XP counts only while a chaser is within N m, or multiplies by proximity.
  - It ties to his item 53 proximity multiplier.
- **E. Hops, for the endless perk.** Consecutive friction-free hops, or air time, score once the perk is owned.
  - Precedent: Evade's bhop culture.
- **F. Near misses.** A chain event when a chaser's reach was within X m and the player escaped by a move (a vault, a slide under, a somersault).
  - [inferred] The "juke" made countable. It needs the chase module to report the moment.
- **How a chain ends, for any option:** a hard stop, a stagger, being touched, or the window running out. Banked at the end, or lost if caught mid-chain? (THPS loses a combo on a bail.)

## The menu for him

1. **Bhop:** keep the rule as it is (friction-free on any buffered hop) and only gate speed gain by the perk; or add the landing-friction window, so base hops are hard and the perk widens it?
2. **Base hop strength:** `air_strafe_gain` at 0 (no gain until the perk) or a little (0.1–0.25: skilled players gain a bit)?
3. **The perk's ceiling:** raise the soft cap to 18–20 m/s, or leave 14, so the evil god is survived, not escaped?
4. **Chain rule:** A (cheapest), A+B, C, D, E or F, or a mix?
5. **The somersault:** worth a sim and chase coupling, or a vault-like move only over static geometry?
6. **Dive steering:** should the dive follow the view (new) or the run (today)?
