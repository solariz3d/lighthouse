## 2. The ease, step by step, and why each step is there (`src/core/extend.js`, diff 496c347..441e869)

1. `smoother` for `C2_CHANNELS = {phi, c, e, er}` in `channelFn` (a fourth argument, the channel). Turn by and Sharp keep their symmetric Bloss ease (turnby.js, sharp.js: untouched).
2. **The fit does not carry the quintic exactly, and the Bloss it did.** The Bloss cubic is a cubic in s, so the cubic B-spline's least-squares fit reproduced it to the quantum: the end was the target, the hold flat, every row of the mesher's smoothstep blend on c(s). The quintic is approximated: the first fit rang 0.04° at the end (a bank typed 300 read 299.96, an edge brought to 0 ended at 0.002 and stayed "live" for the next piece, a cup at 150 ended with slope −0.47), and in the hold (0.02° at s = 120 of a ramp over 100). So `fitWithHold`: every control point whose Greville abscissa is past the transition, and the last two, are set to the target, and the free points are refitted by least squares with those held (normal equations over samples every ~0.5 m, the basis from `channelValue` on one-hot control points, Gaussian elimination with pivoting; a singular system leaves the fit as it was). The ramp reaches 53.92° at the transition of a 54° roll over 100 m and holds 54.000 past it (`bankrow8.js`).
3. `c2Ends`: the end curvature of a clamped cubic B-spline is linear in P[2] (start) and P[n−3] (end), read exactly from four samples on the end span; the 2×2 system is solved and the two points moved (quantised to the channel's decimals). With two interior knots the two points reach both ends; with fewer than six control points there is no free point and the piece is left as fitted. It runs AFTER the cup/tube/edge clamps (they may move a channel's last two points: "an edge that ends at 0 ends flat"), and the clamps run once more on what it moved.
4. **Three interior knots** for a piece with a cross-section target (`D.evenKnots(length, length / 4)` when the even knots give fewer): with two, the ramp is the one C2 cubic spline through its ends and peaks 1.20–1.24× the quintic's rate; with three, 1.07× (`peak.js` → `peak-final.out`: e 30° over 50 m 1.200 °/m against the quintic's 1.125; phi 30° over 100 m 0.568 against 0.562; rms off the quintic 0.5–1.3% of the change).

## 10. Part 3c: the closure is C2 (the chair's dispatch of 2026-10-26T00:04:41Z; plan § "3b in", ruling 1). On d459-p `56a0129`, not pushed

**The choice: curvature rows in the closure system, not an ease after the close.** The closure already meets each channel's VALUE and SLOPE round the seam as rows of
a least-norm Gauss–Newton step (close.js, D185; the Gram metric for a window, D431); a channel's end curvature is as linear in its control points as its end slope
is (the closed forms of continuity.js: the first and last three points and two knot spans), so a curvature row joins the same system and the same step meets it
exactly at convergence, under the same metric, with the same refusal by name when a window's pieces have too few free points. An ease over the window after the
close would have to move control points while keeping every other row met: a second least-norm solve on the same system, without its refusals and without its
report. The ease was not written.

**What the rows are** (`src/core/close.js`, diff 441e869..56a0129):
- at the seam, for each of φ, c, e, er that continues round it (the same `seamJoined` rule as the value and slope rows; c skipped where the cup is held against a
  legacy start, as its value row is), the residual k_end(last) − k_start(first) and its row `valueRow(ch, 'curv')`;
- across every joint A|B of two road pieces where a free column of that channel sits on either side (A's last three points or B's P₂: `c2Pairs`), the residual
  k_end(A) − k_start(B) and its row `curvRow`: the joints the close MOVES stay C2 (before, a whole-lap close broke two of twenty seeds at a joint inside the lap);
- `opts.c2Channels` names the channels, every one by default. **Finish asks for the edges only** (`finish.js FINISH_C2 = ['e', 'er']`): its bank plan owns the
  bank at the closer's joints and the seam (D447: C1, the slope change bounded by BANK_ACCEL), and a φ row there moved the plan's points off their share (0.46° at
  TEST TRACK 10's p42, core_finish_bank rows 1–3 and 10 red); cupHome owns the cup (3b: C2 by its quintic where the rate allows, the slowest straight fade where
