## 1. What it is

- **The mechanism CHANGED from motors to the soft keyframe**, as ruled. This is named in `ragdoll.h`, `ragdoll.cpp` and
  the test header.
  - Each upper-body body is dynamic: the chest, neck, head, eye, shoulders, elbows, wrists and hands.
  - Each step its velocity is a blend of the velocity that would put its centre of mass and rotation on its keyframe
    in that step, and its own. This is Jolt's `SoftKeyframedRigTest.cpp:40-91`, with the keyframe's share of gravity
    taken off.
  - The pelvis, the lower spine and the legs stay keyed, as in step 2.
- **The per-bone strength is the blend:** s = strength × (1 − e^(−dt/τ)), frame-rate free.
  - τ is **0.005 s** for the trunk and **0.01 s** for the arms. These are ours, changed after this mechanism's first
    measured run (section 2).
- **Step 5:** `react()` drops the strength to **0.15** and ramps it back to 1 over **1.0 s** (both ours).
  - The stage calls it the frame the run enters `Ended` (the catch).
- **Kept from the motor attempt:**
  - each body takes its keyframe's velocity on the step after a placement, so a hunter that appears running is not
    flung;
  - the ragdoll's own solver runs 30 velocity and 10 position steps.
- **The drive is renamed** `UpperOnMotors` → `UpperSoftKeyed` (and `on_motors` → `soft_keyed`). The tests change by that
  name only.
- **The stage** (`game/world/stage.{h,cpp}`, `place_figures`):
  - the hunter's pose → `Ragdoll::key` → `drawn_from` → the palette;
  - the ragdoll is made when the hunter is first drawn;
  - it is dropped when the hunter is put away, and placed again when its pose jumps over 1 m in a frame (a new run);
  - each step's dt is the frame's, clamped to 1/1000–1/30 s.
  - `dz-world` now links `dz_ragdoll`.
