## 3 · IF FPS DROPS — the levers, in order, and which lights carry them

**Seven of the 11 cast dynamic shadows:** L045–L050 (all six Track Stadium, at half resolution) and **L077**. The other
four (L076, L078, L079, L080) have shadows off already.

1. **L077 first.** It carries a full-resolution shadow map and, at intensity 0, lights nothing. Whether CSP skips a
   shadow pass for a black light I did not find at source; if it does not, L077 is pure cost. Its shadows off, or
   L077 left out of the keep-set, costs nothing visible.
2. **Shadows off on L045–L050.** Six half-resolution shadow maps.
3. **A shorter range on the four lit Inner Stadium lights** (300 m now). Per the packet: range, never aiming.

**My prediction, and it is a prediction:** FPS on Night Optimized drops from its 300–420 but stays above the stock
220–240. The reasoning: the README's gain came from 20 × 450 m shadowed floodlights plus 27 ambient lights, and these
11 were on in the stock measurement too, so the stock night is a ceiling on what adding them back can cost. **Nothing
was measured** — no game ran on this machine in this lap. The keeper's counter at the README's spot is the test.

## 5 · WHAT CHANGED IN THE LUA, EXACTLY

- Every light row gains a first field `id='Lxxx'`, the inventory's numbering (the generator numbers LIGHT and
  LIGHT_SERIES sections in file order, as `tools/inventory.js` does). The build loop skips `id` like it skips `line`.
- The early `return` on `night_optimized` is gone. In its place: `NIGHT_OPTIMIZED_KEEP` (the 11 ids), `keepOnly =
  layout == NIGHT_OPTIMIZED`, and `wanted(d)`, which is true for every light on the other layouts and only for the 11
  on Night Optimized.
- The log line counts what this layout builds, so it reads 61 / 61 / 11.
- On Normal and No Dogbowls the 61 lights, their order and every value are byte-identical to HEAD — the check's first
  assertion.
- **One behavioural difference on Night Optimized besides the 11 lights:** `script.update` now runs there every frame
  (one sun-angle read and a comparison). Before, the script ended before defining it.
