## 1. The FDN reverb, set from the listener's room

**The mixer** (`audio/mixer.cpp`, "the room"):
- Eight delay lines, mixed by an 8-point Hadamard (energy kept). Each line loses `10^(-3 L / (RT60 fs))` every time round, and a
  one-pole filter darkens the tail at the room's `damp_hz`.
- The lines are the room's mean free path (4V/S) times a spread of factors, so a bigger room's reflections come further apart.
- Everything the room is given follows smoothly, over about 0.25 s, with fractional delay reads, so walking from a bedroom into a
  corridor makes no click.
- Into it go the chasers, the feet, the cues and the place's sounds. Three things stay out, a choice of mine (the Addendum said "one
  reverb on the bed and the footsteps"):
  - the hunter's tone and the reprieve's hum and tell, which are in no place and heard in the middle;
  - the bed, a recording that already has a room.
- **The return is normalised** by how much the lines hold, √(1 − mean g²), so `wet` is the room's level for a steady sound in any
  room. A long room rings longer without being louder. This came from the world selftest; see section 6.
- With `room.decay_s` 0, the default, the reverb does nothing and the dry mix is as before.
  - checked: all 77 existing audio tests pass unchanged (counts in section 6).
  - The tone is now summed through a buffer of its own so the room can leave it out. `l += (0 + s)` is the same float as `l += s`.

**The room** (`game/world/acoustics.cpp`, `listener_room`):
- The listener's open cells on their storey are flooded across `Open`, `Air` and `Rail` edges, and stop at walls and doorways. At most
  96 cells; beyond that, what is left open is counted as an opening.
- Each cell adds volume (9 m² times its clear height; a hall, stairwell or shaft is as tall as its open column, up to 6 storeys) and
  floor and ceiling.
- Each closed side adds wall area. A doorway's 2.1 m² absorbs everything that reaches it.
- **Sabine:** RT60 = 0.161 V / A, clamped to 0.12 to 4 s, with each surface's α from the biome's row (room, corridor, open).
- **The biome rows:** apartment α 0.35 room / 0.12 corridor / 0.06 open, damping 4,200 Hz, wet 0.35. Hotel is softer. Pool rooms
  are 0.02 to 0.03 at 10 kHz (item 76's long tiled reverb). Facility, mall, school and chaos are hard.
  - Only the apartment and the hotel are defined biomes today; the others are rows waiting.
- checked: `world_app_tests in_the_apartment_a_room_is_heard_short_and_damped_and_a_corridor_longer` → seed 1, the apartment forced,
  cells x, z in −20..19 of storey 0: **328 room cells with a median decay of 0.38 s; 549 corridor cells, 0.61 s**. The bounds are a
  room under 0.5 s and a corridor over 1.5 times a room.
  - This row was written after the flood code, not red first.
- In `sound.cpp` the room and the floor are worked out again when the player's cell changes. Seconds of play were not timed (see
  section 7).
