## What it does
1. **The drop-down** (`app/core/panel.js`, `app/core/widthlike.js`): a `width like…` select in its own labelled row right after the width field. Entries read `Thunderhead, 24 m (21–28)`, narrowest first, one per known track (19). **Picking one types the median into the width field**, exactly as typing it: so the ghost and the readout follow, and **on an empty track it is the first piece's start (`first.w`): that IS the new-track default** (D194a already makes the typed values the first piece's start), so there is no separate default control; one select serves both. The select goes back to its placeholder after a pick, so the same entry can be picked again.
2. **A tube (the sweep field at 360, typed or SHOWN from the head)**: the figures become the distance ROUND the tube and each entry adds how wide that is across: `Thunderhead, 24 m round (21–28), a tube 7.6 m across` (w/π), the placeholder reads `width like… (a tube: metres round)`, and a line under the controls says `A tube's width is the distance ROUND it, not across. 38 m round is a tube 12.1 m across (w/π).` (it follows whatever is in the width field). Back below 360 the entries read plain again and the line goes.
3. **The data, numbers only** (`src/doc/widths.json`, `schema t180b.widths/1`): per track its name (the ones FINDINGS uses), `median_m`, `p10_m`, `p90_m`, the station count and `read_closes`. Made by `node tools/widths.cjs reads > src/doc/widths.json`: over each read's stations, every finite `width`, the median and the 10th to 90th percentile (the corpus's own `pct`, linear between order statistics). I recomputed the plan's table from the reads two ways (all stations with a width, and the road stations only as `corpus.cjs` counts): **the "all stations" way reproduces the plan's table exactly**, so that is the rule.
4. **The 19**: Miandros 21 (18–25, its read does not close), T-180 Bowl Track 22 (21–25), Thunderhead 24 (21–28), Hazen Loop 29 (28–32), T-180 Test Track 29 (28–31), Eagleton 30 (29–34), The Bowltrack 30 (30–33), Centrifuge 31 (29–36), Eagleton (short) 31 (29–36), Sakura Speedway 32 (31–35), Coast 33 (32–38), Serpents Spiral 34 (32–38), **Aurora Medium 34 (32–56)**, **Aurora Long 34 (32–53, its read stopped at its limit)**, Nordic 38 (29–53), Onuris Long 39 (36–45), Onuris Medium 39 (36–46), Onuris Short 41 (36–48), Rainbow Road 45 (35–90).
