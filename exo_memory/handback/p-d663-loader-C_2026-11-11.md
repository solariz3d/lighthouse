## 2. The loader, `model/` (CPU only)
- **What it reads:** a `.glb` into the game's meshes (`Mesh` grows `uv0`, `joints`, `weights` at its end), materials, images and one skin.
  - Images are decoded by **the OS's own imaging component** (WIC, `windowscodecs`): no image library added.
  - Only base-colour and emissive images are decoded; the renderer has no normal maps yet.
- **The game's frame:**
  - glTF is right-handed with the model's left at +X, so x is mirrored (positions, normals, and matrices as S·M·S).
  - A mirror turns each face's cross product inward, so the last two indices of each triangle are swapped to keep it outward. My header first claimed the order could stay; I caught that by working a triangle through before any test ran, and `a_mesh_is_mirrored…` now pins it.
- **Skinned vertices are stored at rest** (each through `rest_world × inverse_bind`), so a palette is `posed_world × inverse(rest_world)`.
- **Refused whole on any fault, naming the mesh and primitive** (the green run's own words):
  - `mesh 'panel' primitive 0: texture coordinates runs past the end of the file's data` (truncated);

  - `…: image 'img0' has no data in the file`;

## 3. The renderer: static models with UVs, then skinned (item 2)
- **The vertex and texturing:**
  - the shared vertex is **32 bytes**: position, normal, uv;
  - `Geom::flags` marks UVs (bit 0) and skinned (bit 1);
  - a model's texture is sampled at its UVs, with a **ray-cone mip** against texels per metre on the hit triangle (its UV area over its area); the generator's faces keep their box projection;
  - `TexInfo` carries a texture's height (it was padding).
- **Skinning:**
  - `SetSkinPalette(mesh, palette, joints)`;
  - each frame, `SkinCS` copies each posed vertex's last position aside, then writes the new one into the mesh's own stretch of the shared vertex buffer (now made with unordered access when any mesh is skinned);
  - then the mesh's structure (built `ALLOW_UPDATE`, never compacted) is **refitted**, with **one posed mesh a frame rebuilt in turn** (`Settings::skinRebuild`; `skinFastBuild` is a build flag for later timing).
- **Motion state 3, deforming:** the history and the anti-aliasing move a hit back by its own triangle's movement (same barycentrics, last frame's vertices).
- **`Instance::cull_back`:** the eye's rays (not shadows, not the bounce) cull that instance's back faces; every other instance, the chunks included, is seen from both sides as before (see §5 for why).
