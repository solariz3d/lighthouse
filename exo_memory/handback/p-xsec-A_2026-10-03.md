## 5. Decisions of mine that B or the librarian may overrule (each is one line to change)
1. **Edge profile knots**: `nE = max(64, ceil(1.5·e_max))` outer-zone intervals per piece (the seal inferred `max(47, ...)`), so two edge pieces meeting at a joint draw nearly the same polyline (sag e·0.75/n² <= 0.011°, about 0.5 mm of row step). "Consecutive knots <= 1°" (E1 iii) holds for the EDGE TERM (the mesh fills in between the base cup's own coarser knots); I read it that way.
2. **The heartline ramp**: R(s) = (w/2π)·smoothstep((t − 300°)/60°) (the seal said "ramp, not step" and gave no curve). 300 = 2·CUP_MAX: a cup can only take over from t <= 300, so a tube-to-cup joint has heartline 0. My first choice (from 180°) stepped the road centre 2.9 m at a tube -> cup joint.
3. **`roll-rate` judges the core's own segments only** (`word 'core'`). Unscoped, it reds the lap seams (first 8 m, last 9 m) of five existing word-document export tests; the paused piece builder's documents are not newly judged by a bar measured for the equation core.
4. **`edge-past-cap`** is a validator row beyond the plan's list, tagged by the profile font (`edge`, `tube-edge`).
5. **EDGE_SEAM tolerance** 0.01° (and 0.01°/m): an edge turned off by `e = 0` ends a hair above 0 after the fit; under 0.01° it steps the seam < 1 mm.
6. **A target past the cap is refused by name** (§1), not clamped.
