## 1. What was wrong (from the measurement) and what changed
A drag of a handle rebuilt the ghost of the next piece on every pointer move, in 2 m chords, with every channel of the piece fitted again, three times (the readout, the corner's numbers, the ghost). It grew with the piece's length (20 ms at 1,000 m, 40 at 2,000, 600 at 20,000). Not the glass, not a regression (the measurement). Fixes, all on the preview's side while a handle is held; the placed track, the export and the full ghost built on release are unchanged:
1. **The ghost's piece is cut into at most 48 chords** (never under 2 m, never turning the heading more than 0.03 rad in one): `shell.candidate(opts, { coarse })` (`app/core/coreshell.js`, `ghostSegM`), through a new `toSegments` option `segMFor(piece, index)` (`src/core/adapter.js`: absent or answering undefined, a piece is cut by `segM` exactly as before; the segment cache's key includes each piece's chord length). The panel passes `coarse: scrubbing` (`app/core/panel.js`, one line).
2. **The cheap ghost builds at most 64 path stations on its new piece** and integrates them in 4 m steps (`app/preview/trackmodel.js` `CHEAP_STATIONS`, `CHEAP_SUBSTEP`; `src/geom/path.js` takes an optional `substep` on the path object, set only on the ghost's own copy; the placed path keeps its 2 m stations and its 0.25 m steps). Only when the new chords are coarse: a ghost built from 2 m chords is built as it always was.
3. **One solve a move, not three:** `turnby.extendCorner` remembers its last answers by document and fields, and `solve` remembers its start (the eased piece and its heading weights, which do not depend on the angle aimed at) (`src/core/turnby.js`; frozen documents only, an answer that throws is never kept).
4. **The least-squares fit shares its system across channels** (`src/core/document.js` `fitSystem`: the stations, the basis at each, the normal matrix and its Cholesky factor are built once per knot set, not once per channel, 11 times a piece), and the basis is evaluated without its two derivatives and by binary search where nothing reads them (`tools/piecewise.cjs` `basisN`; `document.js channelValue`, used by the readout's integrals).
- **"Same to the last digit":** `basisN` equals `basis` on 24,000 random cases (all the doubles equal); `channelValue` is `channelAt(...).v`; and a placed track plus three corner solves hash the same before and after (sha256 `78e077051e6b33e1`, taken at c2d7cb5; `app/test/drag-ghost.test.js` row 5). The release mesh: the placed track's mesh and two full ghosts hash `452e72d72eaf731a`, `9fa9a8d4bb63e4dd`, `93b1e4b611250fda` at c2d7cb5 and now (row 3, green on both). The export is built from the same `toSegments` default, and its suites are in the whole runs.
- **What the keeper sees:** while a handle is held the road is a slightly coarser copy (the end of a 1,000 m ghost is within 0.03 m of the full one, 0.13 m at 5 km; the heading is the same; its lane lines are polylines of 15 to 20 m, visible in the shot as slight facets at wide zoom); when the handle is let go the full road returns at once. Shots mid-drag, new and old, in the evidence folder (`mid-new.png`, `mid-old.png`). He judges the feel. Not done: a smoothing of the facets.
