## 1. What it returns (the shape is in `src/core/README.md`, "The readout")
`pieceReadout(doc, i)` and `candidateReadout(doc, opts)` (the same `opts` as `extend`) → `{ type, id, lengthM, turnDeg,
climbDeg, bankFromDeg, bankToDeg, pitchFromDeg, pitchToDeg, offsets }`.
- **turnDeg = ∫κh ds and climbDeg = ∫κv ds, EXACT.** 3-point Gauss–Legendre on each knot span, where the channel is one cubic
  and the rule is exact to degree 5.
- **bank from/to** = φ at the two ends.
- **pitch from** = the start pitch plus every earlier road piece's climb, a flight setting it to its landing pitch;
  **pitch to** = pitch from + climb.
- **A flight:** turn 0, climb = landing − take-off, and its length is the flight plus its landing ramp, summed from the
  adapter's own segments.
- **`candidateReadout` IS `pieceReadout` on `extend(doc, opts)`,** so the ghost's numbers equal the placed piece's by
  construction (tested).
- **Errors, by name:** `EMPTY` (an empty track) and `BAD_INDEX` (−1, n, 0.5, NaN, undefined). A candidate on an empty track
  reads the first piece.

**h/l, as the packet asked ("if they change the effective turn or climb, and say how").** `offsets` is null unless the piece
has h or l. When it has, it gives the EFFECTIVE `{ turnDeg, climbDeg, pitchFromDeg, pitchToDeg, roadLengthM }`:
- **Turn and climb change ONLY through the ends' tangents.** A hill or swerve that fades to zero slope inside the piece moves
  the road in between, and changes neither. One still rising at an end tilts that end: on level road by atan(h′), exactly.
- **Bank is never changed** (the lift keeps the roll).
- **roadLengthM** is the length over the lifted road, ∫|r̃′| ds. That is a little more than `lengthM`, which stays the base
  length along s.

## 2. The maths: the shelf first
**ref 09 §8 (new) says:**
- the integrals are exact by Gauss–Legendre, SOURCED WIKI-GAUSS (Wikipedia, "Gaussian quadrature", opened: "an exact result
  for polynomials of degree 2n − 1 or less", with the 3-point nodes, weights and change of interval);
- the known answers the tests use, DERIVED: κ·L, and ∫₀¹(3u² − 2u³) = ½, giving T·L/2;
- the effective ends with h/l (from ref 09 §7);
- |r̃′|² = 1 + h′² + l′² + (lθ′)² + 2h′ sin p − 2lθ′ cos p. DERIVED from T·R = 0, T·R_θ = −cos p, and ŷ·R = ŷ·R_θ = R·R_θ = 0;
  integrated by composite 5-point GL over 1 m panels, NOT exact, and measured by the test against chords.

**Not used:** a closed-form B-spline integral. I looked for one (Wikipedia "B-spline" has none; the one formula found was in a
paper I did not open), and used the Gauss–Legendre route instead, whose source I opened.
