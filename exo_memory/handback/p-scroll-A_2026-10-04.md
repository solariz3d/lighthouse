## Cause, measured (not inferred)
I served the worktree over http and opened `app/index.html?mode=core` in a **real Chrome (Chromium 154), headed and off-screen, at the keeper's 1818 px over CDP** (the page loads its modules by relative `fetch`, so it runs without the native side): `scratch/sc_probe.js`, under the heavy-run lock (it waited behind E's profiling jobs). **Before the fix it reproduces the screenshot exactly** (the same 15 px classic bar, `#left` 320 px wide, `clientWidth` 304): every Extend field (length, turn, climb, bank, cup, width, the `width like…` select, edge angle, edge start, tube sweep) **ends at x = 359 against the section's content edge at 294: 65 px past it, under the bar and beyond the column, and the column scrolls sideways (`scrollWidth` 359 for a `clientWidth` of 304)**. The brush and water fields, which have no "at start" box, end at 294. Cause: the shared `.picker` grid is `60px 1fr`; a `1fr` track will not shrink below its items' min-content, so a number input's or a select's own width pushed the row out. (The plan's guess of a missing right padding was not it: `scrollbar-gutter: stable` already reserves the bar, `#left`'s `clientWidth` excludes it.)

## The fix (`app/index.html`, CSS only, plus one string)
- `.picker { grid-template-columns: 60px minmax(0, 1fr) }`, and `.picker input:not([type=checkbox]):not([type=radio]), .picker select { min-width: 0; max-width: 100%; box-sizing: border-box }`: the field column can shrink to the column and every field is held to its track.
- **The tube placeholder** of the width select was clipped on its own: `width like… (a tube: metres round)` measured **204 px against 190 px of room**; it is now `width like… (m round)` (130 px) (`app/core/widthlike.js`; the note line under the controls still says what w means). The plain placeholder is 68 px.
