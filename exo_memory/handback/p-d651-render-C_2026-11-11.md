## 1. Step 1: the hand light out of the body. The falsifier did NOT fire
**What:** `Instance::ray_mask` and `Light::shadow_mask` appended to `core/scene.h` (defaults 0xFF: nothing changes unless a scene says so); the renderer uses them as the instance mask and the shadow rays' mask (`LightHidden` → `Occluded(..., L.shadowMask)`). `game/body_figure.h` `kBodyRayGroup = 0x02`; the body's parts are in it; both hand lights (`beam.cpp`) leave it out. The body is still seen, still lit by the hand light, and still shadows the lamps.

**Why this way and not moving the light:** anywhere the light sits, the swinging arm passes within 0.30 m of it; a hand light held by that arm cannot be "clear" of it. Moving it would also change the shadows "the eye can see" it was placed for (`beam.cpp:31`).

## 2. Step 2: per-instance motion vectors
**What:** each frame the renderer keeps every instance's transform as drawn and uploads, per instance, the transform from where a point is now to where it was (last frame's times the inverse of this one's), at t12. A transform that did not change is marked still and reprojects by the camera exactly as before. `TraceCS` looks a moving surface's history up where it was and writes each pixel's motion (a new target, u2/t8). `TaaCS` takes its history from the nearest surface's own motion where that surface moved. A surface moving on its own keeps at most 8 frames of lighting (`MOVING_FRAMES`). `Settings::motionVectors` / `WorldStage::motion_vectors` switch it off, only to measure.

**Measured against the settled picture of the same moment** (same state, held, history reset, 72 frames). This measure was added because frame-to-frame change cannot see it while sprinting.
