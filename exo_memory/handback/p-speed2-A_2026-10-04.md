## What was built, in the chair's order
1. **A coarse grid for cup and plain segments while dragging** (preview). `app/preview/coarse.js`: a segment with no `fractions` gets a grid from `blendSamples` with both limits scaled by the factor (6 m across, 6° turning; full is 1 m and 1°), memoised per (from, profile) object pair, so a held cup shares one grid (no seam zipper) and the 4,000-step search runs once. A held cross-section (`blend: null`) becomes "weight 1 all along" so the mesh follows the grid. Cup oval mesh 347 → 80 ms on its own. A legacy bowl's segments each carry their own profile object, so nothing is shared there (the grid still thins its vertices).
2. **A closed loop rebuilt from the pieces that changed.** `src/geom/mesh.js` `reuseMesh(prev, path, segments, same)` (new; `buildMesh`/`extendMesh`/`sculptMesh` untouched): a piece the caller says is the same, starting at the same pitch and bank, keeps its vertex arrays and only moves; every other piece and every seam that touches one is remeshed; the loop's own joint is always remade; `prev` is not changed (moved pieces are copies). `app/preview/trackmodel.js` keeps the last closed mesh **per detail** (`slots`), with the hashed segment keys and lift keys, so a brush that reaches 60 m of a 6 km loop meshes 60 m while dragging, and full detail comes back from the pre-drag full mesh. **The required equality, proved:** the model's result equals a fresh full build on the cup oval, the tube oval, a closed cup lap, a closed tube lap and a closed plain lap, at every coarse drag step, the release and an undo, for a bank brush and a width brush, a brush across the loop's start/end joint, one at the end of the lap, a hill (a lifted path) and a heading brush that re-closes the whole loop. Standard of `app/test/preview.test.js sameBatches` (indices exact, matrices ≤1e-9, positions ≤1e-5): the largest difference I saw is **2.2e-13 m** (the re-closing brush); zero everywhere else (`sp2/eq_probe.js` on the keeper's two tracks; rows 8, 8b, 9–9e in the committed test on generated laps). The keeper's tracks are not in the repo, so the committed test uses closed laps `extend`+`close` makes.
