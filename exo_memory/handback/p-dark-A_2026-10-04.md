## What it does (the amended plan, items 1 to 3)
1. **An underside skin under EVERY core road cell** (`src/export/underskin.js`, one call in `src/export/fromwords.js` after `withTextureSet`): a second surface `UNDERSKIN_OFFSET_M` = **0.5 m** along the road's own normal on the side away from the driver (below an open road; outward of a closed tube, whose normal faces in, where it is the pipe's outer wall: ONE mechanism), normals facing away from the road, `castShadows: true`, its own plain dark material (`t180b_underskin`, ksAmbient 0.05, ksDiffuse 0.1, no specular). Seam rows are skinned too (all their vertices, the road's own triangles reversed).
2. **The dark interior only on closed-ring cells** (the same closed test as `src/texture/flow.js`, applied to `seg.profile` and `seg.blend.from`): their road material is swapped for a copy named `<material>_dark` with `TUBE_RING_AMBIENT` = **0.02** (0.45 elsewhere), everything else as it was (ksDiffuse 0.55); the textured set's shared floor material is not edited (the dark copy is a new material with the same sampler). A seam row is a ring only when BOTH its segments are (the lap's first seam joins the LAST piece to the first). Open roads, cups, edge curves and open tubes keep 0.45.
3. **The slot zone** (a tube closing or opening): the skin runs under it like everywhere (it is under every cell); the dark material only on the closed cells. **The transition looks like:** the interior steps from the road's light to dark over ONE 2 m cell at each end of a closed run (no ramp), and the pipe's skin is continuous across it. A real transition test is the synthetic ring-rule unit (row 7) and the closing-track test (row 3).
- **The skin is light:** it keeps about `SKIN_COLUMNS` = 32 vertices across (the road's rows carry 63 on a plain 31 m road and 392 round a 40 m tube; chord sagitta at 32 columns round a 6.4 m ring is 3.1 cm against the 0.5 m offset). My first version copied every column and **doubled the tube oval's kn5 (30.1 MB to 60.3 MB)**; decimated, below.
- **Not a physics surface:** meshes are named `UNDERSKIN_…` (dummies `SKIN_…` / `JOIN_…`): no `1ROAD` prefix, no leading digit, never "seam" (`acready.js weldSeams` would take it for a zipper). `surfaces.ini`'s `MESHES=1ROAD?` and AC's key-named surfaces cannot match. **inferred (the repo's naming rule, not a run in AC):** AC reads a mesh as a physics surface by a `1<KEY>` name; a test asserts no skin name starts with a digit, contains 1ROAD or seam, or matches any `MESHES=` pattern in the written `surfaces.ini`, and that the export has the same number of `1ROAD` meshes as the road alone.
- **Word documents are untouched** (gated to segments whose word is `core`): `withUnderskin` returns the very same scene object.
