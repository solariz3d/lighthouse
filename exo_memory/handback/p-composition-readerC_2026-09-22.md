## 4 · A DEFECT IN MY OWN UNIT, found by executing the rule and reported rather than fixed

**Item 5 is 14,048 characters against a median of about 1,200.** The librarian's dated entry is not a `##` section of
a hand-back, so my unit rule could not reach it the same way, and I took the whole entry. **A longer text has more
surface on which a doubt and a resolution can co-occur, and this one is a labelled instance** — so my selection
mildly favours a positive exactly where a positive is expected.

I did not re-cut it, because the rule was fixed before I looked and re-cutting an item after seeing its size is how
a selection rule becomes a preference. **It is a real flaw in the pass and it belongs in the result**: if the two
readers agree on item 5, the agreement is worth less than the others.
