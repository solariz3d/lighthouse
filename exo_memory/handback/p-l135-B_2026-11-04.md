## THE CONTRACT, AS BUILT (`src/core/extend.js`)

**The call:** `extend(doc, { …, second: { phi: { from, target } } })`.
- **The first bank is laid by the existing code, unchanged.** When the first bank eases over the whole piece, its ramp is laid over `[0, from]`. That is done by setting phi's ramp length (`Lof`) to `from`, so the same fitted quintic and the same rounded-path knots apply.
- **`secondRamp`** then lays the second ramp:
  - it inserts 8 knots across `[from, length]`, plus the second ramp's own turning points for a rounded or swell shape (`D.insertKnots`, so every other channel keeps its curve);
  - it refits the bank to *first ramp, hold, second ramp*, through `fitWithHold`'s new `keep` set: every control point whose basis support ends at or before `from` is held as laid, and the last two are set to the target;
  - `endC2` sets the end curvature to exactly 0 through `P[n−3]`, by `continuity.js`'s closed form.
- **The second ramp's shape:**
  - the quintic when the first ramp is (no `bankShape`);
  - rounded when it is rounded (the panel's Extend);
  - **the swell** when the bank is "at start" (the swell, or the two-share F shape). That is the contract's wording, "the swell when 'at start' is ticked, rounded otherwise".
  - See correction 2 for why "the same shape the first ramp used" could not be followed literally for at-start.
- **Refusals:**
  - `second` on any channel but `phi` → `BAD_TARGET` "a second change is bank only for now (phi)".
  - `from` outside the range → `BAD_SECOND`, naming the range: `[first ramp end, length)`, or `(0, length)` when the first bank eases over the whole piece.
  - A second bank with no first bank target → `BAD_SECOND`.
  - A target that is not a number → `BAD_TARGET`.
- **No `second`:** byte-identical to before (row 7).
- **Mirror and save:** no change to `src/core/piece.js` was needed.
  - checked by reading `piece.js`: `ROAD_KEYS` holds `type, length, family, knots, channels, cup, edge, tube, grip`. A piece remembers no "fields", so the second bank IS its bank channel.
  - `insert(…, { mirror })` negates `phi` whole, so both bends flip (row 8).
  - The document and the saved piece round-trip through serialize and parse unchanged (row 8, two asserts).

**One reading of the contract I took, stated:** the contract says `from` is "at or after the first ramp's end". The plan's row 6 says "from ≤ first ramp end" is refused. I followed the contract: `from` equal to the first ramp's end is ALLOWED (row 6 asserts it), and `from` before it is refused.
