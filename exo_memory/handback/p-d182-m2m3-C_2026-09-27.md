### M2, the geodesic share
**The falsifier, verbatim (§8):** "from the T-180 replays (blackbox's parser), split the car's path curvature into geodesic
and normal against the road surface. Prediction: the share tracks load (small on Centrifuge's high-g corners, larger on
Thunderhead). It fails if the geodesic share does not fall as load rises."

**The method:**
- **Replays** (FINDINGS §3b/§6, the files `tools/loads.cjs` already reads):
  - Centrifuge 11-08 and 16-08;
  - Thunderhead night-optimized and no-dogbowls 22-08;
  - the two Sakura laps of §6.
  - A replay blackbox cannot parse is reported by name and left out.
- **Parser:** blackbox `ui/acreplay.js` `extractCar(rep, 0)` (BLACKBOX=C:\Users\nname\Desktop\blackbox), exactly as
  `loads.cjs`: positions smoothed over ±4 frames; velocity and acceleration by central differences.
- **The surface normal N:** the parser's `nrm`, the plane of the four wheel contacts, normalised. That is the road where
  the car is, not the centreline's normal.
- **The path curvature vector:** κ = a⊥ / v², where a⊥ is the acceleration minus its component along the velocity.
- **The split:**
  - normal curvature κn = κ·N;
  - geodesic curvature κg = |κ − κn N|;
  - **geodesic share g = κg / |κ|**, in [0, 1].
- **Load:** `loads.cjs`'s own, the specific force along N in g, on the same frame.
- **Frames kept:**
  - wheels OK, and not a teleport (`loads.cjs`'s two filters);
  - speed ≥ 30 m/s;
  - |κ| ≥ 1/3000 m⁻¹, since the share is undefined on a straight.
- **Load bins (g):** [0, 3), [3, 6), [6, 10), [10, 20), [20, 40), ≥ 40.
- **THE SCORED STATISTIC:**
  - Spearman ρ between load and g over ALL kept frames of the three tracks, pooled.
  - Its 95% CI by moving-block bootstrap: blocks of 250 frames, 1,000 resamples, seed 1.
- **PASS iff both:**
  1. the CI lies entirely below 0;
  2. the per-bin median g, over the bins holding ≥ 300 frames, never rises by more than 0.02 from one bin to the next.
- **FAILS otherwise.** Reported beside it, not scored:
  - ρ per track;
  - the median g on Centrifuge's frames ≥ 20 g against Thunderhead's frames < 10 g (the prediction's own example).
- **Named in advance as the likely confound:** the pooled ρ mixes tracks, so Centrifuge's high loads and its shape come
  together. The per-track ρ is reported so a pooled pass that no single track shows is visible.

### M3, K on the meshes
**The prediction** (design §4: "K < 0 (hyperbolic, saddle): a half-pipe or bowl sweeping through a turn … Parts of T-180
tracks are locally hyperbolic surfaces"): the K < 0 area concentrates in the corners.

**The method:**
- **Meshes:** Sakura's and Centrifuge's road meshes, through `tools/kn5.cjs`, chosen by `read_track.cjs`'s rule (surfaces.ini
  valid keys; PIT and a minority "underside" skipped).
- **Welding:** vertices are welded by position on a 1 mm grid, since AC splits meshes per material and cell.
- **Interior vertices only:** every incident edge is shared by exactly two triangles, and the fan closes. Boundary and seam
  vertices are counted and left out.
- **Per interior vertex:**
  - the angle defect δ = 2π − Σ incident angles;
  - its area A = ⅓ Σ incident triangle areas;
  - K = δ / A.
- **Noise floor:** |δ| < 1e-4 rad counts as flat (K = 0), in neither sign.
- **The K < 0 area share** = Σ A over K < 0, divided by Σ A over all interior vertices, per track.
- **Against the corners:**
  - Each vertex is assigned to the nearest station of `reads/<track>.read.json` (4 m apart) within 60 m.
  - A station's K < 0 area share is taken over its vertices, with |k| = its curvature.
- **THE SCORED STATISTIC:**
  - Spearman ρ between |k| and the station's K < 0 share, over stations holding ≥ 20 interior vertices.
  - Its 95% CI by moving-block bootstrap: blocks of 25 stations, 1,000 resamples, seed 1.
- **PASS iff both:**
  1. the CI lies above 0 on BOTH tracks;
  2. the K < 0 share on corner stations (|k| ≥ 1/500 m⁻¹) exceeds that on straights (|k| < 1/1500) on both.
- **FAILS otherwise.** Reported beside it: the K > 0 share by the same split, and the whole-track K < 0 share.
- **My own expectation, INFERRED and stated so it cannot be claimed after:** a dished road turning about a vertical axis is
  locally the inside of a torus tube. So in a corner, the half of the bowl nearer the turn's centre is K < 0 and the outer
  half K > 0.
  - That predicts BOTH signs rising in corners, not K < 0 alone.
  - The scored statistic above tests the design's claim, not mine.

### M2: PASS
- **Pooled over 46,768 frames:** ρ = −0.50, CI [−0.563, −0.433].
- **Per-bin medians:** 0.925 / 0.795 / 0.734 / 0.604 / 0.456 / 0.029. They fall in every bin, so the largest rise between
  bins is 0.
- **Per replay:** ρ = −0.56 and −0.44 (Centrifuge 11-08, 16-08); −0.41 and −0.47 (Thunderhead); −0.44 (Sakura 27-03). The
  pooled pass is in every track alone.
- **Sakura 28-03 was NOT read** (blackbox: an online multi-car autosave, not supported). It is left out by name, as
  registered.
- **The limit, found after the run and said plainly:** load = the specific force along N, and its curving part is v²κn. So
  a falling share with rising load is partly the same fact twice.
  - The registration did not name this coupling. **It weakens the PASS.**
  - The share falls on every track and at every load step, but load is not an independent axis for it.
  - A clean version bins by speed, or by the surface's own normal curvature. It is not run, and not registered here.
