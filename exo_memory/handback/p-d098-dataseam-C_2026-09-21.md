## 0 · THE ANSWER

1. **The `ready-*` hooks do NOT need the data dir. My own L066 §2.2 guess was wrong.** They use `SHELL_DIR` only to
   `require` `lib/ready.js`. Where the stamp goes is `CONSONANCE_READY_DIR` (`dev/shell/lib/ready.js`, `stamp()`), which
   the app sets on every pane spawn (`main.rs:1151`). The manifest's `ready/*.json` in the data dir comes from that
   variable, not from this one.
2. **The seam was wrong, and it cost more than L066 saw.** Under the leaked env, all eight hooks resolved their
   **dependencies** in the data dir, not just their ledgers: `lib/ready.js`, `lib/ambient.js` and
   `hooks/l*-overseer-worker.js` do not exist there. **Measured on D:** 210 L2 and 297 L3 jobs sit in
   `C:\Consonance\data\l{2,3}-jobs`, queued 09-14 14:42 .. 09-20 23:31 local, and none was ever drained, because
   `data\hooks\` does not exist. The ready stamps failed the same way, silently, into an empty `catch`. Nothing reported
   any of it.
3. **Fixed** by giving the eight their own variable, **`CONSONANCE_SHELL_DIR`** (0 prior uses in the repo), and not
   reading `CONSONANCE_DATA` at all. Test first: **4 red → 4 green**. Mutants **6/6**. Two harnesses that isolated these
   hooks through the old name now set the new one. One of them, `dream-gate.test`, passes **either way** and silently
   writes the real shell dir without the key; the null in §4 shows that.
4. **Installed, and therefore NOT live until an `-Only` install: all eight hook files.** Each installed copy on D equals
   HEAD (§5). No test file is installed.
5. **For chunk 5 (re-measuring L3):** on D, 09-14 14:42 .. 09-20 23:31 is a **mixture**. Sessions carrying the leaked
   variable had no L3 or L2 verdicts (the jobs are stranded), while other sessions kept writing the real ledgers every
   day. An L3 measurement over that window measures a partly dead instrument.
