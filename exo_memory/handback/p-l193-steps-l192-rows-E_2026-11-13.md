## 4. Why row 3 stays red: the limiter

- Matching the synthesised step's 60 ms loudness needs the takes at +9.3 dB, a gain of 2.92.
- At that gain a sprinting step peaks at about 1.55 before the limiter: 0.90 × 0.46 × 2.92, times 1.19 for +1.5 dB of
  variation and 1.12 for pan. The limiter starts at 0.8.
- The limiter runs before the master level, so the player's volume setting does not help. Every step would duck the
  whole mix, the hunter with it.
- So the gain is 1.4, just under the bound 0.8 / (0.90 × 0.46 × 1.19 × 1.12) = 1.45. The step's loudest sample is then
  0.558, against 0.476 for the synthesised step.
- **What it leaves for his ear:** the recorded steps are peakier and 6.5 dB lower by RMS. That may read as quieter, or
  as cleaner. The bar row says the hunter is heard over them better than before: 11 of 14 against 6.
