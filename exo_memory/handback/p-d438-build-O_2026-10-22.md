## 1. What changed in finish.js (§4 steps 1–4 of the measure hand-back, and one more)

1. **The roll's acceleration** (`bankPlan`, BANK_ACCEL = 2e-4 rad/m²). Besides the rate limit, the change of the bank control polygon's slope between
   neighbouring spans is bounded: |Δslope| ≤ BANK_ACCEL·(d₀ + d₁)/2. It works by alternating projections of the free points onto each bound (at most 400
   sweeps), and the pins are never moved. Why 2e-4: at a C1 joint the outer-wheel step is about 8.4 m · 2A, so the bar of 21.7 °/100 m (0.00379 /m) allows
   A ≤ 2.26e-4.
2. **The total bank** (`bankPlan`): the rate and acceleration bounds act on b = laid + added. A span the route already rolls faster than the rate cap keeps its
   own rate as its bound. **Measured to change nothing** on TEST TRACK 10, TEST 10 or row 5 (§4, J3 and J10).
3. **The cup comes home over the closer** (`cupHome`, applied to the laid route before `closeBanked`, and to what `metAsLaid` receives).
   - The cup is moved by Δ·smootherstep(σ) along the window, with σ set by each control point's Greville position over the window's length, and Δ = p1's
     first cup less the laid end's.
   - The first piece's two joint points are kept, and every internal joint keeps the slope rule.
   - The peak rate is 1.875·Δ/length. It applies only when every road piece of the window, and p1, are cup pieces.
4. **Kept apart at the seam:** with (3) the seam's cup row has nothing left to do (TEST TRACK 10's end cup is 10.000 → p1 10.000), so the bank's return no
   longer coincides with a cup drop. Seam step: 75.6 → 9.5.
5. **NOT in the plan, and needed: the roll straight is laid long enough for (1).** On TEST TRACK 10, (1) alone could not fit 54° of roll into 87 m. From rest
   to rest, a roll θ needs at least 2·√(θ/A) ≈ 137 m. The bank's backstop refused all three tries, and the closer fell back to its UNBANKED turns (p42–p44 all
   at −180°; p41|p42 still 33.7). So `finishToStart` now lays the roll straight at least ROLL_ACCEL_FIT·2·√(θ/A), with ROLL_ACCEL_FIT = 1.5. Measured on
   TEST TRACK 10:
   - 1.5 keeps the word, with every joint ≤ 14.5;
   - 2.0 shortened the straight to 154 m;
   - 2.5 turned the route into a roll-LAST LSL whose turns are not banked at all (D434's upright rule).
