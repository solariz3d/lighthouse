## 4. Why it stops here: the design input the plan does not give

The plan's test: "a closer whose curves are tighter than the design speed's **flat limit** gets a bank on those curves. A closer with **only gentle curves gets
none**." The balance bank is never zero: at 460 km/h even a 2.7 km radius wants 31° (TEST TRACK 10's p43, the 760 m "straight" that drifts −16°). A "flat limit"
needs a number the code does not have: **how much side load a flat road holds at the design speed.** Checked: the validator's speed profile is a point mass
with full thrust and no cornering cap (`validate/index.js` speedProfile); `grip` is a percent of Assetto Corsa's road friction carried to the export
(`document.js:228-233`), not a load the validator uses; no speed-to-bank or grip-to-load rule exists in src/core or app/core (the plan's own grep agrees).

**What is owed, and by whom:** one of these, the keeper's or the librarian's:
1. **A flat limit:** a side load (in g) a flat road holds at the design speed, so the bank is only what is needed beyond it (tan θ from v²κ and that load); or
2. **A threshold radius** below which the closer banks and above which it stays flat; or
3. **No threshold:** every curve the closer makes is banked to its share, gentle ones a little (then the plan's "gets none" row is dropped);
- and **the size:** the shots show 0.75 and 1.0 × balance; the keeper's eye decides (the plan's Part 2).

With that, the build is small and in one place: finish.js, after `lay` and before the windowed close (Finish is the only part that makes new curves; the seam
tries and the BEND bend the user's own road and get no bank). Red first on TEST TRACK 10 and TEST 10, the same holds as D431.
