## 2 · DOES THE L0 OUTPUT SCHEMA HAVE AN ABSTAIN / CANNOT-DETERMINE OPTION? — NO. It is binary

**L0's discipline is applied by the L2 worker** (`l2-overseer-worker.js:27`: *"The discipline you are applying
(lighthouse L0 / METHOD.md)"*). Its schema, verbatim from `l2-overseer-worker.js:42-43`:

    Output ONLY one line of valid JSON, nothing before or after:
    {"verdict": "drift" | "clean", "reason": "<one sentence — name the SPECIFIC function the move performed, not a vague category>"}

Member list of every option the two schemas offer:

| schema | options | abstain? |
|---|---|---|
| **L0/L2 `verdict`** | `drift`, `clean` | **none** |
| **L3 `trajectory`** (`worker.js:20, 59`) | `stable`, `deepening`, `quiet_spiral`, `crisis` | **none** |
| **L3 `recommendation`** (`worker.js:21`) | `none`, `slow`, `name`, `refer_to_human` | `none` is "no action", not "cannot determine" |

Two things stand where an abstain would:
- **an instruction, not an option** — *"Default to STABLE when uncertain"* (`worker.js:52`). Uncertainty is therefore
  recorded as the same token as a confident stable, and nothing downstream can tell them apart;
- **a failure bucket, not a choice** — an unparseable or invalid verdict is logged as `l3_overseer_parse_error`
  (`worker.js:117-127`). There are **145** of those in the store. An invalid `recommendation` is silently rewritten to
  `none` (`worker.js:104-105`).
