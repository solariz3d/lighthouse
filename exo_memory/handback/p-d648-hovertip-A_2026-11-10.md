## The fix (hovertip.js only)

- `takeAll(el)`: takes the held element's title, then the title of every ancestor with a NON-EMPTY title. An empty title draws nothing and is left exactly as it is, so it comes back as it was.
- `giveAll()` puts back everything taken, on leave.
- The card still reads `held`'s words.
- The MutationObserver now observes every element taken. A title set on any of them while hovered is moved aside again; the card, if shown, reads the held element's words again.
- `release()` disconnects the observer before giving the titles back. The titles put back are not titles "set while hovered". The real observer would have dropped those records on the next disconnect anyway; the synchronous fake would not have. Without this, the fake re-took them in `over()`'s release.
