## 2. The repair (`app/core/panel.js`, `app/core/coreshell.js`)

- **The shell** forwards `end` to `extend.js` `secondMinM`.
- **`firstHold(minAt, s, L)`** is new. It starts at the pre-O1 mark: s plus the shortest at the piece's end. It walks up by 5 m to the
  first end p with `p − s >= minAt(p)`, then steps back by the metre while that still holds. Nothing within 400 m gives Infinity. It
  mirrors `extend.js` `routeMin`'s search, over the end instead of the span.
- **`changeFroms`** (the blank marks):
  - Each change's shortest is asked at its end, `E(j)`, chained back from the piece's end. `E(j)` is the next typed mark, or where the
    later changes, each at its shortest there, leave room.
  - The room for a blank mark is `min(L − the later shortests, E(k) − m(k, E(k)))`. The second term is new: a blank mark never lands
    where its own change cannot fit before a typed next mark. The old sum ignored that, so with only the first term 14 typed-second
    cases of the sweep were refused where ceb21f5's marks lay.
  - A later blank mark is `firstHold` from the mark before. Where that is past the room, the room's own mark is used, if the change holds
    up to it.
  - A list with every mark typed returns at once, as before.
- **`secSegs`**:
  - each segment's shortest is asked at its own end (`minAt(k, end)`);
  - `reach(k)` is the first place the next mark may stand (`firstHold` from mark k);
  - `secRange`'s low end is `reach(k − 1)`. The flowing bank's `reach` is its mark: unchanged.
- **Unchanged:** a minimum with no end (the corners, the bank's floored minimum) gives the marks it did before. The floored wrapper
  passes `end` through.
