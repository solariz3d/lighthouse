## 1. What the plan's "one row" turned out to need

The Third Place had **no MCP mount at all**. `spawn_claude_pane` withheld it on purpose ("the whole of its Leg 1 safety… a seat with no channel cannot steer"). So a row alone would have reached nothing.

The obvious fix was wrong, too. The Third Place holds a letter in the registry (D423), and its letter's mount resolves to the `committee` seat. That seat has a pane's verbs: `read_board` (the room reaching in), `post_board`, `raise_pull` (a hand raised at a pane). **So the return leg is its own mount, carrying one verb.**

| where | what |
|---|---|
| `mcp.rs` `THIRD_PLACE_MOUNT` | `"third-place"`: a mount identity that is not a letter |
| `mcp.rs` `seat()` | that mount resolves to the seat `third_place`, never `committee` |
| `mcp.rs` `new()` | the Third Place's server keeps `call_librarian` and removes every other route (rmcp 1.8 `ToolRouter::remove_route`) |
| `mcp.rs` `get_info` | its instructions name that one verb: "a POINTER to that file … a way out, not a way in"; none of the committee's control plane |
| `mcp.rs` `auth_station` | exempts the `third_place` seat for `call_librarian` only (§3) |
| `mcp.rs` `serve` | writes `mcp.consonance.third-place.json` and mounts `/mcp/third-place` |
| `main.rs` `ADDRESS_TABLE` | row 3: `third_place → call_librarian → LIBRARIAN_SID`. **No row into the Third Place, from any seat** |
| `main.rs` `spawn_claude_pane` | the Third Place is launched with ITS mount (it was mute); every other pane as before |
| `main.rs` dispatch + `third_place_call_librarian_exec` | its ring is labelled `[third-place]` by the system. The destination comes from the table. Delivery goes through `gate_or_queue`, as the panes' does. The audit row carries **no preview of its words**: its record stays off the shared board, as its keep-warm rows already do |
| `main.rs` `pull_role_allowed` | `deliver_pull`'s inline role rule, pulled out unchanged so a test can hold it: an approved pull never enters the Third Place |
| `main.rs` the shelf | a `third_place` tier right after the librarian's own notes. It carries the newest dated entry (`YYYY-MM-DD.md` at its top level, by the librarian's own date rule) and indexes the rest; the header names the carried entry. Before this, the walk never reached `third_place/` (the root tier isn't recursive) |

## 3. One call for the chair: the station gate

`call_librarian` is also gated by the one-station rule: it's refused unless an open lap is held by the panes. The Third Place isn't a station in the loop. ## 8. C's finding 2: no gate row a Third Place ring triggers carries its words (the chair, 2026-10-26T18:21Z; "C's review ruled")

`d476-o` **60daf38e** on 4c0baa39. **This supersedes §7's staged build.** C's review: `p-d476-review-C_2026-10-26.md` §0 and §6 (7beffb15).

**The leak C found.** The digest and trailer gates were built for hand-backs and echo the text they judge.
- On a malformed pointer, the digest gate's Refuse arm posted `refused_digest_row(&who, &text)`, up to 600 chars of the ring, to the shared board, the Third Place's seat included.
- On a good ring, the "git-blob … computed at ring for <path>" row named its path.

**The fix.** In `call_librarian`, `let third_place = self.seat() == "third_place";`, and on that seat every gate row the ring can trigger is a fixed constant with no place for its words:

| row | on the Third Place's seat |
|---|---|
| digest refusal | `THIRD_PLACE_DIGEST_REFUSED`, and no second row with the gate's reason |
| digest note on delivery (the git-blob row) | `THIRD_PLACE_DIGEST_NOTED` |
| trailer gate, delivered without a trailer | `THIRD_PLACE_TRAILER_ROW` |
| trailer gate, refused | `THIRD_PLACE_TRAILER_ROW` |

A refused ring's reply to the Third Place is the gate's own reason, followed by `THIRD_PLACE_NOT_ECHOED`: "Your words are not echoed on the shared board: a refused ring from the Third Place is recorded there as one fixed line, so nothing you wrote leaves this room by being refused. Fix the pointer … and ring again." That reply goes back only to the Third Place. The panes' rows are unchanged.
