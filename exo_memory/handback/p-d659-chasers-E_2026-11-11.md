## 0. What all five share

**Lore (from D661):**
- **T1** [INV]: they are what lives in the intervening dimensions and feeds. Their shape is borrowed from the dreamer's own
  territory, so the kid's chasers are made of the kid's world.
- **T10 and item 30** (his): it was always the evil god chasing, "playing with its food". So every chaser is one of its hands.

**The one shared tell: the seam.**
- Where a face would be, each has a **seam of the void**: the "beautifully dark and ominous void" beyond the walls (item 29,
  his), showing through.
- It is dark violet-black with a slow, low colour drift, the only part of a chaser that has any light of its own.
- On a catch (the death reveal of item 30), the seam opens across the whole body for a beat, and the player sees what was always
  behind it.
- Readable in haze (the lineup's second row). One rule for all five, so the player learns it once.

**Never seen sharp (item 78), as rules for A or C:**
- **Detail lives in silhouette and motion,** never in texture. Albedo is dark and low in contrast. No fine normal maps: normals
  are baked from large forms only.
- **No face, ever.** The seam is a soft gradient (emissive, no hard edge), not a modelled feature.
- **Geometry:** the blockout's proportions matter more than polygon count. The detail budget goes to the kid (D659 kid sheet).
- **Edges soften from the render side,** not the model: the haze and D653's dream dial (tracers on chasers via TAA history).
- **No real material reads as skin.** The surfaces are cloth, plaster, rubber, ash. Nothing wet, nothing anatomical.

**Speed context** (`SPEED_MODEL.md:10-28,139-157`; numbers are multiples of a 7 m/s reference):
- turn circles at top speed: hunter 2.54 m, interceptor 1.44 m, sprinter 6.46 m;
- the hunter starts 20-25 % slower than the player and passes them at about 5 minutes.

## 1. The hunter: "the one that is always coming" (`archetype.h:18`)

**Role:**
- speed and turning set by the difficulty dials;
- turn circle 2.54 m, so it cannot turn on a dime;
- it loses 40 % of its speed at corners;
- it gains on straights (`run_up`).

**Shape** (~2.15 m):
- **the kid's own outline, grown wrong:** a hooded figure in a zip hoodie the same cut as his, hood up and peaked, shoulders
  hunched high;
- arms long enough to swing at the knee;
- about 35 cm taller than him.
- [INV] In haze it reads as a person in a hood, which is the kid's shape coming for him. Never said what that means.

**Motion reads:**
- **upright and unhurried at a distance;**
- **on a straight it leans forward and its stride lengthens:** the run-up, made visible, so the player can *see* it gaining;
- **at a corner its whole body swings wide:** the wide circle, plus the speed loss as a stumble of the long arms. The player
  learns to juke it by watching it overshoot;
- **it never stops to look around.** It always knows (the hunter "always knows", item 65).

**Materials** (CC0):
- hood and body: Poly Haven `jogging_melange` (the kid's own hoodie fabric, deliberately), tinted near-black `#1f2024`, roughness
  high;
- no other material;
- the seam in the hood's opening.

**Never sharp:** the hood's opening is the seam. There is no face inside, only the drift. Hands are mitten-shapes inside the
sleeves, never fingers.

## 2. The sprinter: "much faster than the player on a straight; poor at corners and at height changes" (`archetype.h:19`)

**Role** (`archetype.cpp:30-50`):
- top speed 1.45× the reference, accel 6, brake 12;
- the widest turn (6.46 m circle; 90°/s at speed);
- stairs at 0.5×;
- half a second lost on a wall hit;
- sight 40 m, 160°.

**Shape** (1.35 m tall, ~2.2 m long):
- a torso pitched almost flat, carried forward on long, back-jointed legs, built for one direction;
- arms trailing behind it like a sprinter's off the blocks, held there;
- the seam at the front tip.
- [INV] In haze it reads as a long low line moving fast: a car's height, a car's length. What came too fast, never said.

**Motion reads:**
- **long, low strides with no bob:** a flat line, fast;
- **corners:** it plants and **slides**, scraping the wall with its flank (`wall_hit_s` 0.5, made visible as a skid), then
  re-launches. The player's lesson is "turn, turn again";
- **stairs:** it climbs them badly, legs scrabbling, hence its 0.5×;
- **at rest:** it does not rest. Seen still only at a distance, quivering.

**Materials** (CC0):
- body: an ambientCG **plaster** (pick a rough, unpainted one at fetch), tinted ash-grey `#5a5651`, dry and chalky;
- under the torso: an ambientCG **concrete**, darker.

**Never sharp:** dust trails it at speed (a few particles, or just TAA tracers), so it is always a little smeared when it matters.

## 3. The interceptor: "heads for where the player is going" (`archetype.h:20`)

**Role** (`archetype.cpp:52-78`):
- top 0.9×, accel 10, brake 16;
- **the tightest turn** (250°/s at speed, a 1.44 m circle);
- aims ahead (0.6-2.5 s of the player's travel);
- **commits to a place for 0.8 s**, so a feint sends it wrong;
- sight 45 m, 220°;
- it may drop.

**Shape** (~1.70 m, 1.4 m arms-out):
- broad, low centre, legs wide;
- arms held out sideways to fill a corridor;
- the head turned 90° on its neck, **always facing where you are going, not where you are**.
- [INV] The one who knew where you'd go: the school's watcher (D658 §5). Never said.

**Motion reads:**
- **the set:** before it commits it plants and **its head snaps to the place ahead.** That is the 0.8 s commit, readable as a
  pose. A feint after the set beats it;
- **side-steps and pivots in place** (the tight circle);
- **at a drop** it lets itself fall rather than climbs.

**Materials** (CC0):
- body: Poly Haven **`denim_fabric`** (one of the darker ones), tinted `#3f3b35`. Workwear, not clothing;
- arms ending in the same.

**Never sharp:** the turned head is the only "feature", and it is the seam. The player reads direction from it, never a face.

## 4. The pack: "several slower bodies: one follows, the others go for the ways ahead" (`archetype.h:21`)

**Role** (`archetype.cpp:80-104`):
- three bodies, 0.92× each;
- shared senses ("what one of them senses they all know");
- the flankers take a place ahead only if they can be there 1 s before the player;
- sight 35 m, 200°.

**Shape** (~1.25 m each, three alike):
- small, narrow, hunched;
- **identical:** the same height, the same stoop.
- [INV] Too alike to be people, too small to be adults: the mall's crowd that lost him for a minute (D658 §6). Never said.
- Proposed: **not child-shaped.** The heads are small for the bodies, the arms long. They read as figures, not children.

**Motion reads:**
- **shared senses, made visible:** when one sees the player, **all three heads turn at once**, wherever they are. The player
  learns that being seen by one is being seen by all;
- the follower trots behind;
- the flankers **drop to all fours** when they cut ahead, low and quick, and rise when they arrive.

**Materials** (CC0):
- body: an ambientCG **fabric**, rough, tinted `#4a4844` grey-brown, the same on all three;
- a slight albedo variation per body (±5 %) so they are not literally clones in light.

**Never sharp:** small and in threes, they are always partly behind each other or a corner. Their seams are the smallest of the
five.

## 5. The charger: "a slow approach, then a committed straight lunge" (`archetype.h:22`; named, not built)

**Role** (`archetype.cpp:106-118`; starting numbers, nothing reads them yet):
- approach 0.8×;
- **wind-up 0.6 s**, then a charge at 3.0× for 1.0 s;
- 1.2 s recover; **2.0 s stunned** if it hits a wall;
- walk only.

**Shape** (~1.55 m, 1.2 m wide):
- low and heavy;
- **the shoulders above the head line**, the head sunk between them;
- forearms thick, reaching almost to the floor.
- [INV] The weight that comes all at once. Never said what.

**Motion reads:**
- **the approach:** slow, swaying, side to side;
- **the wind-up** (0.6 s, the whole tell): it **drops lower and its shoulders rise**, and the seam between them brightens;
- **the charge:** a dead straight line. It cannot turn, so a side-step at the right moment beats it;
- **the wall:** it hits and **stays embedded**, shoulders in the wall, for the 2 s stun. That is the player's window.

**Materials** (CC0):
- body: an ambientCG **concrete** or **rock**, rough, tinted `#33302c`;
- the shoulders' top a slightly lighter plaster. It should look like it could go through a wall, and sometimes does not.

**Never sharp:** its seam is between the shoulders, seen best in the wind-up, which is the one moment the player must look at it.

## 6. Build notes for A or C (after the kid's pass 1)

- **Order:** hunter (always present), then sprinter, interceptor, pack (three instances of one mesh), then charger (named, not
  built in `chase/`; its model can wait for its row).
- **Pipeline:** the D659 models research, §2, steps 1-7.
  - The bases are a stretched MPFB human (hunter, interceptor, pack) or a primitive blockout (sprinter, charger), rigged to the
    universal humanoid rig.
  - Clips: Quaternius UAL (CC0), retargeted, then hand-tuned for the reads above (the hunter's lean, the interceptor's set, the
    pack's head-snap, the charger's wind-up).
  - **Rigid segments or skinned:** per the model research, chasers may start as rigid segments. Their seams hide in the haze, and
    nothing is seen close.
- **Previews:** each pass renders the five at scale in a grey fog (like the lineup's second row). If a silhouette is not
  recognisable there, it fails.
- **Seam:** one emissive material, `#2a1640` to black, with a slow noise in its UV (or world space) for the drift. It is shared by
  all five; on a catch the game raises its coverage.
- **CREDITS.md:** each texture's id as it is fetched.
