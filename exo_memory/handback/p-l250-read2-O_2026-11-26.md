**The rule.** `groundUnder` now keeps a surface when `y <= top`, where `top` is the point's own height, with no tolerance (`road.js` `groundUnder`). A
surface interpolated at the point's own height can come out one ulp above it. Then the point's own road is dropped, and a lower one, or none, is
taken.

**Measured** with the branch's own `groundUnder`: a point exactly at a flat road's height, a second road 6.8 m below it, 20,000 random spots per height.

- checked: `evidence/exact_per_height.json`, and `o_under.js`'s `exactOnFlatRoad`: 9,847 of 240,000.

(`evidence/exact_b_row.json`). Its comment, "comes back as 1.9999999999999998", is the rounding going the lucky way.
**B's hand-back line "A surface at the start's own height is taken" claims more than the code guarantees.**

**On L it cannot happen** (checked, `node evidence/o_under.js <tree> <b26db76 src> <patches> <AC tracks>` → `o_under.txt`):
