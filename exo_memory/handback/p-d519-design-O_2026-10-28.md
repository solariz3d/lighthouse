## 0. Three facts that change the proposal (checked)

1. **The pick gives no across-road offset, and can miss the edges.** `t180-pick` → `app/preview/index.js:85` → `p.pick` (`app/preview/preview.js:470`)
   → `pickAt` (`:576-585`). It returns `{ s, pos, px }`: the CENTRELINE sample nearest the pointer on screen, within 40 px.
   - There is no `u`.
   - On a 32 m road seen close, the rim is often more than 40 px from every centreline sample, so a press on the edge picks nothing.
   - Today's Sculpt `down` (`app/core/panel.js:465-470`) returns on `!hit`.
2. **Cup and edge channels exist only on pieces flagged for them, and the brush refuses the rest by name.**
   - `src/core/sculpt.js:111-112`: NOT_CUP and NOT_EDGE.
   - `src/core/document.js:31-33`: `c` only on a cup piece; `e`, `er`, `s` only on an edge piece; `t` only on a tube.
   So the channel a zone names must depend on the picked piece's type, or a grab on a flat road's "cup" is a refusal.
3. **The edge zone has its own boundary.** `s` is where an edge piece's outer zone starts, a share of the half-width, 0.5-0.95, default
   **0.64** (`document.js:34-35`, `:43` S_DEFAULT). So the edge band starts at the piece's own `s`, not at a fixed 0.85.
   - `e` is the LEFT edge angle and `er` the RIGHT (`document.js:36-38`), so the touched side picks `e` (u > 0, the left) or `er` (u < 0).
   - `w` is ONE width for both sides. A rim dragged out widens both sides, as Build's width marks do (`handles.js:50`, `sides [1, −1]`).

The proposal's shape stands: the place touched names the channel, and it is never a re-route. Its figures change as below.

## 1. The zone table

`u` is the across-road share at the hit: 0 at the centreline, ±1 at the rims, + to the left (the samples' `L`). The drag's DIRECTION decides
between a zone's two channels, the way a 3-D program's vertex drag does:
- At the first move past CLICK_PX, the pointer's motion is compared with the on-screen directions of the road's up (U) and across (L) at the hit
  (§2 gives both).
- The axis it lies closer to wins, and is locked for the drag (`axisOf`).
- A zone with one channel ignores the other axis.
- The value is read along the chosen on-screen axis, as Build's `dragPixels` reads a handle's arrow.

| zone | `|u|` | drag along U (lift) | drag along L (out / in) | Alt (any direction) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **centre** | < 0.2 | height `h` (brush-grab) | sideways `l` (brush-side) | sideways, as Grab's Alt is today |
| **cup** | 0.2 to the edge zone's start (`s` on an edge piece, else 0.9) | cup piece: `c`. Tube: `t`. Other: bank `phi` (lifting one side tilts the road) | none (U only) | bank `phi` |
| **edge** (edge piece only) | `s` to 0.9 | u > 0: `e`, u < 0: `er` (that side's edge) | edge start `s` | bank `phi` |
| **rim** | 0.9 to 1.25 (the pick's tolerance past the rim) | edge piece: `e` / `er`. Cup piece: `c`. Tube: `t`. Half-pipe with walls: wall rise `r`. Flat: bank `phi` | width `w` (both rims, as Build's) | bank `phi` |
| off the road | > 1.25, or no hit | none: the press falls back to the chosen tool on its key, as today | | |

- **Bank's sign** follows the touched side: lifting the left part of the road banks it left-up. So a left-side drag up and a right-side drag down
  give the same bank.
- **Never a re-route:** no zone names `kh`, `kv` or length. Bend, Climb and Stretch stay on their keys.
- **The Grab key becomes this zone grab.** The other keys keep their one channel, and pressing one is how to override the zone.
- **Shift is fine steps, Ctrl snaps**, as in Build's handles. Neither changes the zone.
- **Why 0.2 and 0.9:**
  - 0.2 keeps D425's cup glyph, at 0.3 (`handles.js:100` brush-cup `lat 0.3`), inside the cup zone.
  - 0.2 of a 32 m road is ±3.2 m, wider than the car.
  - 0.9 leaves the outer 1.6 m of a 32 m road as the rim, around Build's width mark at lat 1.
- **An edge piece with `s` ≥ 0.9** has no edge zone, and its rim does the edge.

## 2. The across-offset source: one new pick in the preview

The pick does not give `u` (fact 1). The preview must, because only it has the pose and the sections. I propose `pickAcross(path, segments, pose,
x, y, w, h)`, pure, in `preview.js` beside `pickAt`, answering `t180-pick` with `pickAt`'s three fields kept and four added.

1. **Candidates:** for every sample, project three points: the centreline, and `pos ± hw·L` at the two rims (`hw` from `halfWidth`, `:501`, memoised).
   The candidate's screen distance is the pointer's distance to the polyline rim–centre–rim. Keep the 4 nearest within 40 px. This catches a press
   on the rim that `pickAt` misses.
2. **Refine on those 4:** sample each one's cross-section 41 times across.
   - The points are `profile.js` `readAt(seg, sLocal)`, then `offsetAt(P, u)` → `pos + L·lat + U·up`, as `acrossLines` and `clearanceAtHead` already
     do (`:503-515`, `:618-620`).
   - Take the nearest point on screen, then refine once at the half step.
3. **Return** `{ s, pos, px, u, at, axes }`:
   - `u` is the arc share, signed;
   - `at` is the hit's world point;
   - `axes` is `{ U: [dx, dy], L: [dx, dy] }`: the on-screen pixels of 1 m along the section's local normal and its local across direction at the hit.
     L is signed outward on the touched side.
   - inferred: the cost is about 3× today's pick per pointer move (3 projections a sample against 1), plus 164 at the refine. It runs on hover and
     on the press, never in a drag step (the drag's station is fixed at the press, D425 §4). Part 2 times one hover pick on a 10 km lap.

The labels keep reading `s` and `px` as before (`labels.js:137` `sharedPick`), so the labels are unchanged.

## 3. The dots: stations, density, look

- **Rows** on the selected run (`sculptModel`, `panel.js:547`):
  - u = 0 (the centre), u = +1 and u = −1 (the rims);
  - on a cup piece also u = ±0.5, for cup.
  Each dot is the zone's grab with s0 at the dot and the current radius. A dot is a visible handle onto the same brush (`beginBrush` with that
  `s0`), not a second mechanism, as the plan says.
- **Stations:** the run's control-point stations (≤ 20 m apart, `document.js:61` KNOT_M), thinned by `dotStations`.
  1. Walk in s. Keep a station when it is at least **max(100 m, the radius)** from the last kept in s, AND at least **24 px** from it on screen
     (twice the handles' 12 px hit radius, D509's figure). Stations behind the camera are dropped.
  2. Always keep the run's first and last road stations if they are on screen.
  3. Cap: **40 dots per row on screen** (120 for three rows, 200 with the cup rows). Over the cap, take every ⌈n/40⌉-th.
  - inferred: a 5,000 m piece has about 250 control points a channel; the 100 m rule leaves 51 stations, and the on-screen rules leave fewer.
- **Look:** hollow marks, 12 px hit radius, D425's colours.
  - The centre row is white (height).
  - A rim row is red (width), with green on its inner half when that side has an edge or cup channel to lift.
  - The cup rows are green.
  - Dots are drawn only while a run is selected. They take the press only within their 12 px. A press between dots is grab-anywhere.
- **The cost rule:** like D425 §4, the dots are placed once per pose and run (`handles.js` frameKey), never per drag step. During a drag they are hidden,
  and the drag's own glyph shows.

## 4. The hover cue and the value line, per zone

D425's five cues stand: the reach band, the glyph, Alt, what moves, the value. What changes is that the GLYPH and the WORDS come from the zone under
the pointer, not from the key.

| zone | glyph on hover (D425 kinds) | hover box, line 1 / line 2 | strip value line during the drag |
|---|---|---|---|
| centre | brush-grab at `u`, and a small ↔ for sideways | "height · ↕ lift / sink · ↔ sideways" / "the road after it stays" | "height +1.20 m at the centre · radius 60 m" |
| cup | brush-cup pair (cup piece), brush-twist (other) | "cup · ↕ edges up / flatter" / "the road itself does not move" | "cup +5.00° at the centre" |
| edge | brush-edge on the touched side (hollow, `sides [1]` or `[-1]`), and a small ↔ for edge start | "left edge angle · ↕ more / less · ↔ edge start" / same line 2 | "left edge angle +5.00° …" or "edge start +0.05 …" |
| rim | brush-width pair, plus the side's lift mark | "width ↔ wider / narrower · ↕ <the side's lift channel>" / same line 2 | the locked channel's line |
| Alt held off-centre | brush-twist | "bank · ↕ this side up / down" | "bank +12.00° …" |

- Once the drag locks its axis, the glyph and the strip name only the chosen channel, in the strip's `CHANNEL_NAMES` words (D425 §5: one name per
  channel).
- A zone whose channel the brush would refuse shows the refusal's reason on hover in the hover box's second line, e.g. "no cup on this piece: extend
  with a cup target first". The press then does nothing; it does not fall through to another channel.

`axisOf(motion, axes, zone)` → 'U' or 'L':
- pure U → U; pure L → L.
- 45° → U (lift wins a tie).
- U projected under 4 px per m (the road seen edge-on) → L.
- A zone with L null → U, whatever the motion.

`dotStations(stations, run, project, { radius, minM: 100, minPx: 24, cap: 40 })`, with `project(s)` → `[x, y]` or null:
- empty run → [];
- a 5,000 m piece with 251 knots → spacing ≥ 100 m in s;
- radius 150 → spacing ≥ 150 m;
- stations projecting within 24 px collapse to one;
- stations behind the camera (null) are dropped;
- at most 40;
- the run's first and last road stations are kept when on screen;
- the same input gives the same output (stable while the pose is still).

`pickAcross` (pure, a synthetic path and pose):
- top-down on a straight flat 32 m road: pointer at the left rim → u ≈ +1 (± 0.05); centre → 0; right rim → −1;
- a press at the rim that `pickAt` returns null for → a hit;
- a cup section → u along the arc;
- beyond 40 px of the road → null;
- `s` and `px` at the centreline equal `pickAt`'s (the labels are unchanged).

`handles.js` rows:
- one per zone: the hover glyph's kind and its mark placed at the hit's `u`;
- the dots placed at the thinned stations on the three or five rows;
