## 2. The cause, and the fix (Aqua only)

**The cause.** `app/index.html` gives every look the same dialog rules:
- `.ask-main` takes `color: var(--accent)`;
- `.ask-quiet` takes `color: var(--dim)`.

Those suit a dark button. **Aqua's buttons are pale gel pills** (`aqua.css:51`, dark ink `--gel-text`). So its main button's words were light cyan on a light cyan pill, and Don't save's were pale cyan on the same pill.

**The fix** is in `app/themes/aqua.css`, three rules, in Aqua's own hues:
- **The main button** stays a gel pill, in the pill's dark ink. It is marked out by the white rim and glow a hovered pill wears.
- **The quiet button** is words on the water (`--dim` on the dark card), as Aqua's camera dock buttons are. On hover it gets the dock's tint and white words.
- **Cancel is unchanged.**
- **The other five looks are unchanged:** they already read.
