## 2. Live, or next start? Measured: live

**How the switch works** (`app/sound.js` `setMelody`):
- **While samples play:** only the new melody's place and undo files are fetched; the tick and the loop close are already decoded.
  - The old melody keeps playing until the pair is decoded. Then the engine switches. It builds no node and stops no voice: one graph, one reverb.
  - A pair decoded earlier switches at once, with no fetch.
  - If two choices are made before the first pair arrives, only the last is made.
  - If the load fails, it's logged and the old melody plays on.
- **On the synth** (samples not in yet): the switch is at once, and the new melody's files follow.

**The measurement** (`measure.js` → `measure2.out`), run under the lock:
- **Click:** the real engine with the real WAVs, rendered offline at 48 kHz in headless Chrome (`app/test/soundrender.js`).
  - Clip "switch": an Aqua place at 0.05 s; the switch to Glass at 0.50 s, while that place rings; a place at 1.00 s.
  - Clip "none": the same without the switch.
  - Up to 1.00 s, the two clips differ by at most **7.8e-7**.
  - The control (the same clip rendered twice) differs from itself by up to **9.6e-7**. So the switch adds nothing above render noise.
  - The largest step between adjacent samples within ±10 ms of the switch is 0.012523 in both clips.
  - After 1.00 s they differ by up to 0.276: that place is Glass's.
- **Gap:** fetch and decode of each melody's pair, five times each, with the cache off, from a page served off the worktree. The range is **5.6 to 24.3 ms**:
  - aqua 9.3 / 6.6 / 6.1 / 7.5 / 7.0;
  - skyline 8.4 / 24.3 / 6.9 / 7.3 / 6.3;
  - glass 7.2 / 6.8 / 7.6 / 6.7 / 13.3;
  - aurora 7.9 / 6.5 / 6.2 / 5.6 / 6.8.
  - The old melody plays through that window, so it is a delay, not silence.
- **The menu's words:** the fixed themes' tooltip says the melody "changes a moment after the look, once its files are in". There is no "next start" wording, because it is not needed.
