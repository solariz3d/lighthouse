**Why the first part is not a coincidence of these laps**, from the code at 18d97c1 (same finish.js as 28eb328): `lapReads.out` (:391–396) counts the curved stations (|kh| ≥ 1/600) leaning out past LEAN_OUT_DEG per piece, and W1 withholds a piece whose count rose (:438). Inside a corner the plan's target is `bankOf(kh, share)`, toward the inside, and an eased piece carries no added bank, so neither can produce a station leaning *out* past 5° on a level lap. The only place the bank runs against the next corner's sense is the rate-limited roll-off between opposite corners, and the first station of that roll that counts as curved is the far run's first curved sample, `b`, which is exactly the station the descent check reads (`bankAtS(cand, g[1])`, :430). The roll is monotone through the gap, so `b` is the worst curved station there; hence the 0.01° agreement, and hence a lean-out at a gap is pinned before W1 can see it.
