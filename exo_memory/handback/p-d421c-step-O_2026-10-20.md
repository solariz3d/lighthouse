## 2. The cut — `src/core/adapter.js` toSegments, d421c-o

- `LIFT = {h, l}`. Beside each cache, `LAST_BY_ID` keeps the piece last cached for each id. On an identity miss, the last piece for that id is used if (a) its
  cached inputs equal this piece's inputs under the SAME comparison as the identity path (neighbours, seam role, carried pitch/roll/cross-section, chord
  lengths, options), and (b) `sameButLift`: every field equal by value (`sameValue`), the same channel names in the same order, and every channel but h and l
  equal by value. Then its frozen segments are reused and filed under the new piece too.
- Nothing else changes: an identity hit is as before; a miss that fails (a) or (b) converts as before.
- `LAST_BY_ID` holds one piece per id per cache (a strong Map inside a WeakMap keyed by the cache): an id's last piece stays alive while the cache does.
