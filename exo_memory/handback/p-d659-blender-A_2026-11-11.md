## 3. Step 1b, the kid's base body, fitted to BodyBuild

**Base:**
- MPFB `HumanService.create_human`, with macros gender 1.0, **age 0.30** (MakeHuman's scale is 11 years at 0.1875 and 25 at 0.5,
  so 0.30 ≈ 16, E's age), muscle 0.4, weight 0.35 (E: "lean, not thin"), proportions 0.5, height 0.78, race 0.33 each.
- Then MPFB's built-in **`game_engine` skeleton** (53 bones, weights included). The macro shape keys are baked into the mesh.

**The fit:**
- Each segment is stretched **along its own bone** to BodyBuild's length: the pose bone is scaled f along itself and g across it,
  and placed at its new joint.
  - The armature is applied to the skin.
  - The new joints become the rest pose.
- So the skin stretches evenly along the segment, instead of the joints moving and the skin creasing at them.
- The factors (length f, width g):
  - torso (pelvis, spine, clavicles): 1.037 both ways, so neck to pelvis is 0.60 and the shoulders widen with it;
  - upper arm 1.094; forearm 1.021; thigh 0.948; shin 1.022;
  - **hand 0.849 both ways** (see below); foot 1.024 both ways;
  - hips set to ±0.100.
- The soles are put on the floor.

checked: `fit.py` → MEASURE before / after (the game's joints read off the fitted rig and mesh; eye = the centroid of MPFB's
`joint-l-eye` and `joint-r-eye`):

**Every length that drives a bone matches to the millimetre.** The four left over are placements, and I left them on purpose:
- **eye_to_neck and neck_back** are where the head sits on the neck. MPFB's neck joint (`neck_01` head, the base of the neck) is
  3.5 cm further behind the eye than the game's `Neck` point.
  - Moving it would give him a forward-thrust neck.
  - The plan locks the head to the eye (E's sheet §7: the Eye joint is a bone at the eye midpoint, under the head), so this
    becomes a fixed offset in step 2's `joint_map.json`, not a reshaping.
  - E's posture note (head about 4 cm forward of the shoulders) points the same way.
- **shoulder_drop:** MPFB's shoulder joint is 1 cm higher under the neck. It is absorbed by the clavicle in the map.
- **ankle_height:** the ankle bone sits 6.6 mm higher above the sole than BodyBuild's 0.07. That is in the foot's own geometry.

**FINDING for whoever drives the rig: BodyBuild's lengths do not stand straight under a 1.65 m eye.** This answers E's §7
"reconcile".
- From the code:
  - `body.cpp:410` puts the pelvis 0.60 below the neck, and `:229` puts the neck 0.13 below the eye. So the hips are at
    1.65 − 0.73 = **0.92 m**.
  - At rest the foot target is directly under the hip, `±0.10` (`:265`), with the ankle `ankle_height` above the ground
    (`:421`, `:457`).
  - So hip to ankle is **0.85 m**, against thigh + shin = **0.91 m**.
- inferred (law of cosines on those numbers, not a run of `Body::pose`): the game's body stands with each knee **bent about
  42°** (interior angle 138.2°).
- The legs straighten only with the eye at **1.71 m**.
- In first person nobody sees it. On the pick screen, waking in bed or the out-of-body camera, the model will stand in a half
  squat.
- Two ways out, neither mine to take:
  - (a) shorten thigh and shin to sum to 0.85 (e.g. 0.43 + 0.42); the model refits in one run of `fit.py`;
  - (b) raise the eye to 1.71, which moves the camera, so it is his call (E §1 noted the camera must not move without him).

**FINDING: BodyBuild's hand is short for this body, the opposite of E's "hands slightly large".**
- MPFB's hand for this figure is 0.200 m, wrist to fingertip. BodyBuild's 0.17 needed a 0.849 uniform scale.
- In the renders the hands look in proportion, but small for a 1.84 m boy.
- `hand` sets how far the game's `Hand` joint sits past the wrist (`body.cpp:480`). Changing it to ~0.20 is a one-number change
  for the pose's owner, then one `fit.py` run.
