## 3. What the figures say, for the ruling (not a cut)

- Widening by width (+1 … +8) buys nothing on this lap: every one refused, ~80–110 ms each.
- The cheapest closing window measured is **brushed + the piece before the seam**: 5 pieces changed instead of 47, the gap 0.10 mm; its close took 631 ms
  here, against 380–620 ms for the whole lap (times unlocked, so not decisive between them).
- Adding the seam piece to the window, instead of widening, is the change these figures point at; whether to make it, and which seam piece (0, 49, or both),
  is the librarian's. Every figure above is for row 3's lap and one brush position, plus the two positions in §2.
