## 2. The measure, and why
**Chosen: the cross-slope change over the car's width.** It is the most that the road's angle across the car changes over 2.0 m, read on the side the bank raises, over that side's outer half (from half the half-width to the rim). 2.0 m is the Mach 6's collider box, `SIZE=2.0, 0.5, 3.0`, `docs/research/04_ac_physics_drivability.md` §5. **Amber past 8°.**
- **Why this one, tied to what he felt.** He said the car "clips and becomes unstable" in those turns, and not without the edges. The edge folds the road up across the car's own width: one side of the car sits on a slope 14–19° steeper than the other, and a 2 m box under load meets a road that turns up beneath it. With the edges at 0, the most this measure reads on his turns is the cup's own 4.27°.
- **Why not the roll the car sits in** (bank + cup + edge at the wheel line).
  - It cannot separate his two cases. His bank of 60° alone, edges at 0, at s 2200–2300, reads **60°**. His stacked turn at s 3850–4600 also reads **60°** (10 + 15 + 35).
  - So a bar on roll would warn on the turn he says rode fine, or stay silent on the one he says rode rough.
  - checked: `S\probe-orig.out`, `S\probe-e0.out` (probe.js, the max per 50 m on the outer side). Rows 2200 and 4050–4600, in both files.
- **Why not the vertical curvature.** That is D436's measure, and the C2 jump at the joints where a bank, lip or cup starts. It belongs to D459's ease, not to the stacking, and it is quiet along the steady middle of p12–p16, where the stack is the same all the way.
- **The bar of 8° is a judgment, not a measurement.** On TEST 10: edges at 0 read ≤4.27°, which he says rode fine; the stacked turns read 13.9–19.0°, rough. 8° sits between the two. His next drive is what would move it.
  - checked: `S\probe-orig.out` (curl2m 13.88–19.02 over s 2200–4600); `S\probe-e0.out` (≤4.27).
- **It warns only where all three are present at the station.** Each part is read from geometry the validator already has:
  - **bank:** the bank raises that side by 1° or more (sample `L[1]`, the sine of the bank).
  - **cup:** the profile's angle at half the half-width is 1° or more. This is exact. An edge never starts nearer the centre than 0.5 of the half-width (`document.js` S_MIN), so at that point only the base cross-section speaks.
  - **edge on that side:** the rim angle is more than 3 times the angle at half the half-width. No cross-section without an edge goes past 2.83 times.
    - checked: a scan of `cupProfile` (bowl, half-pipe, flat; w 6–40; c 1–150), legacy `profileAt` (r 0.5–10) and `tubeProfile`. The largest ratio is 2.8333, from the half-pipe cup at w 6, c 5. The command is in this turn's transcript: a node -e over `src/core/adapter.js`.
    - Without this test, a deep cup (about 28° or more) on an edge piece with its edge at 0 would have warned "bank, cup and edge" with no edge there.
  - **edge font only:** tubes are left out, since the car rides inside them.
- **What it leaves out, said plainly:**
  - **Edges under about 10° on a 15° cup.** The rim test does not count them, and they change the slope under the car by less than 8° anyway (row 5: e 10 is silent).
  - **Speed.** The warning is geometric. It does not ask whether the lap's speed actually carries the car up into the edge zone; the words say "may ride rough at speed".
  - **Banked straights inside a turn complex** (s 3750–3850, p12, kh 0) warn too. The bank and the stack are there.
