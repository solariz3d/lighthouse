## 1. The four directions, one line each

1. **Grain, one texture for all five looks:** a static film grain on the header, the left panel and the menus, shown at four strengths. It's safe up to 0.06; from 0.10 it costs text contrast.
2. **Ordered dither:**
   - on the panels, a Bayer dot screen fading down from the top, at 0.08;
   - on the sky, a coarse 12-level ordered dither from 15° up;
   - below that, a one-step dither that removes the sky's 8-bit banding without being seen. That one is a fix, not a look.
3. **ASCII for the empty moments:** the empty builder (and the same treatment for the splash and the About box) shows the track in characters. The working surfaces carry no texture.
4. **One texture per look:** SIGIL film grain 0.06, Skyline a coarse "paper" grain 0.05, Glass ordered dither 0.08, Aqua soft fluid noise 0.10, Aurora a dot matrix 0.10.

**Texture only:** every look keeps its own colours. On a light surface (Skyline's panels) the specks are dark, because white on white shows nothing (see §5).

  - The fix moves each pass by −0.0055 to +0.008 ms, and the look by −0.0045 to +0.007 ms, inside the 0.019 to 0.039 ms spread between styles.
- **CSS paint:** `paint.json`. The header and left panel repaint on each of 120 frames under a DevTools trace, in Edge's software raster, one run per case.
  - The paint step itself changes by −0.03 to +0.11 ms with a texture.
  - The raster step also redraws what lies under the panels. It moves by more than that between cases, in both directions (Aurora 26.7–30.3 ms), so one run can't separate a texture's cost from noise.
- **inferred:** the textures as drawn here are one pre-rendered bitmap per surface, decoded once, so their steady cost is a bitmap composite. A build using an SVG `feTurbulence` would need its own measurement.
