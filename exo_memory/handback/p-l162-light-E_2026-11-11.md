**The change I will make:**
1. The apartment's `layout.lamps` goes from 1.0 to **1.4**. The plan's "per-biome lamp weight" is this existing field; no new one is needed. I say so rather than build a second.
2. A low-frequency **lit-region field**: value noise on a lattice of about 6 cells (18 m) across and one storey high, per seed. It multiplies the lamp chance from about **0.15 (a dark stretch) to about 2.1 (a pool)**, with a mean a little above 1.
3. A new measure, **`lit_share`**: the share of walkable cells (walk-graph nodes) whose centre has a lamp within 7.07 m on the same storey. It **ignores walls**, so it is an upper bound on what is lit. Beside it goes the per-chunk spread of `lit_share`, and the share of "dark" chunks (`lit_share` < 0.35).

### SEEDS CHANGE: a new world for lighting

Every seed lays different lamps.
- The **layout, walk graph and spawn do not move.** The field has its own salt, `kSaltLitField`, placed after every earlier salt (`gen_impl.h`).
- But a lamp is a fixture box, so **chunk geometry and fingerprints change**. Any recorded run, or any test pinned to a seed's play, sees a different world. This is the base-movement change's situation again.

### What was built

1. **The per-biome lamp weight already existed** (`layout.lamps`, 1.0 for both biomes), so the plan's "per-biome lamp weight" is a tuning, not new code.
2. **The lit-region field** (`emit.cpp` `lit_field`): value noise on a 6-cell (18 m) lattice, a fresh one per storey, eased to 0..1. Each biome reads it as a multiplier on the lamp chance, from **`layout.lamp_dark` to `layout.lamp_bright`**, two new per-biome numbers in `gen.h`.
   - The defaults are **0.15 .. 2.1**: deep dark stretches and bright pools. The hotel uses these.
   - Reprieve and light-well lamps are untouched (a tell, and always lit).
3. **The apartment**: `lamps 1.25`, `lamp_dark 0.8`, `lamp_bright 1.6`. Since `lamp_dark × lamps = 1.0`, **no apartment cell has a lower chance of a lamp than before. Pools only add.**
4. **New measures** in `Metrics` and `dz-gen --sweep` (commit `90cc67b`):
   - `lit_share`: walkable cells with a lamp within 7.07 m on their storey, walls ignored;
   - `lit_share_chunk_std`;
   - `dark_chunk_share`.
