## 0. What landed, in one place

- **The transform** (`src/core/smoothramps.js`, new, 92 lines): `smoothRamps(doc) -> { doc, ramps, moved, left }`. A road piece's phi, c, e or er counts as a ramp when it goes from one held value to another: its first two control points are level, its last two are level, and the two values differ. Each such channel is re-laid on the piece's OWN knots with extend.js's own steps: the quintic blend (`channelFn`), the fit with its start pair kept and its hold and end pinned (`fitWithHold`, now exported from extend.js: the one line changed there), and `c2Ends`. It is laid from the piece's start to where the piece reached its end value before: the knot from which every control point holds that value. A ramp over the whole piece stays over the whole piece, and a ramp typed with a transition keeps its hold. Where the fit would turn back (uneven knots, §3), the quintic is read at the knot averages instead, as `rampControl` lays a short ramp. A channel that is not a rest-to-rest ramp, or a piece with under six control points, is left as it was and listed with the reason. When nothing moves, the same document object comes back.
- **The command** (`app/core/coreshell.js smoothRamps`, `app/core/trackmenu.js`): Track ▸ **Smooth ramps**, after a separator under Road surface ▸. Its tooltip reads: "make every bank, cup and edge change on this track start and end without a jolt, as new pieces do; the route stays where it is (the track is copied to its backups first; one undo step)". It copies the track to track-backups first (`backupNow('pre-smooth')`, as Cut and the middle Delete do). A copy that fails refuses by name and changes nothing. Then it commits ONE undo step. An empty track, a track with no ramps, or a track already smooth: nothing changes and the message says so. It is never run on load. The Track menu's hover title adds "smooth ramps" (`app/index.html`).
- **The messages, word for word:** "smoothed 13 bank, cup and edge ramps on 9 pieces: they now start and end without a jolt; the route did not move (Ctrl+Z puts them back)"; "the 13 ramps on this track are already smooth: nothing changed"; "no bank, cup or edge ramps to smooth on this track: nothing changed"; "the track is empty: there are no ramps to smooth"; "not smoothed: the copy from before could not be written (…); nothing changed".
