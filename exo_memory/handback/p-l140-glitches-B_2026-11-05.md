## THE LIST

**G1 · The bank handle cannot be dragged once the bank has a value: its own offer sits on it and takes the press.**
- Repro (`drive2.txt` A2): 1,000 m, type bank 80, no turn. Press the bank handle's + side and drag down 40 px.
- Expected: the bank goes down (to about 64.7°).
- Actual: the bank stays **80**, and a NEW change appears, `phi: [{ from: 500, target: 64.7° }]`.
- From:
  - `app/core/panel.js:1181-1182`: the offer at the middle of the free stretch, which with no change is L/2 = 500, the bank handle's own station;
  - `app/core/handles.js:53`: bank `at: 0.5, lat: 1`;
  - `app/core/secondtick.js:111`: an offer within `HIT_PX` (10, `:26`) takes the press;
  - `app/core/panel.js:1150` vs `:1155`: the layer is mounted before the handles, so its capture listener runs first.
- The faint ring is drawn under the handle at 0 px, so it cannot be seen.

**G2 · The climb handle, the same.**
- Repro (`drive1.txt` F): turn and climb as rate; 1,000 m; climb 4. Press the climb handle and drag up 40 px.
- Expected: the climb grows.
- Actual: the climb stays **4**, and a new change appears, `kv: [{ from: 500, target: 0.0679°/m }]`. The offer is 0 px from the handle.
- From: `panel.js:1181-1182`, `handles.js:59` (climb `at: 0.5, lat: 0`), `secondtick.js:111`.

**G3 · With the turn set, a press on the bank handle makes a TURN change instead (a 0° corner).**
- Repro (`drive1.txt` A): 1,000 m, turn −60 (corner), bank 80. Press the bank handle's + side and drag up 40 px.
- Expected: the bank goes up.
- Actual: the bank stays **80**, and `turnDeg: [{ from: 500, deg: 0 }]` appears.
- From:
  - the turn's offer stands 10.0 px outside the same edge at the same station (`secondtick.js:30`, turn `nudgePx: 10`; `:26` `HIT_PX = 10`, a `<=` test at `:111`);
  - it is found first because the offers are listed in `SECOND_FIELDS` order, turn before bank (`panel.js:62`, `:1174`);
  - a turn offer's value starts at 0 (`panel.js:1183`, `corner ? 0`) and follows the pointer's x (`handles.js:63`, `screen: 'x'`), so a vertical drag makes a 0° corner.
- **This is the most likely source of his second same-way corner.** inferred: his save's corners are −60-ish and −56.84 at 500, and the undo file keeps no fields to prove it.

**G4 · The bank handle's other side then grabs that turn change's mark and slides it along the road.**
- Repro (`drive1.txt` A, continued): after G3, press the bank handle's − side and drag up 40 px.
- Expected: the bank changes.
- Actual: the bank stays **80**; the turn change's mark moves from **500 → 596 m**.
- From:
  - a mark is two strokes, one at each edge (`secondtick.js:56-63`), and is taken on a press within 10 px of either (`:112`);
  - the turn's mark sits at the bank handle's station, nudged 10 px out (`:30`);
  - a mark drag picks the nearest ghost station on screen (`:128-131`, `panel.js:1193`).

**G5 · Every change starts at the middle of the room left, never where he would put it.**
- Repro (`drive1.txt` A, A2, F): on a 1,000 m piece with no change yet, every offer reads `from: 500`. His save has every change at exactly 500 m.
- Expected: near the end (his red marks) or where it is dragged.
- Actual: L/2, and the offer cannot be slid along the road before it is used (its drag sets only the value, `secondtick.js:132-141`).
- From: `panel.js:1180-1182` (form (ii): the middle of R).

**G6 · A drag back toward level stops short of it: nothing holds at 0 unless the drag crosses it.**
- Repro (`drive1.txt` C, `handles.targetFor('bank', 80, px)`, the maths `secondtick.js:138` runs): a drag let go at 0, 2, 5, 10 and 20 px short of level reads **0, 0.9, 2.25, 4.5 and 9°**.
- Expected: level within a few pixels of it.
- Actual: as read.
- His bank ended at **4.58°**, which inferred is a drag about 10 px short (0.45° a pixel, `handles.js:53`).
- From: `handles.js:249-254`. The hold at 0 is applied only when the value crosses zero (`Math.sign(v) !== Math.sign(base)`).

**G7 · An offer shows where its change cannot fit, and using it makes the piece vanish.**
- Repro (`drive1.txt` E): 300 m, bank 30. The offer shows at 150 m. Drag it to level.
- Expected: no offer where a change cannot fit, or a change laid.
- Actual: the core refuses, "the bank (phi) change 1 (its second bank), from 0.523599 to 0, needs at least 172.5 m before the piece's end … from at most 127.5, got 150". The ghost goes.
- From: `panel.js:1181`. An offer shows once R ≥ 2 · `SECOND_FLOOR_M` (40 m), but a change needs its own `secondMinM`, 31.5·√Δ° for the bank.

**G8 · Shortening the piece squeezes a change under its minimum, and the piece vanishes.**
- Repro (`drive3.txt`): 1,000 m, bank 80, a change to 0 made at 500 (share 0.5). Type length 800, 600, 560, 500.
- Expected: the change moves or is held so it still fits, or the shortening is held.
- Actual: laid at 800 and 600 (from 400, 300). At **560 the core refuses** ("needs at least 281.7 m before the piece's end"), and again at 500. The ghost goes.
- From: `panel.js:404` (a dragged change keeps its SHARE of the piece) and `:1193`. Nothing checks the share against the change's minimum.
- Lengthening only spreads them (`drive1.txt` B: a corner change at share 0.596 → 894 at 1,500 m), with no refusal.

**G9 · A mark can be dragged past its neighbour, and the piece vanishes.**
- Repro (`drive1.txt` D): 1,000 m, bank 30, changes to 10 at 400 and to 0 at 800. Drag change 2's mark to 300.
- Expected: it stops at its neighbour plus a minimum.
- Actual: `[{ from: 400 }, { from: 300 }]`; the core refuses, "change 2 (its third bank) starts after change 1 (400 m) … got 300". The ghost goes.
- From: `secondtick.js:128-131` (the nearest station anywhere on the piece) and `panel.js:1193`. No clamp to the neighbours.

**G10 · A press on an offer or a mark that does not move does nothing, and does not reach the handle beneath.** A click on the bank or climb handle (G1, G2) is swallowed.
