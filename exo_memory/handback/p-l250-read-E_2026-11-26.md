### 3. A pick changed while another is still reading: DEFECT
- **What holds:**
  - V3a: two picks in Road with the road read held. The later pick wins.
  - V3d: Clear while a pick is reading. Nothing is drawn when the read lands.
- **The defect.** `setMode` (`panel.js:152-158`) re-picks `shownKey`, the layout **last shown**, not the one still reading. When nothing is shown it does
  not bump `seq`, so the read still out lands in the mode it began in.
  Each row below holds one read open with a promise:

  | row | what was shown | what was still reading | mode change | what he gets (checked, `e_vary_run.txt`) |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | V3e | A, as Road | B, its road read | to Line | **A is drawn as Line. His pick of B is dropped**, and the select goes back to A. |
  | V3f | nothing | B, its road read | to Line | **B is drawn as Road while the mode box says Line.** The readout says road. |
  | V3c | A, as Line | B, its line read | to Road | A is drawn as Road; B is dropped. |
  | V3b | nothing | B, its line read | to Road | B is drawn as Line under "Road". |

  - **Which can a person reach:** V3e and V3f, because the window is the Road read, 0.1-1 s. inferred: the Line read is 6-9 ms in B's figures, so V3b
    and V3c are near unreachable by hand.
- **The fix shape, checked as a scratch fix and reverted:** keep the key being read (`pendingKey`, set when `pick` starts and cleared when it lands or by Clear).
  `setMode` then re-picks `pendingKey || shownKey`. That is 6 lines (`scratch_fix_panel.diff`). V3a-f went 6 of 6 green and the authors' 17 panel rows stayed
  green. It is B's to write as a row, red first: V3e and V3f are the ones to keep.
