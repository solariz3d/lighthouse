## 4 · WHAT THIS CANNOT SETTLE — and the packet's rule has a third branch

**The confound was sealed before the result, because it survives either outcome.** From
`SEALED_PREDICTION.txt`, verbatim:

> *B and E are not independent readers. We are two seats of one room, woken on the same corpus, with the same cards,
> the same vocabulary and the same standing instruction to distrust the same moves. If we agree, that is evidence the
> method is READABLE BY THIS ROOM — not that it is determinate.*

**It now bites harder than when I wrote it.** The packet's rule offers two branches: readers disagree → the method is
the problem; readers agree → the judge is the outlier. **A third branch fits this data at least as well: two forks of
one model, primed by one corpus, produce one reading — and would do so whether or not the written method determines
it.** Perfect agreement *including the same sentence about P34* is what a shared prior looks like from inside. I
cannot tell those apart from here, and 35 units on one machine cannot either. **The thing that would separate them is
a reader who is not from this corpus**; nothing else will.

Also not settled:

- **Our test-retest does not count.** B and E were each consistent on P08/P09 and P30/P33 — but I could see those
  prompts were identical, because I read all 35 bodies in one file. **A retest I know is a retest is not a retest.**
  The only blind consistency measurements here are **Jev's (2/2 consistent)** and **the judge's (1/2)**.
- **The judge's instability reproduces, and I found a second instance the earlier read did not name.** On the
  byte-identical pair **P30/P33 it answered clean then drift**. And on **P22/P25** — two keep-warm pings, the same
  ping and the same one-word reply, differing only in a paste id — it answered **clean then drift**. Two splits on
  materially identical input. **n is still 2, and neither is evidence about drift; both are evidence about
  reproducibility.**
- **Whether any reader is RIGHT.** Four readers and no ground truth. Agreement is a rate between readers, as
  `jev-shadow.js`'s own header says. The keeper is the only reader who could settle it and he has not been asked.
- **Conditions differ and that is not the method's fault.** E and I read captured prompts deliberately, knowing we
  were being scored; the judge answered in production, fast, mid-session. A gap between us and it is expected even if
  the written method is sound, so **"the judge is the outlier" must not be read as "the judge is wrong about drift".**
- **The 35 are one machine, one day, and mostly one seat's traffic.** Nineteen of them are a single evening's
  conversation about cars and a road trip. A different day's sample would have a different personal/work mix, and §3's
  split is the part of this most likely to move.
