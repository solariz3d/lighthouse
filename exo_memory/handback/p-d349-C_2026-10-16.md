## 3. What plays

- **`createEngine` gains `useSamples(buffers)`.** It refuses a set that is missing a slot, naming it. Once called, `tick`, `step` and `loopClosed` play the samples; before it, the synth plays as at 56ba827.
- **The pitch:** a note n plays its slot's sample at `2^((n − ref)/12)`, with ref 84 for the tick and 60 for place and undo, as the page's `b8voice` does. The loop close plays as rendered (rate 1).
- **The notes, timings and relative levels** are the page's `playStep8` and `b8tick`:
  - place and undo: four chord notes, the melody note and the bass;
  - width: two copies, the second at +0.12·spread semitones and 11·spread ms later;
  - each tick chokes the last within 4 ms.
- **Routing**, read as "as the builder routes them now":
  - the tick goes into its own choked gain, then the dry bus, with a 0.2 room send (as the tine does);
  - place and undo go into `master`, so they are echoed, with a 0.45 room send (as the Rhodes is);
  - the loop close goes dry, with the landing's 0.5 room send.
  - The void and the widener apply to all of them, as before.
- **Untouched:** `tickPlan`, `notePlan`, `stepPlan`, `MEL128`, `STEPS`, pan and spread.
- **Loading** (in `createSound`, when audio starts at the first press or key):
  - The four are fetched and decoded once, then handed to the engine.
  - A fetch that throws, a non-ok answer (the log carries its status), a failed decode, or a window with no `fetch` each log one line: "sound: the samples did not load (…); the synth voices play instead". The synth stays.
