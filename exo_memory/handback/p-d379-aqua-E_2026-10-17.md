## 1. What Aqua is (how it differs from Glass, Skyline and Aurora)

Water, glossy, liquid, rounded, caustic light: the mid-2000s gel look, pushed well past SIGIL.
- **Every button and menu name is an aqua gel pill:**
  - fully rounded;
  - a wet white highlight on the upper half and bright aqua below;
  - a dark teal rim and **dark ink text** (`#01303c`).
  - It's the opposite of SIGIL's hairline-on-black, and of Glass's dark square chips.
- **The bar:** a glossy band with a wet highlight along its top edge, deep teal where the words sit. The sigil becomes a water bubble: a clear sphere with a highlight and an aqua core.
- **The left pane, the menus, the dialogs and the onboarding card:** rounded (18 px) slabs of saturated teal water-glass, each with a broad wet highlight across the top and an aqua rim and glow.
- **The camera dock:** one rounded pill of water-glass, its active modes small gel pills. The camera hints get a rounded water backing.
- **Fields:** rounded wells of deep water with an inset shadow.
- **The 3D view:** a **teal** sky (Glass's is navy), crossed by **caustic rings**, light rippling outward from three points. There's a band of surface light along the top edge and a deep-water vignette.
- **Unlike the others:**
  - Skyline is a light sky skin;
  - Aurora is night curtains;
  - Glass is square-cut, dark and cold.
  - Aqua is the only one with round gel buttons in bright aqua with dark text, and with a saturated teal across the whole window.
