## What was built (checked: the diff `cab3530..134e6f2`)
1. **The lean and the look on the hunter** (`game/model_rig.h` GaitMotion; `game/model_rig.cpp` apply_motion; `game/world/stage.cpp` at the hunter's `gait_pose` call):
   - **The lean:**
     - `gait_pose` takes the chase's `lean` (ChaserPose, from `world_app.cpp` gather_poses). The body above the legs is rolled about its line of travel through the feet.
     - The outside foot is set up to 10 cm wider, and the legs are reached again to the feet with the bones' own lengths.
     - The drawn lean is capped at **kGaitMaxLean = 0.42 rad (24°)**. The chase's figure, `atan(speed × turn / g)`, is a body's balance in the turn: 0.77 rad (44°) for the staged corner at 6 m/s and 1.6 rad/s. A runner doesn't show that much. **The cap is my choice, his to tune.**
   - **The look:**
     - `look − heading` turns the chest a third of the way (at most 26°), and the neck and head the rest (at most 75° in all).
     - **A rig change came with it:** the neck and head bones take a frame of their own (`BodyPose::head_right`, `Rig::palette`). Without it, a turned head barely twists, because the rig's shortest swing adds no turn about the neck's own length.
     - With `head_right` unset, every pose draws exactly as before (the kid's included).
   - **The comment** at `game/world/world_app.h:86-88` (ChaserPose's look and lean) now says what is true: the hunter's model draws both; the box figures don't.
2. **Feet on the floor:**
   - **The hunter:** `GaitMotion::floor` sets each foot on the floor under it. The hips sink for the lower foot (up to 30 cm), and the knees bend forward to reach.
   - **The player's running body:** each landing foot goes down on its own floor (`Body::set_floor_probe`, used in the run's foot targets and the hip sink, `game/body.cpp`). Before this it went down on a plane at the player's own height (it was `body.cpp:218`).
   - **The probe:** `WorldApp::floor_below`, a ray of `sim::World::raycast` from 0.5 m above to 0.5 m below.
     - `raycast` "moves nothing and is not part of the fingerprint" (`sim/world.h:117`).
     - The player is a Jolt CharacterVirtual, outside the queried bodies, so the ray can't meet the player.
   - On from the start (`WorldApp::feet_on_floor`).
