## 1. Left Ctrl descends instantly (the keeper: "control lags before going down")
- **D252's 200 ms hold is removed** (`app/preview/preview.js`). Left Ctrl, as the only key down with no button, joins `held` and takes the view over at key-down, exactly like Space.
- **The undo stays.** When another key, the wheel or a button joins Ctrl, that press's descent is undone and the view it found is restored, so Ctrl+Z leaves the camera where it was. The brief dip is accepted, per the librarian's ruling.
- **My 64fa6cf handle-press fix still holds:** a `pointerdown` counts as a button. Its row ("a handle drag (pointerdown only…)") passes unchanged.
- **The 200 ms row is amended BY NAME** (the keeper overrode it). It was "D252: Ctrl then Z WITHIN 200 ms leaves the camera untouched from the very start". It is now **"D255: Left Ctrl descends on the FIRST frame, like Space; Ctrl+Z still puts the camera back where Ctrl found it, and the view it found"**:
  - the first 100 ms frame descends 3 m;
  - Z brings the camera back exactly, and it stays there;
  - from the build view, Ctrl takes over at once, and Ctrl+Z gives the build view back.

## 2. The camera turns with the RIGHT button only (the keeper: "left click will be used for clicking things")
- `onDown` starts a look drag on `button === 2` only. It used to accept `button === 0` in free mode too.
- **New row:** "D255: a LEFT drag never turns the camera, in free mode either; a RIGHT drag still looks". In free mode a left drag leaves yaw and pitch exactly as they were, and a right drag turns. In a follow view a left drag takes nothing over.

## 3. D256 item 1: the design-speed "off" box (the keeper: "it disapears and cannot be turned back on")
**Reproduced first, and the inferred cause was wrong.** Nothing re-renders the row: it never left the DOM. Headless Chrome with a fake native side (`mouse/offprobe.js`, output `offprobe.json`), one 300 m piece:

| state | box (x, y, w, h) | row text |
|---|---|---|
| before | **14, 2149**, 13, 13 | design speed 460 km/h off |
| after clicking off | **250, 2129**, 13, 13 | design speed off off |
| clicked again | 14, 2149 | design speed 460 km/h off |

The value's text changes width ("460 km/h" becomes "off"), the label wraps differently, and **the box jumps 236 px right and up a line**. To the keeper it vanished from under the pointer, and clicking where it had been hit nothing. The probe's second click went to the element itself, which is why it "worked" there.

**Fix** (`app/validate-ui/speed.js`):
- the value has a **fixed-width box** (`display: inline-block; min-width: 7ch`, right-aligned), so "off" and "970 km/h" take the same width;
- the box and its word sit in one `white-space: nowrap` span, so the row wraps the same in both states.

**What the design speed does is unchanged** (E is measuring; the keeper decides after).

**After the fix, the same probe** (`offprobe_after.json`): the box stays at **14, 2149** before, after off, and after on, with 0 page errors.

**Row, red first** (on the old `speed.js`, stashed: 8 pass / 1 fail): "D256: the design-speed value has a FIXED box and the off box stays with its word, so the box does not move; off, on and off again all work". It checks that the value's style has inline-block and a min-width in ch, and that the box's group has `white-space: nowrap`. Off, on, off gives `[null, 460, null]` on the same box element. **A row cannot measure layout**, so the layout itself rests on the headless probe numbers above.

**My order slip here:** I applied the `speed.js` fix before writing its row. I then wrote the row and ran it red by stashing `speed.js` alone, before re-applying.
