## Rule, as coded (src/geom/mesh.js header)
- **A segment carrying `blend: { from, s0, length }`** blends from `from` to its `profile` at weight `smoothstep((s0 + d) / length)`, capped at 1, where d is the distance into the segment. One word's transition can therefore span several segments and continue across the cut.
- **`blend: null`** means no ramp. The geometry then adds none of its own; the document spoke.
- **Only a segment WITHOUT the field** falls back to `profileIn` / the previous-font inheritance of p-d167-bvh-C, for callers that do not use resolve.
- **A malformed field throws:** a missing `from`, s0 < 0 or non-finite, or length ≤ 0 or non-finite.
