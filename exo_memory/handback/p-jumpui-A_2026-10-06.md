## Decisions I made (so you can overrule them)
- **Both measured falls are drawn, not one.** The core has two (3.2 g and 6.3 g) and the landing must catch both (`checkJump`); drawing one would hide the case where the heavy fall lands short.
- **The default gap is 20 m, not 30.** At 30 m on a flat take-off the 6.3 g fall already falls short at 460 km/h (it needs about 600 km/h), so the first hover would greet the keeper with a red line; at 20 m both clear. A take-off with a climb (5.7° in my probes) clears 30 m; the panel's hint says to set the climb first.
- **The Jump block is inside the Extend section, with no new heading** (the panel's section list is pinned by a test), and **the jump ghost replaces the Extend ghost while the jump fields are used**, so the drag handles are off then and return after the pointer leaves the button.
- **Placed flights are drawn too**, always (a flight is a gap in the road; without the arc it is invisible once placed). If that is clutter on a long track it is one line to limit it to the candidate.
- The arc is drawn at 460 km/h whatever the validation box says; that box is only mentioned in words. It is the speed the ramp is built for, which is what the packet asked to show.
