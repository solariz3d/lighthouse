### 8.1 (b) The design, my call: one flip finder, on the road, shared by the plan and the rows

- **`flipsOf(doc)`** (finish.js, exported):
  - It samples the stored turn rate every FLIP_STEP_M = 2 m along every road piece.
  - A flip is the stretch [s0, s1] from the last turning sample (|kh| > 1/600) of one sign to the first of the other, when no straight run longer than FLIP_STRAIGHT_M = 60 m lies between them. A jump breaks the stretch.
  - **Why 60 m:** the longest dead band of a flip in P's sweep is 38 m (+1/300 → −1/300 over 100 m). A straight longer than 60 m between two turns is one the bank comes down on anyway, and pinning across a straight only pins points that are already at no bank (my G1 mutant was equivalent for that reason).
- **`bankPlan`** pins every control point inside each flip's stretch, and the nearest point on either side, at no added bank. The rate and acceleration limits are untouched.
- **The rows read their flip zones from the same function** (`require('../src/core/finish.js').flipsOf`), so the two finders can't disagree. That disagreement was P's whole finding.
