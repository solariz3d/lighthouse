## 1. What was built (Part 1)

**`finish.js` `bankLap(closedDoc)`** (exported), and a `relax` option on `bankPlan`:
- **What is banked:** every road piece of a closed lap whose bank stays within ROLL_FREE (5°) of the start's at every point AND that is LEVEL. Level means the road's up within LEVEL_DEG = 30° of the world's at every station, read from the lap's path at 5 m.
- **How:** banked by BANK_SHARE 0.75 of the balance bank at the design speed, through `bankPlan`: BANK_ROLL, BANK_ACCEL, C1 at every joint, and the share scaled down while the lap rolls past the amber (BANK_TRIES).
- **Not banked:**
  - a piece banked or rolled by hand;
  - a piece that isn't level (a loop, its way in and out, a steep ramp);
  - a tube;
  - a piece that isn't road;
  - the road beside a pit lane (from the leave anchor's piece to the rejoin's).

  Finish's own banked closer pieces aren't upright, so they stay as Finish banked them.
- **(d) the joints, my call:**
  - Where two banked upright pieces that BOTH curve (some |kh| control point over 1/600) meet, the joint isn't pinned. Its points are banked and limited like any other, and the later piece's first two points follow the joint by the C1 slope rule.
  - So a curve laid as several pieces banks as one: row 3, where the lean holds ≥ 30° within 20 m either side of the joint.
  - A joint next to a straight, a piece not banked, or the seam (p1 and the last piece keep the start's bank there) stays pinned.
  - Why: today's pin gives a curve split across pieces a dip to the laid bank at every joint, and that is what (d) asked to remove. Pinning beside straights keeps the D434 rule that a straight between turns gets no bank, and pinning at the seam keeps the start line on the start's bank.
- **A backstop:** if the banked lap would lean out of a curve at more stations than the closed lap did (D440's `closerLoad`, the horizontal turn), the lap is returned as closed. It is unreached on every lap here (Q7, §4).

**`app/core/closejob.js` `runCloseBest`** (what the page's Close press runs, `coreshell.js closeBest`): every route's closed lap goes through `bankLap` before it is ranked and checked. So:
- the lap the press proves is the banked one;
- the near path banks (it added no window, so nothing was banked before);
- the bank is in the same result the shell commits as one history step.

`finishToStart` itself is unchanged: its rows still hold his pieces as the very same objects. The old one-click `close()` and the route tools' re-close after a drag (`runClose` with a window) are NOT banked. Only the Close press is, as the brief scopes it.

**Gentle curves get a gentle bank:** after a whole-lap `close()` bends a straight to R ~1,450 m, the share there is 37° and the rate limit holds it to 6.1°. That is the 0.75 rule at the design speed, not a defect.
