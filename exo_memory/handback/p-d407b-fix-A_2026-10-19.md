## 1. What changed (17f4d5b)

**A drag let go off the stage now ends** (`handles.js offStage`).

- About 100 ms into a handle drag, the pointer capture was lost, in 0.4.8 too. A release over the camera bar or the palette, both of which float over the stage, never reached the handles. The drag went on and the cheap ghost stayed.
- While a handle is held, a release anywhere on the page now ends it. A release on the stage is still the stage's own.
- `inferred:` the capture loss itself is headless Chrome under DevTools mouse events. On a bare page there it never holds a capture at all (`scratchpad/d407/cap2.js`: no capture events, and the release goes to whatever is under the pointer). Whether a real mouse in WebView2 loses it is not shown here. The fix holds either way.

**The release.**

- The pointer-up handler is now the drag's own end. It was 7 ms at 2 km in D407, the candidate built inside it; it is now under 1 ms.
  - The hint and the readout come on the next frame, and the candidate on the one after (`panel.js releaseGhost`).
- The exact ghost spends at most 1.5 ms a frame building (`GHOST_SLICE_MS`, was 2.5).
  - Its assembly and its batches are steps too (`mesh.js assembleSteps`, `batches.js batchesOfSteps`).
  - Its chunks and lines are made before its first draw (`renderer.prepareGhost`).
  - It is swapped in on a frame of its own.
  - It is not stepped in the frame that asked for it.
- The candidate itself is about 40% cheaper (node, 2 km: 7.7 → 4.1 ms). The adapter now keeps one frozen `profileAt` per family, width and rate in nested maps, with no string key, and reuses a chord's start values.

**Placing a piece.**

- The next piece's readout waits for an idle moment (`readoutSoon`), and so does the carried corner's.
- The next piece's ghost is asked for as the release asks for it.
- The start's marks are made in idle slices of 2 ms (`coreshell spawnsInfoSteps`, `spawnslayer.js`), and the old marks stay meanwhile (D284: adding at the open end does not move the start).
- A newly placed piece's first draw is made ready a slice a frame (`renderer.prepareTrack`, `preview placed` / `preparePlaced`). The track before it stays drawn, with the new piece shown as its exact ghost, until its textures, chunks, tangents and lines are made.
- Cells keep their textures across a new texture set whose floor for their piece reads the same (`preview flowFor`, a key of the floor's content, made once per piece and set).
  - This one was found by profiling. The textures panel makes a new set at every new piece, so every cell of the track was textured again on every placement.
- A hover over Extend that asks for the ghost already on show builds nothing; before, it built the same exact ghost again.
- The new segments' keys are hashed in the ghost's own slices.

## 4. Found on the way, and corrections

- **My D407 place and edit figures were understated (my error).** `ord407.js` counted only frames whose timestamp came at or after the event. The redraw right after a click or an input runs in a frame that began just before it, so that frame was left out.
  - The accepted D407 ABAB rows for place and edit are therefore too low for both trees. v0.4.8's placement is 126–263 ms, not 108–262. D407's frame after a placement was 17–37 ms, unseen.
  - The release rows used a 20 ms margin and are not affected.
  - The harness and test row 4 are corrected, and the D407b ABAB re-times v0.4.8, D407 and D407b with the corrected window.
- **Idle callbacks are timed as tasks**, in the harnesses and in counting placement's worst task, so work moved to idle time is not hidden from the measurement.
- **The textures panel builds a new texture set at every new piece** (`textures.js`, "a new piece is a new segment id the set must know"). Before D407b this re-textured the whole track on every placement, and that cost grows with the track.
- **The labels layer rebuilds its DOM every frame:** about 2.3 ms of each frame on a 12-piece track. Not changed here; it is in every frame's cost.
