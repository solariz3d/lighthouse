## 1. What was built

- **The core step on a set** (`src/core/sculpt.js` `brushSet`, through `brush(doc, { mode: 'value', channels: { h, l, w, phi, c }, s0, r })`). Each channel is brushed exactly as a
  single value brush is, one after another, in the document's channel order (they are separate control-point arrays, so the order changes nothing). A delta of 0 is skipped;
  sharp refines once, with the first channel. Refused by name: a rate channel, any mode but value, an empty or non-object set, a delta that is not a finite number.
  A single-channel call goes through the old path untouched.
- **The grab plane** (`app/preview/preview.js` `rayAt`, the preview's `grabRay()`, the event `'t180-grab-ray'` in `app/preview/index.js`; `app/core/zonegrab.js` `planePoint`): the
  pointer's ray under the camera as it was at the press meets the plane through the hit facing the camera. The drag reads P′(pointer) − P′(press), so a press a few px off a
  dot does not jump.
- **follow()** (`app/core/zonegrab.js`, pure; `frameAt` reads the road's frame and section at the press). The forward part of Δ is dropped; the rest lies in the section's plane
  (R, U0), and:
  - **the centre**, and partway out with nothing to lift (a plain road, a tube, an edge band): the section slides whole: l = Δ·R, h = Δ·U0 / (U0·world up);
  - **partway out on a cup piece**: the section slides along its own across and the cup turns, solved together by Newton on the cup's own profile (the shell's
    `zoneInfo(s, u).cupMove`, from `adapter.js cupProfile`); a cup whose lift there runs along the across, or past its limits, slides whole instead;
  - **a rim**: the far rim stays and the chord from it to the pressed point turns (phi) and stretches (w, the section scaled about the far rim), with the centre moved to
    match (l, h). A rim pulled toward the far rim keeps a tenth of the chord, so the road never folds over.
- **The shell** (`app/core/coreshell.js` `beginFollow`; `brushed` → `followed`): one drag, one undo step, Esc drops it. Each channel's delta is divided by the value one unit of brush
  gives at the press (`followRatio`, D335's reading at s0), so the point lands where follow asked.
- **The panel** (`app/core/panel.js`): with Grab, a press on the road (pick or dot) opens the follow (`followOf`); every frame's move goes through `followAt`. D519's axis lock
  (`lockZone`, `ZONE_TOOL`, `zoneRefusalOf`, the zone branch of `deltaAt`) is removed; `axisOf` stays exported with its D519 rows. The hover says what follows there ("this
  edge follows the pointer · the far edge stays"), the drag's value line lists what changed ("height +0.40 m · sideways … at the point"), and the Grab hint is the follow's.
  Off the road, or where the view gives no ray or frame, the press is Grab's one channel as before.
- `app/core/handles.js` `dotAt` also names the dot's world point `at`.
- CHANGELOG [Unreleased] ▸ Changed: D519's line is replaced by D520's (D519 never landed, and its line no longer says what the grab does).

## 4. Where it does not land exactly

- **A pitched road.** h is along world up, so on a climbing road a lift also slides the section along the road by dh·sin(pitch). No channel can take that back: l is horizontal and
  square to the heading. Measured (probe2.out): the pressed point's own material lands 0.13–0.33 m along the road from the pointer on an 11.5° climb. The road SURFACE stays under
  the pointer: over 216 presses on roads pitched 11.5° and 22.9° at the press, the pointer is at most **4.9 mm** off the road at its share, and the far rim's line at most **4.3 mm**
  from where it was (probe3.out; row 14j holds it to 1 cm). inferred: on the helix it reads as the road sliding a little along itself under the pointer, not as a drift off it.
- **A rim on a pre-cup (legacy) bowl piece.** The legacy profile's angles depend on the width (adapter.js profileAt: rise × w/8), so it is not exactly the same shape when
  widened. Row 25's bowl road (legacy) still holds the far rim and the point to 1 cm. Cup, edge and tube pieces scale exactly.
- **A closed tube's heartline.** A tube rolled about an axis off the road turns about that axis, not the centre, so the rim solve there is approximate. Not measured.
- **The cup.** 81 cup presses at banks 0, 60 and −85, cups 10–80°, three shares, three drags (probe4.out): the worst miss is **0.052 mm**. 11 of the 81 slid whole instead of
  turning the cup (the cup's lift there runs along the across, or a limit).
- **Past 87° of pitch**, follow gives nothing, and the drag holds its last step.

## 5. Where this departs from the plan, and why

- **The rim is solved, not added.** The plan's `w += side·e, l += e/2` and `phi += asin(z/w), h += z/2` hold on a flat road only (§2). The solve gives the plan's own numbers
  there: for a press at the rim's share the width grows by e / that share. On a lift it widens the road a little: √(w² + z²) − w, 6 cm for a 2 m lift on 32 m. That is what keeps
  the point exactly under the pointer; `asin` with a fixed w would pull it inward by w(1 − cos δ).
- **The centre and the cup zone carry the point exactly in both directions** rather than splitting "sideways → l, up → h or c". On a banked road "up" and "the road's up" differ,
  and a split by world axes would move the point off the pointer.
- **Partway out with nothing to lift** (a plain road, a tube, an edge piece's edge band) slides the road whole. The plan names cup pieces only; edge angles stay on their key (the
  plan's "kept out").
- **A rim pulled toward the far rim** stops at a tenth of the chord, so the road cannot fold over or flip its bank in one drag. A bank past a quarter turn takes two drags.
- **Alt does nothing under Grab now**: the hover's glyph no longer shows an Alt variant.
