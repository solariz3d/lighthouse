## 3. The code that makes the flip

**`src/geom/pitlane.js:95`** (b6969da): `const phi = psiEdge * (lane.offsetM > 0 ? 1 - Math.min(1, g / lane.offsetM) : 1)`.
- The lane is tilted by the road's edge angle where the gap is 0 (the leave and the rejoin), and eases to level as the gap opens.
- `:96` builds the lane's across-direction `A` from it, and `:97` sets `inner = edge + A·g` and `centre = edge + A·(g + w/2)`.
- So at a join, a lane 8 m wide on a 30° edge has its outer edge 8·sin 30° = 4 m up. While the gap is still small the tilted A also lifts the inner edge.
- This is the rule from my own D182 (header comment `:15-22`): G1 across the shared edge, so the lane continues in the edge's plane. On a bowl edge of 15.5° it was a 2.1 m lift. On his 30° edge it is 4.2 m, and he sees it.
- `src/export/pitlane.js` (the infill, `:73-`) builds from the meshes' own edges and adds no lift of its own.
