## What it does (his 07:02 ask)
- **Click a saved piece (row, Preview at head, Preview mirrored): nothing is laid.** The piece is the see-through ghost at the head, the boxes hold the numbers that made it, and the panel shows **Place piece** and **Cancel piece**. Change any box and the ghost becomes the panel's own next piece from the boxes (the same ghost Extend always shows); **Place piece** then lays what the ghost shows. Untouched, Place lays the saved piece's own bytes. Esc, Cancel piece, a new document or picking a piece on the track drops the ghost. The Pieces bin closes on the click so the ghost is in view.
- **A saved piece keeps its numbers in `<name>.t180recipe` beside the `.t180piece`.** Written by Save as piece when ONE Extend laid exactly the selected pieces and they are still those pieces (same objects); the recipe names the piece file by a hash (FNV-1a + length), so a sidecar beside another file of that name is not used. Rename carries it; Delete removes it. The piece file is byte for byte what the core writes (row P1, P1b, P11); `piece.js` is unchanged since 3b33b38 (checked: `git diff 3b33b38..HEAD -- src/core/piece.js` is 0 lines), and the native listing asks for `.t180piece` only.
- **No recipe** (an older piece, a run of several steps, a piece changed since, a non-Extend command's piece, a mirrored ghost): the ghost is the piece, the panel says "No numbers were kept ... what its shape does is read off below" with the same words L229 gives an undo, the boxes are not filled, and Place lays it as saved.
