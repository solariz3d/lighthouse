### How it works
- **Two pieces in ONE commit.** The TURN piece ramps the rate to ±1/R over `ramp` with a per-channel transition `{kh: ramp}` (extend's `rampControl`, set
  rather than fitted, so it cannot ring) and holds it. The EXIT piece, `ramp` m long, ramps it to 0, with its last two kh set to 0 (rate 0, slope 0).
- Both pieces have `knotM = min(2, ramp)`. R is kept as typed. The HOLD length is bisected so the two pieces turn exactly the angle on the adapter's path
  (turnby.js `turnWeights`).
- **Corrections I made to myself:** at R 12 / width 24 the bisection missed by 0.02° (SHARP_NOT_EXACT), because the knot count moves in whole steps with the
  hold length. A least-norm step on the hold's own control points takes up the rest; the entry ramp and the joint are untouched, so it stays C1 into the exit.
  R moves about 0.02%.
- **Minimum radius:** judged on a PROBE: 60 m straight with the head's width and cross-section (endState, kind), the corner, 60 m after it.
  `validate(…, {csp, fullSpeed})` as the app runs it. Any red or amber within 5 m of the corner refuses (SHARP_TOO_TIGHT); the tightest is bisected to 0.05 m
  and rounded UP to 0.1 m, then re-checked green. Refusals cost about 1.4–2.4 s; a placed corner about 0.15 s.
