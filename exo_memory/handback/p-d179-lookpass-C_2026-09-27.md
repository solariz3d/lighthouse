- The views:

**The render ("the builder renders those same views").**
- Node has no WebGL, so `raster.js` rasterises on the CPU: a z-buffer, perspective-correct attributes, triangles clipped at the near plane, bilinear texture sampling with repeat, alpha testing.
- Every pixel's colour is `app/preview/aclook.js` `uniformsFor(material)` + `shade(…)` under `LIGHT`, the same functions the GLSL mirrors, plus the preview's fog.
- **Stated differences from the WebGL window:** no MSAA and no mip filtering (one mip ≤ 1024 px); ksPerPixelNM shaded with the vertex normal (its normal map not sampled); ksMultilayer with its base txDiffuse only.
- A pixel nothing covers stays transparent, and the metric leaves it out (the sky).

**The metric: mean CIEDE2000 (ΔE00) over the compared pixels, with the 95th percentile beside it.** Why this and not SSIM:
- §5.3 tunes the LOOK (light, material colour, brightness) of a scene whose GEOMETRY is the same in both images (the same kn5 from the same fixed camera).
- ΔE00 measures exactly that, per pixel, in perceptual units: about 1 is a just-noticeable difference.
- SSIM compares local structure, is largely blind to the global tone and brightness shifts being tuned, and has no perceptual unit.
- The p95 catches a small wrong area (a material, a glare) that a mean dilutes.

**The formula and the tests:**
- The formula is Sharma, Wu and Dalal (2005); sRGB (D65) → Lab. A pixel masked (alpha < 128) in either image is not compared.
- **Tested on synthetic images:**
  - identical images → exactly 0;
  - a pure L* shift of 10 around L* 50 → exactly 10 (the lightness weight is 1 there);
  - a one-pixel shift of a two-tone 8-px-wide image → exactly (1/8) × ΔE00 of the two tones;
  - Sharma's published pairs 1, 2 and 17 (2.0425, 2.8615, 27.1492) → within 1e-4;
  - sRGB white and black → L* 100 and 0;
  - masking; a size mismatch or a fully masked pair refused.
- `diff` without reference shots prints "PARKED: no reference shot" per view (tested).
