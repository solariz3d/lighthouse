## 1 · What it builds

Reproduce the summary: `node scripts/platform_test.js`. Every figure below is from that run unless marked otherwise.

**The loop.** A stadium run counter-clockwise, with only left turns, in four words:

| word | length | font [ψ left / ψ right] | carries |
|---|---|---|---|
| straight B | 159.909 m | flat 0°/0° (26 m of road) | grid, pit box, hotlap run-up, start/finish gate |
| turn 1 | 180°, R 28.662 m | half-pipe 60°/60° | the half-pipe section |
| straight A | 160 m | flat | the jump |
| turn 2 | 180°, R 28.662 m | wall-ride 30°/**110°** | the wall-ride: the outside wall turns past vertical |

**Closure is by construction.**
- Straight B is set to straight A's integrated horizontal run.
- R is solved so the path is exactly 500 m.
- Arc steps use the exact chord.
- Result: **path 500.000 m; the end is 2.52e-12 m from the start; heading 360.000000°.**

**Geometry choices, each stated in the script header:**
- **Stations** every 0.5 m: 977 stations.
- **The frame is gravity-referenced (forward, left = up×forward, up), NOT the rotation-minimising frame of ARCHITECTURE
  §3.** On this track the two coincide: no roll, no torsion, and the tangent never pitches past 4°. The RMF is the
  editor's job.
- **The cross-section is one type.** It is a 10 m floor plus an 8 m wall arc each side, and the wall's turning angle
  ψ is the font's knob (ψ = 0 is a flat shoulder). Every station has the same 203 vertices, so fonts blend by
  smoothstep over ±10 m at word boundaries without re-meshing.
- **Seams:** 0.5 m / 28.66 m ≈ 1.0° along, and 110°/96 ≈ 1.15° across (inferred: arithmetic on the constants).
  FINDINGS.md:24 gives proven median seams of 0.2–1.1°.
- **Mesh choice: one mesh per cell that is both physics and visual** (`1ROAD_0…4`, renderable). A separate visual mesh
  buys nothing on an untextured test track. The wall-ride is ROAD, not WALL, so there is no `1WALL` (ARCHITECTURE §4:
  a WALL-object wall-ride is red).
- **Cells:** 5 cells. Three have 50,953 vertices / 101,000 triangles; the others have 20,503 and 25,781 vertices. All
  are under 65,536. The cells are split at the jump gap and every 250 stations, and neighbouring cells share their
  boundary row.
- **World coordinates with identity matrices,** not cell-relative. The track spans under 200 m, where a float32 step is
  about 1.5e-5 m (inferred).
- **Material:** one, `t180b_road`, `ksPerPixel`, no textures and no samplers. Props are ksAmbient 0.45, ksDiffuse 0.55,
  ksSpecular 0.15, ksSpecularEXP 20, ksEmissive 0,0,0 and ksAlphaRef 0. These are my choices, not measured.
- **Winding:** triangles are (A, C, B) with the profile running right→left and rows running forward, so each normal is
  on the drivable side (CCW seen from the road). Vertex normals are the profile normal in world space. Any flip for AC
  is the writer's job, per the interface.

**Markers,** in track coordinates (s along, u across with + = left, h above the surface):

- The grid is two staggered columns 8 m apart, and the line is 10 m ahead of the pole. The hotlap run-up is about 100 m.
- s snaps to the nearest station, which is why it shows 109.9 and not 110.
- **Matrix layout:** row 0 = left, row 1 = up, row 2 = **forward** ([8..10]), translation at [12..14].
- **Left** = cross(up, forward), the same convention E measured on five reference tracks (`src/export/markers.js`

## 2 · The jump, and its arithmetic

**Numbers used, with source lines:**
- **The fall in the air, 3.2–6.3 g:** FINDINGS.md:336–337 (§7d), measured at 375–640 km/h. ARCHITECTURE §4 says to
  show two landings.
- **Reachability** `dist·tan10° + ½·6.5 g·(dist / 375 km/h)²`: FINDINGS.md:313 (§7d).
- **The speed trend,** +1.9 g per 100 km/h: FINDINGS.md:377 (§7e).
- **Vertical curve radius:** the kicker ease 20 m / 4° ≈ 286 m (inferred), inside FINDINGS §2's median 65–320 m.

**Shape.**
- Run-in 15 m.
- Kicker: pitch eases 0→4° over 20 m, then holds 4° for 15 m. **Lip at 1.744 m, take-off 4.00°.**
- **Gap 12 m horizontal,** landing lip 0.70 m lower (at 1.044 m).
- Landing ramp at −2°, held for 12.417 m (solved so straight A ends level), then eased to 0 over 35 m.
- Run-out to the turn.

**The check (`jumpCheck`).** It reads the landing road from the built stations, not the design numbers. For every
10 km/h in the **250–350 km/h** band, and g_eff ∈ {**3.2**, **6.3**}, it flies a projectile from the lip and requires:
1. clearance over the landing lip ≥ 0.5 m;
2. a touchdown on straight A before the font blend (x ≤ 99.5 m);
3. a touchdown angle against the road ≤ 10°.

**The 10° limit and the 0.5 m clearance are MY design limits, not measured.**

**Hand arithmetic, the tightest case (250 km/h at 6.3 g):**
- v = 69.44 m/s, so vx = 69.27 m/s and vy = 4.844 m/s.
- t to the far edge = 12 / 69.27 = 0.1732 s.
- y = 1.744 + 4.844·0.1732 − ½·61.80·0.1732² = 1.744 + 0.839 − 0.927 = 1.656 m.
- Clearance = 1.656 − 1.044 = **0.61 m**. The script prints 0.61.

**Result: all 22 flights hold.** The table below comes from `node scripts/platform_test.js`.
- The worst clearance is 0.61 m (250 km/h at 6.3 g).
- The longest flight is 59.5 m (350 km/h at 3.2 g).
- The worst touchdown angle is 7.64°.
- The landing lip is reachable per §7d: a 0.70 m drop against a 2.54 m bound.

| km/h | g | clear (m) | lands (m past lip) | impact (°) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 250 | 3.2 / 6.3 | 1.07 / 0.61 | 34.4 / 18.6 | 7.17 / 7.64 |
| 300 | 3.2 / 6.3 | 1.21 / 0.90 | 46.8 / 25.9 | 7.54 / 7.18 |
| 350 | 3.2 / 6.3 | 1.30 / 1.07 | 59.5 / 34.2 | 7.33 / 7.17 |

**Outside the band, informational only, not asserted** (run with `jumpCheck` options):
- At 350–450 km/h it still holds: the longest landing is 88.7 m and the worst impact 7.57°.
- With the fall at **2.0 g** instead of 3.2 g it still holds: the longest landing is 86.4 m.

**`tools/jump_flight.cjs` does not apply to building.** It fits a flight from a replay. It applies after the keeper's
drive, to measure the real g_eff on this jump and set it against the two landings above.

**Say "this is wrong" here if the band is wrong.**
- The measured 3.2–6.3 g range comes from 375–640 km/h (FINDINGS.md:336).
- My 250–350 km/h band is below that. §7e's trend implies an even lower fall at these speeds, so real flights may run
  longer than the 3.2 g row.
- The 2.0 g row suggests the ramp still catches them, but that is extrapolation.
- I did not verify what speed a T-180 actually reaches at this lip on a 500 m loop with 28.7 m turns. That is inferred
  unknown.

## 3 · Tests

**Tests, and what each checks** (the checks are exported functions, so the mutations run the same code):
1. **Length is 500 m ± 0.5 m** (stated tolerance). It is measured as the chord sum over the built stations, including
   the closing step: 499.9977 m.
2. **The loop closes:** the end position is within 1e-6 m of the start, and the end frame (forward, left, up) is within
   1e-9 of it.
3. **The wall-ride turns past 90°,** measured on the built surface: the largest angle between a road vertex's normal and
   its station's up, over the wall-ride word, is **110.00°**.
4. **Fold check.** Method:
   - (a) every triangle's normal, from its world vertices in the mesh's own winding, is on the drivable side of its
     vertex normal and agrees with its across-profile neighbour (dot > 0);
   - (b) the two triangles of each quad agree, so a bow-tie fails;
   - (c) for every profile vertex, (P[i+1][j] − P[i][j])·forward_i > 0. That is the discrete form of §3's
     1 − κ(q·N) > 0, on every vertex;
   - (d) each distinct profile, as a 2D polyline, has no two crossing segments (119 distinct profiles, pairwise test).
   - The jump gap has no quads and is skipped.
   - **Not done:** §3's BVH test between non-adjacent cells. The track does not cross itself (a flat stadium), so it is
     not needed here. It is not implemented.
5. **The jump holds at 3.2 g and 6.3 g** across the band, both g values are present, and the landing is reachable.
6. **Cells** are under 65,536 vertices, named `<digit><KEY>`, with consistent array sizes, indices in range and finite
   positions.
7. **Markers:**
   - all six names are present;
   - each is 1–2 m above its surface point (measured along the station's up);
   - each points along the road (within 5°);
   - L is left of the race direction, taken from the back slot to the pole;
   - the line is ahead of every grid slot;
   - the hotlap start is at least 50 m before the line;
   - no two markers overlap in a ±1.0 m × ±2.4 m slot (E's inferred box).
8. **The shared T1 shape:** the field types (Float32Array, Uint16Array), material 0, ksPerPixel, and the matrix length.
9. **Edge case:** a zero-length straight, a zero-radius arc, a zero-angle arc, a zero-width gap and a NaN length are all
   refused with "must be a positive finite number".

**E's own marker checks** (`checkMarkers` in the uncommitted `src/export/markers.js`) also pass on my scene, 5 of 5:
start-ahead-of-grid, gate-orientation, height-and-heading, slots, pit-count.
