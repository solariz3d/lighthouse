## Addition 2: a single deliberate step always ticks (plan :1115-1118; a default, his to veto). Commit 4298cfc

**What it does:** a wheel notch, an arrow nudge on a held handle and a double-click reset each give one tick and move the melody one place (down for a step down), whatever the step's size. A drag keeps the whole-number rule.

**How:**
- `handles.js` `stepped(kind, was, value, single)`:
  - the wheel and the reset pass `single`;
  - the arrow passes it through `apply` (a `singleNext` flag set around its one `apply`);
  - the event's detail carries `single: true` only then.
- `sound.js`:
  - `tickPlan`'s note choice moved, unchanged, into `notePlan`;
  - a new `stepPlan(kind, prev, value, melAt, single)` sends a single step to `notePlan` with no whole-number test, and everything else to `tickPlan`;
  - `onStep` calls `stepPlan` with `d.single === true`, so a drag ticks exactly as before.
- For climb and bank, which pick their note from the value and not the melody, a 0.01 notch ticks every time on the same note. inferred: that is what "whatever its size" gives for those two.

**Tests** (`app/test/handle-step.test.js`), red first:
- **New rows 4 and 5,** both red before:
  - Row 4: the wheel, the arrow and the reset send `single: true`; a drag's steps carry no flag.
  - Row 5: through `sound.js`, 10 notches up give 10 ticks, exactly MEL128[1..10]; 10 down walk it back; and 10 drag steps of 0.01 give 1 tick, at 100.
- **Row 3, amended by name** (a comment above it). Its counts were "a notch onto 100.01: none" and "a 60-notch scroll: one tick". They are now one a notch: 2, and 60. Its point is unchanged and still asserted: after a scroll, a drag from 100.1 to 100.4 ticks nowhere.
