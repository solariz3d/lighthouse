## What the panel asked, and the fold

**Where the 13 asks a move came from**, checked with E's counter plus caller attribution (`scripts/who.js`, 40 moves on d87ba99): 623 probe roads and

- **372** came from `changeFroms`, inside every `extendOptions` build. It computed every change's minimum up front, though a dragged change always
  has its mark.
- **169** came from the fit checks (`secSegs`) and `offersAt`.

**The fold** (`87792d1`, rename `7e29ccd`; `panel.js`, `coreshell.js`):
- **`changeFroms`:** asks a change's minimum only when it lays that change's mark.
- **`secSegs`:** the shortest only on demand (`min(k)`), plus `holds(k)`, "does this segment hold at its span". That is one probe at most, through
  `shell.spanHolds` → `extend.js spanHolds` (B's export, `9050da5`).
  - `secFits` is the first mark's lower bound plus every segment holding. It is the same test, since a mark is past its range exactly when the span
    before it does not hold.
  - A core without `spanHolds` falls back to the span against the shortest, as before.
- **B's G8 move (`showSecond`):** asks `holds` first, and searches for the shortest only when a segment does not hold.
- **`offersAt({ unused: true })`** for the extra handles: only the fields with no change yet, since one extra handle per field rides its change. The
  next offer after a change, a search under a moving first ramp, is no longer asked every move. Other callers get the full list as before.
- **The hold in `secondExtra`:**
  - a value past one already known not to fit, the same way, is not asked;
  - a move that does not fit takes ONE step of the search between what fits and the nearest that does not;
  - the release (`final`, the handle's last apply, which `apply` now passes through) finishes the search, so the drag ends where it did.
