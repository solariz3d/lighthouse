## HOW IT IS BUILT

- **Rate lists** (`extend.js` `changeLists`, `secondRamp`):
  - the piece is laid as before;
  - then every field's changes insert knots in ONE insertion, in one fixed field order, sorted;
  - each changed field alone is refitted (`fitWithHold`), with every control point wholly before its first mark held as laid;
  - C2 fields get `endC2`.
  - **The order of keys cannot matter (A1):** the knots are sorted before insertion, and each refit touches only its own channel. So a mutant varying key order would be equivalent, and none is in the harness.
- **Every change blends by the C2 quintic, route fields included** (a deviation from E's emulator, which used each channel's own blend). Measured by `<scratch>/l139/slopes.js <tree>` on five lists:

  | case | quintic: leak, slope at its mark | cubic: leak, slope at its mark |
  |---|---|---|
  | t2 | 2.2e-8, 5.6e-10 | 1.2e-6, 1.8e-7 |
  | m3b | 4.1e-8, 8.6e-11 | 2.4e-6, 1.2e-6 |
  | x2 | 1.2e-7, 4.0e-8 | 4.6e-6, 1.8e-7 |
  | w2 | 4.5e-4, 1.1e-4 | 8.4e-3, 1.8e-3 |

  The quintic's rate of change peaks at 1.875·Δ/L, which is 0.625 × ROUTE_BAR at the turn's and climb's minimum.
- **Knot gap:** an inserted knot keeps 5 m (KNOT_M/4) from the piece's own knots and from the knot before it.
  - Why: a span of millimetres beside a 20 m knot made the cubic's third derivative jump.
  - checked by `<scratch>/l139/wscan.js <tree>`: a width change at its minimum read 1.22 × ROUTE_BAR at 1.06 × the minimum and 0.84 at 1.02 ×.
  - With the 5 m gap, every case at 1.04 × or more is ≤ 0.903 (the gap-2 and gap-5 trees, `<scratch>/l139/g2`, `g5`, built by the inline patch in this lap's transcript).
- **Corner lists** (`turnby.js` `cornerLists`, `solveList`, `extendList`):
  - 8 knots are inserted across each corner's segment;
  - each corner is the symmetric ease of its own segment, so the rate is 0 at both of its marks;
  - the heights are solved together so the angle up to each mark is exact: one row per mark plus the end (`weightsTo`, the adapter's 2 m trapezoid cut at the mark);
  - then the least-norm correction δ = Wᵀ(WWᵀ)⁻¹(−r) on the free columns.
  - Refused by name (TURN_TOO_SHORT / CLIMB_TOO_SHORT):
    - a later corner whose segment is under `cornerMinM`, the shortest corner from a straight (21 m on a bowl);
    - a first corner that `shortestTurnBy` says the head cannot make before mark 1.
