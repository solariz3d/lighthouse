## 1. Hearing, then moving slowly to the sound, then seeing

**One state machine per lesser chaser** (the hunter excluded: it always knows and always comes, item 65):

    unaware ──hears──▶ alerted ──(0.4-0.8 s)──▶ investigating ──sees──▶ SCARE (early) or COMMIT (late)
       ▲                                │                               │
       └──────── loses interest ◀───────┘ (reaches the sound, nothing) ◀┘ (scare done, or loses sight)

**Hearing, along the walk graph** (C's D654 step 3, deterministic):
- **A noise is an event** at the player's cell: footsteps by gait, landings, a slide, a vault, a Park door.
- **Its loudness is a range in metres of walk-graph path.** Proposed:

  | gait or act | range |
  |---|---|
  | walk | 8 m |
  | run | 14 m |
  | sprint | 22 m |
  | slide | 18 m |
  | landing from a drop | 26 m |

- **One bounded search** from the player's cell per noise event, at most every 0.5 s, in a fixed order, serves every lesser chaser.
  - A chaser hears it if its path is within range × its kind's `hearing` multiple.
  - A wall's way-through counts only for thin partitions (C).
- **They hear WHERE, not WHO.** A heard noise stores the *point it came from* (the cell at that tick), never the live player. The
  chaser investigates that point, so a player who has moved on is not tracked.

**Alerted:** the chaser stops and turns toward the sound's direction along the path (its next doorway, not through the wall).
- The pack's heads all turn at once, because their senses are shared (`archetype.cpp`: what one senses they all know).
- 0.4-0.8 s by kind, read from a salted hash so it is the same every replay.

**Investigating, slowly** (his "slowly move toward the sound"):
- It walks the path to the heard point at **an investigate speed**:
  - Toying: 0.35 × its top;
  - Testing: 0.5 ×;
  - Hunting: 0.7 ×.
- A new noise on the way updates the point.
- Reaching the point with nothing seen: it lingers 1-2 s, turning, then loses interest and wanders off the way it came. That is the
  "sound round a corner that never arrives" of the plan, made by real behaviour, not staged.
- **The audible tell:** its footsteps travel back to him along the same graph (C's propagation). He hears it coming, or stopping, or
  turning back.

**Seeing:** the existing senses (`sight`, `sight_fov_deg`, line of sight).
- On sight it does not chase at once: it **decides, scare or commit** (§3).
- **The fairness rule:** inside **3 m** it always commits, in every phase. Walking into one is always dangerous.

## 2. The scare moves, each kind keeping its role

Each move is a short scripted intent on top of the kind's own steering. It is the same steering, given a target and a stop rule, so
it obeys the speed model, corners and all.

| kind (its role, `archetype.h`) | scare moves (Toying and Testing) | its commit (Hunting, or a commit roll) |
|---|---|---|
| **sprinter** (commits hard; fast on a straight; poor at corners) | **the lunge that stops:** a full-speed charge down the corridor that brakes 6-10 m short of him, holds a beat, then turns off through the nearest side opening. **Seen at the end:** standing at the far end of a long straight (B: half his corridors run 100 m or more), there for a second, gone on his next look | the hard straight commit it has today |
| **interceptor** (aims ahead; tightest turn) | **already there:** stands at the junction ahead, its turned head toward where he is going (chaser sheet §3), and as he nears it **steps aside into a doorway**: a near pass that does not commit. Its "set" pose shows, and it does not fire | the cut-off it has today |
| **pack** (three; shared senses; one follows, two go ahead) | **the shared look:** three at the far end, all heads turning at once when one sees him. **The followers:** they follow at a fixed 15-25 m, matching his speed, **stopping when he stops**, starting when he starts. They never close | flankers take places ahead, as today (the catch of A's run) |
| **charger** (named, not built) | **the wind-up that does not fire:** it drops and its shoulders rise, then it lets him pass | the lunge |
| **crawler** | not in the mix (the director's own rule) | — |

**The Park** (his name; erosion reads as the Park dissolving, items 54-56, 95):
- Lesser chasers never enter it.
- While toying, one may **wait at the Park's far mouth** and step back into the dark as he comes out. That is a scare at the exit,
  never a catch inside the Park's last second.
- From Testing on they do not wait there: the Park keeps its promise of a breather.

**Placement, which is what makes any of this happen** (A's finding: 63 sent, most never found him):
- The director places lesser chasers **ahead** of him, along his likely route, at 40-80 m of path, **in the openings off the
  corridor**: side rooms, doorways, the 2 % of cells that are rooms (B), where a noise reaches them as he passes. Not dropped behind or
  beside him.
- Fewer, so each one is a scene: one every 25-35 s while toying, 15-20 s while testing, 8-12 s while hunting.
- The long straight corridors are the stage: a sprinter seen at the far end is the corridor's own scare.

## 3. Escalation by run phase (the evil god playing with its food, items 30 and 92)

**The commit roll.** On sight (outside 3 m), the chaser commits with a chance, else it plays a scare move:

| phase | from | commit chance on sight | investigate speed | placement interval | what it feels like |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Toying** | 0 s | **0.05** | 0.35 × top | 25-35 s | seen, heard, followed; almost never chased |
| **Testing** | 150 s | **0.35** | 0.5 × | 15-20 s | some commit; a scare may turn real |
| **Hunting** | 360 s | **0.8** | 0.7 × | 8-12 s | they mean it now |
| **Overtime** | the timer | 1.0, and the lesser ones clear out | — | — | the evil god itself (items 28, 66): the lesser chasers step back for it |

- **Stress shifts the roll** (items 28, 32): the commit chance + 0.3 × stress (0-1), capped at 1. A frightened player is fed on sooner:
  "they fixate on fear and instability".
- **Recognition** (item 60) holds the roll at the Toying value for as long as it lasts. They cannot touch the signal.
- **Difficulty:** brutal scales the phase boundaries by 0.7 (Testing at 105 s, Hunting at 252 s). The rates are unchanged.
- **Every random choice is a salted hash** of (seed, chaser id, the tick bucket): the commit roll, the scare move picked, the alert
  delay. So a replay makes the same choices, and R8 holds.

## 4. The hunter: unchanged, and why that matters here

- It is "always coming" (item 92), always knows (item 65), and is 2-7 s behind all run (A).
- It does not toy and does not hear; it does not take part in this arc.
- **That contrast is the design:** the lesser ones play, the one behind does not. The player learns that the thing at the end of the
  corridor may be a game, and the thing behind never is.
- R6 checks that this design moves it by under 10 %.
