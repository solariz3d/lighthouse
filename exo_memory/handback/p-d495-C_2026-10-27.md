## 4. What changed in arcs.js from D490, and why (each was found by a test or a render, and judged by a render)

1. **(i) The mouth.**
   - The ray tops fade out softly over 160–360 km above the hem. They had a hard end at 260 km in a sheet and 340 km in the volume.
   - The south curtain moved to 25 km south: at 15 km its bright hem fold passed within a few degrees of the magnetic zenith.
   - The curtains' light fades within 0.04–0.12 rad of the magnetic zenith.
   - A sheet seen edge-on brightens at most 4.5× (it was 12.5×, which drew hard bright ribbons overhead).
2. **(ii) The banding.** Each pixel starts its volume steps at its own offset (interleaved gradient noise). A curl shorter than one step is left out of the fold (an LOD on step length), since that detail was what banded.
3. **D403's 15% a second** (`_evidence/pulseprobe.js`, `pulseprobe-final.out`: row 4's own measure, with all motion but one part held still).
   - On the first build a column changed by 98% in a second, at full-contrast running rays. With those fixed it was still 25%, and the probe named the parts:
     - the big folds alone, 35%: a curtain seen along its arc is seen edge-on, and there a slow drift moves its bright fold lines a long way;
     - the curls, 21%;
     - the rays, 15%.
   - **The fixes:**
     - big folds 5× slower, curls 3× slower, rays 2× slower;
     - the running shimmer at ±3.5%;
     - the 9 s patch now 8% over 12 s;
     - the highlights pressed softly, `L → 1.25 L / (1 + 0.8 L)`, which keeps the hem's usual level and brings edge-on lines under twice it.
   - Now: **12.5% as it is, 11.4% for the fallback.** No single moving part is over 7.1%, and with all motion still it is 0.0% (a check on the probe itself).
4. **The zenith glow** added for (i) was taken out again (§6).
