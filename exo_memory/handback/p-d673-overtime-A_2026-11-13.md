Why smallest: a gentler dark one that still meets "certain but skill lasts longer" leaves the room to raise it, and the other way round leaves none.

**Abuse condition (named in advance):** "it is close enough after the fact" is the degenerating move. A cell that misses a band by a second is a cell that misses it.
