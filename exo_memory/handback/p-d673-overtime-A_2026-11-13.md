The pick: **among cells that meet F1, F2 and F3 on the tuning block, the one with the smallest `dark_speed_start`, then the smallest `dark_speed_rise`.** The pick is then run once on the held-out block (seeds 21 to 40) and F1 to F3 are read there; **a failure on the held-out block is reported as a failure, and no second grid is run on it.** If no cell meets all three on the tuning block, that is reported (with the table) and the defaults stay; a different grid is a new registration. Why smallest: a gentler dark one that still meets "certain but skill lasts longer" leaves the room to raise it, and the other way round leaves none.

**Abuse condition (named in advance):** "it is close enough after the fact" is the degenerating move. A cell that misses a band by a second is a cell that misses it.
