## What it proposes

- **The rule:**
  - a claim about state is **checked** (its check and result shown beside it) or **inferred** (everything else,
    including conclusions);
  - both are allowed;
  - no check beside a claim means it reads as inferred;
  - a seat that ACTS on an inferred claim checks it first.
- **Cited:** the keeper, 02:02, verbatim (from `loop/plan_claim_recognition_2026-09-27.md:150`), and the score
  `c127075` (unchecked 8/163 = 0.049; checked 1/72).
- **Placement:**
  - **Item 7** under WHAT A HAND-BACK OWES in `consonance/src-tauri/brief/BUILDING.md`. Inserted after item 6, before the
    section's closing `---` at `:480`. The text is given verbatim in a fenced block.
  - **A one-line pointer, given verbatim,** for replies to the keeper. It goes in COMMITTEE.md "What a hand-back should
    contain" (`:98`, after the map bullet) for panes, and in LIBRARIAN.md "Talking to the other seats" (`:220`) for the
    librarian.
  - **Why two pointers:** BUILDING.md is seated only in the chair's shell (`main.rs:6759`).
- **How to mark:**
  - `checked: <command or path:line> → <result>` against `inferred: <claim>`.
  - A printed command-plus-result block already satisfies it.
  - The new habit is writing `inferred:` where a claim would otherwise sound checked.
  - Real examples: CHECKED `handback/p-l114-flags-E_2026-09-23.md:121`; INFERRED `handback/p-d118-outside-B_2026-09-22.md:171`.
- **Not covered:**
  - future, intent, plans, opinions and questions (routed out as in the base-rate registration §3);
  - the correctness of a check (1 of 72 CHECKED claims was still wrong);
  - claims inside commit messages (W123).
