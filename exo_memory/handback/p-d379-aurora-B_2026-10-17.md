## The design: night under the northern lights

- **Behind the track:** green and violet curtains of light high up, an indigo night down to the horizon, a few stars. They **drift** (`aurora-drift`, 26 s, ease-in-out). The curtain in the left panel's top **breathes** (9 s). Both stop under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
- **The top bar:** night, with the light along its lower edge, a green and a violet glow, and the title glowing.
- **Buttons:** night-glass pills (10 px radius) lit at the edge; hover glows green. An open menu is a green-to-violet glass pill. **Export to Assetto Corsa** is filled with the curtain itself, green through teal to violet, in dark text.
- **Glass:** the left panel, the dock, the menus, the guide card and the values overlay are deep indigo glass with violet rims.
- **Colour:** headings in aurora green with a green-to-violet rule.
- **Warning hues:** SIGIL's own on a surface as dark as SIGIL's; only `--bad` is lifted (#ef6a52) to stay clear on indigo.
- **Fonts:** the page's own; no metric property changed anywhere.
- **The accent is green (#6ff5c0), and it is close to `--ok` (#8fc9a2).** A raised this in D378. Here the violet carries the "open/chosen" state (open menu, chosen option), so green is not the only signal.
- **The 3D night is the same screen-blend stand-in as Skyline's** (`#preview::after`, under the overlay canvases). It is darker, so WebGL's own colours wash less: the orange marker stays orange in the shots. Part 2's token reader replaces it.
