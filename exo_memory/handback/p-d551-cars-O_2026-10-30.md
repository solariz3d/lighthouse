## Not read, and why

- **The load at which each suspension runs out, as a number,** for any car but the measured C00 (25 g, D515) and C23 (about 50 g, D544).
  - The coilover's rest length is not in the data in a form I can follow: a free length of MAX_LENGTH predicts about 47 cm of travel, where C00 measured about 26 cm.
  - So group C's "earlier" is a direction from MIN_LENGTH, not a figure.
- **The wishbone cars' (C01, C02, C17) stop and chassis clearance.** Their model is a different one.
  - inferred, from their own numbers (wheel rate × travel): C01 and C02 carry about 2,600–2,770 N per wheel on an 8,000 N/m wheel rate, about 0.33–0.35 m of sag, against 0.02 / 0.06 m to the bump stop. So they likely sit on their bump stops from rest.
  - C17: about 2,085 N per wheel at 40,000 N/m, 5.2 cm of sag, against a 4 cm packer range.
  - Not certified: rod length and packers enter AC's solver in ways I did not reproduce.
- **`collider.kn5`** (the wall/car collision mesh, not the ground box) has the same bounding box in the 25 T-180-bodied cars: lowest point 0.485 m above the origin, length −2.712…3.236 m. In C01 and C02 it differs: lowest 0.111 / 0.018 m, length −2.577…2.738 / −2.25…2.197. The ground box the profile's clearance rests on is `colliders.ini`, which is the same in all 27.
