## §2 The parts, by owner (before anyone cuts)

### B page: the Extend readout on every brush step (rows 2, 3)

**What happens:** `panel.js:737`, in `draw`, calls `readout()` on every new document, and a Sculpt brush step is a new document.
- `readout()` (`panel.js:264`) calls `shell.candidateReadout(opts())` and `shell.candidateCorner(opts())`. Each one, in `readout.js:216-224`, does two things:
  - builds a whole new document with `extendCorner` (`checkDoc` over every piece: `document.js:452`, 1.8 ms a tail frame);
  - reads the ghost piece, posing it through every earlier piece.
- The readout skips a closed loop (`panel.js:267`). Bend on the closed 10 km lap pays it anyway: the shell opens the lap for the drag. Row 4a's Grab keeps the tube closed and pays nothing.
