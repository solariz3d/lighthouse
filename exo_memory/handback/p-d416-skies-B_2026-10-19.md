## 1. What changed

- **Aurora, Skyline Night, Aqua** (`app/preview/sky/aurora.js`, `city.js`, `caustics.js`):
  - Each star (Aqua's motes) was a gaussian sized in grid cells. It is now a cone 1.25 device px in radius, sized from the pass's uPx.
  - Each star is drawn at its cell's own projected point and kept only if it lies in the middle of its cell, so no star is cut at a cell edge.
  - Thresholds, colours, curtains, glow and twinkle are unchanged.
- **The view's foreshortening** (all five night styles, prism included):
  - A flat screen spreads an angle over 1/cos² as many pixels outward from its middle, and 1/cos across. A star sized by uPx alone grew to about 2.4 px at a 2560×1440 corner; the probe in §3 found it.
  - Each style's `X_px()` is now `uPx * pow(dot(vRay, vRay), -0.75)`, which is cos^1.5. The spot then covers the same area of screen everywhere: about 1.6 × 1.0 px at a corner, never under a pixel.
  - `vRay` is the pass's existing varying, now named in the contract in `sky/index.js`.
- **Strength:** a point carries less light than a soft spot, so the crisp stars read fainter in the 1:1 crops. They are now 1.8× (Aurora), 2.6× (Skyline Night) and 6× (Aqua) as strong. The measured light per star is in §2.
- **Glass's new sky, `app/preview/sky/river.js`** (a look; the keeper judges it, and it is not a test gate):
  - Two fields of one-pixel stars, each with a slight warm or cold tint:
    - a sparse open field (grid 180, chance 0.035);
    - a crowd (grid 300) whose chance follows a "river": a great circle arching to 55.7° up, about 30° wide, clumped by noise along its length and cut by dark lanes.
  - A faint glow lies under the river.
  - Nothing is drawn under 12° (`up = smoothstep(0.21, 0.42, h)`), so the band behind the work is only the gradient. Nothing moves; the twinkle is at most 15% and stays in place.
  - It is unlike Aurora (curtains) and Skyline Night (horizon city glow): all of its light is points.
  - `app/themes/glass.css` names `river`. `prism.js` stays a built style that no theme names.
- **CHANGELOG:**
  - The D410 entry "Glass's night sky is crisp" is replaced by one D416 entry, since E puts D416 in place of D410. The prism changes from D410 still exist, but no theme shows them now.
  - The entry's first draft said "the stars are as bright as before or brighter" before anything had measured it. Corrected before the commit to the measured "about the light of the soft spot it replaces (in Aqua, about two thirds)".
- **The sizing rule's scope, stated plainly:** only points (stars, motes) and prism's moon limb are sized in device pixels. Aurora's curtains, Skyline Night's city glow, Aqua's Snell window and rays, and the river's glow stay soft, because softness is part of each look. They are not edges in the packet's sense.
