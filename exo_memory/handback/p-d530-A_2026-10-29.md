## 2. What it does

**Shell** (`coreshell.js`):
- `proposeBankSwing({ peakDeg, peakAt = 0.5, leadIn = 80, runOut = 80, exitDeg = null })`, `applyBankSwing()`, `cancelBankSwing()`, and the state `bankProposal`.
- **The stretch** is the selected run, with lead-in metres before it and run-out after it. On an open track the stretch stops at the track's ends; on a closed lap it is passed through for the core to wrap.
- **Peak at** is a share of the selected run (see §6).
- **Shape:** always `'rounded'`, because the Extend bank setting is fixed at 'rounded' today (`panel.js:81`).
- **The roll read:** `rollOn(d, s0, s1)`, from validate's own `rollRates` with its 20 m chord, the same call close.js and finish.js make. It returns the worst rate on the road over [s0, s1]; on a closed lap it wraps.
- **The message:** `bank the bend preview: 30° at 550 m, over 260 m of road (420 to 680 m); peak roll 0.42 °/m; amber above 0.93, red above 1.21. Apply or Cancel`. Past amber or red it adds "(AMBER: a longer lead-in or run-out rolls it slower)".
- **Apply:** `backupNow('pre-swing')`, then one `D.commit`, as applyCut does. A backup that fails is refused by name, with nothing changed.
- **The preview drops** on any change of the track (the same rule as cutProposal in `set`), on a new selection or clearSelection, and on Cancel.
- **A core refusal** sets the message to the core's own `e.message`.

**Page:**
- `preview.js`'s proposal ghost now also takes `bankProposal`, beside the close and delete previews.
- `panel.js` adds a "Bank the bend ▾" fold to the Sculpt strip, beside the tool picker and "more ▾" (Twist is an entry in that picker). It holds:
  - the fields **peak bank °, peak at, lead-in m, run-out m, exit bank °**, all blank or default;
  - the buttons **Preview, Apply, Cancel**. Apply and Cancel are off unless a preview stands for the track on screen.

## 6. Field and default notes for a player

- **"Peak at" is a share of the selected run, not of the whole stretch.** The run is what the player clicked as "the bend", so 0.5 is the middle of the bend whatever the lead-in and run-out. With the defaults (80/80, 0.5) both readings give the same point.
- **The plan's "piece under the pointer with nothing selected" is not built.** With nothing selected, the message says "select the bend first: click a piece on the track (shift-click for a run)". A single click is a run of one, so the common case is one click away.
- **inferred: the 80 m defaults look right for the bank a player is likely to want.** On a 100 m bend with 80/80, 30° reads 0.42 °/m and 45° reads 0.62 °/m, both under amber (checked: the shell's message on 20/30/45°, a node run; 20° read 0.28). So amber would need roughly 70° on a short bend. The plan's falsifier (the default swing reads amber at the bank the user wants) can only be scored on his own bend.
- **After Apply the selection is dropped**, as after a cut, because the selection belongs to the old document. To re-tune, click the bend again; the fields keep what was typed.
