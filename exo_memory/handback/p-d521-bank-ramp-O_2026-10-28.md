## 2. What changed, and why it keeps everything else

- **`extend.js`:**
  - `opts.bankShape: 'rounded'` (any other value is refused, BAD_SHAPE) blends the bank, and only the bank, by `roundedLinear(u, ℓ/L)`. The rate eases in on the Bloss cubic over ℓ = min(10 m, a quarter of the ramp), holds Δ/(L − ℓ), and eases out the same way.
  - With knots every 20 m, the bank's first span is one cubic, which a C2 joint makes a·s³: only 1.41° at 10 m. So knots are added at ℓ/2 and ℓ in from each end.
  - They are **inserted** (`document.js insertKnots`, Boehm) into the piece laid exactly as today. Every other channel keeps its curve, and only the bank is refitted and pinned C2 on them.
  - checked, on a plain rate turn with a cup and a width beside the rounded bank: `kh` within 2.8e-10, cup within 2.9e-7 (their stored step), `w` and `kv` within 1e-14 or exactly. On his corner piece `kh` differs by up to 2.9e-5 rad/m, because the corner's solver re-converges; the heading is exactly 11.70° either way.
- **Without the option nothing changes.** Close, Finish (whose roll sizing assumes the quintic), smoothramps and every internal caller keep today's bytes. Pinned in row 4: his piece and a plain bank ramp at 9069bd4's sha.
- **`panel.js extendOptions`** asks for the shape whenever a bank is typed: Extend, and through it Level, To floor, corner turns and Sharp.
- **No version flag or opt-in for this exists in the document format, and I added none.** `bankShape` is an Extend option only. The shape is stored the way every ramp is, as the piece's control points.
