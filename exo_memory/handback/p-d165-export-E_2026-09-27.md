## 2 · THE CHOICES AND WHERE THEY COME FROM

**The soft-collision block** (`trackfiles.js`, `SOFT_COLLISION_BLOCK`):
- **Keys and values from `docs/FINDINGS.md` §4c, lines 80-81:** `MESHES=1ROAD?`, `SOFT_ERP=0.8`, `SOFT_CFM=0.0002`,
  `BOUNCE=0.1`, `MAX_DEPTH=4`, `FRICTION=0.05`.
- **The section header `[COLLISION_PARAMS_...]`** is CSP's syntax, as all three reference files I read use it
  (centrifuge, sakura_speedway, ohyeah2389_t180testtrack). No other author's text is copied.
- **The difference to name:** those reference blocks also set `INTENSITY=1`, `RIGID_WITH_BODIES=0` and
  `RIGID_WITH_BOXES=0`. FINDINGS §4c does not list them, so they are left to CSP's defaults. `inferred:` those are the
  defaults. **If the soft road does not reproduce, test this difference first.**
- **The no-block variant is the same text minus the block** (tested byte-exact: `soft === hard + '\n' + BLOCK`).
- **§6's `WAV_PITCH=extended-0` is NOT written.** It belongs to a surface definition. This track redefines no surfaces:
  `1ROAD` uses AC's system ROAD (`system/data/surfaces.ini` keys: ROAD, GRASS, KERB, SAND).

**map.ini is Content Manager's formula,** from AcTools `AcTools.Render/Kn5SpecificSpecial/TrackMapRenderer.cs`
(github.com/gro-ove/actools, master @ `812f856`):

| lines | what |
|---|---|
| `:762-783` | image `W = (int)(sizeX·Scale + 2·Margin)` |
| `:786-793` | `X_OFFSET = −minX + Margin/Scale`, `Z_OFFSET` likewise, `SCALE_FACTOR = 1/Scale` |
| `:394-400` | the ini keys |
| `:285`, `:289`, `:388` | the defaults: Margin 10, Scale 1, DrawingSize 10 |

The box is the drivable physics meshes. `inferred:` scene X/Z are AC's X/Z. A's writer applies no axis mirror (A §2.2).

**The marker conventions were measured, not assumed.** The command is a `node -e` over `tools/kn5.cjs` on the five
reference kn5s.
- The race direction comes from the grid (back slot → pole), as `tools/read_track.cjs:74-79` does.
- **LEFT = cross(up, race direction):** `dot(L−R, up×fwd) > 0` on **5 of 5** reference tracks (centrifuge 2772.0,
  sktrack 19137.5, ohyeah2389 13200.0, bowltrack 749.2, rainbow 13644.7).
- **The start line is ahead of pole on 5 of 5.**

**The defaults that are choices** (overridable, and named in the file):
- the height band is 1–2 m (§5c);
- "along the road" means |fwd·n| ≤ sin 15° and fwd·race ≥ cos 30°;
- a car slot is ±1.0 m across and ±2.4 m along. `inferred:` the T-180's dimensions were not measured.

**The two variants are two FOLDERS, not layouts.** The reason:
- The prediction needs the block to be READ.
- All the working T-180 tracks keep it in the root `data/surfaces.ini`.
- Whether CSP reads `[COLLISION_PARAMS_...]` from a layout's own `data/surfaces.ini` is unverified. A null result could
  then not be scored.
- The two folders carry the **identical kn5** (checked by sha256 in the build).

The folders:
- `t180b_platform_test`: WITH the block;
- `t180b_platform_test_noblock`: WITHOUT it.

**`ui_track.json`:**
- `pitboxes` is counted from the `AC_PIT_n` markers. The hand-typed `desc.pitboxes` is ignored, and a test holds it.
- `run` is `counterclockwise`, from C's design (the loop turns left only, `p-d165-geometry-C` §1).
