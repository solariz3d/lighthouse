## 2. The FBX step: `src/export/blender_fbx.py`

- **What it does:** it imports the glb and writes a binary FBX. It prints `T180FBX ok N` or `T180FBX error …`. Command: `blender.exe --background --factory-startup --python blender_fbx.py -- <in.glb> <out.fbx>` (`blender.js fbxArgs`).
- **The settings, chosen by measuring three candidates and reading each FBX back with `test/fbx_read.js`:**

  | candidate | UnitScaleFactor | vertices | transforms |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | A: FBX_SCALE_NONE, no bake | 1 | in Blender's frame | group R[−90,0,0] S[100,100,100] |
  | B: bake_space_transform, FBX_SCALE_ALL | 100 | **equal to the kn5's** | identity |
  | C: bake, FBX_SCALE_UNITS | 100 | equal | groups scaled 100 |

  **B is shipped**: `axis_up 'Y'`, `axis_forward '-Z'`, `bake_space_transform`, `apply_unit_scale`, `apply_scale_options 'FBX_SCALE_ALL'`, mesh and empties only, no animation and no leaf bones, textures embedded.
- **No official ksEditor preset exists** (searched). These are the sources I used:
  - the community thread on a first track spawning in the air: https://assettocorsamods.net/threads/first-track-spawning-in-the-air.972/latest (scale and apply-transform trouble);
  - Blender's FBX exporter manual: https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/4.5/addons/import_export/scene_fbx.html
- **`blender.js` README:** it now names these settings in the Blender UI's words, for someone doing the FBX by hand.

## 7. A real fault this caught in Part 1's glTF writer

`gltf.js` ran in node, but **in the page** (the node shim, `app/export/node-shim.js`) it would have failed or written wrong bytes. Three shim gaps:

- `Buffer#write(string)` does not exist there (`head.write is not a function`, red in run 5).
- `Buffer.alloc(n, 0x20)` ignores the fill, so the JSON padding would be zeros.
- **`Buffer.from(arrayBuffer, offset, length)` ignores offset and length and copies the WHOLE buffer.** Every view of a shared buffer would have been wrong, silently.

All three are rewritten to calls that node and the shim agree on. Row 1 of `app/test/blenderexport.test.js` builds the same export through the shim and through node:

    checked: same JSON (views aside), every accessor byte for byte (61 of 61), the PNGs decode to the same pixels.
    The PNG bytes themselves differ, 136 vs 75: the shim's zlib stores, node's compresses (measured with a one-off compare).
