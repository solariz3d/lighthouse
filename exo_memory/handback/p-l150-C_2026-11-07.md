## 3. Three ways

### Way 1: start the extra bank before the extra turn (panel only)
- **Change:** the extra bank's default `from` becomes the extra turn's mark minus a lead, instead of 0.5·L (`panel.js:1305` and `changeFroms` `:93`).
  - The step then begins while the first ramp is still in its first half, so it eats into the first ramp from earlier.
  - The lead has to be a share or a fixed length: the mark is fixed at the drag's first value (`:1327-1328`), before Δ is known.
  - Today he can already slide the bank's mark earlier by hand (`secPending`, `:1306`). Way 1 only changes the default.
- **Limit:** the ramp still runs to the piece's end (`extend.js:709`), so a longer span makes it SLOWER, not earlier-finishing.
  - With from = 380 its middle is at 690 m, 60 m before the peak; at 750 it is about 65% done (inferred, by hand).
  - It moves the lag; it does not remove it.
- **Bytes that move:**
  - only new Extends that make an extra bank by its handle;
  - saves: none (checked: both his saves hold laid `knots`/`channels` and no options — `pieces[i]` keys `id,type,length,family,knots,channels`, no `changes`/`turnDeg` in either file — so loading never re-lays a piece);
  - pieces with no extra: none (the core is untouched).
- **Additive rule:** holds as today, because the core is unchanged.
- **K = 31.5 / tight probe:**
  - the span is longer, so `own` = 31.5·√Δ is easier to meet;
  - the probe still models the change at the end (`probePeak`), which is where it still is.
- **Size:** about 5–15 lines in `panel.js` and one panel test row.

### Way 2: the extra bank gets its own ramp length, centred on the extra turn's rise (core + panel) — the one I'd draw first
- **Change:**
  - a bank change carries `to` as well as `from` (`{ from, to, target }`): the step is laid over [from, to] and held after it;
  - the panel puts the ramp's middle near where the extra turn's curvature is half up, with `to` at or before the curvature's peak;
  - `from = to − the ramp's length`, which reaches back before the turn's mark into the first ramp's span. That is "eating into the original banking": the extra rolls earlier and finishes sooner.
  - Example (inferred): a 69.6° change, if that is his (B has the values), needs at least 31.5·√69.6 = 263 m, so from ≈ 487 and to ≈ 750 on his piece.
- **Core touch points:**
  - `changeLists` takes and checks `to` (from < to ≤ next mark or end); the span checked becomes `to − from`;
  - the plan's `channelFn` gets `L2 = to − F` (`:709`, `:713`). `g` already holds a step once made, because `u ≥ 1` returns the target (`:116`).
  - `secondRamp` needs no change, but its inserted knots (`turns`) go across [from, to].
  - Doesn't fit today: a list of two changes (ramp, then an equal one to hold) is cut back to one by the trailing-equal drop (`:531`), so this can't be faked from the panel.
- **Bytes that move:** only pieces made with `to`. Without `to`, every byte is today's (the core's old path). Saves: none (as above). Pieces with no extra: none.
- **Additive rule:** holds.
  - An extra of 0 is still dropped at `:531`.
  - Short of the drop, a step of 0 adds a fitted 0, so dragging through 0 is continuous.
  - Not checked: whether the knots inserted across [from, to] re-quantise the other channels the same way as today's (today's knots are inserted across [from, end] (`extend.js:715`)).
- **K = 31.5 / tight probe — the real cost:**
  - the span is shorter than today's (it ends at `to`, not the end), so the minimum binds harder, and the panel has to put `from` far enough back to meet it;
  - `probePeak` (`:446-450`) lays the change at the piece's END, then a straight. A change that ends mid-piece, with the first ramp still rolling after it, is NOT what the probe builds;
  - so `spanHolds`/`gridMin` need a `to` (the change laid at [from, to] on the probe piece, read 20 m either side of both ends), and the probe memo key gains it.
  - Without that, the tight minimum would be read off the wrong road. It is the D642 R2 class of error (the panel asked for something other than what the core lays).
- **Size:**
  - core ~40–70 lines across `changeLists`, the plan, `spanHolds`/`probePeak`/`memoKey`;
  - panel ~20 (the default `from`/`to` from the extra turn's mark, the mark drawn at `from`);
  - tests: the additive rows with `to`, a minimum row on the probe, his S case.

### Way 3: the extra REPLACES the rest of the first ramp — a literal eat (core)
- **Change:** over [F − λ, end] the bank becomes `(1 − S)·first(s) + S·T`, extend's own blend with the first lay as the "continuation" (`channelFn` `:116`), instead of `first(s) + S·(T − prev)`.
  - Laid additively, the added curve is `S·(T − first(s))`.
  - The first ramp's remaining roll is faded out, not kept.
  - This is the closest reading of his words.
- **Bytes that move:** every piece with an extra bank whose first ramp is still moving at the mark, i.e. every whole-piece first bank. Saves: none (as above). Pieces with no extra: none.
- **Additive rule:** BROKEN as a limit, kept only at exactly 0.
  - At T = the first target the change is dropped (`:531`), so those bytes are equal.
  - At T = first target + ε the road is `(1 − S)·first + S·(first target + ε)`, which pulls the bank to the first target early. A drag across 0 jumps.
  - That is the shape of his 11-05 19:54Z complaint, "even turnin it to 0 … takes the original shape away", which D642 fixed by making extras add (`extend.js:16`, `turnby.js:174-179`).
  - So this way goes against a ruling of his. I list it because it is what his words literally say, and because the page should show him the jump if it is drawn.
- **K = 31.5 / tight probe:**
  - the change is no longer a fixed step: its size is `T − first(s)`, which varies along the span;
  - `minLaw`'s Δ, `ceilOf`'s share and the probe (which lays an additive step from 0, `:446`) all model the wrong curve and would need re-deriving.
- **Size:** the largest. Core ~60–100 lines plus a new minimum, a new probe and new additive rows. It also re-opens D642.
