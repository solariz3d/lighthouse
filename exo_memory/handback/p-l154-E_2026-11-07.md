## 5. Would a new user understand the label? (plain-English read)

- **"Extra bank · 380.00 m → end"**: yes. A second bank change that runs from 380 m to the end of the piece.
- **"rolls over 620.00 m"**: mostly. "Rolls" is the right word for a bank, but "rolls over" can read as "flips over". **"banks over 620.00 m"** or **"changes bank over 620.00 m"** would leave no doubt. The turn's "turns over 500.00 m" reads fine.
- **"drag to start earlier or later"**: it does not say WHAT to drag. Beside the tick it is guessable. **"drag this mark to start earlier or later"** says it.
- **The hover, "drag ← → along the road: move where the extra bank starts · now 500.00 m"**: this is the line that answers his complaint directly ("move the mouse right and left … drags the other point"). It is clear.
- **Minor:** the hover's first line is lower case ("extra bank 0.00°") and the label is capitalised ("Extra bank"). Width and cup say "changes over", which is fine.
- These are wording suggestions for his eye, not rows.
