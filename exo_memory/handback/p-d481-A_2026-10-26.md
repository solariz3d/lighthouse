## 1. The finding

**The lag is `fitWithHold` in `src/core/extend.js:123` (t180), added at commit 90f5809, "D459 (3b, widened): the C2
instrument, the continuity note, the fit that holds its target", first shipped in v0.4.20.** It runs on every pointer move of a
drag whenever **bank, cup or edge is typed** in the Extend fields. It refits every free control point of the whole piece. Its cost grows
about as the cube of the length. With none of those fields typed, the drag costs what it did in 0.4.19 on every length and
every tag.

Why it is cubic, read from the source (checked: `src/core/extend.js:123-141` at v0.4.21):
- `ds = max(0.25, min(0.5, L/400))`, so S ≈ 2·L samples. The free control points are m ≈ L/20 (KNOT_M = 20, `src/core/document.js`). Nearly
  all are free, because the hold length defaults to the piece's length.
- **The main cost, :131:** `B` is a dense m × S table of basis values, each value one `D.channelValue` call on a piece built by `one(i)` (:130).
  Those pieces are not frozen, so `knotVectorOf` (`document.js:74`) misses its WeakMap and copies a fresh (m+7)-long knot array on **every**
  call (`knotVector`, `document.js:70`). That is m·S calls of O(m) each, so cubic in L on its own.
- **The second cost, :134:** the normal equations are a dense O(m²·S) double loop, although each B-spline basis function is non-zero over only 4 spans.
  The comment at :135 says "m is at most a few tens"; m is about 50 at 1 km and 500 at 10 km.
