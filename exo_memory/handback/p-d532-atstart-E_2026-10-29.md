## 2. The plan's formula was wrong in two places, and the rows that show it

1. **`|Δ| / 0.9338` alone does not keep the road under the bar.** This is the plan's falsifier firing in advance.
   - Extend lays a short ramp's change inside about 0.75 of it (rampControl compresses the ideal ramp to `R − 2R/sub`), and rounds it. So the laid roll peaks near **2·|Δ|/R**, not |Δ|/R.
   - The checker's roll on a 1,000 m piece after a straight (`S/d532/probe.js`):

     | bank | R = 20 m | R = 25 m | R = 30 m | R = 40 m | R = 60 m |
     |---|---|---|---|---|---|
     | 20° | 1.000 | 0.998 | 0.977 | 0.856 | 0.605 °/m |

   - The plan's 21.4 m for 20° therefore rolls about 0.998, above the 0.9338 amber.
   - **K, the factor on |Δ|/rate, is measured** (`S/d532/probek.js`). Banks of 8, 12, 20, 30, 54, 90 and 180° at start give these worst rolls:

     | K | worst roll |
     |---|---|
     | 1.9 | 1.010 °/m (90°) |
     | 2.0 | 0.965 |
     | 2.1 | 0.924 |
     | **2.2** | **0.886, all under the bar** |

     I chose 2.2. At 2.2, 20° ramps over 47.2 m and 54° over 127.3 m.
   - **The plan's falsifier wording cannot hold together with its own red row.** "Still reads bank 20 within the first 25 m" needs a ramp of 25 m or less, and no ramp that short stays under the amber (20° at 30 m reads 0.977). I kept the roll bar and dropped the 25 m. If the user prefers the earlier arrival and a little twist, the knob is one constant (START_SPREAD), or the floor.
2. **The clamp `clamp(…, 20, length/2)` makes short pieces WORSE.** On a 30 m piece, 20° would go from today's 20 m to 15 m, a faster twist.
   - The cap is now `max(min(20, length), length/2)`, so a piece under 40 m keeps exactly the ramp it had.
   - The rows: 30 m piece → 20 m; 12 m piece → 12 m; 54° on a 100 m piece → 50 m (half held).
