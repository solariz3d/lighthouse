## 0 · The answer first

- **A run of one class is mostly ONE word** (median 1 word per run in every class). The classes change as a corner
  opens and closes, so a same-class run is a PART of a corner, not a whole one.
- **So there is a third aggregate, CORNERS:** consecutive curved words (sweep, turn, tight) turning the same way,
  keyed by the tightest class reached. **This is what a user's "turn" piece should span:**

| a corner reaching | N | words | length m | heading ° |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| sweep | 257 | 1 (1–1) | 24 (12–78) | 1.2 (0.6–4.3) |
| turn | 116 | 3 (1–6.5) | 100 (24–482) | 12 (3–62) |
| **tight** | **383** | **5 (2–10)** | **212 (64–618)** | **88 (32–256)** |

- **Against one word:** a tight word is 48 m and 30°, a whole tight corner 212 m and 88°.
- **The grammar, measured (P):**
  - straight → sweep 0.78;
  - sweep → straight 0.49 or turn 0.44;
  - turn → tight 0.46 or sweep 0.43;
  - tight → turn 0.76.
  - Straight → tight is 0.03: FINDINGS §7's "never straight into tight", as a number.
  - Every jump is followed by a straight (19/19): the landing ramp.

**The full run table, the corner table and the 7×7 transition table** (with each row's N) are in FINDINGS §7f
("RUNS, CORNERS AND TRANSITIONS") and in `corpus.json` (`runs`, `corners`, `transitions`).

## 1 · How a run is delimited, and why

**Exactly the same class and the same turn direction. No tolerance for a one-token interruption.**
- **No tolerance, because** the reader already folds any run under 12 m into its neighbour. A second tolerance would
  absorb real short pieces and make a run depend on a tuning parameter.
- **The same direction, because** a builder piece turns one way: a left into a right is two pieces (an S).
- **This covers wall-rides and inversions too:** the reader names them by the turn under them (e.g.
  `bowl-turnL^wall`), which is why wall-ride → wall-ride (0.55) and inversion → inversion (0.70) are real direction
  reversals. Only a straight has no direction, and cannot follow itself.
- **A skipped (shared-road) word breaks a run,** and no transition is counted across it. The lap is not wrapped from
  its end to its start.

**Corners:** consecutive sweep/turn/tight words turning the same way, whatever the class. A straight, a wall-ride, an
inversion, a jump, a direction change or a shared word ends one.

**Corrected before this ring:** my first write-up said runs split by direction "for sweep, turn and tight". The table's
wall-ride and inversion diagonals showed they split too, and the text, the tool's comment and its method string now
say so. **The numbers did not change;** checked field by field against the run before the fix.
