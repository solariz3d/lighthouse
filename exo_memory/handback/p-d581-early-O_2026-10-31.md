### How it works (`app/hovertip.js`)

- **One listener on the document,** so every element with a `title` gets the card: those in `index.html`, those any module sets, and any added later. No element is wired one by one.
- **The title stays the source.**
  - While the pointer is over an element, its `title` moves to `data-tip`, so Windows has nothing to draw; on leave it moves back.
  - A title set while hovered (the turn box's hint) is moved aside again, and the shown card takes the new words (a MutationObserver on that one element).
  - **So no code or test that reads or sets a `title` had to change.**
- **Shown** after 400 ms (`DELAY_MS`), below-right of the pointer. It turns to the other side near the window's edges and is clamped wholly inside the window, 8 px from each edge.
- **Hidden** on leaving the element, any key, a press (a drag starts with one), a wheel, a scroll, or the window losing focus. It does not come back until the pointer enters an element again.
- **Never takes focus:** `pointer-events: none`, no tabindex. `role="tooltip"`, and NOT `aria-hidden`: while hovered, the element's title is aside, so these are its words.
- **The look:**
  - The card carries the `more-menu` class, so every look's existing menu-panel rule draws it (surface, rim, radius, inset light, and the blur that look gives its menus, per D547 Part 2).
  - `index.html`'s `#hovertip` rule only undoes the menu's layout (position, size, padding) and sets the look's `--accent` as its top edge and `--text` for its words.
  - **No look is named in the module or its CSS**, so a seventh look gets the card from its own menu rule.
