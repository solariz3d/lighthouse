## 0. The answer

- **`src/core/piece.js` `cutRun(doc, from, to)`**, beside `deleteRun`. It returns `{ doc, kept, removed, notes }`.
  - **On a closed lap** the cut can be anywhere, including across the start line (from > to, as a selection gives it). The kept pieces are **rotated**: the piece after the cut comes first, and the piece before the cut is the open end.
  - The new `start` is that piece's block start on the path (`path.starts[g]`: position, heading, pitch), quantised as `createDoc` does.
  - That's exact by construction: `src/geom/path.js:128-137` (BLOCKS AND THE PLACEMENT CHAIN) carries only x, θ and p between blocks, and the frame always restarts at H(0).
  - The kept pieces are the **very same objects**, and `nextId` is unchanged.
- **On an open track:** a cut at its start or its end leaves one piece of road, with nothing moved.
  - **A cut in its middle is refused by name, `CUT_OPEN_MIDDLE`.** The brief says "middle or end" on an open track too. But that would leave two separate pieces of road, which one document can't hold; every way I could see of making it fit drops or moves built road. **This is the one place I narrowed the brief.** Yours to rule; the message tells the user to close the loop first or cut from an end.
- **Other refusals, by name:**
  - `CUT_STARTS_IN_AIR`: the track would start with a jump. Cut the jump too, or cut after its landing.
  - `CUT_ALL` and `BAD_RANGE`.
  - `CUT_REJOIN`, if the old lap seam ever failed `checkDoc`. It didn't on any lap tried (§1).
- **The notes:**
  - **Start line:** `spawns.line` is "metres into the FIRST piece" (`document.js:213`). It is **kept when its piece is still first**, which is a cut at or across the lap's end. Otherwise it's **reset to the start of the new first piece** (`along: 0`). The grid pack is kept either way.
  - **Hotlap:** dropped if its piece is cut.
  - **Pit lane:** dropped if a cut piece lies between its leave and rejoin (a lane never crosses the start line, `src/geom/pitlane.js:79`).
- **`app/core/coreshell.js`:**
  - `cutSelection()` previews (`state.cutProposal`; the track is unchanged).
  - `applyCut()` copies the track to its backups first (`pre-cut`) and refuses by name if that fails ("not cut: the copy from before the cut could not be written (…); nothing changed"). Then it commits **one undo step**.
  - `cancelCut()` drops the preview. A change of track or selection drops it too (the same rules as the delete preview).
  - **No overlap check:** nothing kept moves, so a cut can add no overlap.
  - The preview line and the applied message both say what goes, where the open track now starts and ends, and what happened to the start line, hotlap and pit lane.
