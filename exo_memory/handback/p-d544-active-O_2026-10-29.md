## 1a. The car diff

Method:
- I unpacked the regular car's `data.acd` into the scratchpad with `acd.js`, the known folder-name key: 76 files, all reading as text.
- The active car's root has no `data.acd`. Its `data\data.acd` is 16 zero bytes, a placeholder, so the game reads the unpacked `data\` folder.
- I diffed the two by file.

**Identical, byte for byte (66 files):**
- `aero.ini` and every wing/fin AOA table;
- `tyres.ini` and every tire curve;
- `colliders.ini`, `brakes.ini`, `drivetrain.ini`, `engine.ini`, `electronics.ini`, `damage.ini`, `ratios.rto`, `final.rto`;
- **all twelve `ctrl_steer_*` and `ctrl_susp_*` controller files** (the regular car has them too);
- `script_physics.lua`, `script_pid.lua`, `script_threesixtyctrlr.lua` and the rest.

So mass, aero, tyres and the collision box are the same. The D515 box measures, collider bottom 0.05 m and tyre radius 0.395 m, hold for both cars unchanged.

**Different (10 files), in the files' own units:**

| file | regular | active |
|---|---|---|
| `suspensions.ini` front coilover | RATE 80,000 · MIN_LENGTH 0.9 · END_RATE 250,000 | **RATE 15,000 · MIN_LENGTH 0.975 · END_RATE 750,000** |
| `suspensions.ini` rear coilover | RATE 80,000 · MIN_LENGTH 0.9 · END_RATE 250,000 | **RATE 100,000 · MIN_LENGTH 0.975 · END_RATE 750,000** |
| `power.lut` (piston torque, Nm by rpm) | peak 820 at 7,200; 620 at 9,600 | peak 515 at 3,500; 200 from 8,500 up. The old curve is kept as `power_alt.lut`, picked by a setup item. |

| `script.lua`, `script_controls.lua`, `script_state.lua` | — | the plumbing for the above |
| `car.ini` | SCREEN_NAME "…Chassis" | "…Chassis active"; nothing else |

**inferred:** for the checker, the suspension lines are the change that matters.
- The bump travel is 7.5 cm shorter (MIN_LENGTH 0.9 → 0.975 m).
- The end stop is three times stiffer (250k → 750k N/m).

Both fit what §1b measures: the body stops sinking at about −19 cm and is held there to 72.8 g, where the regular's softer end stop let it fall to −48 cm. The steering and thrust changes explain the speed and the line (below), not the bottoming.

## The falsifier, checked

The plan's registered falsifier: "if the active car's loads, depth and lift-off on the same stretches sit inside the regular car's own run-to-run scatter (D515 §4's bands), the handling difference he feels is not in these measures".

- **Not fired.** At every band from 20 g up, the active car's h p50 lies outside the regular car's p10–p90 for that band.
  - At 25–30 g: −7.4 against −19.6..−18.6.
  - At 55–60 g: −18.5 against −48.2..−28.6.
- The box over the mesh changes sign from 47 g.
- **My limit:** there is one run of each car. D515 has no second regular run on this track, so the scatter used is the within-band spread of one run, not run-to-run.
- inferred: a 12–29 cm difference in body height at matched load is far larger than a driver could change at matched load.
- The different line (6.5 m against 2.4 m off on p13) and the higher speed are driver-and-car, not separated here.
