## 2 · S2 — THE KIND TEST, run on all four, and why none of them is `acknowledged`

**The registry README's own definitions** (`carrier-drift.registry.json`, `_README`):
- *acknowledged*: *"the claim is deliberately left standing … and the correction lives elsewhere in the same file.
  Requires `see`, and the marker must be present."*
- *withdrawal*: *"the occurrence IS the correction text, quoting the claim in order to withdraw it."*
- *mention*: *"the wording appears but asserts nothing — a census listing carriers, a grep command."*

**One test, applied the same way to all four:** a line is **withdrawal** when its job is to tell the reader the claim
is withdrawn, and **mention** when its job is to talk *about* a document, a carrier or a search. **Acknowledged needs
the claim to stand, and none of the four stands it.**
