**The mechanism** (`consonance/tools/carrier-drift.js:587-595`):

**Which of the three to move, argued:**

  - D096 was right to drop the row.

**The change** (`carrier-drift.test.js:257-268`). It is stricter than before, not weaker:
- The old check was that `'exo_memory/BOOT.md'` appears somewhere in the list of `UNMARKED-CARRIER` files, for any withdrawal and any line.
