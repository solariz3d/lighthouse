## 2. The source of the rings

**It was the vignette, not the sky.**
- `app/index.html:259` (at b497c5d) drew `#preview::after { background: radial-gradient(120% 90% at 55% 38%, transparent 55%, rgba(0, 0, 0, .55) 100%) }` over the whole 3D view.
- A CSS gradient is composited in 8 bits with no dither. Over the dark skies, the darkening toward the edges came out as concentric 1-level steps.
- Those steps are screen-pinned, which is why it "follows me where ever I go". They sit on an ellipse centred above the view, which is the curve he drew.
- The sky passes already dither (`renderer.js` `pass_bayer4`, `app/test/sky-dither.test.js`). A sky graded by elevation also bands in straight horizontal lines on a level camera, not in ellipses.
- **A CSS-side fix cannot work.** The step is in the final colour: the dark scene times (1 − alpha) is rounded to 8 bits after the page composites it. Only a pass that writes the final colour can dither it.

## 3. The fix, at the source

`app/preview/renderer.js`, "THE VIGNETTE":
- **The vignette is now the frame's last pass**, over everything the renderer draws.
- **Same shape and strength as the gradient:** centre 55% across and 38% down, radii 120% of the width and 90% of the height, clear to 55%, darkening linearly to 0.55.
- **The sky pass's own 4×4 ordered dither** is added to what it leaves. The pattern helpers are now one shared `PASS_BAYER`, and `skyFS` is byte-for-byte the same text.
- **The blend** is `blendFuncSeparate(ONE, SRC_ALPHA, ZERO, ONE)`: what is drawn is scaled by 1 − 0.55 t, plus up to one 8-bit step of dither where t > 0, and the canvas's alpha is left opaque.
- **A skin names it** with the word token `--vignette` (`themeFrom`, default `on`):
  - `app/themes/skyline.css` sets `--vignette: none`, replacing its old `#preview::after { background: none; }`, so Skyline's light sky still has none.
  - A renderer never told draws none, so the pinned frame of a 'none' sky is unchanged.
- `app/index.html`: the `::after` rule is gone, and a comment points at the renderer.
- `aqua.css` / `aurora.css`: one comment each, now pointing at the renderer.
- **Lost in the move (inferred):** the CSS layer also dimmed the HTML inside `#preview` near its edges (the small view label at the bottom left). The renderer's pass dims only the 3D picture.
