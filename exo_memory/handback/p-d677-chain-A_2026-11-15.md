## What the chain is

Each segment hangs on its parent: the chest on the pelvis that the legs' plan puts down, the neck and the shoulders on the chest,
the head on the neck, and each arm on its shoulder. A segment's frame is (yaw, pitch, roll) in space. Its joints are its rest
offsets, turned to face +Z and put through that frame from the parent's point.

What a segment takes from its parent is carried through a spring at the link. The head's spring follows the chest's spring, not
the pelvis, so the head comes round later. That is follow-through.

What the stride does is laid on top, unsprung, so the runner's phases are kept: the thorax's turn, the trunk's lean, and the
shoulder's swing and elbow's bend. These are interpolated by speed over 3.56, 5.20, 7.00 and 9.49 m/s, held above the fastest, and
scaled to nothing below the slowest and when not running.
