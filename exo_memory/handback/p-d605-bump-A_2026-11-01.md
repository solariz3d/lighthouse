## Redo, part 2: the roll carried through the joint (the trace and the renders; the proof runs follow in part 3)

Code on t180 d605-a, NOT yet committed (the proof runs are in progress): src/core/extend.js `carriedInto` (new) and `fitWithHold`'s optional end rate, plus six new rows in test/core_bank_onset.test.js.

**What it does.** The new piece is added with a shaped bank (the panel's Extend). If it rolls on the way the last piece was rolling, the last piece's fall is taken out:
- its rate holds to its end instead of falling to 0;
- the roll that fall gave up is taken back over the whole piece, easing its rate by a share that grows as the Bloss cubic from 0 at its start;
- the piece keeps its start (value, slope, curvature) and its end bank, and its end stays C2;
- the new piece continues that rate, because extend already continues every channel's end slope, and rolls on to its target.

It applies only to the bank, and only where the last piece arrives at rest from a roll of at least 0.02 °/m the same way as the new target, with its fall under a quarter of the piece and its rate eased by under a quarter. A roll that turns back, a bank held, or a long fall keeps its rest and its bytes. Saved documents are not touched: only a new Extend re-lays the piece before it.
