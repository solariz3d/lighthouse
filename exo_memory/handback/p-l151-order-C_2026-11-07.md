## 3. Where order acts in the code (fc3cc63)
- **The only gate is "does the field have a value":**
  - `app/core/panel.js:397` `secHasTarget`;
  - the bank's is `bankTarget() !== ''` (`:375`), and `asTyped` (`:369`) reads a field left at the head's value as blank (`HEAD[k][0].value === shown[k] ? ''`);
  - the extra handles and offers skip a field without one (`:873`, `:1288`).
  - So an extra bank needs the bank typed first, and an extra turn needs the turn first. Past that gate, nothing depends on order.
- **The extra bank's mark doesn't read the turn.** `offersAt` places it from the bank's own first ramp and the length (`:1293`, `:1305`). The extra turn's mark is set by its own rule. Neither looks at the other field.
- **A bank typed equal to the head's bank is no bank** (checked, first run: with bank 0 on a level head the log shows `extras []` after the bank step, and no extra bank handle in any order).
  - So the S cannot have had first bank 0 and an extra bank, which narrows B's unrecoverable first bank (`p-l150-B` :53) to a value other than the head's.
- inferred: this gate is the most likely reading of the 11-06 "the extra bank only worked with the bank set first". The extra bank handle is not offered until the bank has a value. That is by design, not a defect, and in my L142 runs it appeared as soon as the bank was set, in either order.
