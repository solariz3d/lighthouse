## What it is

- **The API:**
  - `make_rest(BodyPose)` → each joint's rest frame and its offset from its parent;
  - `to_rotations(rest, pose)` → the root's position plus every joint's rotation relative to its parent (the root's absolute);
  - `to_positions(rest, rotations)` → joints rebuilt with the rest bone lengths, as a ragdoll's bodies have them.
- **A small quaternion of its own** (`Quat`, `quat_mul`, `quat_rotate`, `quat_from_basis` by Shepperd's method). `sim/physics.cpp` is "the only file in the project that talks to Jolt" (its line 1), so game/ cannot borrow Jolt's.
- **The tree:**
  - Pelvis → Chest → Neck → Head → Eye;
  - Chest → Shoulder → Elbow → Wrist → Hand (both arms);
  - Pelvis → Hip → Knee → Ankle → Toe (both legs).
- **Each joint's frame:** its axis is the bone to its first child. Its roll comes from the pose's own axes:
  - the hips and legs from `right`;
  - the trunk and arms from `chest_right`;
  - the neck and head from `head_right` (else `chest_right`).
  - Leaf joints take their parent's frame.
- **Nothing uses it yet.** Nothing drawn changes. Step 2 (the hunter hard-keyed in its own `PhysicsSystem`) is its first user.
