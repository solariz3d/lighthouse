## 1. The cause, and which test showed it

It was **none of the three as stated, but closest to test 2: the lines were built from the wrong cell's record.**
- **Where it was:** `app/preview/batches.js` took each mesh node's cell record by NAME (`byName.get(m.name)`). mesh.js names every cell
  of a piece alike: all 275 cells of his "test bump" are `1ROAD_p1_body_0`, and so are all 150 of a plain 300 m straight. So every
  batch got the LAST cell's record:
  - **its `rowS`:** look.js drew a tie on every cell (each 2 m, or packed far closer on a bump), not one every 10 m;
  - **its `cols` and `us`:** where a piece's parts differ (his bump changes width), `linesFor` read the vertices with another part's
    stride and joined rows that do not belong together. That is the stack of short white lines over the bump.
- **The bug is old:** it has been there since D168 (`git log -S byName`: f0b0d75), and v0.4.8 had it too.

The figures (my probe in `scratchpad\d524\render524b.js`, on his file read only, in the page's own preview, world space by each batch's
matrix):

| | before (07cee60) | after (f28bd69) |
|---|---|---|
| distinct rowS across the 250 cell batches | **1** (every batch 498.00..500.00) | **250** |
| tie segments on the 156-176 m stretch | **3,100** | **756** |

| edge vertices at 158 m (left / centre / right) vs the surface | 3.312 / 0.788 / 2.678 | 3.312 / 0.784 / 2.930, against the surface's top 3.283 and the path's 0.754 (+0.03 lift) |

**Test 1 (stale caches): not the cause.** The fresh load and the load after a drag (a real `beginBrush` Grab at 165 m, five `brushTo`
steps, `endBrush`, the exact road awaited) gave the same counts, and the ladder was there on the fresh load.

**Test 3 (the surface stale): not the cause.** The surface and the path agree:
- at 166 m the path is 0.962 m and the surface's lowest vertex 0.953 m;
- after the drag they move together, 1.161 and 1.152 m.

The surface "reads flat" because his road is a **bowl**: `family: "bowl"`, r 2.993, walls about 2.6 m up, so a 1 m bump in its floor
is small against the walls. The road was never stale.

## 2. The fix

- **`batches.js`:** each node's cell record is taken by POSITION, `mesh.cells[k]`. mesh.js pushes `cells[k]` and `children[k]` in one
  loop, and the code's own comment already relies on that order. A name that does not match fails as "no cell record". The by-name map
  is gone. Then `pc.cells[n]` is the batch's own part's cell.
- **Commits:** `f28bd69`; the CHANGELOG line rewritten in `ad7f087` (the Part 1 line had credited the depth bias).
- **The Part 1 depth bias (0eb6f25) stays:** it does no harm and keeps far lines solid.
- **Visible everywhere, intended:** on every track the ties now come every 10 m, as look.js always said, not on every 2 m cell. The
  road reads less hatched.
