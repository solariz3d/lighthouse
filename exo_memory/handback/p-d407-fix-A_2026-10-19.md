## 1. What changed

All three hitches had one cause: the FULL ghost of the next piece was built inside the event. That happened on the release (pointer-up), on every field change, and on every hover over Extend. A placement then built the same piece again. The measured chain was `panel.js ghost` → `trackmodel.ghostFor` → `mesh.js meshPiece`.

1. **The exact ghost is built a slice a frame.** `preview.showGhost(c, { sliced })` steps `trackModel.ghostSteps` for at most 2.5 ms a frame (`GHOST_SLICE_MS`). `ghostSteps` is a generator over the same work, in the same order, as `ghostFor`, which is that generator run to the end.
   - geom gains `buildPathSteps`, `extendPathSteps`, `buildMeshSteps` and `extendMeshSteps`.
   - `extendMesh` keeps its own body, because the mutation harnesses anchor on its lines. Row 5 below holds the stepped and plain builds to the byte.
   - The ghost on show stays until the exact one is whole, so nothing blinks.
   - The first step runs inside `showGhost`, so a candidate that cannot be a ghost still throws to the panel, which shows its "no preview of this piece" message as before.
   - Every ghost except a drag's goes this way: a field change, a hover, the release (`panel.js`, `sliced: !scrubbing`).
   - On the release, the drag's last position is typed in without building a cheap ghost that would be thrown away at once.
2. **The work itself is about a third of what it was** (node, a 1,000 m full ghost: 96 → 25 ms):
   - A piece's cross-section is walked and sealed once per set of numbers, not once per 2 m chord. Before, 500 chords meant 500 walks of 4,000 samples, with 5 distinct cross-sections among them (`profile.js samplesShared`, `sealedNormal`).
   - Its offsets are computed once per profile and set of samples (`mesh.js shapeOf`).
   - The adapter keeps one frozen `profileAt` per family, width and rate.
   - `linesFor` writes straight into its typed arrays.
   - A full ghost keys only the placed prefix it must match.
3. **Placing the ghost on show takes its path and mesh** (`trackmodel lastFull`). The conditions: the very same segment objects, the same placed path and mesh, and no offsets to lift. The old track stays whole for the undo without being copied, because the ghost grew copies.
   - The start's marks grow their path instead of rebuilding the whole track (`coreshell spawnPath`; `startLayout` and `spawnsLayout` take it as `base`).
   - The drag handles lay out and redraw only when something they read changed (`handles.js frameKey`).
4. **Docs:** `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §9b, two bullets (letting go, typing and hovering; placing a piece). `CHANGELOG.md`: one Fixed entry under Unreleased.
