## R0: the app's `/compact` write (L218 Addendum 3; checked)

**The bytes, and how.** The close path is:

`close_small_send` → `gate_or_queue` (or, when queued, `drain_inboxes`, which writes the stored text unchanged) → `inject_to_pane` →
`deliver`.

`deliver` writes to the pane's PTY master:
1. One write, a bracketed paste: `ESC[200~` + `/compact` + `ESC[201~`.
2. Then, as a separate write `submit_delay_ms(18)` = 120 ms later, a lone `\r`.

There is no label, and no send stamp: the label and stamp are added only by the ring senders, before they reach the gate. The
NEXT-trailer gate is in `mcp.rs`'s verbs only (`trailer_gate` in `chair_inject`, `call_chair` and `call_librarian`), and this path
never passes through `mcp.rs`.

### Item 1, the race: a hand-back cut belongs to the turn that rang (checked by rows; the live race is not run)

- **The registry.** `cut_at_handback::Pending` holds pending cuts by pane id, each with a `gen`, the ring time and `sent`.
  `main.rs` keeps it as `PENDING_CUTS`. `cut_at_handback_after_ring` sets it at the ring, before its thread starts, so a dispatch
  arriving in the next quarter second is already held.
- **The hold.** While a pane is pending, two things wait:
  - `drain_inboxes` takes nothing for it (`if cut_pending(&pane) { return; }`, before the gate is read);
  - `chair_inject_exec` puts a dispatch to it in its inbox (`hold_for_pending_cut`, before `cut_before_dispatch` and the gate), with
    a `HELD ->` row.

  The drain delivers both once the cut is released, whatever the outcome. So a dispatch that arrives before the cut's send, or
  during the compaction, is held and delivered after.
- **The drop.** The cut's thread looks every 5 s (`cut_at_handback::poll`):
  - **Dropped** if a later turn began before `/compact` was sent (`later_turn_began`: a prompt row stamped after the ring; tool
    results and the model's replies never count);
  - **Dropped** if the ringing turn has not ended within 30 min;
  - **Sent** if the ringing turn ended and nothing began after it.

  Every drop releases the pane with a board line ("…; anything held for it is delivered now").
- **One cut per pane.** A newer hand-back replaces a cut not yet sent. The older thread sees its `gen` gone and stops. A cut
  already sending is never replaced.
- **The sends never queue on the L218 paths.** `compact_and_confirm(…, may_queue: false)` writes `/compact` only when the pane's Stop
  stamp says ready and its composer is empty; otherwise it writes nothing ("NOT cut: … not ready with an empty composer"). A queued
  `/compact` would wait out the hold and land in a later turn, the race itself.
  - `every_write_into_a_pane_asks_the_inbox_first` caught this direct write (red on the first suite run of the fix). It is now a
    NAMED exception in that row's `ALLOWED_TO_BYPASS`, with this reason.
  - The click's cut keeps `may_queue: true`, its old path.
- **The cut before a dispatch** now uses the same registry. It sets the pane pending and sending, puts the dispatch in the inbox,
  cuts, and releases. The drain then delivers the dispatch, whatever the cut's outcome.
- **Rows, red first** (`fix_rows.txt`), against stubs of today's behaviour (nothing recorded, nothing held, a later turn never
  seen):
  - **5 of the new pure rows failed:** the dispatch before the send; the dispatch during the compaction; the later turn drops the
    cut; a newer hand-back takes over; the reader of a later turn.
  - **The 30-minute abandon row passed against the stub, for the wrong reason:** the stub held nothing, so "released" was trivially
    true. I added an assertion that the dispatch IS held before the abandon. Its red is then shown by the mutants (P1, P6).
- **The wiring row** `a_pending_cut_holds_the_drain_and_the_dispatch_and_is_set_before_its_thread` pins:
  - the drain asks the registry before it reads or takes;
  - the dispatch asks it before `cut_before_dispatch` and the gate;
  - the cut is pending before its thread starts;
  - it is marked sent before the one send, and released after it and on the drop;
  - the two L218 sends pass `false`;
  - only the `may_queue` arm queues.
- **Not shown, inferred:**
