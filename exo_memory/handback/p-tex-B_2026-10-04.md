## 1. The seam: **the UVs wrap without stretch, and the test has teeth.**

- **By reading `src/texture/flow.js`:** a closed ring takes `u = n · arc / w` with `n = max(1, round(w / tileWidth))`. That gives
  even density (n / w per metre), and the two ends at the ceiling differ by exactly n, a whole number of repeats. `v = (row's path
  arc length + offset) / tileLength`, continuous through every 2 m segment and seam. The one along-seam is at the start/finish line,
  where `v` returns to 0 once per lap. A disclosed it, and it's the honest choice short of tying the tile length to the lap.
