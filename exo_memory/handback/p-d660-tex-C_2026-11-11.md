## What was built (commit A)
- **`ReadDds`** (`render/src/dds.cpp`, 65 lines, no dependency): the one kind the art tool writes. That is uncompressed RGBA8 sRGB (DXGI 29) with a DX10 header and mips. Anything else is refused with its reason.
- **`Renderer::AddTexture(image, tileMetres)`**: an `R8G8B8A8_UNORM_SRGB` texture, uploaded through `CopyTextureRegion` per mip, its SRV written into slot `kTextureBase + n`. A `TexInfo` buffer at t13 carries `{tile, width, mips}`, with a wrapping sampler at s1. Failures return -1 with `LastError`.
- **Box projection and the ray-cone mip** (`dz.hlsl` `TexelAt`, `SurfaceAlbedo`):
  - floors and ceilings take `P.xz / tile`; walls take the along-wall axis and the height;
  - mip = log2(spread · t / max(|cos|, 0.15) · width / tile);
  - the primary spread is 2·tanFovY / height, and the bounce's cone is 16× wider.
- **`Material::textureDown`** (in `matEmissive.w`): faces looking down can take their own map, so the ceiling differs from the floor without another material.
- **Grime over the texture:** the grime field and the wall's skirting band still apply. The shader's own carpet, popcorn, roller and wood are drawn only where there is no texture.
- **`art/tools/textures.py`:**
  - runs in headless Blender only (`blender --background --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python art/tools/textures.py -- SRC OUT`; Blender 5.2.3 LTS);
  - takes `*_Color.jpg` with no colour management, rows top first;
  - mip 0 is the source's own bytes; levels 1 to 10 are box-filtered in linear light and re-encoded to sRGB;
  - writes `.part`, then renames.
