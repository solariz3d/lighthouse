## 2. The four items

1. **Slots in the document (schema 2).** A word holds OVERRIDES only. Its effective slots are its font's defaults with
   the overrides laid over them. Every font's default, in v1, is no texture with 10 m tiling; the maker (part 2) is
   where built-in looks come from. A jump takes no overrides: its landing ramp wears the road word before it. Schema 1
   documents and libraries migrate; schema 3 is refused as SCHEMA_TOO_NEW, and 0 and "1" as SCHEMA_UNKNOWN.

2. **Mapping.**
   - Along: each lateral line's OWN arc length, accumulated row to row.
   - Across: the profile's own u (which is arc length, on walls past 90° too), outward from the band's inner edge on
     each side (so the kerbs are mirror images), and right to left across the floor.
   - Handles: tileLength, fit/tile (with tileWidth), offset, dir (a swap).
   - Bands:
     - the edge glow is the last 0.5 m;
     - the wall runs from where |ψ| first reaches 30° up to the glow;
     - the kerb (1 m) and then the line (0.3 m) sit just inside the wall (or just inside the glow where there is no
       wall);
     - the floor is the rest.

     These widths and the 30° are **inferred** defaults; the caller can pass its own.
   - **Density test, bounds stated in the file header before the first run:** along within 0.2%, across within 0.1%.
     Rows are every 2 m; the truth is walked at the path's 0.25 m samples and at 40 sub-steps across. **Checked**
     (`node --test test/texture-mapping.test.js`, and the worst values printed by a scratch copy of its `densities()`):

     | case | along worst | across worst | mesh.js today (centreline s) |
     |---|---|---|---|
     | tight turn R 15 m, 26 m flat | 0.0729 % | 0.00000 % | 650.0 % |
     | wall-ride 110° edge, in a turn | 0.0182 % | 0.00000 % | 44.0 % |
     | loop-the-loop R 12, half-pipe | 0.1139 % | 0.00000 % | 46.7 % |

     Across is essentially exact because u *is* arc length; the truth there is an independent chord walk. The control
     rows are a test too ("off by more than 30%"), so the test is shown able to fail.

3. **Bring your own.**
   - PNG: every colour type at every legal depth, filters 0–4, tRNS, CRC. Interlaced PNG is refused as
     TEXTURE_UNSUPPORTED.
   - Baseline JPEG: SOF0/SOF1, 1 or 3 components, sampling 1–2, DRI, 16-bit DQT.
   - Refused as TEXTURE_UNSUPPORTED, by name: progressive, lossless or hierarchical, arithmetic coding, 12-bit samples,
     2 or 4 components (CMYK), sampling factors above 2, and non-interleaved multi-scan files.
   - DDS: uncompressed A8R8G8B8, full mip chain, a box average over the covered block (an odd side drops no texel).
     Header flags CAPS|HEIGHT|WIDTH|PITCH|PIXELFORMAT|MIPMAPCOUNT; caps TEXTURE|MIPMAP|COMPLEX.
   - Refused: more than 8192 on a side (TEXTURE_TOO_LARGE); a normal map with compression asked for (COMPRESSED_NORMAL);
     not a PNG or JPEG (NOT_AN_IMAGE, and a DDS is told to bring its source).
   - Warned: NOT_POWER_OF_TWO, ALPHA_IGNORED, TEXTURE_BUDGET. The 256 MiB budget is **inferred**, a round figure, not
     measured.
   - Inflate is my own rather than zlib, because the webview's zlib shim only writes (`app/export/node-shim.js`) and the
     preview must decode the same files. Node's zlib appears only in the tests, to MAKE files.

4. **One set.** `buildTextureSet(doc, assets)` converts only the textures some slot uses. `applyToScene` appends the
   DDS files and the slot materials; `previewTextures` reads level 0 back **from the DDS bytes**. Checked
   (`test/texture-set.test.js`, which reads the kn5 texture block with its own walker because `tools/kn5.cjs` skips
   it): the preview's list equals the kn5's embedded list (names, order and bytes), and each preview's pixels equal the
   kn5's DDS level 0, texel for texel.

## 6. What is NOT verified

- **Nothing was run in AC** (the keeper's rule: no AC launches). That AC reads these uncompressed A8R8G8B8 DDS files
  with a MIPMAPCOUNT header is **inferred** from the DDS spec and common modding practice, not measured here.
- My JPEG decoder is tested only against my own encoder (the same author on both sides). No camera JPEG, no JPEG from
  another tool, no EXIF orientation (ignored), no Adobe APP14 RGB transform (3-component files are assumed YCbCr).
- The mip filter is a box average in stored (not linear) colour and is not alpha-weighted. That is stated in
  `dds.js`, and its visual effect is not measured.
- The band widths (kerb 1 m, line 0.3 m, glow 0.5 m, wall at 30°) and the 256 MiB budget are inferred defaults.
- Along-continuity across piece joints: each piece starts at `startAlong = 0`, so a tile can break where two pieces
  meet unless the caller passes the running distance. That trades against C's piece-local rule, and it is C's and the
  keeper's call.
- The per-slot mesh split (seam b), the preview drawing (c), and the page/export wiring (d) are not built.
- The panel's DOM (`app/texture/index.js`) is only load-tested, not rendered in headless Edge this lap.
