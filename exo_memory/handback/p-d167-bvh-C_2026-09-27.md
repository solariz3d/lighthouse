## 2 · What was built

**Self-intersection (ARCHITECTURE §3 `:58`, red §4 `:84`).** `selfCheck` reads the **finished** mesh: every exported triangle, rebuilt in world coordinates from the float32 arrays × the node matrices, so it checks what the kn5 carries.
- **BVH:** triangle bounding boxes, a median split on the longest axis, 4 per leaf.
- **Same pass:** triangles closer than `minSeparationM` along s (default 25 m, the validator's own inferred default, src/validate/index.js `stacked`) are one pass and are never tested. On a closed loop the gap is measured both ways round.
- **Triangle–triangle:** non-coplanar triangles meet iff an edge of one crosses the other. Coplanar triangles (all vertices within 1e-4 m of the other's plane) are tested in 2D. **Two roads crossing at one height ARE coplanar**, so this branch is the same-height case, and mutant M16 shows it is load-bearing.
- **Output:** findings are grouped per pair of cells. Each group keeps its lowest-s hit and a triangle-pair count. `buildMesh(…, { selfCheck: true })` adds them to `folds[]` as `{ s, u, margin: null, other: <cell name> }`, both sides, exactly the docs/INTERFACES.md §2 shape that src/validate/index.js:179 already turns into `self-intersection` reds.

**Stacked (§4 `:85`, "drivable surfaces stacked within about 2 m", src/validate/limits.js:27 `stackedM: 2`).**
- **Method:** from every surface vertex, a segment runs along its normal to 2 m on both sides. A triangle of another pass crossing it is a stack, with gap = the distance along the normal.
- **Grouping:** per cell pair, keeping the smallest gap.
- **Sampling limit:** it is sampled at the vertices, which are at most 1 m apart across (`maxAcross`) and 4 m along (`maxStep`).
- **Overlap with E's check:** E's validator has its own point-grid stack check (Euclidean, 1 m cross-samples). Mine is along the normal, on the exported triangles. They overlap in purpose; which one validation reports is E's and B's call.

**Font ramps (the geometry half).**
- **Where the ramp lives:** the ENTERING piece ramps from the previous font over its first `rampM` metres (default 20, inferred per the ruling; shortened to the piece's length if the piece is shorter), smoothstep in s.
- **What blends:** width, ψ(u) and, through ψ, wall height. Both profiles are matched at FRACTIONS of each side's width: `edge_w = lerp(edgeA, edgeB, w)`, `ψ_w(f) = lerp(ψA(f·edgeA), ψB(f·edgeB), w)`. This is exactly piecewise linear on the union of both profiles' knots.
- **Vertex count:** one vertex count across the whole ramp (`blendSamples`). Its measure bounds every blend in between, because ψ_w is a convex combination, so the 1° seam and 1 m across limits hold throughout.
- **The entry font is a handle:** `profileIn`, plus `rampM`. A document may set them. Otherwise `effective()` takes the entry font from the previous road piece (on a closed loop, the first piece follows the last). `rampM: 0` turns ramps off. So a saved piece stays a pure function of its handles plus the font it is attached after, and sculpt reuse compares these effective handles.
- **For A (the model half):** should resolve write `profileIn`/`rampM` explicitly, or leave the default? Nothing in src/doc sets them today (`checked: grep -rn "profileIn\|rampM" src/doc` → none).

**Loop-the-loop.** No code change was needed. path.js already runs pitch through ±90° and round: a probe at step 0.25, R 12 gave a frame change of 0.02083 per sample (= step/R), no NaN, and closure at 3.3e-13 m. §4 has the tests.

## 6 · Findings for other seats (measured, `checked: node scratchpad/d167fig.js` and the probes above)

1. **A climbing turn exits BANKED against gravity (for B, path.js, and A, the model).** Under the rotation-minimising frame, the `over` straight after the 270° climbing turn has bankG −3.438° after a 1.5 m climb and **−13.743° after a 6 m climb**. The user asked for a level exit and gets a 13.7° bank.
   - ARCHITECTURE §3 says "RMF … plus explicit roll; show the user bank relative to gravity", so this may be intended, with the model compensating roll. But nothing compensates it today.
   - It also made my own fixture's "1.5 m" a centreline-only figure: the local gap is 0.72–0.96 m at one edge. The stacked check reported the true local gaps.
2. **A loop-the-loop is not drivable as a pure vertical loop (for A).** Its exit runs over its own entry at the bottom, and selfCheck finds it: `1ROAD_in_0 × 1ROAD_loop_0` and more. A drivable loop needs a sideways offset of about a road width, and yaw about world up during a vertical loop barely produces one (yaw 0.004 1/m over the loop moved the exit 0.17 m). The offset has to be a model word (a corkscrew-style lateral shift, or roll plus yaw). It is a vocabulary question, not a geometry one.
3. **Cost.** On the stadium (428.5 m, 169,346 triangles, 86,792 vertices), `selfCheck` took 662 ms, after 96 ms for the mesh (single run, wall time, `inferred:` a shared machine). It ran 63 triangle tests and 423 ray tests after the pruning. It is opt-in (`selfCheck: true`) and is **not incremental**: extendMesh and sculptMesh do not run it. A whole-track check after an edit is O(T log T). An incremental version (query only the changed cells' triangles) is the natural next step, and the trace hook makes it testable against this one.
