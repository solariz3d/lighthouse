## The answer

**At all eight bends, the road across is one smooth, symmetric dish, banked as a whole, with a lip of about 0.33 m at each edge. The two sides do not differ in shape.**

- **The dish:** at 10 m from the centre, inside every lip, each side turns 12.0–16.4° and rises 0.87–1.34 m.
  - Left and right agree within 1.6° and 0.22 m at every bend.
  - It curves from the centre outward, with no flat floor: one side's start or both sit at the fit grid's floor of 0.10 of 12 m.
  - The best two-part fit (a cup to the start, then an arc or G) holds a side to 1.4–6.7 cm RMS in height. Over the eight bends an arc wins on 137 side-stations, G on 43, a straight face on none.
- **What reads as one side more banked than the other:** the whole dish is tilted by the bank, 13–52° at these bends.
  - On a banked dish the outer half is steeper by the bank and the inner half flatter by it. With a 14° dish at a 48° bank (S2), that is about 62° against 34°.
  - **inferred:** this, not a per-side edge shape, is what the keeper sees. I have not asked him.
- **The lip:** each side ends 12.4–14.1 m from the centre with a step of about 0.33 m (p50 0.33, p90 0.34 over the lap).
  - Where each side's lip begins differs by up to 1.2 m between the two sides.
  - That alone made two bends look asymmetric at a 12 m reach: their ψ12 reads the lip, not the dish.
- **Along the bend, the bank moves a lot.** Within ±100 m of a bend it swings through up to 81° (A3, from one bend into the next).
  - The rate runs p50 0.10–0.63 and p90 0.31–1.11 °/m, peaking at 1.47 °/m.
  - Over the whole lap: p50 0.15, p90 0.51, p99 0.97 °/m. Against the builder: our corpus p90 is 0.849 °/m (vocab.js BANK_RATE).
- **No lead or lag between turn and bank was found.** The best-matching lags spread from −12 to +60 m, with correlations 0.16–0.77 and changing sign between bends. The read's curvature is spiky (its frames are re-settled every station), so this instrument cannot see one.

**The falsifier:** whether our one-s family fits these sections to under 2 cm RMS is Part 2's, C's fitter on `C:\Consonance\private\d527\out\sections.json`. Part 1's reading, inferred, is a symmetric banked dish.

## How it was measured

- **The road:** every read station (4 m).
  - The plane across the road through the read's centre is intersected with the road meshes, read only.
  - Those are 125 meshes, 1,359,172 triangles, chosen by `tools/read_track.cjs`'s rule.
- **The cut:** joined by vertex position, and walked from the centre to each side's end. A side ends past 100° up or 45° down.
  - 7,455 of 7,459 stations cut. Sides sum to 0.93 of the read's width at the median.
- **Sign checks:**
  - "Left" is read_track.cjs's f × n, the side the turn sign calls +.
  - 2,523 of 2,639 stations turning tighter than R 500 m with more than 3° of bank lean into their turn.
- **Each side is read at a fixed 10 m and 12 m of arc**, so the lip does not set the figures.
