## 4 · What the numbers are, and are not

- **A word is short:** 28–48 m at the median. The reader names every 4 m station and merges runs under 12 m. A
  builder piece is several words, so A should not set a piece's length to one word's.
- **ψ describes the section up to the lip, not the lip.** The reader stops its side walk at a fold sharper than 35°,
  so ψ tops out near 30° and the edge can sit below ¾. The steepness of a wall is in the bank (80° on a wall-ride).
- **Radius is the centreline's** (§7: Sakura 454 m on the centreline, about 300 m on a replay's line).
- **One word is one sample,** however long. Weighted by length, the straights and sweeps would count for more.
