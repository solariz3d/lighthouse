## 1. The floor follows the room above (gen/src/emit.cpp)

- **The cause, as C found it:** a slab is one box: its underside is the ceiling of the storey below, its top the floor of the storey above. It took the
  material of the storey below (`chunk_biome.kit.floor`). Regions are 8 storeys tall, so where two biomes meet one above the other the upper one stood on
  the lower one's floor.
- **Red first** (`floor_red.txt`, at b212ae8): `geometry_tests` `a_floor_is_the_floor_of_the_room_above_it`: seeds 1 to 4, storeys -2 to 1 round the
  origin, **19,779 slabs, 5,028 where two biomes meet one above the other, all 5,028 with the wrong floor**. At seed 1 the apartment's lowest storey stood
  on the **hospital's** floor (kit 4) at b212ae8; C saw the school's before the lattice moved to 4.1.
- **The fix:** each slab takes the floor of the cell above it (`kit_of(above).floor`), and slabs merge only across equal materials. Each refined place
  reads the owned cell it belongs to; regions are whole chunks across, so that cell is always this chunk's. **Green: 0 of 5,028 wrong** (`floor_fixed.txt`).
- **The cost, chosen and stated:** where two biomes meet one above the other, the lower storey's **ceiling** now shows the upper one's slab. Getting both
  faces right would need splitting the slab, which makes a box thinner than the generator allows (`no_solid_is_thin_or_overlaps_another`, kSlab), or a new
  kind of solid for physics and navigation to learn. If both faces must be right, that is the bigger change, and it was not made.
