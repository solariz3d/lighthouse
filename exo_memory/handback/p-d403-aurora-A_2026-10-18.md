## 5. Where the targets fought, and what I did (the brief invited this)
- The tall, high-contrast rays and "slow": strong ray edges moved by the folds shimmered 24% a second; I cut the rays' own time terms by half and let them follow the folds only 10%, and fine rays are carried 0.0003 rad/s or less. The cost: the ray pattern barely drifts (under one column in 5 s). Most visible motion is the folds and hem swaying, the rays breathing, the corona fan turning very slowly. If it feels too still when the keeper watches it, that is the knob (the rays' time constants), not a bug.
- The corona vs "nothing near the zenith": the old curtains faded out before the zenith. The corona is its own term and the curtains still fade; the corona's reach is capped so nothing is lit below about 20 degrees (and the band row holds).
- The far curtain "lower": its hem is at 13 degrees, 3 degrees above the quiet band's edge, so it is partly clipped by the band's own fade. That is the limit the band puts on "lower".
