## The answer in short
- **The grain:**
  - Every lighting term but the emitted light is drawn with random rays, about one sample per pixel per frame.
  - Nothing smooths that noise across neighbouring pixels.
  - The only smoothing is over time: up to 32 frames of lighting history, and the TAA.
  - The history is cut short whenever the camera moves fast across the screen, and wherever a surface is newly seen. At those moments a pixel shows its raw one-sample noise.
- **The ghost trails:** **no instance writes motion vectors.**
  - Both the lighting history and the TAA find last frame's pixel through the camera alone.
  - So anything that moves on its own leaves its old lighting and shadow behind for up to 32 frames. That covers the chasers, my body's limbs and the shadows they cast.
  - The renderer's own notes already say so (`render/CHANGES.md:176-178`, and `:75-78`).
- **A cause I can pin on my body (inferred from the numbers, not seen):**
  - The hand light sits inside the reach of the body's right shoulder and upper arm, and its emitter is a 0.30 m disc.
  - So every shadow ray toward it can pass through the swinging arm. The body throws large shadows that move with every stride, and they fill the light the player carries.
  - Before L161 there was no body there. This fits "after images from the player model cutting through it".

## 1. Which terms are random, and what smooths them (checked: read)
The frame is one compute pass for the trace and lighting, then TAA, bloom and the tone curve. There is no denoise pass (`render/src/renderer.cpp:1891` TraceCS, `:1903` TaaCS, then bloom; `render/shaders/dz.hlsl:4-9`).

**The settings dz-world runs with:**
- `world_render_settings` (`game/world/stage.cpp:80-88`) keeps the renderer's defaults for all of these (`render/renderer.h:63-76`).
- The only change is `aoSamples = 1` at 1440 lines and taller (`stage.cpp:197`).

**Per pixel, per frame:**

| Term | How it's sampled | Where |
|---|---|---|
| The primary ray | One per pixel, sub-pixel jittered on a 16-frame Halton cycle | `dz.hlsl:307`, `renderer.cpp:1810-1813` |
| Direct light (`directSamples = 0`) | One shadow ray per light in range, toward one random point on the light's disc, so soft shadows are one sample a frame | `dz.hlsl:243-251`, disc at `:188-195`. The point comes from a per-pixel hashed offset stepped along R2 each frame (`:201-204`), so the noise is white across the screen, not blue. |
| Ambient occlusion | 2 rays (1 at 1440 lines and up) over the hemisphere, 2.5 m | `dz.hlsl:320-335` |
| One bounce | 1 cosine ray, 14 m. Where it lands, 1 light picked in proportion with independent random numbers (not the sequence), plus its own shadow ray. Clamped at `giClamp = 1.0`. | `dz.hlsl:339-345`; the pick at `:253-266` |

**Smoothing over time, the lighting history (`dz.hlsl:347-377`):**
- **How long:** a pixel averages up to `lightFrames = 32` frames, then blends at 1/32.
- **How it is found:** through **last frame's camera** (`PrevPixel`, `:278-287`).
- **Rejection:** by distance alone, with tolerance max(0.15 m, 2% of the distance) (`:360, :367`).
- **Camera speed:** the history is cut by screen speed from the camera's motion, to nothing at 40 px a frame (`:371-372`).
- So the grain comes up when:
  - the view turns fast;
  - a surface is newly revealed;
  - the history is rejected near an edge.
- In those pixels the picture is the raw one-sample noise. That is what `--capture-walk`'s settled 72-frame shots never show, because each one is shot after `ResetHistory` plus 72 still frames (`game/world/offscreen.cpp:85-95`).

**Smoothing over time, the TAA (`dz.hlsl:413-443`):**
- The colour history is weighted 0.9.
- It is reprojected by the camera at the nearest distance in the 3x3 (`:422-436`).
- It is clamped to mean ± 1.25 standard deviations of the current 3x3, in a compressed colour space (`:430-439`).

**Not smoothing it:** the film grain. `Settings::grain` is 0 in both looks (`renderer.h:93`; `Settings::ForLook`, `renderer.cpp:214-226`, never sets it). checked by reading; the only noise added at output is a one-level dither (`dz.hlsl:571`).

## 2. Motion vectors (checked: read)
- **None.** In `render/`, nothing writes or reads per-instance motion.
  - checked: a grep for `motion|velocity|reactive|disocc|denois|atrous|svgf` across `renderer.cpp`, `dz.hlsl`, `renderer.h` and `CHANGES.md` finds only the comment at `dz.hlsl:390` and the limits in `CHANGES.md:77, :176`.
- `SetInstanceTransform` copies the new transform and marks the top-level structure dirty, nothing more. Last frame's transform is not kept (`renderer.cpp:1761-1771`).
- Both reprojections use the camera alone:
  - the lighting history at `dz.hlsl:354-356`;
  - the TAA at `:432-436`.
- **What that does to a moving thing (inferred from the code):**
  - **A floor where a limb's shadow just was:** it passes the distance check, because it is the same floor at the same distance. So it keeps up to 31 frames of the old shadow and fades it at 1/32 a frame. That is the trail.
  - **A limb's own pixels:** these are mostly rejected by distance, because what was there last frame was something else. They show raw noise, or take the light of a surface within 0.15 m: a foot near the floor.
  - **TAA's clamp lets ghosts through where the frame is noisy.** The mean ± 1.25 σ box is set by the current 3x3, and a noisy 3x3 has a wide box, so stale colour passes.
  - **The body barely moves on screen.** It is hung from the eye (`game/world/stage.cpp:168-176`), but camera-only reprojection treats it as fixed in the world. So when moving, the body's pixels fetch history from the wrong place. At body distances (0.3–1 m) that miss is large.

## 3. Clamping, disocclusion and reactive masks (checked: read)
- **Neighbourhood clamp:** yes, in TAA only.
  - It is a per-channel clamp to mean ± `taaClip`·σ (`dz.hlsl:438`): variance clamping, not clipping toward the mean along a line.
  - The lighting history has no neighbourhood clamp.
- **Disocclusion:**
  - **Lighting history:** a distance test per tap (`dz.hlsl:360-368`), plus the camera-speed cut (`:371-372`).
  - **TAA:** no disocclusion test at all. Its only guards are the off-screen test (`PrevPixel` false) and the clamp. It reads the lighting's distance only to choose where to reproject (`:427`).
- **Reactive mask:** none. Nothing marks pixels whose lighting changed this frame because a light or an instance moved.
  - This matters for the hand light and the flashlight beam. They move with the view every frame (`game/world/beam.cpp:32`, `:48-49`), so their light on a still floor is always changing. The 32-frame history makes that light lag behind the view (inferred).
