### 2.1 REAL behaviour at the new scale: 1, the CODE is fixed

**`app/test/validate-ui-jumpdefault.test.js`, "the slider swept over its whole range …":** red `landing-misses-zone` at
755, 760 and 764 km/h, where the car clears the gap.
- **The cause** (reproduced directly, `scratchpad/jd.js`): at 755 km/h the measured jump (81 m gap, 14 m drop)
  throws the 3.2 g flight 151.6 m past the landing lip. Resolve sized the landing ramp to 161.6 m to catch it. But
  validation searched only **150 m** of landing road (`opts.landingSearchM || 150`), so a touchdown ON the jump's own
  ramp read as a miss. Under the old 12 m jump no flight went that far.
- **The fix** (`src/validate/index.js`): the landing search is max(150 m, the jump's OWN landing ramp). The ramp is the
  road resolve sized to catch both measured falls, and a check that looks at less than the thing it checks is wrong.
  - Jumps are recomputed whole (never incrementally), so incremental validation is unaffected.
  - The file-header comment is updated to match.
- **The test first** (`test/validate_jumps.test.js`, a NAMED 81 m / 14 m jump at 755 km/h): **red on the unfixed
  code** (`scratchpad/vj1.txt`: 16 pass, 1 fail), green after it.
  - With validate, the incval, jumpdefault and jumps UI tests: **76/76** (`vj2.txt`, under the lock).
