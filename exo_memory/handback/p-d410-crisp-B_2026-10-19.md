## 3. Corrections, including my own

- **Row 2's limb measure was changed twice after I first saw it red.** The test is new, but these are still changes made after seeing numbers, so here is the reason for each:
  1. *First form:* 10–90 % crossings between the levels 18 px either side of the limb. On d410-b it read median 1.80, worst 19.50 (`crisp-try1.out`). I decoded the after shot (`probe/limb.js`): on radii 3–7 a darker patch of the face reaches the limb (level 98, against 112 deeper in), so the 90 % level was reached only deep inside the disc.
  2. *Second form,* ±4 px: worst 3.30, median 1.60 (`crisp-try2.out`). Fine profiles (`probe/limb2.js`) show every radius falling from disc to sky within about 2 px. Radii 1, 11 and 12 shade gently over their last 4–6 px (120 → 104 → 90), and fixed-level crossings count that shading as limb.
  3. *Final form:* 0.8·(inside − outside)/(steepest slope), the 10–90 % width of a straight ramp with that rise and slope.
- **The final measure saturates for a soft limb.** Inside a ±4 px window it cannot read much past 6.4 px, so 5.55 on b306309 is a floor, not the old limb's size (16.7 px by arithmetic; the first measure's median was 15.90). It still separates crisp from soft against a 2 px gate. Mutant P2 (the old 0.020 rad ramp) is caught by it.
- **My CHANGELOG before-figures were wrong twice before the commit.** First I wrote "about six" for the stars and "more than twenty" for the limb (from the contaminated first measure). Then I wrote "about five as well" for the limb (from the saturated final measure). The committed text says stars about five (median 5.43) and limb about sixteen. The prism.js header says the same.
- **Row 5 was red at 0.76 levels against 0.8 on the first try.** That was a real shortfall, and I fixed it in the shader (more weight on the fine filaments, same 1.30 peak), not in the test.
- **One red re-check exited 127** right after its quiet wait, before it took the lock, with no error line (`crisp-red3.out`). The cause is unknown; heavy-run exits only with 3, 4 or 2. The same run succeeded at 19:58Z (`crisp-red5.out`).
- **One pass printed "RED at sky-prism-crisp" without running a test** (`sky-prism-crisp.out`, 16:07Z): heavy-run gave up after 60 min with both slots held (A D407, O D412). Not a red.
