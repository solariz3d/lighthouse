## 2. What changed (cf0102c)

- **(a) `src/core/extend.js`.** The order's reading of (a) was partly out of date: readout and ghost **already share one
  fit when their options agree** (extendCorner's D404 MEMO, keyed by doc and options). In a stretch they cannot share
  bit-exactly, because the readout uses the exact length and the ghost rounds to 2 m (`app/core/panel.js` previewLength),
  and the knots are evenKnots(L) = j·L/m, so every knot moves with L: **no samples carry across a stretch**. What I did
  instead: `samplesOf` is keyed by the knot vector's **values** (4 kept, LRU), so samples carry wherever the same knots
  recur (a turn drag, the corner solve), and a new set is built by `sampleBasis`, a monotone span walk with Cox–de Boor
  in two Float64Array(4) buffers, replacing a per-sample `basisN` search. That was the readout's biggest cost
  (profile, 600 moves: samplesOf 352 ms → 52 ms).
- **(b) `src/core/adapter.js` + `src/geom/profile.js`.** In `xsecSegments`, what does not depend on e (`edgeFrame`: the
  base's knots, zone fractions, middle ψ) is built once per base and start a piece, not once per chord end;
  `edgeProfile` takes the frame. `commonFractions` groups profiles by identical knots, so each fine fraction's span and
  weight are found once per group, and its cache key reuses the knot text while knots repeat.
