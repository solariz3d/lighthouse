## R3 — all three parts of the ruling built; none refused
**(a) the reverse morph.** `adapter.js tailZone` (mirror of `morphZone`): the last ≥ 10 m of a cup piece fade its shape into the next legacy profile — the legacy START's first segment at a lap seam. The fade starts at the last segment boundary at or before length − 10 m, so the runs end exactly where it begins (no step at the zone's edge). When the cup is the lap's START and the legacy piece the END, the existing `morphZone` takes the legacy END's last profile as its join. **Beyond the ruling, stated:** the same fade is applied to ANY cup piece followed by a legacy road piece inside the track (a loaded file can hold one; Extend never makes one). The hand-built mirror lap showed a mid-track cup→legacy joint stepping 148.2 mm (half-pipe 24) and 23.3 mm (bowl 16), the same defect one piece earlier, and (c) would otherwise red it. Say if you want that scope cut.

**(b) close().** `heldCup`: an extra residual row and Jacobian row hold the cup's last value (or, for a cup START, its first) at the legacy piece's RENDERED edge, so the two shapes agree at the edge and the morph only carries the shape difference. After a converged solve, `close()` measures the lap seam's curve step on the segments the lap will be drawn from (`geom/profile.js jointSteps`) and REFUSES by name, `CUP_SEAM`, when it is over 1 mm — never converged over a step.

**(c) validation.** `src/validate/index.js` reds `joint-step` for any cup joint or lap seam whose curve steps more than 1 mm (source string in `SRC`; words in `app/validate-ui/labels.js`). Only where a cup is (`jointSteps` looks at segments tagged `cup`); a word document's joints are untouched.
