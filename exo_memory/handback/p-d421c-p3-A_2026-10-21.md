### What the cut is

**1. Every piece's readout at once** (`readout.js` `pieceReadouts(doc, prev)`):
- **The old cost.** Each `pieceReadout` checked the whole document (`checkDoc`) and summed the pose from the start. On a 50-piece lap that is 50 checks and 1,225 pose sums.
  - Node, warm: 13 ms of checks and 24 ms of pose sums, of 40 ms.
- **The batch** checks once and carries the pose on (`posesOf`, in `poseBefore`'s own order of sums, so each readout is `pieceReadout`'s to the bit).
- **The carry is by pose, not by "every piece before it".** A road piece keeps its readout (the same object) when all of these hold:
  - it is the same piece at the same place;
  - it enters at the same pitch (and heading, when it has offsets);
  - its corners read pieces alike in id, length, knots and that channel.
- **A flight is always read again.**
- `pieceReadout` returns what it did; its body moved to `readoutAt`, with the lines the cup and xsec mutants anchor on unchanged.

**2. A frozen piece's whole-piece reads are kept:** its whole integral of a channel, and where it was last straight before its end.
- After the whole-lap re-close every piece carries about 1e-6 rad/m of turn, so nothing is "straight". Every piece's corner walked back to the start, scanning each earlier piece again: 49 ms of the labels at 3c681db.
- A document's pieces are deep-frozen, so the same read is the same number. The precedent is `knotVectorOf` (D404). An unfrozen piece is read afresh.

**The chair's premise, checked:** "a pose-only change can carry the readouts across" does not hold for row 3.
- Its release falls back to the **whole-lap** re-close, which rewrites every piece's turn channel (piece 40's `kh` goes from 0 to about 1e-6), so every readout really changes.
