1. **Two sky styles call `smoothstep` with their edges reversed** (`app/preview/sky/sun.js:33`, `app/preview/sky/prism.js:51` at `6591b73`; B's)
   - **What this is:** below the horizon, each sky darkens with `smoothstep(0.0, -0.6, h)`.
   - **Problem:** GLSL ES 1.00 leaves `smoothstep` undefined when edge0 ≥ edge1, as here.
     - Most drivers compute the formula and get the intended ramp. A driver that doesn't could paint the lower half of the view flat or wrong in Skyline and Glass.
     - inferred: no driver tried here does that; SwiftShader and this machine's WebView2 draw the ramp.
     - checked: `git show 6591b73:app/preview/sky/{sun,prism}.js | grep -n "smoothstep(0.0, -0.6"` → lines 33 and 51.
     - The same pattern was in my first aurora; I fixed it there before committing.
   - **Fix:** in each line, `smoothstep(0.0, 0.6, -h)`. It's the same value wherever the formula is used, one line each.
   - **If we skip it:** on a driver that follows the spec's freedom, the sky under the horizon draws wrong in two skins.

**Nice to have**

3. **The aurora's stars and the caustics' motes use a `sin`-based hash** (`app/preview/sky/aurora.js:15`, `caustics.js:24`; **mine**)
   - **What this is:** `fract(sin(dot(p, vec3(127.1, 311.7, 74.7))) * 43758.5453)`, with |p| up to 180 (aurora) or 70 (caustics). So `sin` takes arguments around 10^4–10^5.
   - **Problem:** GPUs differ in how they reduce large `sin` arguments. On one that reduces coarsely, the star field could come out banded or repeating instead of random.
     - inferred: I tried only SwiftShader and this machine's WebView2, and both drew a random field.
     - B's `prism_hash` (`prism.js:10`) is the sin-free form, with no such risk.
   - **Fix:** use the prism-style hash in both, as `aurora_hash` and `caustics_hash`. The pattern changes once, then stays the same everywhere.
   - **If we skip it:** at worst, a visible pattern in the stars or motes on some graphics cards.

4. **Two definitions of the quiet band under 10°** (B's hand-back `:175` and `:250`; mine, `p-d384-sky-E_2026-10-18.md` §2)
   - **What this is:** the brief's "luminance within a set band of `--fog`" (`plan_t180_handoff_2026-10-15.md:2487`) left the band to each pane.
   - **Problem:** B's band is the WCAG ratio against `--fog` ≤ 1.30:1, either side (largest 1.148). Mine is "between `--sky` and `--fog`, never brighter" (at most 0.996× the fog).
     - Both are met as each pane stated them. But the release says one thing ("held close to the skin's fog") with two meanings behind it.
   - **Fix:** a one-line ruling naming the band, so the next sky is measured to it. No code.
   - **If we skip it:** a later sky picks either, and "quiet" drifts.

## Refuted candidates

- **`atan(dir.z, dir.x)` with no guard at the zenith or nadir** (all four styles; B's old sun had `+ 1e-6`): undefined only at exactly (0, ±1, 0). The free camera keeps its pitch within 89°, and no pixel centre is ever exactly on the axis. No case, so no finding.
- **D385's corner carry being overwritten by a redraw:** checked in `panel.js` at `4e61acb` (:204, :243, :302, :583-591).
  - `draw` refills the fields only on a NEW document.
  - The carry runs right after `shell.extend` has produced it, and sets `applied`, so the undo guard treats the carried value as applied.
  - C's `core-carry.test.js` pins the behaviour.
- **D388's fetch list and `useSamples`' required set drifting apart:** they are one function, `samplesFor`, and row 12 pins the four files for each melody. The two amended rows are named, with the reason (D386 finding 1), as ruled.
