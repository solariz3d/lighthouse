      holder: chair | panes | librarian | none   a STATION, never a pane name
      --by:   the station WRITING the row. REQUIRED when the row moves the baton.
              --by == --holder is a re-take; --by != --holder is a hand-off and needs a RING FIRST
              (writing the row revokes your standing to send it — mcp.rs:397).
      --to:   the pane letters the baton fanned out to, e.g. --to A,B,C,E (with --holder panes)
      --parts: on a dispatched row, how many parts the plan split the work into (1 = a single pane); optional, observational
      written by whoever COMPLETES a stage; --holder names who must act NEXT
  lap-row.js --void <lap-id> --reason <text> --by <seat>
      withdraws the lap's guess/map MEASUREMENT from every total; the lap stays visible and its chain is untouched
  lap-row.js --report [--last N]

  lap-row.js --open --initiator <human|chair|pane|librarian> --entry <orch|lib|ring> --inquiry <text> --guess <p[,p...]> [--blind]
      entry:  orch | lib | ring   orch = the ask reached the chair first; lib = straight to the librarian;

  lap-row.js --opened <lap-id> --paths <p[,p...]>
      --paths none is LEGAL and records the falsifier firing; it is NOT the same as never calling this.
      REQUIRED before --stage <lap> dispatched|filed on a lap that has a map (the opened-row gate).
  lap-row.js --stage <lap-id> <stage> --holder <station> [--by <station>] [--to <letters>] [--parts <n>] [--note <text>]
      stages: inquiry -> map -> dispatched -> working -> handbacks-in -> return-leg -> filed
      holder: chair | panes | librarian | none   a STATION, never a pane name
      --by:   the station WRITING the row. REQUIRED when the row moves the baton.
              --by == --holder is a re-take; --by != --holder is a hand-off and needs a RING FIRST
              (writing the row revokes your standing to send it — mcp.rs:397).

      --parts: on a dispatched row, how many parts the plan split the work into (1 = a single pane); optional, observational
      written by whoever COMPLETES a stage; --holder names who must act NEXT
  lap-row.js --void <lap-id> --reason <text> --by <seat>
      withdraws the lap's guess/map MEASUREMENT from every total; the lap stays visible and its chain is untouched
  lap-row.js --report [--last N]
