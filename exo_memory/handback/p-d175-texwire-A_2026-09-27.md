## 3. The budget (checked)

**§5b gives the counter and no number**: "Budget: a live count of texture memory and resolution per track, because
textures are the easiest way to make a track heavy" (`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:125`). FINDINGS mentions textures 0 times
(`grep -c -i "texture\|dds" docs/FINDINGS.md` → 0). So the levels are **measured**:
`node scratchpad/d172/texmem.js > texmem.json` reads only the texture block of every kn5 in the eleven T-180 tracks
FINDINGS §1 studies (`results/t180.json`), read only. The embedded texture bytes per track, sorted, in MiB:

| 0.8 | 6.2 | 14.9 | 47.8 | 50.5 | **53.7** | 93.7 | 173.3 | 191.7 | 236.9 | **313.6** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- **AMBER** above the median, **56,278,061 B** (ohyeah2389_t180testtrack): heavier than half of them.
- **RED** above the largest, **328,874,531 B** (thunderhead_raceway): heavier than every one.

What these figures are and are not:
- They are FILE bytes, and those tracks are mostly block-compressed (DXT1/DXT5; rainbow_rd and thunderhead_raceway also
  carry large uncompressed RGB32), while this builder writes uncompressed A8R8G8B8. So the comparison is of what the kn5
  carries, not of GPU memory.
- Red is a level shown, not an export refusal; whether it refuses is the keeper's call.
- These replace D172's inferred 256 MiB. `limitBytes` still works as the amber line under its D172 name, so D172's test
  is unchanged.

Tests:
- **Hand-computed DDS sizes:** 1024² = 128 + 4·(4¹¹ − 1)/3 = 128 + 4·1,398,101 = **5,592,532 B**. It is asserted equal
  to `ddsBytes`, to the rendered made texture's DDS length, and to the budget's total and floor figure (11 mips, format
  A8R8G8B8, level ok).
- 48×20 → 24×10 → 12×5 → 6×2 → 3×1 → 1×1: 128 + 4·1,276 = **5,232 B**. It is asserted equal to `ddsBytes` and to
  `encodeDds`'s length.
- Per slot vs total: a texture two slots share counts in each slot, once in the total.
- Levels at 50 / 54 / 300 / 314 MiB: ok / amber / amber / red.

## 4. Packs (checked)

`node --test --test-concurrency=4 test/doc-packs.test.js`: **7/7**.
- A pack is ONE file: canonical text with the images' original PNG/JPG bytes as base64.
- A word's look saves with its font, all five slots in full (a made texture as text), and ONLY the images its slots
  name.
- The round-trip is byte-exact, images included, from any key order. Base64 was checked against node's for lengths 0 to
  5 and a PNG.
- On import, a clash is handled **as the piece library does** (`library.js` `key()`: trimmed, any case → NAME_TAKEN).
  An image of the same name with different bytes → NAME_TAKEN naming the image; the same bytes → one shared image.
- **Refused as §5b warns:**
  - a DDS in a pack → PACK_BAD_IMAGE (packs carry PNG/JPG; the builder makes its own DDS);
  - a block-compressed normal map (a DDS with FourCC DXT5, role normal) → COMPRESSED_NORMAL;
  - a GIF → PACK_BAD_IMAGE.
- Also refused: a missing image, an unused image, a bad font, and pack format 2 (SCHEMA_TOO_NEW).
- Wearing a pack sets every word of its font (phrase words too) and nothing else, and it is ONE undo step (the shell's
  `commitDoc`, tested in the panel: `past.length` + 1, and undo restores `{}`).
