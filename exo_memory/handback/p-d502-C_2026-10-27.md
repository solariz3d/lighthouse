## 1–2. Spacing: measured, then a scale

**The spacing is identical in all six looks.** The six per-look histograms are equal value for value (`node agg.js` over `raw\before\*.json`). The
theme files declare no padding, margin or gap at all. Every spacing value lives in `app/index.html`, `app/core/panel.js`, `app/onboarding/index.js`
and `app/camera/index.js`. So one scale serves every look.

Declared in the source (a regex over those files and the five theme files, 116 declarations):

| px | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 10 | 11 | 12 | 14 | 16 | 18 | 20 | 22 | 24 | −8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| times | 14 | 5 | 10 | 8 | **22** | 3 | 15 | **18** | 1 | 8 | 4 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

That is 17 distinct values. In the rendered page (sigil, all six regions): **20 distinct declared values, 32 distinct vertical gaps and 19 distinct
horizontal gaps**. Per region:

| region | declared values | vertical gaps | most common |
|---|---|---|---|
| main | 16 | 20 | 2 ×21, 18 ×9, 3 ×5, 43.5 ×5 |
| menu-file | 9 | 3 | 6 ×9, 17 ×2, 29 |
| menu-settings | 9 | 10 | 6 ×15, 11 ×9, 12 ×6, 17 ×6 |
| sculpt | 7 | 3 | 2, 7, 6.5 |
| layouts | 12 | 9 | 6 ×6, 14 ×5, 20 ×5, 10 ×4 |

The one-offs: 11 (button side padding), 13, 14, 18, 20, 22 and 7. Section headings take 18 above and 8 below (`h3`, index.html:225), but the
headings in the menus take 6 above (`.more-menu h3`, :151). That's why the Settings menu reads 11/12/17 between sections and 6 inside them.

**Proposed scale: 2 · 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24**, each value with its own job:

- 2 is for hairlines inside a control.
- 4 by 8 is the inside of every button and summary (today 5×11).
- 8 is the gap between rows and controls.
- 12 is the panel inset.
- 16 is the gap between groups.
- 24 sets off a section.

Snapping rule for the render: nearest value, ties go up. One exception: buttons and summaries take 8 on their sides. With 12 the palette's four
buttons wrapped to a second row in a trial render, so that cost is real.

**Result, and it isn't the hoped-for one:** declared values go from **20 to 9**, but rendered vertical gaps only go from **32 to 30** (`raw\spacing-after\sigil.json`).
- Most visible vertical gaps in this UI come from line heights and control heights (13/16 px text in 1.45 line boxes, 26–34 px controls), not from padding
  and margin. The guide's dashboard had cards whose gaps were the spacing.
- So a scale on padding and margin alone does **not** stop the eye catching here. It also needs a row-height scale: one control height, and one line
  height for labels and values. I didn't render that, because it changes the type.
- inferred: the 6 left in "after" is in elements built after the snap ran. Check: `raw\spacing-after\sigil.json`, the declared values.

**Cost:** about 66 values changed in 4 files (index.html about 45, panel.js about 8, onboarding about 6, camera about 4). It's look-independent, and
the menubar fit rows (menubar-page rows 8 and 17) would need a rerun, since the summaries' padding changes.

## 3. Borders and corner radius (the amendment)

Measured in all six looks (`harness-borders.js`; `raw\borders-guides\<look>.json`, boxes with a visible border or a filled background):

- **Border width is already consistent.** Every container and control border is **1 px**. Where a box has a border on one side only, that border is a
  divider (header bottom, side panel top, hr). Nothing to change.
- **Corner radius is not.** Within one look, the same kind of card takes 3–4 radii:

| look | left panel | menus | dialog | guide card | camera bars | controls |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| aqua | 18 | 18 | 18 | 18, **no border** | pill | buttons pill, inputs 10 |
| skyline, skyline-night | 16 | **14** | 16 | **14**, no border | 18 | buttons 13 / 9 / pill, inputs 7 |
| aurora | 16 | **14** | 16 | **14**, no border | 16 | buttons 10 / 8, inputs 8 |
| glass | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0, no border | 0 | 0 |
| sigil | 0 | **6** | 0 | **8**, no border | 0 | 2 |

- The HUD readout box is 8 in every look, and borderless. It floats on the 3D view, so I left it.

**Proposal: one card radius per look** on the left panel, menus, dialogs and guide card. Each look keeps the value most of its cards already use:
aqua 18, skyline/skyline-night/aurora 16, glass 0, sigil 8. The guide card gets the 1 px border its siblings have. After
(`raw\borders-after\*.json`): each look's four card kinds read one radius and border 1. Controls are left alone. Buttons as pills beside rounded
inputs is a deliberate split in aqua, and evening it out is a taste call, so I didn't render it.

**Cost:** a radius token and 4 selectors. Each theme file declares `border-radius` 9–14 times (`grep -c border-radius app/themes/*.css` → aqua 12,
aurora 14, glass 9, skyline 14, skyline-night 14; index.html 14). Only the card rules change, so inferred: about 3–5 lines per theme, about 20 in
all. Plus 2 lines in index.html (the default 8 and the guide card's border).

Per look, counted as an element in a region (`raw\before\<look>.json`, "colour"):

| look | gradient fills | box-shadow/glow | text-shadow | saturated text | saturated fill |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| skyline-night | 64 | 86 | 8 | 21 | 26 |

- **SIGIL already is the guide's pass 4.** Its only saturated colour is data:
  - the pressed camera toggles (Auto, Off);
  - the HUD numbers;
  - the Layouts dialog's problem line;
  - its main button.
- The four skins draw:
  - a gradient pill on every button, summary and input (about 50 per screen);
  - glows on the same;
  - skyline tints every text in its blue (264).
- Colour that carries data, kept in both after variants:
  - state: a pressed toggle, a ticked box, an open menu's summary;
  - the warning tokens `--bad`, `--warn`, `--ok` and `--mine` (index.html:34): reds and ambers, a panel error, Layouts' problem line;
  - in the 3D view, out of scope, the handle colours (`app/core/handles.js`: width red, and so on).

**Two variants, and my recommendation is the second.**

- **`colour-strip\`**: the guide's pass 4 in full. Every look collapses to near-SIGIL: grey panels and grey controls, with only the state colours left.
  That removes what the user picks a skin for: D381's "a skin per melody". The page is cleaner, but it's the wrong trade for this app.
- **`colour-controls\`**: decoration off the controls only. No gradient pills, no glows, control text in the look's neutral text colour. The panels
  keep their tint and texture, so each look stays itself, and the pressed toggles are now the only bright fills.

The fix that went into both colour renders: a control not in a state and not disabled takes the body's text colour. Without it, aqua's dark-on-light
pills and skyline's white-on-blue Export button rendered unreadable. The same would bite the real change, so it belongs in the cost.

**Cost (colour-controls):** in each of the 5 theme files, the button, summary and input fill and shadow rules. About 6–10 lines each, roughly 40 in
all. `theme.test.js` and `texture-page.test.js` (the 4.5:1 contrast row) need a rerun.

## What I'd pick, in the guide's order

1. **Borders:** one card radius per look. Cheapest, with no visible downside; the guide card gaining its border is the most visible part.
2. **Edges:** the File and Settings menus to one text edge. 2 rules.
3. **Copy:** the five CSS rules and the four strings. That removes the shouting the guide is about, and the app has none of its other copy faults.
4. **Colour, the controls variant:** keeps each look as a look and makes the pressed toggles the only bright fills. I'd **not** take the full strip.
5. **Spacing scale:** it's real and the declared values drop from 20 to 9, but on its own it barely changes what the eye sees (32 → 30 gaps). Worth it
   only together with a row-height scale, which is a type change and wasn't rendered.

## Corrections, mine

- Six looks, not five. The chair's correction 1, confirmed from `settings.js:20`.
- My first gap overlay counted the items of closed menus. Chrome keeps layout boxes for a closed `<details>`, so a column of 6 px gaps from the File
  menu showed over the 3D view. Fixed: elements inside a closed `details` outside its summary are skipped. The overlays in `spacing-gaps\` are from
  after the fix.
- My first text-edge reading took a button's box edge as its text start. The menus' centred labels only showed once it read the first text node.
  Fixed before the edges renders.
- My first colour-controls renders left aqua's pill text dark on dark and skyline's Export button white on light. Fixed by giving such controls the
  body text colour.
- That first fix was too narrow. It caught gradient controls only, and skyline's Export to Assetto Corsa is a solid blue fill. After a crop of
  `colour-controls\skyline-main-after.png` showed its text still invisible, the rule became: every control not in a state and not disabled takes the
  body text colour, and loses its fill if that fill was a gradient or saturated. colour-strip, colour-controls and all were rendered a third time
  after that.
- In the strip variant, skyline's header went see-through blue. Its whole fill is a gradient over a transparent colour, so removing the gradient showed
  the 3D sky behind it. In strip mode, a container that loses its gradient now takes the look's `--panel`, greyed. colour-strip was rendered a fourth
  time; colour-controls doesn't use that branch.
- A load race: one `all` run threw on skyline-night, because the palette inputs weren't built yet when the track was made ("Illegal invocation" in
  `setVal`). The builder step now waits up to 10 s for the four inputs. No render from the failed run was kept.
- The palette's four buttons wrapped under a 12 px side padding. The scale's 4×8 button rule comes from that render.
