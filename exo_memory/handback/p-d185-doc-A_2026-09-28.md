## 1. What was built, against the spec's GO

**§1 the document** (`document.js`), schema `t180b.core/1`:
- pieces of clamped cubic B-splines in each piece's own arc length, one shared knot vector, channels κh, κv, φ, w and r;
- **every road joint is C1 in every channel by construction.** P0 is the previous end value, and P1 is set by its slope
  through c′(0) = 3(P1 − P0)/t4 (ref 09 §1). `checkDoc` refuses a joint that is not C1 (`JOINT`);
- canonical text: a fixed key order, one piece per line, quantised on entry, a version check;
- undo as history (commit, drag as one entry, undo, redo);
- a flight piece (gap, drop, land).

**Loading a real track** (`fromPositionFit(fit, read)`): from D184's `pieces.json`:
- κh and κv come from the position spline's derivatives, re-parameterised by true arc length (ref 09 §4);
- φ comes from the read's normals against the geometry's gravity frame;
- w comes from the read;
- r is the family's rate;
- the lap is returned OPEN, for E's `close.js` to close.

**§2 extend** (`extend.js`):
- the continuation v + m·s from the end value and slope, blended to any handle target by Bloss (1 − S)·cont + S·T,
  S = 3u² − 2u³ (ref 02 §4, ref 09 §2);
- no handle means pure continuation.

**§6 the adapter** (`adapter.js`): `toPath(doc)` → `{ segments, path }`:
- `path = src/geom buildPath(segments, { closed, start })`, so the samples are the same `{ s, seg, pos, T, L, U, kvec, roll,
  bankG, grade }`;
- road pieces become ≤ 2 m clothoid segments carrying the profile;
- a flight becomes the old jump's gap plus validation's landing ramp;
- the cross-section is C's measured floor with each quarter's rise capped by r(s)·w/8 (ref 09 §3). At the family's own
  rate it IS `fonts.fontProfile` (tested for all three families at 24, 31 and 45 m).

## 2. Deviations, with why
1. **The adapter's field list follows the SPEC, not the packet's paraphrase.** The packet listed `{ s, pos, T, N, B, kappa,
   grade }`. The master's GO §6 says `{ s, pos, T, L, U, kvec, roll, bankG, grade }`, "that src/geom builds". Only the
   latter reuses preview, mesh and export unchanged.
2. **Roll between segment ends is src/geom's smoothstep, not the cubic.**
   - The bound was derived and put on the shelf first (ref 09 §5): |S(t) − t| ≤ 1/(6√3) ≈ 0.0962 of a segment's roll change,
     plus |φ″|·len²/8.
   - MEASURED on the test track: 1.02e-3 rad (0.06°) at a 0.31°/m bank rate. The line moves < 1 cm over 530 m against
     0.25 m segments.
3. **A real track's bank comes from the read's normals, not the fit's bank.** D184's bank is against a rotation-minimising
   frame whose starting normal is not in `pieces.json`, so it cannot be turned into a gravity roll.
4. **After a jump, a loaded track sits one landing ramp further on** than the real one, because the core adds validation's
   ramp. This is stated in the code, and is for the spec's test 1 to measure.
5. **I first wrote "a track cannot end in a flight", and removed it.** It made placing a jump impossible: appending the
   flight leaves it last, and the adapter's landing ramp already puts the head on road.
