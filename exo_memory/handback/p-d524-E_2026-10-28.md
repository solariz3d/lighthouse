## 2. The fix

- **The line vertex shader** (`renderer.js` LVS) now pulls each line vertex toward the camera by `LINE_BIAS = 2e-5` in normalised depth (`gl_Position.z -= 2e-5 * w`).
- **What that is in metres:** with the near plane at 0.5 m (`app/camera/math.js:34`), it is 2e-5·d² metres toward the camera at distance d: 2 mm at 10 m, 7 cm at 60 m, 45 cm at 150 m, 20 m at 1 km. That grows as the depth buffer's own steps do (24-bit here, `DEPTH_BITS` read from the page).
- **The cause:** look.js lifts a line 3 cm along the road's normal. Seen nearly edge-on (a low camera, or a bump's tilted rows), those 3 cm part line and road by under a millimetre along the view ray.
- **Scope:** the grid and marker passes run without depth and are unchanged. The export is untouched.
