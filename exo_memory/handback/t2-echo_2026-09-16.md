## 1 · THE FALSE CLAIMS

### Claim 4 — "failing to write a lap makes the check PASS, which is the asymmetry that makes it safe" · FALSE, and it inverts the tool's whole repair

**Truth: failing to write a lap makes the check FIRE.** `consonance/tools/boundary-check.js:32-38`, the header
block titled THE INVERSION THIS TOOL IS BUILT ON:

> *"The numerator is `lap.jsonl`, which IS self-reported — but here under-reporting makes the check FIRE rather
> than pass. That asymmetry is the whole repair:*
>     *the old check:  no lap row -> reads GREEN   (absence accepted)*
>     *this check:     no lap row + a dispatch that rendered -> FIRES  (absence classified)"*

The note states the **old** check's behaviour, attributes it to the replacement, and then praises it as the safety
property. In code: `boundary-check.js:243-245` — `for (const a of board.arrivals) if (!coveringLap(laps, a.ts))
unsealed.push(a)`, and any non-empty `unsealed` returns `EXIT.FIRES`. A missing lap ledger does not exempt anything;
`readLaps` returns `{rows: [], missing: true}` and every arrival then goes unsealed.

**This is the most serious error in the note.** A reader who believed it would conclude the instrument is safe
*because* it can be silenced by not writing a lap — which is precisely the defect that killed its predecessor
(`boundary-check.js:24-30`, defect 3: *"ITS SIGNAL WAS AN ABSENCE ON A LEDGER THE LICENSED BEHAVIOUR SUPPRESSES"*).

### Claim 17 — "there is a window to fill and a rate to reach before it can fire" · FALSE

**Truth: it fires on the first unsealed dispatch. There is no window and no rate.** `boundary-check.js:245-247`:

    if (unsealed.length) {
      say(`FIRES - ${unsealed.length} of ${N} dispatch(es) rendered with NO sealed lap open.`);

No threshold appears anywhere in the file. The registered predicate is singular —
`exo_memory/loop/boundary_falsifier_2026-08-28.md:52`: *"If **a** chair dispatch renders in a receiving pane while
no lap holds a sealed guess, then work left…"*

**And claim 17 has its reasoning backwards as well.** "A window to fill and a rate to reach" is the shape of the
*predecessor* — "three consecutive cycles" — and removing it is the point of the replacement. The note gives the
replacement the discarded structure and offers "because the check is a boundary test" as the reason, when being a
boundary test is exactly why it needs no window.

### Claim 6 — "a brief pasted into a pane by hand appears in the denominator, so the rate it prints is exact" · FALSE, inverted, and self-contradicting

**Truth: it does not appear, and the denominator is explicitly a floor.** `boundary-check.js:63-66`, under
WHAT IT CANNOT SEE / NON-CHAIR HANDOFFS:

> *"Only `[chair:...]` arrivals count. A brief pasted into a pane by hand, a file carried to the other machine, a
> pane-to-pane handoff — all left the room and NONE of them appear. **The denominator is a floor.**"*

Enforced at `boundary-check.js:91` (`const CHAIR = /^\s*\[chair:/`) and applied at `:161`
(`if (!CHAIR.test(o.text)) return;`). Corroborated at `boundary_falsifier_2026-08-28.md:18`: *"**The denominator is
a floor.** The true rate can only be worse."* "Exact" is the one word the source rules out.

### Claim 15 — "reads whether a guess existed and also whether the guess was any good" · FALSE

**Truth: it cannot read whether the guess was any good.** `boundary-check.js:67-68`, A GOOD SEAL: *"It reads that a
guess existed and was sealed before the dispatch. **It cannot read whether the guess was any good. Four junk paths
pass.**"* The tool prints the same limit on every passing run, `:255`: *"It does not say the seals were good, only
that they existed before the work left."* `sealedLaps` (`:107-116`) tests only `stage === 'open'`,
`Array.isArray(r.guess)` and `r.guess.length > 0` — a non-empty array, never its content.

### Claim 12 — "struck because it was a boundary test aimed at a presence harm" · FALSE, exactly inverted

**Truth: a PRESENCE test aimed at a BOUNDARY harm.** `boundary-check.js:16-19`, defect 2: *"It was a PRESENCE test

The two words are not interchangeable and the direction is the finding: the harm was at the boundary, the test

**This machine's board spans the whole of the named window — it begins on 2026-06-30, the day the window is said to

**The observation that follows, and it bears on claims 7 and 9.** `blindOverlaps` (`boundary-check.js:180-192`)

is empty at every launch, so blind rows may never have been mirrored here, or the window may have been the other
machine's. **Which of those it is, is not resolvable from this disk, and it is the thing a builder should check
