## What was built (dreamzone-3 `b1-lean-c`, one commit on e091beb)

- **`game/body_lean.h` / `.cpp`, new: the one law.**
  - `lean_asked(a, grip)` = atan(clamp(a, ±grip g)/g).
  - `BodyLean::step`: the drawn lean goes to that at no more than **300 degrees a second**. Off its feet it goes back to upright
    the same way.
  - `tip_about_feet(pose, feet, lean, floor)`:
    - every joint above the ankles turns about the line through the feet along the body's forward;
    - a foot that is down (its lowest joint within 5 cm of its floor) stays where it is, and a foot in the air turns with the
      body;
    - the hips sink, along the tipped body's own up, as far as the longer leg needs;
    - each leg reaches again.
  - A kind's numbers are one row, `LeanRow {grip, rate}`: `kPlayerLean` and `kChaserLean`.
  - A chaser's grip is its chase body's own (`chase::Body::grip_mu`, the number the grip law caps its turns by). The row stands in
    only where the simulation has none.
- **The hunter and the Dark:**
  - `gather_poses` (game/world/world_app.cpp) carries the chase's own `speed x turn` and `grip_mu` on the pose.
  - `ease_poses` steps a `BodyLean` per chaser.
  - The stage passes `motion.tip`, and `gait_pose` ends with `tip_about_feet`.
  - With the switch on, DrawnChaser's bank and the chain's bank shares are zeroed. Its pitch, look and springs are as they were.
- **The player's body:**
  - `Body::set_lean`, from the world setting.
  - `Body::tick` steps the same `BodyLean` from the change in the player's velocity across the tick, along its right, on its
    feet when the move is Run (run, stand, walk). It ends with the same `tip_about_feet`.
- **One function** (the packet's registered falsifier: "this lap is wrong if the player's body and the hunter's take their lean
