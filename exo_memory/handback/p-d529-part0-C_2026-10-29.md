## 2. What this means for Part 1 (for the librarian's pick)

1. **The sliced build (Part 1 as planned) would take** about 1.6 s (CRAZY TEST) or 4.4 s (ELYSIUM) off the one task.
2. **Two costs stay in that task, or right after it, and Part 1 does not touch them:**
   - **The texture set is rebuilt on every open: about 0.5–0.57 s,** in the same task. It is the procedural maker making its textures (`makeTexture`, `fbm`, `hash`), not the track. Sliced build alone, the open would still hold the thread about 0.6–0.8 s on CRAZY TEST.
   - **The first frame that draws the new road: 322 ms (CRAZY TEST) and 596 ms (ELYSIUM),** then a second frame of 92 / 186 ms.
     - These are the AC look's draw, the road lines and the tangents made on first use, plus the buffer uploads (`renderer.js draw`, `drawAc`, `drawRoadLines`, `chunkTangents`, `bufferFor`).
     - The D421c slicing already makes lines and tangents ahead for a sculpt release (`preview.js` stepExact). The same would apply to the open's sliced build.
     - The GPU upload itself is not in this profile's page-thread time beyond the GL calls.
3. **I would take Part 1 as planned, plus:**
   - the texture set's rebuild moved off the open's task, or made once;
   - the measurement repeated on the same rig.

   **What would change my mind:** if the texture rebuild turns out to run only on a first open in a session, it is a one-time cost and can wait.

## 3. Corrections
