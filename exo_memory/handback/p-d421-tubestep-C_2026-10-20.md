## 2. What was left after O's cut, and why

The **segment cache key** (`toSegments` `inputsOf`) holds the cross-section carried in from the piece before, `lastProfile` and `lastPlain`, and it
compared them **by object identity**. A brush step rebuilds the brushed piece, which hands on a new object even when its last row is unchanged.
So the next piece missed and was rebuilt, it handed on a new object in turn, and so on to the lap's end, at every step.

O's memo made each of those rebuilds cheaper, since `commonFractions` became a hit, but the rebuilds still happened: `valuesAt`, `tubeProfile`, the
segments and `deepFreeze` for every untouched piece.

The cut compares those two inputs **by value**: arrays and plain objects key by key, every number by `Object.is`, anything else by identity
(`sameValue`, `BY_VALUE = {10, 13}`). `piece` reads a carried cross-section only for its numbers and strings, so a hit returns what a rebuild
would.

How it was found:
- before O's cut, `who.js` counted the callers of `commonFractions` per 4a step: the four untouched quarter-turns were converted
  twice in `brush`'s `pieceOffsets` and once in `resolvedOf`;
- after O's cut, the same rebuilds remained, which the numbers in §3 show.
