## What changed
- **`src/core/adapter.js` (`legacySeg`).** For a legacy segment the adapter computes the cross-section at the segment's START and at its END. If they are the same cross-section (every u and ψ within 1e-12: a constant width and r, or an r that does not bind the rise cap) the segment is **exactly today's**: `profileAt` at the middle width, `blend: null`, no new key. Otherwise it is a **CHORD**: `profile` = the cross-section at its END, `blend: { from: <start>, s0: 0, length }` (the mesh's smoothstep weight, as the cup's chord segments), and the segment carries `chord: true`. `lastProfile` (the next piece's / a flight's / the cup morph's join) is the chord's END row; `legacyFirst` and `legacyLast` (the lap-seam fades) are the first segment's START row and the last segment's END row. The header comment names it.
- **Readers evaluate the blend, as for the cup** (`readsBlend(seg) = seg.cup || seg.chord`; never a word document's font transition). My own D190 reader files, one line each: `src/geom/profile.js` (`readsBlend` added and exported; `readAt` and `jointSteps` use it), `src/validate/index.js` (`constAt`: `P.readsBlend(g) && g.blend`), `app/core/coreshell.js` (the water's `profilerOf`: `Prof.readsBlend(g) && g.blend`). `src/markers/layout.js` and `src/export/fromwords.js` call `readAt`, so they follow with no edit; `markers/place.js`, `geom/pitlane.js` and `geom/mesh.js` already evaluated any blend.
