- **Toe:** the RTOE and LTOE markers. The figure is the 3D speed in the lab frame, by central difference of the low-passed
  marker (2nd-order Butterworth run forward and back, as in the spot-check's `angles.js`). It counts only frames where the
  marker was seen at both neighbours, so no gap-fill line makes a peak.
- **Running speed:** the pelvis centre, the mean of RASI, LASI and SACR. It is the horizontal distance from its first seen
  frame to its last, over the time. It matches the named speed within 0.03 m/s.
- **The cut-off:** moves the ratio by at most 0.04 (10 to 25 Hz), so the filter is not making or hiding the peak.
- **The ratio is flat with speed:** 2.20 to 2.37, highest at the jog. At sprint speed the runner's toe peaks at about
  **2.2 to 2.25 times** the body's speed.
