## What was built
**D244, handles on the Extend ghost** (`app/core/handles.js`, wired in `app/core/panel.js`, the preview's new `ghostInfo()` and the read-only `t180:ghost-request` / `t180:handles-request` events):
- Six kinds, each with sides on BOTH sides: **length** (centreline, far end), **width** (both edges, far end), **bank** (both edges, mid piece), **cup** (just inside the centre, both sides, mid piece), **turn** (both edges, three quarters along), **climb** (centreline, three quarters along). Colours and the double arrow follow the keeper's marks (white, red, blue, green, purple, yellow).
- A drag types the value into the matching Extend field and fires the field's own `oninput`, so the ghost, the readout and the cup-or-tube rule follow exactly as typing does. **It is no document edit: nothing reaches Undo until Extend** (asserted: same `history.present`, same `past.length` after ten drags). Both mirrored handles change the same field: outward is positive for width and cup, LEFT for turn from either side, the left edge up and the right edge down for bank. Shift is a tenth of the speed, Ctrl snaps (5°, 10 m of length, 5 m of width). Hover shows "bank 12.0°" and the cursor. Rates: length 1 m/m, width 2 m per m of one edge, cup 2°/m, turn and climb 0.5 °/100 m per m, bank = the edge's rise over the half-width.
- A press ON a handle is the handle's (capture phase, `stopImmediatePropagation`); a press anywhere else is still the brush's or the camera's. Right-drag is the camera's.
- **A "drag handles" box (default ON) keeps the ghost up while it is on**; with it off the ghost shows only while the pointer is on Extend, as before. **This is a visible default change on the main screen. It is yours to overrule.**

**D244b, Sculpt** (shell: `setSculpt`, `sculptChannels`, `beginSculpt`, `sculptTo`, `endSculpt`, `sculptInfo`; guard: `app/core/centreline.js`):
- A "sculpt (shape only)" box at the top of the Brush section (a new section heading broke `core-readout-display`'s exact section list, so it went into Brush instead of changing that test). On: the brush is the value brush on **phi, c, w, e, s, r, t** only; turn, climb, height and sideways are refused BY NAME (`SCULPT_CHANNEL`), no drag opens.
- ONE selected road piece (the D240 selection) gets its handles (bank and width both edges, cup where the piece has one). A drag is the brush centred on the piece and as wide as it is: **one undo step, joints untouched and C1, neighbours the very same objects** (measured for pieces of 60, 100, 119, 120 and 200 m: `app/test/core-sculpt.test.js` row 8).
- **The guard:** every step checks that nothing the path is made of moved (every segment's length, k0, k1, kp0, kp1, heartline, heartline1, and the roll fields where a heartline is in play, the start pose, the h and l offsets) and **refuses by name, `SCULPT_MOVES_CENTRELINE`**, leaving the drag at its last good step; at the end of a drag both centrelines are built and compared bit for bit, and a drag that moved it is dropped whole.
- **A limit the guard found, not me:** on a CLOSED tube (sweep 300 to 360) bank, width and sweep ARE refused, because the tube's roll axis is its own heartline (width / 2π, growing with the sweep): they feed the route. An open tube's are not. Sculpt still offers wall rise on a closed tube.

2. **A far handle was read as "edge-on".** The far end of a ghost seen from behind has an axis of 0.3 px per metre, under my 0.8 threshold, so the length handle did nothing from the Build view. Found by the window run; the threshold is now 0.05 px/m (a truly end-on axis is still ~0); `handles.test.js` row 5 asserts both.
3. **A stale half-width** (the road's width, captured at layout) made the two mirrored bank handles disagree (7.4° against 7.2°). Found by my own panel row 11; now read when asked.
4. **My expectations about a closed tube were wrong and the guard was right** (see above): I had listed sweep and bank as sculptable on a 360° tube.
5. The Sculpt "brush widened" note said a thing that was not true for Sculpt on a short piece (the change stayed inside it); it is only said now when a neighbour really changed.
6. **Two of the harness anchors went NOT APPLIED because of my edits** (the chair's rule exists because of exactly this): the same lesson I had written down on D240, and I broke it again (a copied string, a rewritten line). Caught by running the harnesses once at the end, as asked; fixed in my code.
7. **Tooling:** my first hand mutation run was killed mid-mutant and left `app/core/centreline.js` mutated in the worktree; I restored it from the commit (checked: `git diff` showed only the mutant line, `git checkout --` put it back, `git status` clean) and re-ran on an archive copy.
