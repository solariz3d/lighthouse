## 0. Two premises corrected first

1. **The builder does nothing for the sky today, and "its six looks" never reach AC.** The six looks (aqua, skyline,
   skyline-night, glass, aurora, sigil) are the builder's UI themes (`app/settings.js:20`, `t180.settings.theme`). The
   exporter never reads them (`grep -rnE "theme|look" src/export/*.js` → no use of the UI look), and it writes no sky,
   horizon or `ext_config.ini` (`grep -rnE "horizon|sky|ext_config|WEATHER" src/export` → only an AI-line "horizontal").
   `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:159` names ext_config for start lights as unverified. In AC today the sky is drawn by AC or CSP
   weather, not by the track. So a pack's sky work starts from zero; nothing exists to extend.
2. **There is no primary Kunos document for tracks.** The AC install's SDK (`G:\SteamLibrary\steamapps\common\assettocorsa\sdk`)
   ships the car pipeline (`dev/car_pipeline_2.0rev/AC_Pipeline_PUB_Rev2.0.pdf`), the audio pipeline, ksEditor and one
   FBX export-settings image (`editor/docs/fbx_max_export_settings.png`). It has no track pipeline. Kunos's own primary track references on disk are
   `system/data/surfaces.ini` and ksEditor itself. Everything else about tracks comes from CSP's wiki (primary for CSP
   features), AcTools (the open kn5 reader Content Manager uses), and community forums (secondary). The car pipeline
   PDF's text is font-encoded and unreadable without a PDF tool, which is not installed (INSTALL NOTHING), so its
   budgets are not used here.

## 1. How a track ships scenery

**Folder and files.**
- A track is `content/tracks/<id>/`, with a main kn5 named after the folder. With layouts or several kn5,
  `models.ini` / `models_<layout>.ini` lists `[MODEL_n] FILE= POSITION= ROTATION=`. The main kn5 must match the folder
  name or the track will not start. Folder names are at most 32 characters, layout names 15, lowercase Latin.
  Sources: [assettocorsamods "Track layouts - How-To"](https://assettocorsamods.net/goto/post?id=21922) and
  [post 21926](https://assettocorsamods.net/goto/post?id=21926); [basic guide](https://assettocorsamods.net/threads/build-your-first-track-basic-guide.12/page-20);
  the builder's own research `docs/research/01_ac_export_pipeline.md` §2, and `src/export/trackfiles.js:85-91`, which
  already writes `models.ini` this way.
- **Splitting scenery into its own kn5 is normal practice.** The main kn5 holds the shared geometry, extra kn5 hold
  layout- or part-specific geometry, and all pieces share one coordinate frame. Source: the same
  [layouts thread](https://assettocorsamods.net/goto/post?id=21925).
  inferred: a pack can be one more kn5 named in `models.ini`, without touching the road's kn5.

**Object-name prefixes and collision.**
- **Physics meshes are named `<digit><KEY>…`** (`1ROAD`, `1KERB`, `1GRASS`, `1SAND`, `1WALL`). KEY is a surface from
  `surfaces.ini`. A leading 0 or no digit means visual only, and materials do not affect physics.
  Sources: Kunos `system/data/surfaces.ini` (ROAD, GRASS, KERB, SAND defined, on disk); [hagn99 surfaces.ini notes](https://www.sites.google.com/site/hagn99/assettocorsa/modding/tracks/data/surfaces-ini)
  (WALL for collision objects); the [GTPlanet thread](https://www.gtplanet.net/forum/threads/assetto-corsa-pc-mods-general-discussion.307899/post-13154844)
  (plain `WALL` does not collide, `1WALL` does, suffixes allowed, e.g. `1WALL_46_s0`); `docs/research/01_ac_export_pipeline.md` §3.
- **Markers** are empties named `AC_START_n`, `AC_PIT_n`, `AC_TIME_n_L/R`, `AC_HOTLAP_START_0`, `AC_AB_*`, `AC_CREW_n`,
  `AC_AUDIO_*`, `AC_POBJECT_*`. Source: `docs/research/01_ac_export_pipeline.md` §3 (from AcTools and the guides).
  For a pack: inferred: props must never use these names, and must never start with a digit unless meant to collide.
- **Collidable props cost performance.** "Collidable trees can halve FPS"; walls should be simple, non-renderable and
  low-poly. Source: `docs/research/01_ac_export_pipeline.md` §3 (community FAQ). So a prop is a visual mesh (`0…` or no
  digit), plus, only where a car must hit it, a separate simple `1WALL…` box that is not rendered.
- **A kn5 mesh carries `IsRenderable`, `IsVisible`, `CastShadows`, `IsTransparent`, `Layer`, `LodIn`, `LodOut` and a
  bounding sphere.** Source: [AcTools Kn5Node.cs](https://github.com/gro-ove/actools/blob/master/AcTools/Kn5File/Kn5Node.cs).
  A physics-only mesh is written with `IsRenderable=false` (`docs/research/01_ac_export_pipeline.md` §1).

**LODs.**
- Each kn5 mesh has `LodIn` / `LodOut` floats (AcTools, above). UNVERIFIED: their unit (metres from the camera is the
  common reading, but the source has no comment). Community advice: low-poly versions for distant objects, set up "with
  one of the ini files" ([assettocorsamods "Track optimization"](https://assettocorsamods.net/threads/track-optimization-high-fram-rate.723/),
  quoted in search results; the page was down, HTTP 530, when I fetched it). UNVERIFIED: which ini that is.
- **CSP trees have a defined LOD scheme:** three LODs at about 10K, 6K and 3K triangles, each a single mesh with its
  pivot at the middle of the base, plus billboards for distant trees, made automatically. Source: CSP wiki
  [Tracks – Trees](https://github.com/ac-custom-shaders-patch/acc-extension-config/wiki/Tracks-%E2%80%93-Trees).

## 2. Trackside props and their budgets

**What CSP gives without our assets.**
- **Grass:** Grass FX spawns grass procedurally around the camera on the meshes or materials listed
  (`[GRASS_FX] GRASS_MATERIALS`, `GRASS_MESHES`, `OCCLUDING_MESHES`, colour masks), and needs no grass models.
  An optional texture atlas (`TEXTURE`, `TEXTURE_GRID`) can be supplied. Source: CSP wiki
  [Tracks – Grass FX](https://github.com/ac-custom-shaders-patch/acc-extension-config/wiki/Tracks-%E2%80%93-Grass-FX).
  So a pack's ground cover can be a green ground mesh plus config, not authored grass.
- **Trees:** `[TREES] LIST_0 = trees/trees.txt` places trees by line (`tree: <model>.bin; x, y, z`), with variance and
  seed controls. The trees themselves are **track-supplied**: "CSP does not provide generic trees." They are built FBX →
  BIN with Content Manager's tree converter, each with a diffuse and a normal texture. They live in
  `content/tracks/<id>/extension/trees/`. "Hundreds of thousands of trees" are claimed manageable via LOD, billboards
  and instancing. Source: [Tracks – Trees](https://github.com/ac-custom-shaders-patch/acc-extension-config/wiki/Tracks-%E2%80%93-Trees).
  inferred: placed trees are a good fit for a generated track: positions come from the centreline, and no kn5 work is
  needed. They need CSP, which T-180 tracks already need.
- **Night lights:** `[LIGHT_...]` (by `POSITION`, `MESH` with `OFFSET`, or `LINE_FROM`/`LINE_TO`) and
  `[LIGHT_SERIES_...]` (from `MESHES` / `MATERIALS`, with `DIRECTION = NORMAL`). Defaults: `SPOT` 120, `RANGE` 40, `COLOR`
  1,1,1,40, `FADE_AT` 400. Lights are on at night by `CONDITION` (default `NightLights`). The cost is screen pixels lit,
  not light count. There are 15 high- and 16 low-resolution shadow slots, and moving shadowed lights rebuild shadows each
  frame. Source: CSP wiki [Tracks – Lights](https://github.com/ac-custom-shaders-patch/acc-extension-config/wiki/Tracks-%E2%80%93-Lights).
  `[CONDITION_...]` inputs include `TIME`, `SUN`, `AMBIENT` (1.0 at night), `RAIN`
  ([Tracks – Conditions](https://github.com/ac-custom-shaders-patch/acc-extension-config/wiki/Tracks-%E2%80%93-Conditions)).
  inferred: a night pack is mostly config over a lamppost or gantry mesh with an emissive material, so it is cheap to author.

**Budgets (no official numbers exist; these are the community's).**
- **Draw calls dominate.** One track author saw draw calls ("DIP") pass 4,000 as objects were added, and cut them by
  merging textures into atlases and reducing materials. The advice: keep a whole track under about 200 textures; 500 is
  too many. Source: search excerpts of [assettocorsamods "Maximize performance: merge all track objects"](https://assettocorsamods.net/threads/maximize-performance-merge-all-track-objects.777/)
  and [Track optimization](https://assettocorsamods.net/threads/track-optimization-high-fram-rate.723/) (pages down at fetch time, HTTP 530).
- **Textures:** DDS for release ("Just 1 PNG will cause stutters"). The ceiling is the GPU's (usually 8192), and 8192
  is strongly advised against. Source: same threads (excerpts).
  inferred: 2048 atlases per prop family, 1024 for small props.
- **Triangles:** the only sourced per-object figure is CSP's tree LODs (10K / 6K / 3K). About 3M vertices per track is
  known to work (`docs/research/01_ac_export_pipeline.md` §3, community FAQ). UNVERIFIED: per-prop budgets for
  barriers, kerbs or grandstands; none is published.
  inferred: a prop family is merged into few meshes per kn5, under one shared atlas, so it adds a handful of draw calls
  however many instances are placed. UNVERIFIED: whether CSP instances kn5 props the way it instances trees.

## 3. Sky and horizon

- **AC/CSP draw the sky; a track does not ship a skybox system.** The weather (Kunos's own, or CSP WeatherFX scripts
  such as Sol or Pure) renders sky, clouds and light. CSP's default WeatherFX is
  [ac-custom-shaders-patch/acc-weatherfx-base](https://github.com/ac-custom-shaders-patch/acc-weatherfx-base), and it
  renders transitions and rain client-side ([AssettoServer weather docs](https://xgamingserver.com/docs/assetto-corsa-freeroam/weather)).
- **What a track controls:** `[WEATHER_FX] SKY_BELOW_HORIZON` (default 0.2115; 1 gives a 360° sky). Source: CSP wiki
  [Tracks – General options](https://github.com/ac-custom-shaders-patch/acc-extension-config/wiki/Tracks-%E2%80%93-General-options).
  This matters for T-180 tracks, which are often high in the air, so the sky below the horizon shows.
- **Horizon objects are per track,** with no standard naming; fixes are made one track at a time
  ([GTPlanet, 2019](https://www.gtplanet.net/forum/threads/assetto-corsa-pc-mods-general-discussion.307899/post-12826711)).
  inferred: an "environment" in a pack is a distant backdrop (mountains, city silhouette, a ground plane far below),
  visual only, with no physics prefix, plus `SKY_BELOW_HORIZON` and optional fog or lighting config. It is not a sky texture.

## 4. Installing a pack next to a generated track

- **Everything a track needs, CSP config included, can live inside `content/tracks/<id>/`:** the kn5 files and
  `models*.ini` (§1); `extension/ext_config.ini` for CSP; and `extension/trees/` for trees (Trees page, above).
  CSP also reads track configs from `extension/config/tracks/` (and a `loaded/` subfolder that Content Manager
  auto-updates). Whether both load, or one overrides, is **disputed and UNVERIFIED** (forum posts at
  [GTPlanet p.4735](https://www.gtplanet.net/forum/threads/assetto-corsa-pc-mods-general-discussion.307899/page-4735)
  and [p.2143](https://www.gtplanet.net/forum/threads/assetto-corsa-pc-mods-general-discussion.307899/page-2143)).
  The track-folder copy is not auto-updated by CM/CSP (same thread).
  `[BASIC] IGNORE_OTHER_CONFIGS = 1` makes a track's config ignore others (General options page). One user warns it can
  drop other settings (grass, lighting) when misused (GTPlanet, same search).
- **So the install that touches nothing outside the track:** copy the pack's kn5 files into the generated track's own
  folder, add `[MODEL_n]` lines to its `models*.ini`, and write the pack's CSP sections into the track's
  `extension/ext_config.ini` (and `extension/trees/`). Never write to `extension/config/tracks/`, which is shared CSP
  state. inferred: the builder already writes that folder and `models.ini` (`src/export/trackfiles.js`), so the pack
  installs at export time, as more files in the same folder.
- UNVERIFIED: whether `models.ini` `FILE=` may point outside the track folder (a shared pack folder used by many
  tracks). Don't rely on it. A per-track copy costs disk space but is certain.
- **Two cautions.**
  - A track whose surfaces.ini carries `WAV_PITCH=extended-0` is CSP-only, and online servers check surfaces.ini
    integrity (`docs/research/01_ac_export_pipeline.md` §5). A pack must not touch surfaces.ini.
  - inferred: adding a kn5 changes what a server's checksum sees for that track, so on a server everyone needs the same pack.

## 5. Licensing

- **The AC EULA grants no mod rights at all.** It says "LICENSEE may not use, disclose, modify, reproduce or distribute
  the SOFTWARE except as expressly permitted", "ALL RIGHTS NOT EXPRESSLY GRANTED HEREIN ARE RESERVED BY KUNOS", and
  that use "FOR … COMMERCIAL PURPOSES, FOR FREE OR NOT" needs written consent; it never mentions mods, derivative works
  or the SDK. Source: [Steam EULA 244210](https://store.steampowered.com/eula/244210_eula_0). I found no Kunos statement
  on paid mods ([search notes](https://traxion.gg/assetto-corsa-evo-open-to-supporting-mods-in-a-more-creative-way-than-the-series-original/)
  cover only AC EVO, still undecided). Paid AC mods exist widely in practice; that is tolerance, not a grant.
  inferred: a pack of **our own original files**, which contains and derives from nothing of Kunos's, is the
  defensible ground. Whether selling it is acceptable to Kunos is the keeper's call. Asking Kunos in writing is the only
  certain answer. This is not legal advice.
- **What we must author ourselves:** every mesh and texture, unless it comes from a licence that allows sale. Never
  anything from Kunos content, another game, or another modder's track (the same line D487/D488 drew). inferred: no real
  brands or logos on banners or grandstands, because trademarks need their owners' licence.
- **Free asset licences that allow a paid pack:**
  - **Poly Haven: CC0.** "You can use our assets for any purpose", including "in a product you sell", with no credit
    required. The site's logos and renders are excluded ([polyhaven.com/license](https://polyhaven.com/license)).
  - **ambientCG: CC0 1.0.** "copy, modify, distribute … even for commercial purposes, all without asking permission",
    and "include the raw files in your project, for example a video game"; no credit required
    ([docs.ambientcg.com/license](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/)).
  - inferred: CC-BY assets are usable with attribution in the pack's notes. CC-BY-NC, "editorial", and Sketchfab's or
    marketplace standard licences that forbid redistribution of the raw files are not, because a kn5 carries the textures
    inside it (`docs/research/01_ac_export_pipeline.md` §1: kn5 embeds textures). Check each asset's own page.
- **A paid pack has no technical protection.** kn5 files are read by the open AcTools reader that Content Manager uses
  ([AcTools Kn5File](https://github.com/gro-ove/actools/tree/master/AcTools/Kn5File)), so anyone can unpack the meshes
  and textures. inferred: the value has to be the authoring and the builder integration, not the files' secrecy.

## 6. What this means for the merge (for the librarian and O)

- inferred: the cheapest, safest first environment is **config-heavy and mesh-light**: CSP trees placed from the
  centreline, Grass FX on a ground mesh, night lights on a few emissive props, `SKY_BELOW_HORIZON` and a distant
  backdrop kn5. All of it goes inside the track folder, nothing is collidable except simple `1WALL` boxes, and every asset
  is CC0 or our own.
- The UNVERIFIED points that one in-game test would settle: `LodIn`/`LodOut` units; whether both CSP config locations
  load; whether `models.ini` can reference outside the folder; and whether kn5 props get instanced.
