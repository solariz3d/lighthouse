Each test runs one commit against itself first. Each tree is a separate worktree at the same sha, alternated exactly as a pair would be. The commits:

From those samples, `analyse678.js` reports:
1. **The NULL control.** The registered rule must say "not told" both ways in every case. If a case is told on one commit against itself, the instrument tells noise there. That case's verdict in the real pairs is then void, and said as void.
2. **The resolution,** per case. This is the smallest uniform shift, on a 0.01 grid, that the registered rule tells when it is added to one side's real samples, each way, taking the larger. The rule may be unable to fire at that n even at full separation: 1/C(n1+n2, n2) ≥ α, for example 4 against 4 at α 0.01, which gives 1/70. Then the case is said as "cannot fire at this n".
3. **The sign test's resolution:** the smallest uniform cost on every case at once that reaches 8 of 9 (or 14 of 17).

A pair's "not told" is then said with what D would have told at that n.

The results go in an addendum, committed before the first real pair runs.

### The one departure: sculpt-frame cannot time under `--exclusive`

sculpt-frame's timed rows run only while BOTH heavy-run slots are held by live processes, and one of them must be the test's own run (`sculpt-frame.test.js`, `bothSlots` at lines 48–56 of the blob above, its comment from line 41, used at line 57). Under `--exclusive` this cannot happen:
- a child of the holder carries its token;
- a run carrying the token re-enters the holder's own slot (`heavy-run.js:433-436`);
- so nothing under the hold can hold the second slot.

A sculpt-frame session is therefore B's phase A:
- **`idle.js`** holds one slot at idle priority. Its cmd says "held IDLE on purpose", and it is started from the non-git `ev\` folder.
- **The runner** holds the other slot at BelowNormal. It is started from a worktree, so the two are two trees.
- **Both are marked `--timing`**, so every other seat's `--status` exits 1 while they stand. Every other heavy run waits on the slots, which is the same parking as a hold.
- **Before every run,** the runner checks that the other slot is my idle holder. It waits up to 30 min for that, or stops and says so. It also stops if the timed rows were skipped.

heavy-run's idle-holder guard (L202) names a holder that has used under 1 CPU-s in 5 min. It may name `idle.js` to a waiting seat. It does not take the slot over.
