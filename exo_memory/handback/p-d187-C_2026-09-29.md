## 2. The fix (`app/core/labels.js`)

- **(d): the head's own label is never culled.**
  - `layout()` gives an item marked `head: true` a place even when no ring around its anchor fits. The new `headPlace()` tries
    every place in the view on an 8 px grid (plus flush with the far edges), nearest the anchor first:
    - first, clear of the head's keep-out;
    - failing that, clear of only the head's point ± 7 px (M4 needs 6);
    - last, clamped inside the view.
  - It is placed first (priority −1, as before), so the other labels make way for it and never the reverse.
  - `mount()`'s frame now gives the head's piece an item **even when no station of it is on screen**. It is pinned at the head's
    projection clamped into the view, or at the top-left corner when the head is behind the camera.
- **(b): the box holds only an opaque background and its text:** `color:#ffffff; background:#0b0d11; border:0; border-radius:0;
  box-shadow:0 0 0 2px #0b0d11`. The same-colour ring outside the box keeps the road off the box's own edge pixels. Text against
  background is about 18:1 by formula.
