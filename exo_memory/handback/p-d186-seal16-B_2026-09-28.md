## Choices I made, stated
- **Test 1 has no alignment.** The spec says "the same track", and the exporter writes world coordinates. A moved track is a
  finding, not something to fit away.
- **Test 1 has coverage both ways.** Otherwise an export that lost half the road could still put every original station near SOME
  exported road.
- **Test 6 uses p95, plus a hard max of 150 ms.** A literal "every step < 50 ms" would let one garbage-collection pause decide the
  verdict. The max keeps a real freeze from hiding behind the p95.

  so a fresh reference costs nothing).
