## 2. The second press (E §4): which function, and real or a quirk

**Measured.**
- checked (`E-press9.out`, the shell's own `bankLap()` pressed 9 times on E's row 2 lap): p3's sampled max |phi| is 1.06, 1.92, 2.78, 3.65° after presses 1–4, and then 3.65° on presses 5–9, with nothing changed and no new step.
- E's "up to 1.9°" is press 2's sampled total.
- The drift is bounded and stops at press 5. inferred: the stop is p3's control-point bank passing ROLL_FREE (the sampled 3.65° is under the control polygon), which takes it out of `flat`.

**The mechanism** (src/core/finish.js on a4bfe52):
- `bankLapRun` :486 puts a road piece in `flat` when every phi is within ROLL_FREE (5°) of the start's bank. That set is how bankLap tells "upright, mine to bank" from "his own bank".
- `bankPlan` :233 gives each unpinned control point the whole target `bankOf(kh, share)`, the share of the balance bank, whatever bank the point already has.
- `banked()` :350–351 adds the plan onto the existing phi.
- So a piece that the first press banked by less than 5° is still "upright" on the second press, and gets its plan added again.

**Why p3, on that lap.** p3 is an ordinary 100 m `extend` after a Turn by (stored max |kh| 2.66e-4: the turn's ease-out tail), and it leads into the loop p4.
- p4 is held back by the level rule, so p3's far joint is pinned at no added bank, and the rate and acceleration limits keep p3's own bank small (1.06°).
- checked (`E-noloop-all.out`): the same lap with the loop replaced by a level 30° turn is idempotent. There p3 ends at 5.92° on press 1, past ROLL_FREE, so press 2 leaves it.
- checked (`E-twice.out`): flatLap and TEST 10 are idempotent too, because every piece the first press banks there either passes 5° or has no plan of its own on press 2.

**Real, not a quirk.** Nothing in p3 is special to the test.
- It needs a piece with some turn of its own (a turn's tail on the straight after it is the builder's ordinary output), next to a banked curve, whose first-press bank is held under 5° by a pinned joint.
- Pinned joints on a real lap: a loop or steep ramp (the level rule), a piece of his own bank, a pit-lane or tube piece, a withheld piece, a flip.
- TEST 10 has loops and his own banked pieces; on its current save, though, the second press changes nothing.

It is reachable only through Bank the lap (Close's press banks once and lands a closed lap, and Close is then disabled), so it arrives with this command. It is bounded: at most a few presses, to about ROLL_FREE on the piece concerned. Each extra press is one more undo step with the route unmoved.
