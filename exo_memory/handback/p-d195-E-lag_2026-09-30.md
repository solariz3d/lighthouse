## 1. The cause: the empty-track view does not draw at all, and input queues until a ghost appears

The librarian's candidates (a per-frame rebuild in the ghost, the labels layer or the readout) are **refuted** as the cause:
- checked: none costs anything measurable (§2);
- checked: the frame rate never drops.

**The actual mechanism.** checked in the real window, and in the code at `app/preview/preview.js` `ctx()`, 4ccdd58 lines 94–98: with NOTHING placed and no ghost, the camera's context is `null`. So every frame skips `rig.update` and `renderer.draw`:
- the view is frozen: no drawn pose, the last picture left on the canvas;
- the wheel and W change nothing on screen;
- a movement key pressed meanwhile arms the free-mode takeover, which then fires LATE, the moment a ghost appears (hovering Extend). Measured: the view was already in `free` when the ghost state began.
- Moving the mouse from Extend onto the canvas fires mouseleave, clears the ghost (`panel.js:115`) and freezes the view again.

- inferred: this freeze-then-late-jump is what the keeper called "very laggy … until the first piece is put down". Once a piece exists, ctx is never null, which fits "until the first piece".

## 3. The fix

- With nothing placed and no ghost, `ctx()` returns the track's **start**: new `startHead(start)`.
  - The start is the document's `resolved.start` (the equation core), or the origin, level, heading 0 (buildPath's default; the piece builder).
  - It uses the geometry's gravity frame at zero roll.
- The path is a one-sample path, so chase works.
- A ghost still takes precedence, and a placed track takes precedence over both.
