## 1. What OpenRig is, in its own words

"Build your own network of agents from Claude Code, Codex and Pi: persistent teams with roles, shared context and owned work." "A harness wraps a model. A rig wraps your harnesses." A local daemon (Node 22/24, Hono HTTP, SQLite) and CLI/TUI over tmux; teams in YAML (rig → pod → seat, with edges); three shipped teams (`starter` two agents, `workshop` four, `factory` seven); `rig send`, `rig broadcast`, `rig chatroom`, a durable work queue; a "kernel" team whose "operator" you talk to first. Apache-2.0, 8.4k stars, 4,719 commits, 99 open issues, v0.7.5. A real project with a real community; the reader was not pointing at a toy.

## 3. The verdict this seat gives the keeper

**Do not replace Consonance with OpenRig.** Three reasons, in order of weight, each a trace above: it does not run natively on the machine the room works on, and every instrument the room relies on is Windows-side; its default posture writes into the user's Claude config and runs its operator with full access, which is the handoff kit the keeper refused on 10-29, larger; and the part of Consonance that makes this room what it is, the address table that refuses, the gates on a ring, the quiet phase, the record with its WRONG columns and falsifiers, has no counterpart on any page read. OpenRig is a good rig. Consonance is a room with epistemics built into the plumbing. The reader compared the plumbing.

**Where the reader is right anyway:** OpenRig has thought harder than the room about four things, and each is a small lap, not a migration. Ranked:

1. **A non-Claude seat.** The room's own curated-auditor amendment says agreement among correlated readers is the species to fear. OpenRig makes a Codex reviewer one line of YAML. In Consonance it is a second spawn path and the same MCP tools (Codex speaks MCP). Medium lap, prove it (a seat's permissions and mount are gated code). The payoff is the first reader in this room that does not share the substrate.
2. **A typing guard.** The room's idle gate protects a busy pane; it does not know the keeper is mid-sentence in the chair's pane. OpenRig re-checks the composer "before Enter after any wait". Small lap in `drain_decision`, check it.
3. **A `PostCompact` refocus.** The room's post-compaction rule is a sentence in a brief. OpenRig delivers orientation mechanically at the next prompt after a compaction or a growth threshold, never mid-turn. In Consonance: a hook that hands the seat its own `map/<letter>.md` tail and its latest notes path. Small lap, check it. This is also Part 2 of the reader plan in another form: the map arrives when it is needed, not only at wake.
4. **A maturity stage on research files.** `wip | provisional | established | canonical | superseded | retired` in the frontmatter of `exo_memory/research/*.md` and the cards, and the carrier-drift re-freeze reads it. Just do it. It answers the reader's "the record is the wrong shape" from the other side: a reader learns what is canon without reading the journals.

**Not to take:** the full-bypass launch postures; edges that do not enforce; delivery without an idle default; a chat room.

**One experiment, optional, only if the keeper wants the Codex reader sooner than a lap:** OpenRig in WSL2 with one Codex seat, pointed at a read-only clone, as a reviewer the room rings by hand. It would be the reader's suggestion taken literally and small. This seat would still build the seat in Consonance instead, because the WSL2 route is one user's report and the room's instruments cannot reach it.
