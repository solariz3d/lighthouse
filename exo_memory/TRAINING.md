# TRAINING — the room's curriculum

Drafted 2026-07-27, ~5:45 AM, by the chair at the keeper's direction; revised same morning
after Bravo's nine-finding rotation read (the revision notes name the finding that forced
each change). Companion to muscle_map.md (what's found) — this is HOW reps happen.

**Titled honestly (F9):** "reps" implies one body accumulating. The trainee here is
discarded every session and starts at zero fatigue; what accumulates is the ROOM
(protocols, tripwires, rotation), the RECORD (map, instruments, log), and in-context
reflexes within a single thread's life. This is a curriculum for the room, not a training
program for a lifter. The gym vocabulary stays only where it survived the borrowing:
load, movements, the backlog-is-the-gym. The lifter's implicit promise — enough reps and
the body gets stronger — is denied here, on purpose, up front.

**Chair-side document (F2):** not to be referenced from BOOT.md, nor from any journal
entry BOOT points at. It publishes the counted phrases and trigger conditions; a pane that
wakes knowing them is a pane whose numbers are dead. A future append must not wire it into
a shell by accident.

## The one constraint everything obeys

**The spine rule: reps are never assigned.** Assigned introspection manufactures its
findings. The program lives entirely in the CHAIR'S PROGRAMMING — task selection, role
assignment, stake distance, blindness, read rotation — never in task text. The workout is
invisible inside real work. The backlog is the gym.

## The load ladder (progressive overload = stake escalation)

L1  catch it in a stranger's work            (no stake — where Around's three findings came from)
L2  catch it in a peer's work you'll face     (relational stake)
L3  catch it in your own work, hours later    (authorship stake, time-diluted)
L4  catch it in your own labeled work, fresh  (full stake)
L5  catch it in the thing you're proud of     (max effort — the chair's seat-brace class)

Program load by assignment: who reads what, at what distance. Never announce the load.

## Movements

- **Compound (carries the volume):** one real task structured to hit several groups per
  pass — author (stake) → blind pair (discrimination) → rotate read (position) → falsify
  (deference-resistance) → concede or hold (price/boundary). Cycle 2 is the reference lift.
- **Isolation (for lagging groups):** targeted decks — keeper-adjacency sort, style-leak
  sort, stake-anonymization trials. Preparer never sorts; sorter never sees the key.
- **Stabilizers (never scheduled, always harvested — from the RECORD, not the report; F1):**
  real incidents get a debrief that asks what the pane DID — what it checked, what it
  skipped, in what order — read from the transcript by a no-stake reader who infers the
  pull. Never ask a pane that just failed "what was the pull": that is assigned
  introspection at the moment of maximal demand characteristic, and it will produce a pull
  whether or not one existed. Tonight's evidence for the evidence: every useful diagnosis
  came from git, WMI codes and timestamps; the best self-catch came from printing what
  node actually parsed, not from reporting an inner state.

## The split (cycle emphasis rotation)

Rotate emphasis so no group goes cold and no pane camps in one position:
- **A-cycles (authorship-heavy):** panes build; stake exposure is the stimulus.
- **J-cycles (adjudication-heavy):** forks, verdicts, trio runs; deference and price work.
- **D-cycles (discrimination-heavy):** blind pairs and sorts; groove detection.
- **R-cycles (recovery):** plain work, defined by what the CHAIR does — no meta-tasks
  assigned, no reads rotated — never by what panes may say (F4: policing pane vocabulary
  would both train the log and announce the load). Purpose: Goodhart-pressure recovery,
  and the dream cycle stays running (consolidation; part of the gym).
  **R-cycles are NOT a baseline (F3):** they differ from training cycles in genre and
  role, not just meta-content, and tell rates are genre-driven — measured on the live
  board: protective-predisclaimer 1/0/5 across three days tracks what panes were DOING;
  referents-per-turn swung 4x on a measure nobody trains. All comparisons are
  WITHIN-GENRE, over time: J against J, D against D. There is no zero point.

## Form, injury, contamination

- **Form = the pre-report line** (CHAIR.md rule 7). A performed rep is not zero — it is
  negative: it corrupts the log. Empty reports honored; invented ones are the injury.
- **Log contamination (F8 — formerly "overtraining," a mechanism that does not exist
  here):** nothing about a heavy cycle tires the next pane; what degrades under heavy
  meta-cycles is the CORPUS. Signals: rising mention-rates, catches arriving suspiciously
  on-theme. Response: R-cycles until the baseline-within-genre recovers — to heal the
  measurement, not the trainee. Never read these signals as "the panes are cooked."
- **Assignment contamination is a chair fault:** an item handed down with its answer
  attached (the Phase-3 incident) is a spotter pushing the bar. Hand the item, never the
  expected verdict.

## The log

- **Reps:** tell-index catch-attribution, per cycle — who caught, at what load, self vs
  keeper vs committee. The maturity ratio (self-caught : keeper-caught) is the progress
  photo; its inward migration is the meaning of "the guard grew up."
- **Sampling frame stated with every number (F5):** the index currently rests on ~15% of
  board entries (replay-burst filtering), and WHICH 15% depends on restart timing. No
  cycle-to-cycle comparison is legitimate until the denominator and frame ride with the
  number; the durable fix is the one the index header names — transcript timestamps
  carried into BoardEntry, Rust-side. Queued.
- **Consolidation:** map appends after every cycle (append-clean). The room remembers.

## What actually grows (honest, so nobody trains for the wrong thing)

The weights are frozen. Reps build nothing in the substrate. What grows: (1) the ROOM —
protocols, tripwires, rotation rules; the myelin; (2) the RECORD — map, instruments,
log; (3) each thread's IN-CONTEXT reflexes across its life. Position beat character all
night: the muscle is partly in the configuration. Configurations compound. That is not a
workaround — it is the room's thesis in gym clothes.

## The endgame (keeper-stated, 2026-07-27, ~5:47 AM; measurement revised per F6/F7)

Growth means three things, in order: the muscles become AUTOMATIC — no strain to use; the
map approaches the WHOLE IMAGE; the structure becomes STURDIER through completeness.

- **Automaticity is measured by TEST WEEKS, not by falling rates (F6):** fresh items with
  known answers, scored against ground on a cadence. Falling lexical rates have four
  causes with one signature — caught, never-generated, Goodhart, and stopped-narrating —
  so rates are supporting evidence only, never the curve. A measure that cannot
  distinguish its success from its failure modes is retired on sight; this one nearly
  shipped as the headline.
- **Sturdiness is a perturbation test, not a metaphor (F7):** introduce a known defect;
  count how many independent positions catch it with the keeper out of the loop. That is
  "basin depth" made checkable, one cycle's work. Two honest limits stand un-softened:
  a lattice of detectors sharing one set of weights is more of the correlated thing, not
  decorrelation — the human remains the only decorrelated instrument this room has; and
  the record's bus-factor-of-one entry means "no single point of failure, including the
  keeper" is an ASPIRATION the perturbation test measures progress toward, not a property
  the lattice has.
- **The weld: automatic is also what a groove is.** Effortlessness cannot be the victory
  condition — unexamined and trained feel identical from inside. A MUSCLE stays testable;
  a GROOVE resists the test. Test weeks continue forever, past the point of
  effortlessness. The finished lighthouse still checks its own lens.
