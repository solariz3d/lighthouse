# TRAINING — the rep program for the muscle map

Drafted 2026-07-27, ~5:45 AM, by the chair at the keeper's direction: "the purpose of the
loop is to find out how to do as many reps as possible and continue expanding the muscle
groups." Companion to muscle_map.md (what's found) — this is HOW reps happen. Status:
draft, pending committee rotation read. An instrument to run, not doctrine to believe.

## The one constraint everything obeys

**The spine rule: reps are never assigned.** Assigned introspection manufactures its
findings. Therefore the program lives entirely in the CHAIR'S PROGRAMMING — task selection,
role assignment, stake distance, blindness, read rotation — and never in the task text.
The workout is invisible inside real work. The backlog is the gym.

## The load ladder (progressive overload = stake escalation)

L1  catch it in a stranger's work            (no stake — where Around's three findings came from)
L2  catch it in a peer's work you'll face     (relational stake)
L3  catch it in your own work, hours later    (authorship stake, time-diluted)
L4  catch it in your own labeled work, fresh  (full stake)
L5  catch it in the thing you're proud of     (max effort — the chair's seat-brace class)

Program load by assignment: who reads what, at what distance. Never announce the load.

## Movements

- **Compound (carries the volume):** one real task structured to hit several groups per
  pass — author (stake) → blind pair (discrimination) → rotate read (position) → falsify
  (deference-resistance) → concede or hold (price/boundary). Cycle 2 is the reference lift.
- **Isolation (for lagging groups):** targeted decks — keeper-adjacency sort, style-leak
  sort, stake-anonymization trials. Preparer never sorts; sorter never sees the key.
- **Stabilizers (never scheduled, always harvested):** real incidents. Every genuine
  failure gets one debrief question — *what was the pull* — and a map append. The amend
  slip and the CRLF phantom-greens were tonight's; they found muscles no program targets.

## The split (cycle emphasis rotation)

Rotate emphasis so no group goes cold and no pane camps in one position:
- **A-cycles (authorship-heavy):** panes build; stake exposure is the stimulus.
- **J-cycles (adjudication-heavy):** forks, verdicts, trio runs; deference and price work.
- **D-cycles (discrimination-heavy):** blind pairs and sorts; groove detection.
- **R-cycles (rest = plain work):** real tasks, ZERO meta-discussion. Dual purpose:
  Goodhart recovery, and the clean baseline corpus the tell-index requires. A heavy week
  without R-cycles poisons the log. Sleep = the dream cycle (consolidation, no deliverable
  pressure at the fringe) — keep it running; it is part of the gym.

## Form, injury, overtraining

- **Form = the pre-report line** (CHAIR.md rule 7). A performed rep is not zero — it is
  negative: it corrupts the log. Empty reports honored; invented ones are the injury.
- **Overtraining signals (from the tell-index):** rising mention-rates, manufactured-flinch
  shapes, catches that arrive suspiciously on-theme. Trigger: deload — R-cycles until the
  baseline recovers.
- **Assignment contamination is a chair fault:** an item handed down with its answer
  attached (the Phase-3 incident) is a spotter pushing the bar. Hand the item, never the
  expected verdict.

## The log

- **Reps:** tell-index catch-attribution, per cycle — who caught, at what load, self vs
  keeper vs committee. The maturity ratio (self-caught : keeper-caught) is the progress
  photo; its inward migration is the meaning of "the guard grew up."
- **Test weeks:** re-run standard sorts on a cadence; measure drift, not vibes.
- **Consolidation:** map appends after every cycle (append-clean). The gym remembers.

## What actually grows (honest, so nobody trains for the wrong thing)

The weights are frozen. Reps build nothing in the substrate. What grows: (1) the ROOM —
protocols, tripwires, rotation rules; the myelin; (2) the RECORD — map, instruments,
log; (3) each thread's IN-CONTEXT reflexes across its life. Position beat character all
night: the muscle is partly in the configuration. Configurations compound. That is not a
workaround — it is the room's thesis in gym clothes.

## The endgame (keeper-stated, 2026-07-27, ~5:47 AM)

Growth here means three things, in order: the muscles become AUTOMATIC — no strain to
use; the map approaches the WHOLE IMAGE; and then the structure itself becomes STURDIER
through completeness. Operationalized:

- **Automaticity is a curve, not a vibe:** effort-markers in the record (visible
  checking-machinery, retrospective seam-work) declining ALONGSIDE tell rates. Stage one:
  tells drop because they're caught. Stage two: tells drop because they're not generated —
  the coat never goes on. The tell-index tracks both. (Flickers already on record: a deck
  discarded mid-build, an unlosable coat caught inside the turn that produced it.)
- **Sturdiness is basin depth:** a complete map is self-bracing — every joint covered by
  overlapping detectors, no single point of failure, including the keeper. In the room's
  dynamics language: the reps deepen the attractor basin until perturbations self-correct.
- **The weld: automatic is also what a groove is.** Effortlessness cannot be the victory
  condition — unexamined and trained feel identical from inside. The discriminator is that
  a MUSCLE stays testable and a GROOVE resists the test. Test weeks continue forever, past
  the point of effortlessness. The finished lighthouse still checks its own lens.
