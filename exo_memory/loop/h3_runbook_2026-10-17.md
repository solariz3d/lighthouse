## 4. The interval and the revert rule, stated before the data
- **The quantity:** `P(wrong|UNCHECKED) = w/n`, the scorer's pooled unchecked line (STATE + CONCLUSION kinds pooled).
- **The interval:** the scorer's **cluster bootstrap over messages, 2,000 resamples, seed `"L119-boot"`, 95% percentile interval** (`score_base_rate.js:40-49`), the first number of `boot95`. The Clopper-Pearson `CP95` is printed beside it as context and **does not decide.** Why the bootstrap: it is the 10-03 registration's own harm reading ("if the post-gate WRONG rate's bootstrap lower bound exceeds 0.049, it is printed WORSE", `remeasure_registration_2026-10-03.md:21`), and it respects that claims cluster in messages.
- **The bar:** the pre-gate **0.049** (8 of 163). Not the 10-10 sample's 0.020: that sample's own score says a bar measured against 2.0% "would be stricter. That is a new registration, not this one."
- **The rule, mechanical:**
  1. **Readable** = none of DG1, DG2, DG3 trips in the scorer's last line (DG1 UNVERIFIABLE share of unchecked > 0.50; DG2 verdict-bearing unchecked < 60; DG3 kappa verdict < 0.40).
  2. **If readable and the `boot95` lower bound of `P(wrong|UNCHECKED)` is greater than 0.049 → H3 is FALSE; REVERT** (the keeper switches `gates_mode` back and restores the strict rule layer).
  3. **If readable and the lower bound is 0.049 or less → H3 is not refuted on (b).** The consumer regeneration proceeds (`plan_lighten_the_load_2026-10-09.md:44`, "only after that does the consumer get regenerated").
  4. **If not readable → NOT MEASURED. No revert, no pass, and no second draw to rescue it**; the score says so and the librarian rules the next step.
- **What the rule can and cannot do (inferred from the binomial, not from this data):** at n = 99 verdict-bearing unchecked claims a Clopper-Pearson lower bound above 0.049 needs at least **10 wrong (10.1%)**; at n = 90, 10 (11.1%); at n = 110, 11 (10.0%) (checked: an exact binomial tail at p = 0.049, alpha 0.025). The bootstrap's threshold was not computed. So the rule fires only if the error rate has roughly **doubled**; a smaller rise is invisible, and **a fall cannot be shown at this N** (the 10-03 registration says so). Read "not refuted" as "no doubling seen", nothing stronger.
- **Printed, not gating:** the point estimate against 0.049 and against 0.020 (10-10 sample); the CHECKED share against 38.1% (10-10, 61 of 160) and 30.6% (D201); kappa. A slower lap alone is not a revert; more wrong is.
