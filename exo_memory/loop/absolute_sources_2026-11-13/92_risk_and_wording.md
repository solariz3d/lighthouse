### 2.2 What the evidence implies

**Effect**
- Of 14 EEG studies: "five studies reporting results in line with the brainwave entrainment hypothesis, eight studies
  reporting contradictory, and one mixed results". [read] R6, Abstract
- "the majority reported negative results. Technically, this casts serious doubts on whether the brainwave entrainment

  the condition". [read] R7, Abstract
- Every group in that study was told sounds would play, so it does not test an unmentioned layer. [summary: 15 F19 (read,
  corrected)]

  heterogeneous effects on surgical anxiety; mixed Hemi-Sync anaesthesia trials. [summary: 15 F1, F4, F6, F27-F31

**Safety**
- The maker: "If you have a tendency towards seizures, auditory disorders, or adverse mental conditions(s), DO NOT listen
  to Hemi-Sync® without first consulting your physician." [read] R5
- Trials routinely exclude a history of epilepsy; no seizure outcome from binaural beats was found in what was read.
  [summary: 15 F48, F49 (read, OK), F50 (summary there)]
- Side effects are reported. An unpublished pilot found "dizziness and head-wringing, following six to eight minutes of
  continuous BB exposure". [read] R8, Introduction
- That study then capped exposure at 5 min; 20% to 46% of its qualitative responses were negative, and about 20% even in
  silent control. [summary: 15 F24, F25 (read, OK)]
- Tinnitus can make the tones uncomfortable; a four-person study saw a depression subscale rise. [summary: 15 F51 (read,
  OK), F17 (summary there: abstract)]

**Fairness**

## 5. A checklist for any new lore text

W-numbers point to section 3. Each item is a proposal.

1. **Whose voice?** A real document (quoted, dated), a fictional speaker, or the game itself. The game itself claims
   nothing about death, grief, the mind or the audio. (W2, 4.1) [ours]
2. **Effect words.** Does it say or imply that a sound, the chair, a tape or a breathing game changes the player's brain,
   mood, sleep or grief? Rewrite it physically, or give it to a dated speaker. (W1-W3, W7) [ours]
3. **Words kept out of the game's own voice:** "binaural" as a claim, "theta", "entrain", "therapy", "heal",
   "brainwave". (W1, W4) [ours]
4. **Quotation.** Verbatim, from the scan, under 40 words, sourced; the memo cited as "cover ¶n" or "¶n"; in-copyright
   books and manuals paraphrased. (W9, W21; 01 O19) [ours]
5. **Real person.** Only inside a quoted real page, accurately, with no new act; no look-alike, voice or likeness.
   (W22-W28) [ours]
6. **Real organisation or mark.** Is the name a label for our goods, a mechanic, a store surface, or a place in passing?
   Does the text give the institute an act: paying, owning the PC, choosing the child? (1.2, 1.3) [ours]
7. **History fact.** Army, not CIA; "non-corporal"; dates from 1977; the paper named for any length; the KGB as an
   assumption; walls and spoons only as rumour; no named tasker; later Monroe terms never given to the memo. (W8-W19)
   [ours]
8. **Invented page.** No real release stamp, no lifted real bars, no real sums, the seal cropped or redrawn. (W20, 1.3)
   [ours]
9. **Death.** Does it say what happens after death in the game's own voice, or add "death" to the memo's "return to the
   Absolute"? Give it to a speaker, or cut it. (4.1) [ours]
10. **Advice.** Does it tell a reader how to grieve, what helps, or that grief always or never ends? Make it a character's
    view, or use "can". (4.2) [ours]
11. **Harm.** Could it read as the child choosing to die, or as the child having caused the death? Flag it for the author.
    (4.2, 4.3) [ours]
12. **The unsaid death.** Does it, or any content note, state the father's death directly? (4.2; item 46's rule) [ours]
13. **Real dying children, real cultures.** No case material; cultures only as documented. (W29, W30) [ours]
14. **Reading.** Player-paced, kept in a log, legible at its size and contrast. (12 O13, O15, O16) [ours]
15. **Record.** In lap 0's beat table: the item cited, the finding cited, the mark, the voice, and a third-party flag for
    anything not ours. [ours]
