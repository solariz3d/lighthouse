## 2. Findings

**The preparatory process**

F1. [read] MEMO ¶28 gives the opening sequence: the box, then "resonate tuning" (the memo's spelling), then the
    affirmation. The box is a way to "isolate extraneous concerns in a visualization device called an "energy
    conversion box."" (MEMO ¶28).
F2. [read] From tape 4 on, the Wave I tapes name a fixed five-step order: box, resonant tuning, balloon, affirmation,
    Focus 10. Tape 4 opens "MOVE NOW INTO YOUR PREPARATORY PROCESS. FIRST: YOUR ENERGY CONVERSION BOX." Tapes 5 and 6
    call it the "PREPREPARATION PROCESS" (TR1, Tape 4 p.1, Tape 5 p.1, Tape 6 p.1).
F3. [read] The early tapes use other orders. Tape 1 runs box, affirmation, then resonant tuning. Tape 2 runs box,
    resonant tuning, then affirmation, and has no balloon. MEMO ¶29 puts the balloon after Hemi-Sync and body relaxation
    (TR1, Tape 1 pp.3-5, Tape 2 pp.1-2; MEMO ¶29). The order changes from tape to tape. *(second reader: Tape 3 runs
    box, resonant tuning, balloon, then Focus 10, with no affirmation; TR1 PDF text layer, Tape 3 pp.1-3.)*
F4. [read] ~~The 1983 residential program~~ *(second reader: the week MEN83 describes was not in 1983. The article ran
    in the March/April 1983 issue, and its author reached his group "seven months after our one-week session" (MEN83,
    "Postscript" paragraph), so the week was in 1982 or earlier [inferred]. Read: a residential program described in
    early 1983.)* used four of the same steps (box, tuning, balloon, affirmation), "a procedure
    that would serve as the opening ritual of every tape" (MEN83, the "pre-preparation process" paragraph). Old manual, Wave V: "the now-familiar preparatory
    process" (GEM:816-817).

**The energy conversion box**

F5. [read] On the tape the box is "A LARGE, STRONG BOX WITH A HEAVY LID." The listener puts in worries, then is told to
    "CLOSE THE HEAVY LID TIGHTLY AND TURN AWAY FROM YOUR ENERGY CONVERSION BOX." (TR1, Tape 1 p.3).
F6. [read] The manual lets the box take any form: "a wooden chest, a sphere of light, a vacuum cleaner or a nuclear
    particle collector". Symbols go in, for example "a photo or doll representing someone who is on your mind", and the
    box can be revisited at any time during an exercise (GEM:51-58; GE1:135-145).
F7. [read] The FAQ uses the box for problems in the exercise itself: put "a symbol for sleep" in it if you fall asleep
    (GE1:357), and put a distracting heartbeat or breath in it (GEM:260-263).
F8. [read] Monroe used "box" in a second sense, as belief systems: "All of us are encased in a box created by our
    belief systems." He also said "The box is not enough." (TALK90, side 1 pp.8-9).

**Resonant tuning**

F9. [read] The tape's resonant tuning is a breath cycle. The listener breathes in with eyes open, holds, and breathes
    out with eyes closed "AS IF YOU WERE BLOWING OUT A CANDLE", then hums or sings with the tape's chorus (TR1, Tape 1
    pp.3-5). The manual: it "accelerates the gathering of your vibrational energy while reducing internal dialogue" and
    invites "different vowel sounds" (GE1:153-156). It is to be done out loud; the FAQ says the discomfort passes
    (GE1:365-370). MEN83 heard participants chant ""aaah," "oooh," or "ummm"". The memo: "a monotonous, protracted
    humming sound that sets up a feeling of vibration particularly in the head" (MEMO ¶28).

**The affirmation**

F10. [read] The affirmation opens "I am more than my physical body." Its second half asks for the help of those
    "whose wisdom, development, and experience are equal or greater than my own" (GE1:30-34; TR1, Tape 1 p.3). The
    manual invites the user to rewrite it: "Experiment. Make it yours." (GEM:61-62; GE1:148-150).
F11. [read] Versions differ. The 1998 Voyage page adds a protection clause: "I ask their guidance and protection from
    any influence or any source that might provide me with less than my stated desires." (GV 1998:112-114). The 2013
    manual and the Wave I transcript do not have it. Wave VIII (©2022) has its own affirmation, opening "I am much more
    than my physical body." (GE8:58). *(second reader: the clause is not only a 1998 wording. MEN83 (March/April 1983)
    quotes the residential affirmation's opening as "I can perceive that which is greater than the physical world" and
    says it goes on to the desire "to be guided and protected" (MEN83, the "Last, we were to repeat" paragraph). That
    opening matches the 1998 page (GV 1998:107-108), not the 2013 manual. The split may be residential against home
    course rather than early against late [inferred]. MEN83 paraphrases the end, so the 1983 wording is unread.)*
F12. [read] The manual's account of the second half: Explorer-session helpers would "position themselves two on each
    side of the Explorer", and "This much-appreciated cooperation gave rise to the latter half of the Gateway
    Affirmation" (GEM:1177-1182; same text in GE6).
F13. [read] The 1998 Voyage page quotes *Far Journeys*: after the affirmation was added in 1974, Explorers "encountered
    intelligent beings who were more or less willing to communicate" (GV 1998, the affirmation section). The book itself
    was not opened, so the quotation is [read] on the page and [summary] as to the book.
F14. [read] A journalist in ~~the 1983~~ *(second reader: a 1982-or-earlier; see F4)* residential program used it
    under fear: "I instinctively chanted the Gateway
    Affirmation ("I am more than my physical body....") out loud to get a hold on myself." (MEN83, "Intro Focus 12").

**Counting in, counting out**

F15. [read] Focus 3 is reached by a count from one to three and is "THE STARTING POINT OF THE GATEWAY EXPERIENCE" (TR1,
    Tape 1 p.5). The manual calls it "the first signpost along your journey" (GE1:172).
F16. [read] Focus 10 is reached by "THE TEN POINT SYSTEM OF TOTAL RELAXATION": body part by body part, each with "LOOK
    WITH YOUR CLOSED EYES AT" and "RELAX, LET GO, SLEEP", ending "TEN........TEN.......TEN" (TR1, Tape 2 pp.2-4).
F17. [read] The manual describes Focus 10 as the mind "awake and alert while your body sleeps", with a heightened
    sensitivity that includes "hearing the swish of blood flow behind your ears" (GE1:184-191).
F18. [read] The exits and triggers are bodily gestures. To return: "SAY IN YOUR MIND OR THINK THE NUMBER ONE, AND MOVE
    THE PHYSICAL FINGERS OF YOUR RIGHT HAND" (TR1, Tape 3 p.4). To stay awake: fingers of the right hand to the back of
    the neck and the number one. To remember: fingers to the centre of the forehead (Tape 2 pp.4-5). To enter Focus 10:
    inhale, think 10, exhale (Tape 3 p.4). The manual stresses the way back: "The more completely you come "back" from
    each exercise, the further you can go into the next." (GE1:178-179).

**The resonant energy balloon (REBAL)**

F19. [read] On the tape the balloon is "A BALL OF ENERGY ALL AROUND YOU WHERE YOU FLOAT INSIDE". Energy flows out of
    the top of the head "LIKE A FOUNTAIN", down around the body, and back in through the soles of the feet. It then
    spirals (TR1, Tape 3 p.2).
F20. [read] The tape gives it two functions. It holds in "ALL RADIATION THAT IS YOU", and it protects from "ANY
    EXTERNAL, UNWANTED OTHER ENERGY". It does both "UNLESS YOU OPEN IT BY YOUR OWN ACTION" (TR1, Tape 3 p.2).
F21. [read] It has a breath switch: inhale, hold, think of "A BRIGHT MOVING CIRCLE WITH THE NUMBER 10 INSIDE IT", and
    blow it out. It re-absorbs at the end of an exercise (TR1, Tape 3 p.3; GEM:125-127).
F22. [read] The manual: "Your REBAL acts as a magnet, attracting influences you desire and shielding you from
    undesired energy." It may "become radiant like an intensified aura", and the manual suggests "popping" it in a group
    (GEM:118-128; GE1:202-214).
F23. [read] The memo adds a purpose the room has not taken yet: the balloon "encourages early achievement of a suitable
    resonant state" and "serves a precautionary purpose" if a first out-of-body experience goes "outside the
    terrestrial sphere" (MEMO ¶29).

**The toolkit against fear (Wave I tape 4, Wave III)**

F24. [read] "Release and Recharge" works through fear in three steps. First, "PERCEIVE THE RADIATION OF FEAR" in the
    box and let it go "LIKE A BUBBLE FLOATING UPWARD IN WATER". Next, release the emotion the fear covered. Last,
    "PERCEIVE THE MEMORY, THE EVENT THAT WAS HIDDEN BY THE FEAR AND EMOTION" and pull it back in as clean energy (TR1,
    Tape 4 pp.1-3). *(second reader: the transcript PDF has no Tape 4 p.2, and its first cycle jumps from the fear to
    "NOW FREE OF FEAR AND EMOTION". The emotion step is read only in the second cycle, Tape 4 p.3: "PERCEIVE THE
    EMOTION THAT THE FEAR COVERED".)*
F25. [read] The manual frames fear as a signal: "Learned fear can be a useful warning signal, but you no longer need it
    after you acknowledge the warning." It is "not a quick fix", because most limits "have many layers" (GE1:221-254).
F26. [read] Wave III lists ~~every tool~~ *(second reader: six tools, introduced "For example", not every tool;
    GEM:593)* as a tool against fear of leaving the body: the Box; a calming line added to the
    Affirmation; "know that your REBAL comes with you and keeps you safe and comfortable"; the bar tool as "a two lane
    highway"; and repeating Discovery #4 (GEM:592-600, "About Fear"). *(second reader: the list also names Threshold #2,
    to "ask for guidance on how best to ease your fears", GEM:595-596.)*
F27. [read] Monroe's own framing, 1990: "What is unknown we fear." The work is "converting unknowns into knowns", and
    "you will be erasing fear - step by step" (TALK90, side 1 pp.7-8).

**Sleep and the bed**

F28. [read] Discovery #5 rests on the idea that "sleep is a natural Gateway into other states of consciousness". The
    signals fade at the end "leaving you in natural sleep" (GE1:261-279).
F29. [read] On the tape, the listener lies down, rolls "AS IF YOU WERE A LOG FLOATING IN WATER", floats up, comes back
    the same way, then sleeps on a count from eleven to twenty. The count from one to twenty is given as a sleep trigger
    (TR1, Tape 5 pp.1-2).
F30. [read] Monroe claimed in 1990 that "the out of body state is what takes place with everyone during delta sleep"
    (TALK90, side 1 p.13). This is his claim, not a finding. MEMO ¶32 has a parallel: ~~a trainer reportedly used REM
    sleep~~ *(second reader: Monroe told the class that "an ex-trainer of his operating in Charlottesville, Virginia"
    had found he could use REM sleep with the tapes; MEMO ¶32)* to "guarantee out-of-body movements".

**Focus 12 tools (Wave II, MEMO ¶30)**

F31. [read] The memo says that at Focus 12 the participant uses "a series of specific techniques or "tools" as the
    Monroe Institute characterizes them" (MEMO ¶30). Problem Solving answers may come as "a sudden, holistic perception"
    (MEMO ¶30A; GEM:452-469).
F32. [read] Patterning has rules: present tense, "Pattern only for yourself.", and do not keep checking: "You wouldn't
    dig up a seed to see how it's growing, would you?" A pattern can be changed or cancelled only in Focus 12
    (GEM:472-502). The memo warns that trainers "caution against attempting to force the pace of this process because the
    individual could succeed in dislocating his existing reality with drastic consequences." (MEMO ¶30B).
F33. [read] Colour Breathing assigns jobs to colours: "green for reducing excess or harmful emotional energy; red for
    increasing physical strength, speed, and coordination; purple for restoring and normalizing your physical
    condition" (GEM:512-514). The memo frames it as healing that rebalances "bodily energy flows" (MEMO ¶30C).
F34. [read] In the memo, the Energy Bar Tool begins as "a small, intensely pulsating dot of light", which the user
    "extrudes ... into the shape of a sparkling, vibrating cylinder of energy" (MEMO ¶30D). The manual likens it to
    "power sticks", sceptres and "sabers of light". It says "Your hands may become your EBT" and calls it "a beacon to
    attract guidance", "a vehicle for space travel, or a comforting symbol of safety and security". It adds "as you
    recharge it, it recharges you" (GEM:524-537). In the memo, the user also "turns his bar of energy into a whirling
    vortex" to start remote viewing (MEMO ¶30E).
F35. [read] The Living Body Map starts with a white outline of the body. It then turns red for circulation, blue for
    the nervous system, yellow for the glands and orange for muscle and bone. Purple healing energy is sent with the bar
    tool (GEM:548-552; MEMO ¶30F).

**Wave III to VI tools**

F36. [read] First Stage Separation teaches five ways out: log-rolling, pole, backing away, flowing (out through the top
    of the head "in the form of a cloud or fog") and floating. The way back from log-rolling ends with "something akin to
    a little "click,"" (GEM:701-727). The memo lists "rolling out, lifting out after the fashion of a telephone pole" and
    "sliding out through either end of his body", and notes "Only one tape" of the many is about this (MEMO ¶31).
F37. [read] Vectors places an imagined clock face around the body, "with 12 always just above the top of your head",
    3 outside the left hip, 9 outside the right and 6 below the feet. The face is then turned to cover front and back
    (GEM:652-667).
F38. [read] Smaller tools. Liftoff: "you can help yourself rise by "settling down."" (GEM:619). Energy Food is a
    method "for restoring depleted energy in C-1" (GEM:692). Wave IV's Compoint is "a location you establish along the
    continuum of consciousness"; the examples are a computer station, a garden by a pond and a log cabin, and it serves
    as a "convenient rendezvous with your nonphysical friends" (GEM:797-806). Free Flow 12 introduces a "beacon
    guidepost" (GEM:765).
