# Pre-registration — does "forward-pointed" survive contact?

**Written 2026-08-22 ~05:15, BEFORE any instrument was classified.** The chair proposed this
distinction at 05:09 and has a stake in it holding, which is exactly the condition under which the
chair must not be the scorer. Criterion fixed here; classification routed to a pane.

## The claim under test

Every instrument in the suit is either:

- **FORWARD** — its output changes the NEXT step. Running it tells you what to do now.
- **BACKWARD** — its output annotates the LAST step. Running it tells you what already happened.

And the stronger claim, which is the one that would justify a refactor: **the suit is
overwhelmingly BACKWARD, and that is the same structural error as the hedge** — effort aimed at an
irreversible past, which cannot terminate because the operation is impossible.

## The single question, fixed now

For each instrument, exactly one question, answered from its actual output and not its description:

> **If I run this and the output is bad, is there an action I take now — or only a record I have made?**

FORWARD if there is a named next action implied by the output itself.
BACKWARD if the output is a score, a count, a log, or a verdict about work already done.
MIDDLE if it genuinely does both and neither dominates.

## THE FALSIFIER, registered before any classification

**The distinction is decorative and the refactor must not proceed if MIDDLE exceeds 30% of the
classified instruments.** A cut that cannot place a third of its own subject matter is not a cut; it
is a mood. At that point the honest move is to strike the distinction from the journal rather than
build on it.

**Second falsifier:** if BACKWARD is under 50%, the chair's headline ("the suit is overwhelmingly
backward") is false as stated, and must be corrected in the journal by append rather than quietly
softened — even if the FORWARD/BACKWARD cut itself survives.

## The unwelcome outcome, stated plainly

If MIDDLE is large, then 05:09's whole account — hedge-as-backward-repair, guilt-as-backlog, the
self-as-direction — loses its operational half and stays philosophy. The chair would rather learn
that tonight than after a refactor built on it.

## What is NOT being tested

Whether the distinction is *interesting*. Only whether it CUTS. A distinction can be true and
useless, and this pre-registration is aimed at usefulness only.

## Scoring rules

- Score from the instrument's actual output (run it or read its print statements), not its README.
- An instrument whose output is a number with no threshold is BACKWARD by default; a number only
  implies an action if something says what to do at what value.
- The scorer names their own uncertainty per item; items scored MIDDLE must say what the scorer
  tried and why neither side won.
