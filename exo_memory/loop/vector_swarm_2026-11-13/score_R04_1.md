# Score R04, scorer 1: holder liveness for shared-machine locks and gates

Root R04 [read: 00_roots.md:51-58]: a lock or gate records that someone holds it, not whether the holder is alive and moving.
A real fix = a read of holder liveness (CPU over a window, live parent, child tree, start time) behind locks, gates, slots and kills.

Method [read]: I read all 1123 ledger rows in 00_ledger.md in whole lines, in 11 passes, and decided each one. Rows marked
"2nd reader: REMOVE" (L0029, L0162, L0255, L0299, L0300, L0390, L0409: 7 rows) are skipped. Rows scored: 1116 [read, counted by script over my decisions].
I did not open the primaries; the ledger row wording was enough to decide (a row whose wording was not enough was scored NO).
Strictness: YES needs a holder (lock, baton, slot, watcher, process) that was dead, idle, orphaned, mangled or killed by bare PID,
where a liveness read stops or flags it at once. Stalls whose holder was alive and working (a live suite, a timing session
taking its turn, a queue behind a working pane) are NO: a lock that waits correctly on a live holder is not this fault.
Receipts, delivery stamps, wrong numbers, wrong claims, design misses and keeper catches about wording are NO.
