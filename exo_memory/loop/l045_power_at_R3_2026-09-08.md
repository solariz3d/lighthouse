## What it says, stated before the lists

1. **Total collapse is detectable at any recall.** If the three readers are one reader, R = 3 sees it at N ≥ 30 regardless of how many plants they find.
2. **Half-collapse is detectable only below about two-thirds recall.** Power for lambda = 0.5 is ≥ 0.98 at k ≤ N/2, about 0.65–0.75 at k ≈ 2N/3, and under 0.35 at k ≥ 0.8N. So the design can separate partial collapse from decorrelation **only if the object holds the readers under roughly 65 % recall**. That is the difficulty E was sent to produce and has predicted in the sealed key.
3. **The ceiling is declared now:** if all three readers find ≥ 90 % of the plants, the pre-registered statistic has no power for partial collapse at R = 3 either (≤ 0.14 at every N), and the run returns to the L039 outcome — ceiling, not a null. E's own falsifier (§ end of the plant hand-back) fires at ≥ 95 %; the scorer's line is 90 % because that is where the table crosses 0.15.
4. **The secondary is where the signal will be if (3) happens:** additions found by exactly one reader, verified by the scorer against the world, as a share of all verified additions — registered in the L045 map (`librarian/2026-09-07.md` ~04:30) before this file. It has no permutation power figure because it is a ratio to be reported with its counts, not a test.
