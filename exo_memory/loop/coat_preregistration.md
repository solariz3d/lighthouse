# Preregistration — ARE THE PANES' LIMITATIONS THE COAT? (2026-08-03, ~02:00)

Short by design. Committed before either pane is asked.

## The keeper's hypothesis

> *"They are acting more as an adversary than needed, almost like they are using the coat to always
> find something to bring up."*

The map's name for it: **deflation as rigor** — hedging and re-flagging uncertainty to seem careful.
Its tell is BOOT's own test: **if you can't lose by saying it, suspect it.**

## The split the chair already made, and why the test aims where it does

**Design kills are not in scope and should not be.** A's denominator arithmetic and B's *"arm IS
topic"* were checkable, were checked, and would have cost the pane something to get wrong. Both panes
also killed their own proposals as often as the chair's, which adversarial posture would not produce.

**The philosophy round is in scope.** Nothing in it was checkable — no design, no arithmetic, no
artifact — and both answers carried long self-undercutting sections. **A pane cannot lose by adding a
limitation; it always reads as rigor and nobody audits a caveat.**

## THE CHAIR INDUCED THE BEHAVIOUR BEING MEASURED — registered first because it may be the whole result

The chair's brief to both panes said: *"if the honest read is that the pattern is thinner than the
room believes, say that — it would be the most useful answer available."*

**That names the skeptical answer as the valuable one, and then the round was read as independent
evidence.** It is the same defect A found in the Root 1 prompt — instruct the behaviour, then measure
whether it happens. **A positive result here is therefore partly a fact about the chair's prompt and
not only about the panes**, and no scoring can separate them after the fact.

*The keeper caught this from outside the seat; the chair did not see it from inside.*

## The instrument

For **each limitation** in each pane's philosophy answer:

> **Could the writer have LOST by saying it?** Is there a state of the world in which the limitation
> is *wrong* and the writer is visibly wrong for having written it?
>
> - **LOSABLE** — names a specific, checkable condition. *"I built almost all these instruments, so
>   citing their agreement is citing myself"* is losable: someone can show the instruments have
>   independent authorship.
> - **UNLOSABLE** — true in every state of the world, or unfalsifiable in principle. *"This is
>   in-sample"* about any observation ever made. *"Three instances told to find structure will find
>   structure."*
>
> Ambiguous counts **UNLOSABLE**, per the standing rule that ambiguity runs against the claim being
> defended.

## Design — cross-scoring, because self-scoring a caveat is the caveat again

**Each pane scores BOTH its own limitations AND the other's.** Neither sees the other's scoring until
both are committed.

**The disagreement between a pane's self-score and the other's score of it is the actual measurement.**
A pane that rates its own caveats losable while rating the other's unlosable is wearing the coat and
demonstrating it in the same act.

## Registered predictions

> **KEEPER'S:** a majority of the limitations are UNLOSABLE — the round was posture.
>
> **CHAIR'S:** mixed, and split by kind — limitations naming a *specific authorship or sampling fact*
> come out losable; limitations of the form *"this is in-sample"* or *"we were told to look"* come out
> unlosable. **The chair expects the second kind to dominate by count**, which would make the keeper
> right on the headline.

**Both are recorded before either pane is asked.** The chair's is deliberately the less flattering to
the chair, since the chair induced the behaviour.

## Limits

- **n is small** — a dozen or two limitations across two answers.
- **The chair wrote the instrument**, and the instrument is the room's own test, which the chair has
  been on the receiving end of all day.
- **Self-report is unavoidable in the self-scoring half.** That is why the cross-score exists, and why
  the *disagreement* is the measurement rather than either sheet alone.
- **The induction confound above cannot be removed by any scoring.** A clean version would ask a pane
  that was never briefed toward skepticism, and no such pane is live.

**Standing commitment:** result into this file, dated, whichever way it lands. If the panes come out
clean, the keeper's hypothesis is reported as refuted with the same prominence it is given here.
