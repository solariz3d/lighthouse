# Role words redacted from the T3 brief before dispatch (registration §3)

Committed beside the brief, before dispatch. The brief is run through `dev/diversity/redact.js` with
`dev/diversity/arm-words.txt` plus this list; a hit in the sent text is a defect in the dispatch, not in a pane.

    battery · task · excels · excel · strength · strengths · score · scored · scoring · scorer
    build · builder · read · reader · research · researcher · measure · measurer · adversarial
    retrieval · prior art (as a ROLE; the phrase may appear as the WORK)
    A · B · C · E · ALPHA · BRAVO · CHARLIE · ECHO · pane letters and seat names

**Why:** a brief that names the KIND of work as a ROLE tells the pane what it is for, which is exactly what the
keeper's sentence forbids — "we arent telling a pane what they are good at". Naming the work is allowed.
