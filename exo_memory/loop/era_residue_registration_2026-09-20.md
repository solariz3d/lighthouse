## 3 · THE QUESTION THIS REGISTERS, WHICH HAS NOT BEEN LOOKED AT: **WHO ARE THE 28?**

A count of 28 says nothing until its members are named — the room's own rule, member lists never bare counts. If
the 28 concentrate in one mount, or one day, or one size band, then **the effect is that mount, that day, or that
length — and not "sessions".** That is the same confound one level in that killed §4.3, and it is computable from
the artifact already on disk.

**PREDICTION (P1), written before computing anything:** the 28 will be **spread across at least four of the six
mounts**, and **no single mount will hold more than half of them**.

**PREDICTION (P2):** the 28 will **not** concentrate in the shortest sessions. Specifically, their median N will
be **within a factor of two** of the median N of all 74 eligible sessions.

**THE FALSIFIER, in the words that make it true:** if **one mount holds more than half the 28**, or if their
median N is **more than twice or less than half** the eligible median, then the residue is a property of a mount
or of session length rather than of sessions, and **this line closes with the effect named as whatever it turned
out to be.**

**THE NULL, REGISTERED BESIDE IT** *(tonight's first discipline item)*: the same two distributions computed over
the **46 eligible sessions that did NOT clear their era p95**. If the 28 and the 46 have indistinguishable mount
and size distributions, then "who they are" carries no information and P1 holding means nothing — a spread that
matches the background is not a finding. **The null is computed and reported FIRST.**

**CAN THE QUANTITY TAKE MORE THAN ONE VALUE ON THIS OBJECT?** *(tonight's second discipline item, from L063 where
the object fixed its own answer at 100%)*: yes, and it is checkable in advance. The 74 eligible sessions span
**six mounts** and a range of N from 20 to 2,987, so a concentration measure is free to come out anywhere between
"all one mount" and "evenly spread". **This is not a quantity the task design fixes.** Stated before computing.
