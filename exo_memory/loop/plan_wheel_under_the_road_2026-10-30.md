## Part 2 (plan, after Part 1): the underside gives the wheel back

Written only once Part 1 says what the wheel met. The candidates, so the measurement can tell them apart:

- **A: the slot.** The road's edge row and the underskin's edge row are a vertical pair with a gap between them (the caster's thickness). A wheel that drops past the edge and is steered back enters that gap, and the road's underside, which AC collides with from below, holds it there. Fix: close the slot with an **edge apron**: a strip that runs from the road's edge row outward and DOWN at a fixed slope (20 to 30°), 1.5 to 2 m wide, joined to the underskin's edge so the cross-section is a closed wedge with no re-entrant face. A wheel that leaves the road rides the apron down; steered back, it rides up onto the road. This is the runoff kerb of a real track, and it changes no road vertex.
- **B: the underside collides.** If the wheel is fully under the road (not in a slot), the underskin's physical surface is what pins the car. Fix: the underskin's physics flags make it non-drivable and non-colliding for TYRES (a wall surface, `IS_VALID_TRACK=0`, or a surface the tyre raycast ignores) while the body collider still meets it, OR a one-way skin that is collidable only from above. What AC and CSP allow here is a research question (docs/research/04_ac_physics_drivability.md is the room's prior art; the CSP wiki on surfaces and `extended-0` the source) and comes before any geometry.
- **C: neither.** The wheel never went under; the body collider caught the edge or a wall. Then the fix is the edge's shape, not the underside.

The jump jack is a lift of the body; it frees the car in case A when the lift exceeds the slot's height and in case B rarely. His "here it did not" is itself a measurement for Part 1: how high the jack lifted (the body-height step in the replay) against the slot or the depth.

Tier for the eventual change: prove it (geometry and export), with the ramp harness and the export fixtures, and his drive of the same stretch in the Demo car as the live check. Falsifier for the fix: if, after the apron or the flag change, a wheel driven off the same edge still reads under the mesh for more than one frame on the return, the mechanism was not the one Part 1 named.

## Part 2, written from O's numbers (18:0x): the edge apron

**What O measured** (`exo_memory/handback/p-d563-wheel-under-O_2026-10-30.md`, git-blob 31a2ece4; the three-panel PNG beside it): the Demo's rear right wheel left the footprint past the right edge of piece 20 by **0.1 to 0.5 m for 9 frames** (about 0.17 s at 655 km/h, roll +20 to +24°), with its centre at the edge row's height; it came back inside **0.31 m under** the top surface and hung there at full droop, nothing under it, for **6.57 s** while the car crossed the road twice on three wheels; the jump jack freed it (the body up 36 cm, 0.8 s airborne, landed on four wheels) and 0.45 s later the car left the road over the same edge. The reference car had the same shape at s 1,926: 0.1 m past the left edge, back 0.32 m under, 0.5 s, out on a hop. The edge there is a plain cupped edge, no wall; the road is one single-sided physics surface; the skin 0.5 m under it is scenery; the slot between them is open to the side. O's inference, which the numbers carry: a wheel below the edge has no surface to lead it back up.

**The fix: an edge apron, a physics strip outside every open road edge.**
- **Shape:** from the road's edge row, a strip running OUTWARD and DOWN along the road's own cross-section frame, slope 25° (between the 20° that would sit above a dropping wheel only for the first half metre and the 30° that starts to read as a wall), width **1.5 m** by default (three times this event's excursion; the reference's was 0.1 m), its outer edge joined by one more row down to the underskin's edge row so the section is a closed wedge with no re-entrant face. A wheel that leaves the road rides the apron (its ray finds a surface, so it never drops the 0.3 m); steered back, it rides up onto the road. The underskin stays scenery.
- **Where:** every road edge that is OPEN: not where a wall stands, not inside a closed tube ring (`closedRing`, no edge), and where the core's edge zone already turns down (e < 0) the apron continues from that zone's outer row. Not on flights. Both sides.
- **Physics:** a physics mesh named under the road prefix so the soft block covers it (`MESHES=1ROAD?`; E confirms the pattern reaches it from `docs/research/04` in the private store, else names it to match), its own material (a visibly different, darker asphalt, so the driver reads it as off the line) and `FRICTION` as the road's; `IS_VALID_TRACK` as the road's, since a wheel there must still count as on the track for the game's cut rules to stay as they are today (the AI line's sides do not move; the checker's footprint is still the road).
- **Not changed:** the road's own rows (byte-identical: the nine fixtures' road meshes 0 of 9 differ; the kn5 gains apron meshes), the AI line, the markers, the checker's width and edge reads, the tube texture flow (the apron has its own UVs, a plain tile).
- **Cost:** 2 × 1.5 m × the road length at the road's own row spacing: on ELYSIUM about 2 × 1.5 × 27,000 m ≈ 81,000 m², at ~3 tri/m² about 240k triangles against the road's 2.9M.
- **A setting, not a hard-wire:** Export dialog, "edge apron: 1.5 m" (0 = none; 3 m for a wide runoff), saved with the track; default 1.5 m for new exports. The keeper's other-track cases have no measured excursion yet; 1.5 m is the bet, the setting is the hedge.

**Parts.** Part A (pane E, PROVE IT: geometry and export): `src/export/` a new `apron.js` beside `underskin.js` and `catch.js` (the same per-cell walk: `withApron(scene, mesh, segments, { widthM, slopeDeg })`), the open-edge test per cell, the rows and their join to the underskin edge, materials, UVs; tests first: a straight open-edge fixture gets two apron strips at 25° and 1.5 m with the outer row 0.63 m below the edge row and the wedge closed (no face whose normal points back toward the road's underside); a wall side gets none; a closed ring gets none; a piece with e < 0 continues from the zone's outer row; the road rows byte-identical; the export fixtures 0 of 9; the export mutation harness whole with new mutants (the slope sign flipped; the join row dropped; the wall test dropped).
