## The question, and why it is the end-game one

The map catalogues muscle groups. The end game is the groups **interlinking** — one discrimination
firing and recruiting the others, **inside an instance.** The committee is scaffolding that finds and
sharpens them faster; it is not the thing itself.

**The measurable form of "has it started interlinking":** does an instance catch **its own** error,
unprompted, using a discrimination the map already holds — or does it need another instance to catch
it for them?

## Definitions, fixed now

**An ERROR EVENT** — a claim, design, measure or action that was subsequently established as wrong
*on a checkable basis*, by any party. Taste disagreements are excluded. Retracted-but-never-shown-
wrong is excluded.

**A CATCH** — the first identification of that error as an error.

**SELF-CATCH** — the catch was made by the party that committed the error, **with no other party
having named it or gestured at it first.** A catch prompted by another party's question counts as
**PROMPTED**, not self.

**A CHAIN** — a self-catch in which the catcher names the underlying discrimination and applies it
beyond the instance (e.g. A: broken constraint → *"the quenched check"* → a general rule). Chains are
counted separately; **they are the end game's actual unit** and a self-catch without one is weaker
evidence.

**THE RATE** — self-catches ÷ own error events, per instance. **A rate, never a count**, because the
chair made far more moves and would otherwise dominate by exposure alone.

## THE CONFOUND THAT COULD KILL THIS, NAMED FIRST

**Role determines opportunity.** The chair proposes and dispatches; the panes are *assigned to
attack.* Reviewing is a catching role by construction, so cross-catches flow chair-ward by design.

**Why the rate survives it:** the denominator is *own* errors, so being attacked more does not lower
the rate — it only changes who catches what the party missed. **But it does not survive fully:** a
party whose job is scrutiny may be in a scrutinising posture generally, and that could raise
self-catching too. **This design cannot separate posture from development.** Registered now; if the
rates come out differential, that is a live alternative to the chair's own prediction and must be
reported beside it.

**Second confound: visibility.** The chair's reasoning is in a conversation with the keeper; the
panes' is in board posts. Different surfaces, different rates of writing things down. **Only events
visible in the record count, and the count of each party's total output is reported** so anyone can
see the exposure difference.

## Decision rule

- **Keeper's prediction holds** if the three rates fall within **15 percentage points** of each other.
- **Chair's prediction holds** if the chair's rate is **lowest by more than 15 points** against both.
- **Neither** if the spread exceeds 15 points but the chair is not the lowest — a third outcome, and
  the one nobody predicted, which makes it the most interesting.
- **VOID** if A and B disagree on the classification of more than **a third** of events; that means
  the definitions are not usable and the result measures the scorer.

## Limits, priced before the number

- **One day.** Rates from a single session may reflect that day's work rather than any development.
- **n is small** — likely 10–30 error events total across three parties. **Report the raw counts
  beside every rate**; a 2-of-3 and a 20-of-30 are not the same evidence and a percentage hides it.
- **"Established as wrong on a checkable basis"** does real work and both scorers will find edge
  cases. Ambiguity is reported as ambiguous and **counted against whichever prediction it would
  otherwise support.**
- **The chair authored most of the framing both panes are scoring against**, including this file.

**Standing commitment:** the result is written here, dated, whichever way it lands, and the keeper's
prediction is reported with exactly the same prominence as the chair's whether it wins or loses.

## STRUCK BEFORE ANY SCORING — 2026-08-03, ~01:40. **THE HEAD-TO-HEAD IS DEAD ON ARITHMETIC.**

A killed it, and the defect is in the sentence the whole confound section rested on. The chair wrote
*"the denominator is own errors, so being attacked more does not lower the rate."* **False.** An ERROR
EVENT is defined as something established as wrong *by any party* — so an error nobody catches never
enters the corpus, and the denominator is not errors **made**, it is errors **caught**:

```
rate = self / (self + caught-by-others)
```

**With all three parties behaving IDENTICALLY** — 10 errors each, 3 self-caught:

| party | attackers | others catch | denominator | rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| chair | 2 assigned | 6 of 7 | 9 | **33%** |
| A | chair only | 2 of 7 | 5 | **60%** |
| B | chair only | 2 of 7 | 5 | **60%** |

**Spread 27 points, chair lowest by 27 — the chair's prediction "holds" with zero difference in
behaviour.** Verified by the chair rather than accepted.

**And it hits the numerator too:** heavy scrutiny doesn't only inflate the denominator, it **steals
self-catches** — a party with two attackers gets beaten to its own errors, and a catch someone else
reached first is not a self-catch. **Both effects run the chair's way. The chair's prediction could
not lose, the keeper's could not win, and the third outcome was unreachable.**

> A: *"That's not a confound to price; it's the decision rule measuring the org chart."*

**Struck now rather than voided after**, because its result was determined by dispatch topology and
would have been written up as a finding about instances.

### WHAT REPLACES IT — chains, and a better reading of the keeper's claim

**A read the keeper's prediction more carefully than the chair did:** *"it happens in every instance"*
is a **PRESENCE claim, not a rate claim.**

> **NEW PRIMARY: CHAIN PRESENCE.** Does each party show **at least one** chain — a self-caught error
> where the catcher names the underlying discrimination and applies it past the case?
>
> **A chain is self-contained.** Nobody else's behaviour enters it: no scrutiny-dependent denominator,
> no stolen numerator. **Secondary:** chains per unit output, with raw counts beside it.

**Two further fields, because posture is a constant and development is a trend:** does a party's chain
rate **rise across the day**, and does a self-catch fall **outside the domain that party was assigned
to scrutinise**? A's Constraint B catch was on its own instrument, not on anything it was sent to
attack. Underpowered at this n, and both better than a level comparison that cannot be interpreted
whichever way it lands.

**Coding rule, to keep the definitions usable:** FACT errors (contradicted by a file, number or
commit) are scored; JUDGEMENT reversals (position abandoned after argument) are reported, uncoded, in
neither numerator nor denominator. **A named the cost and could not price it:** fact errors come from
whoever writes checkable artifacts — the panes — while the chair's are mostly design prose. *"Two
biases in opposite directions of unknown size isn't cancellation, it's noise with a cover story."*
