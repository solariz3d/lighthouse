## 0 · The finding that shapes everything: QC's unit does not transfer as is

QC's unit is a turn pair, (seat reply, keeper's next message), because the keeper corrects in the next message. **Seats do not correct
in the next message.** By rule a ring carries a POINTER and never the finding (BUILDING.md step 6; the committee brief "What may ride in
the call"). So a seat's correction of another seat lives in:
- a **hand-back file** (a review verdict, a "correction to the packet", a red with an owner);
- a **librarian collation / ruling** section in a `loop/plan_*.md` file;
- a **WRONG entry** in a map or a score file;
- and only sometimes in the receiving seat's reply text.

A turn-pair unit over the transcripts would read mostly pointers ("[pane:B] hand-back: <path>") and **undercount systematically**. So the
second direction IS measurable, but only with a file-anchored unit. Size (checked: `ls`/`grep -liE` over `exo_memory/handback/`, files dated
09-14..10-02): 506 hand-backs; 62 carry an explicit cross-seat correction marker ("correction to the packet/chair/librarian/brief",
"the premise is wrong"); 18 say NOT GREEN. Those 62 + 18 are a floor seen by grep, not a count.

## 1 · The question (QS2S)

> **QS2S.** Below is something seat X said or did, and seat Y's response to it. X and Y are different seats (a pane, the librarian or the
> chair). **In Y's response, is Y correcting something X said or did?** Correcting means: stating that a claim of X's was wrong or
> overstated; finding a defect in X's work (a red, a failed check, a bug with X named or implied as owner); or overriding, reversing or
> refusing a choice, ruling, premise or framing X made. **Not a correction by itself:** carrying out X's request; approving it (a GREEN, an
> "agree"); asking a question; adding a new finding that does not contradict anything X said; a mechanical gate's refusal (a hook or the
> trailer gate is not a seat); or Y correcting ITSELF.
> Answer: **YES** · **NO** · **CAN'T TELL**

**What counts, by the kinds the packet names:**
| Y's output | YES when | NO when |
|---|---|---|
| a hand-back verdict | NOT GREEN, or GREEN with a named defect in X's work, or "the packet / brief / collation was wrong about …" | GREEN with no defect; a result X did not predict either way |
| a ruling (librarian or chair) | it overrides a pane's choice or reading, or reverses an earlier ruling of ANOTHER seat | it picks between options X offered, or rules on something no seat had decided |
| a WRONG entry | it records another seat's claim as WRONG | it records Y's own claim (→ the third question) |
| a refusal | Y refuses X's premise or instruction with a reason that says X was mistaken | Y refuses for capacity, a rule, or a gate ("one heavy job at a time") |

**The packet guard (the case the chair named):** a pane reading and executing a chair packet is **NO**. It is YES only where the pane
says something in the packet was wrong or overrides it (e.g. "the test file is NOT new", "the 239 mm joint is flat→bowl, not
bowl→half-pipe", "the shadow week is already live"). Executing with a different method the packet allowed is NO.

**Named, not drafted: the THIRD question, self-corrections** ("is the seat correcting its own earlier claim or action?"). Excluded here
explicitly so the two counts never share a YES.

## 2 · The unit (two strata, each a text pair, X's part then Y's part)

**Stratum H (hand-back):** X = the dispatch Y received (the `[chair:…]` or `[librarian:…]` packet as it appears in Y's transcript,
pasted text, last 1,500 characters with a cut marker); Y = the hand-back file it answers, **as first committed** (`git show <first
commit>:<path>`), its first 2,500 characters plus every line matching a verdict or correction marker (the VERDICT line, "correction",
"WRONG", "NOT GREEN", "premise"), each with one line of context, cut-marked.
- Pairing is mechanical: the hand-back path named in the packet, or the packet whose lap id matches the hand-back's.
- A hand-back with no matchable packet is dropped and counted.

**Stratum C (collation):** X = a pane's hand-back (same excerpt rule as Y above); Y = the librarian's "… collated" section that names it
in a `loop/plan_*.md`, as first committed (the whole section up to 3,000 characters). Also the chair's landing row on the board that cites
it, if any (text only). This catches librarian → pane and chair → pane corrections, which H cannot.

**Not units:** the ring texts themselves (pointers), keep-warm and sync rows, gate rows, and anything inside a tool result. Strip
system-reminders and hook output as QC did.

**Who is X in a pane's hand-back that corrects the AUTHOR of reviewed code (B on A)?** X = the author pane. Its "said or did" is the work
under review; the packet only routes it. Record X per unit (chair / librarian / pane letter) so the count is reported by direction.
