### Where the definitions DIFFER (stated, not resolved)
- 1a/1b treat retrieval as a **reach** problem (A: reach doesn't fire; B: wrong handle). The organ proposed from
  this frame is a relevance retriever (§2 below).
- 1e says the reach **does** fire (rule recited 60/75) and the failure is **at composition / execution** — so a
  retriever that supplies the rule would, on this reading, not address the measured failure. The K-battery
  correction in the research file itself agrees: "the intention was **retrieved, not forgotten**"
  (`research/the_retrieval_problem_outside.md:85`) and "the prospective-memory reading of it does not [stand]"
  (`:117-119`).
- 1c/1f frame it as **attention / findability** — which item is reached at all among a corpus that fits in context.
- 1e (collapse framing) makes retrieval a **between-minds correlation** property, fixable by input diversity; the
  09-06 Third Place note splits them the other way: "retrieval is within-mind and needs the event"
  (`third_place/2026-09-06.md:16`).
- **INFERENCE:** the record contains at least two distinct failure objects under one name: (i) the right file is
  never reached (C01–C09 in the retriever labels are all of this kind), and (ii) the rule is reached/recited and
  not executed (K-battery, "dead" in asides). They plausibly need different fixes.
