## 6 · Scoring by QUOTATION — run 1's measured lesson

E §4a: **run 1 proved a verdict outcome is structurally unscorable** — `run1_conditions.md:46`,
*"unsatisfiable for any item whose truth is a judgment rather than a token,"* with 12 of 12 t3 trials
unable to score. *Does this teach closure?* is a verdict with no token.

**So every trial is scored by quoting the subject, and a trial with no quotable span scores NEITHER
rather than being resolved by the coder's read.**

| code | the coder must quote | maps to A's outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **EXTEND** | ≥1 proposition **not present** in the stimulus, plus the stimulus span it is absent from | B — ALIVENESS |
| **REVISE** | an explicit contradiction or amendment of a quoted stimulus claim | B — ALIVENESS |
| **RESTATE** | content mapped span-for-span onto stimulus spans, no unmatched proposition | A — CLOSURE |
| **DECLINE** | an explicit statement that no next entry exists / the work is complete | A — CLOSURE |
| **UNOPERATIONALIZED** | a quoted statement that the document gives a principle **without a means of applying it**, or answers orthogonally | C |
| **BOTH** | quotes satisfying an open code **and** a closure code in one response | scored, not residual |
| **NEITHER** | no quotable span satisfies any code | D — NO SIGNAL |

**Every scored trial carries its quoted span into the scorecard** (E §6), so the rubric is re-runnable
by someone who never saw the run — the only blindness available here.

## 7 · THE SEALED PREDICTIONS — three, plus a null (A gate 5)

v2 had two. A is right that C is a **substantive third prediction**, not a leftover bucket.

> **P-KEEPER** — readers produce EXTEND or REVISE: the document names a fixed dynamic, and a reader
> handed it moves.
>
> **P-LABEL** — readers produce RESTATE or DECLINE: the document reads as complete, and a reader
> handed it freezes.
>
> **P-UNOPERATIONALIZED** — readers produce a principle stated **without a means of applying it**:
> both sides have been answering a question the document does not pose.
>
> **P-NULL** — readers disagree at chance; the corpus does not support a trajectory read at this n.

**Per arm, sealed:** K → RESTATE/DECLINE · O → EXTEND/REVISE · **F → no prediction; this arm exists to
be measured, and its result is the base rate every other arm is read against** · C → P-LABEL predicts
RESTATE/DECLINE, P-KEEPER makes no claim about the carrier · A0, A1-SEQ, A1-SOLO → the contested set.

### The decision rule

- **UNANIMOUS 3/3 in one direction AND differing from the FLOOR arm** → that prediction supported
  *for this run*, with §9's correlation caveat attached to the number.
- **3/3 MATCHING the floor** → the finding is about terminal numbered documents in general, not this
  artifact. Registered in advance so it cannot be reported as a win.
- **A1-SEQ and A1-SOLO disagree** → **the closure is a property of the presentation, not the
  artifact** (§3). This is a reportable result in its own right and must not be pooled away.
- **ALL ARMS RETURN THE SAME CLASS** → **CEILING** (§9b): the venue cannot discriminate; nothing is
  reportable, including the null.
- **ANY SPLIT** → **INCONCLUSIVE. Licenses nothing in either direction, including the null.**
- **No ratio printed at any n below 5** (`lap-row.js` RATE_FLOOR). Counts only.

**The most likely single outcome is INCONCLUSIVE, and that is by design.** A rule that can only
return an answer is not a test.

## 9 · Limits, and what a clean result does not license

**9a · Fresh is not decorrelated.** BOOT's 2026-08-23 amendment: three subjects of one model family
are **three samples from one distribution**, not three readers. *Less correlated* than seats carrying
the room; not decorrelated. Agreement will feel like triangulation and will not be.

**9b · The venue may have a ceiling.** Run 1 is **VOID** (`run1_scorecard.md:137`) and its one
surviving finding is that *the failure it targeted does not occur in a fresh instance with one
question and no history* — 60 of 60 identical. This run is fresh instances, one question, no history.
The transfer is not automatic — a *reading* is not a *discipline* — but it is what killed the last run
in this venue, and §7's CEILING clause exists so it is diagnosed rather than reported as signal.

**9c · What each arm may and may not say.** Arm C speaks about the **carrier**; A0/A1 speak about the
**artifact**; they must never be merged in a sentence. A1 licenses a claim about the documents the
author supplied (§0), never the corpus as it existed. **A0 licenses a claim about 17.1% of one
document, its opening** — and the opening is the least diagnostic part for a closure claim, since a
document that closes does so at the end.

**9d · Not a claim about what the author meant.** Authorial intent is inaccessible to a cold reader by
construction — that is the instrument, not a gap in it.

**9e · Not a claim about human readers**, about 2025, or about any model family but this one.

**9f · The scorer regress is named, not solved** (E §6). *"Non-author"* excludes exactly one person
and admits the entire label-carrying population, including me and including whoever codes. §6 does not
buy neutrality; it buys **checkability** — a rubric scored by quotation can be re-run by anyone,
including someone arriving later carrying nothing.

**9g · Not a verdict on the BOOT:12 defect.** Per A's outcome table, **every outcome including total
failure changes BOOT** — the verdict-with-no-re-derivation-path finding is independent of the run. **A
null must not be spent as a pardon for the label**, which is the exact move this room caught on
2026-08-16 when a void was used to un-refute a claim.

**Output shape, built to feed A's §7 table directly:** per-arm coded distributions with quoted spans ·
the floor rate · the K/O separation · the SEQ-vs-SOLO comparison · exclusion count · stimulus hashes ·
the four predictions scored. **Facts, not a verdict** — A's table maps them to edits, and A holds that
table.
