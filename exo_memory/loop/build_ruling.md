## 3. The ruling

**BUILD WITH CONDITIONS.** The conditions are §5 and the speech rules §7; both are part of the
ruling, not commentary on it. If the conditions are not acceptable, the ruling is DO NOT BUILD —
there is no conditions-stripped version of this yes.

Why build, in the ruling's own order of weight:

1. **The demonstrated non-zero.** Two correct blind DISAGREEs on known-wrong shipped claims,
   clean on command audit, against an all-time mechanical total of zero. n=2, permanently
   weakly powered, class-bounded — but the room has killed four gauges and three diversity
   designs for returning *nothing real*; this returned something real on its first registered
   contact, and the bar it passed was fixed before the run at a commit that precedes the result.
2. **The class it reaches is the class where the damage is.** The record's standing figure
   errors lived in committed artifacts with downstream readers (the 97.2% in a commit message,
   the ~43 KB in a shipped brief, run 1's reversed P6 in a committed scorecard, the
   234-assertions unit error quoted for weeks). Token-claims are precisely where a wrong value
   is load-bearing and precisely what the reader can re-derive. The moves-class it cannot reach
   is caught, when caught, by humans in minutes. Complementary, not substitutive.
3. **It can die.** F1 (thirty days, zero true DISAGREEs → delete) gives it an expiry the cards
   never had; F0 already cost it its strongest sales sentence before anyone was invested. An
   instrument that ships with its own death conditions is the only kind this room should still
   be building — L's §3 prices the real scarce resource correctly as the room's remaining
   tolerance for meta-instrumentation, and expiry is what keeps this from spending it.

## 4. The criterion ruled against (composed today; see §1)

A mechanism of this kind may be built when all five hold:

- **(i)** a registered positive control with a pre-fixed bar has returned non-zero, at a commit
  preceding the result — **met** (2/2; `098e080` → `19d46dd`);
- **(ii)** its firing event is command-observable, no judge in the loop — **met by
  construction** (verdict + audited commands);
- **(iii)** kill conditions are registered before build **and each has a live measurement
  channel** — *partially met*: L's F1–F4 and K's F1–F3 exist and predate this ruling, but
  F2/halo has no guaranteed instrument — closed by condition C4;
- **(iv)** speech rules are fixed before ship — this ruling's §7;
- **(v)** the routed claim-class matches the demonstrated-catchable class, with any widening
  gated on a fresh F0-shaped control over a **claim-shaped** corpus — condition C1.

## 5. Conditions of the build — each with its instrument, because a condition without one is a sentence

- **C1 — scope lock.** The reader routes token-truth claims only: the §3 tier as designed
  (artifact-bound at 100%, the unconditional 2–5% floor, the middle at a modest rate). No
  routing of verdict-shaped or move-shaped claims, ever, without a new registered control on a
  claim-shaped corpus (F0 finding 2). The floor stays unconditional — it is the anti-Goodhart
  clause, not a tuning knob.
- **C2 — the row is load-bearing.** No reader launches on a ledger row lacking the matched
  sentence, the touched paths, and claim-time HEAD. F0 finding 1 ("BOOT.md" matched four files)
  makes this a build requirement, not an enrichment. WORLD-MOVED ≠ WRONG (L's Q2) is binding:
  a DISAGREE that flips between claim-time and current tree surfaces as nothing.
- **C3 — command audit before surfacing.** Every DISAGREE is audited for answer-key
  contamination before it reaches a pane (F0 finding 3: the corrections live in the tree the
  readers are handed). An unaudited DISAGREE does not surface.
- **C4 — falsifiers with guaranteed instruments.** L's F1–F4 and K's F1–F3 are adopted as
  binding. Addition closing §4(iii): **F2/halo is measurable only while the correction-pair
  ledger keeps operating.** If a season passes with the pair ledger unmaintained, the halo
  falsifier cannot fire, and *that lapse itself* trips the furniture clause — delete the reader
  rather than run it unfalsifiable. A guard must be shown able to fail; this room has paid for
  that lesson five separate times.
- **C5 — surfacing discipline.** DISAGREEs ride the next real user turn to the originating
  pane; AGREEs surface nowhere; nothing synchronous enters Stop (the four refusals hold).
  Surfaces, never hauls.
- **C6 — the caveat travels.** Any citation of F0, anywhere, carries both numbers: 2/2 on the
  reachable subset, 2 of 16 of the record. A citation carrying only the first is the
  degenerating tell.
- **C7 — minimal build.** The three pieces of §2 (row enrichment, clock-fired reader,
  next-turn return) and nothing else. Anything additional is a new proposal with its own gate.

## 6. The halo question, answered rather than filed

The chair asked whether an instrument covering 12% of the record makes the other 88% more
dangerous by contrast. Three parts:

**The mechanism is real and it lives in the label, not the instrument.** A halo forms when a
reader of the room's outputs generalizes "checked" past the checked class. The instrument
itself asserts nothing about the 88%; sentences about the instrument do. That is why half of
this ruling is speech rules — the halo is a property of what gets *said*, which is the one
surface this room has measured as its least guarded (the compression step, BOOT's
curated-auditor gap, second_vantage §0's own denominator swap inside the commissioning brief).

**The specific danger is silence misread as cleanliness.** AGREEs surface nowhere and the
uncovered class produces no rows at all — so the absence of a DISAGREE is the system's most
common output and means *nothing*. The room already has a name for this error: treating absence
as the default that needs no proof. Anti-halo is therefore **visible non-coverage**: C6's dual
denominators, and the standing sentence (§7) that silence is non-coverage, never verification.

**Whether attention withdraws from the moves-class is an empirical question, and it has an
instrument.** The fear's operational form is: corrections filed against uncovered-class failures
flatten because humans stopped looking, while the covered class gleams. That is K's F2 as
written — displacement measured in the pair ledger as a share. C4 makes it binding and
guarantees the ledger runs so it can fire. I will not rule the halo away on argument; I rule
that it be *measurable*, with retirement as the registered consequence. On the current record
the direction actually runs the other way — the covered class is where errors stood for days to
weeks with downstream consumers, while moves are caught in minutes when caught at all — so the
reader adds a vantage where none existed rather than replacing one that did. If that reading is
wrong, F2 is how it gets to say so.

## 7. What may and may not be said about it once it ships

**Barred** (additions to J's §4 bar, which stands):

1. "The reader would have caught the record's instances" — it would have caught two (J's,
   restated as binding).
2. "The second vantage covers what the keeper was catching" — he was mostly catching moves;
   the reader does not see moves; nothing mechanized ever has (L's appendix, made binding).
3. Any citation of an AGREE row, a quiet ledger, or an absent DISAGREE as evidence a claim or a
   class is correct. Silence is non-coverage, never verification (F3, extended).
4. Any coverage, compliance, or sourced-rate figure quoted as an error rate or its movement as
   improvement (K's tell + F2's Goodhart clause).
5. Any sentence of the form "our numbers are checked now" — coverage claims carry both
   denominators or do not ship (C6).
6. Any claim that the reader reduces the need for human routing. "Only human routing can catch"
   was never falsifiable before this build; the reader is the first thing that could make it
   false, which means the question is now *open*, not answered (Attack C).
7. "The catch rate is X%" for any live figure without a walkable row set behind it — claim,
   command, both outputs, per F3.

**Permitted**, exactly: "A blind reader re-derived two known-wrong shipped figure-claims from
the repo alone — the first mechanical catches in this room's record. Its demonstrated reach is
the figure-class, retrospectively, at n=2: two of the record's sixteen instances, with three
more transcript-bound rows untested because the transcripts are off-machine — untested, not
failed. Everything else it might do is undemonstrated." Plus, after live catches, the walkable
rows themselves.
