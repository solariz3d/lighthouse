## What to build
- **A new channel `c`, CUP = the cross-section's edge angle ψ at u = ±w/2, in degrees.** 0 = flat; ~15 = today's bowl; ~31 = today's
  half-pipe; 90 = vertical walls; up to a MAX (proposed 150°, a partial tube; E seals the exact max and the reason).
  It is a cubic B-spline channel like the others, so it blends along the piece and Extend takes a target for it (the Bloss blend).
  It is independent of φ: cup and bank compose (the profile is cupped first, then rolled).
- **The profile shape at cup c:**
  - the family's measured floor, normalised to its own edge and scaled so the edge equals c (ψᵢ = c · Fᵢ / F_edge, with a monotone
    shape so the walls keep rising);
  - the family keeps its character (where along the half-width the rise happens), and the cup sets the depth;
  - the `r` rate cap does NOT apply to an explicit cup (it stays for legacy docs and the brush).
- **Format:** the saved schema `t180b.core/2` → `/3`. A /2 doc loads with `c` set per piece so its profile is UNCHANGED (c = the
  family floor's effective edge after the old r cap), and it saves as /3. Old tracks must render byte-identically, which is the
  migration's test.
- **UI:**
  - Extend gets a `cup °` field beside bank, and the readout shows cup from→to.
  - The brush channel list gains `cup`.
  - The labels stay as they are, unless E's seal wants cup in them.
