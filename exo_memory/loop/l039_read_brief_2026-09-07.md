## WHAT THE SUBJECTS ARE NOT TOLD, and why — the chair's judgement, on the record

**They are told they are measured. They are NOT told:**

- **that the statistic is pairwise overlap between readers**, or
- **that other readers are reading the same object at all.**

**Reason, and it is the one that changed my mind.** Telling a reader that overlap is the measure
gives them a reason to *deliberately diversify* — to chase the unusual finding rather than the true
one. That would bias the result toward REJECTING the null, which is the direction that flatters the
thesis being tested. A reader who does not know peers exist cannot game overlap in either direction.

**What telling them costs, measured rather than assumed:** effort may rise uniformly because they
know they are scored. **The statistic conditions on each reader's observed set size**
(`l039_preregistration_2026-09-07.md`), so uniform effort inflation cancels. Deliberate
diversification would not.

**So: honest about being measured, silent about what is measured.** If that cut is wrong, it is
wrong in this file where it can be read, rather than in a decision nobody wrote down.
