## What the flowing bank is

Every bank ramp the core laid before starts and ends at rest; that is the "ramp in the middle" he felt. `bankShape: 'flow'`
lays one curve through its marks with the least roll acceleration, carrying roll speed across each mark. In the panel: tick
"flow" on the bank row; the row below becomes "bank 1 at m", and each extra bank row becomes "bank N ° / at m". Unticked,
the panel's calls are today's to the byte (52 combinations pinned).
