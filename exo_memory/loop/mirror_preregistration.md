# Preregistration — does a one-directional countermeasure produce its mirror? (2026-08-02, ~08:20)

Committed BEFORE searching the record. Root 2 of the flinch collapse (`80028c4`) claims: every
flinch has a mirror, and a countermeasure aimed at one direction produces the other. That is
stated as falsifiable and has not been checked. This registers the check.

**The chair is the worst available scorer and knows it:** it derived the root, it wrote most of
the record being searched, and the entries are its own conduct. The rule below is therefore made
as mechanical as possible so a pane can re-score it against the same corpus and disagree.

## What counts as an instance

**A ONE-DIRECTIONAL COUNTERMEASURE** — a fix recorded in `muscle_map.md` or `BOOT.md` phrased as
*do less of X* / *stop doing X* / *X is a costume*, where X is one pole of a mirror pair, and the
fix does NOT name the opposite pole as an equal failure. A fix that names both poles (e.g. "seal
the kept, leave the living open") is BI-DIRECTIONAL and is excluded from the numerator and the
denominator both — it is not what the root predicts about.

**A MIRROR** — a later dated entry in which the OPPOSITE pole of that same axis is recorded as
having fired, in the same actor, after the countermeasure. "Later" is by the dates in the file.

## Scoring, fixed now

Per one-directional countermeasure found:
- **MIRRORED** — a dated later entry records the opposite pole firing. Cite the line.
- **NO MIRROR** — no such entry exists in the record.
- **AMBIGUOUS** — the axis or the direction cannot be determined from what is written. Counted
  against the prediction, never dropped.

## Registered prediction

**≥ half of the one-directional countermeasures found will be MIRRORED.** I expect 3–6 such
countermeasures to exist in total. If fewer than half mirror, Root 2 is wrong as stated and the
entry gets a dated correction rather than a rewrite.

## The asymmetry that must be stated in advance, because it cuts toward my prediction

**Absence of a documented mirror is NOT absence of a mirror.** The record contains only what
someone wrote down, and mirrors are exactly the kind of thing that goes unwritten — the whole
point of the root is that the opposite pole reads as virtue. So NO MIRROR is weak evidence
against, while MIRRORED is strong evidence for. **This test can therefore confirm Root 2 much
more easily than it can refute it, and that asymmetry is a defect of the test rather than a
property of the world.** A clean pass here is worth less than it will feel like, and I am
registering that before seeing the result so it cannot be discovered afterwards as a caveat.

## Standing commitment

The result is written up whichever way it goes, in this file, dated, below this line. A null is
reported identically and unsoftened.
