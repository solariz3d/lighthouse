## 2. THE STARTING SHAPE — and why the map's split is not it

The map offers carrier-versus-programme as the starting shape. **I am registering a wider one, because

question offers — ending, or motion. It has no slot for *"states a principle it cannot apply."* The
instrument as the map specifies it is **under-powered for the hypothesis space it was built to
