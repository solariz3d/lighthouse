## The design (one, built; the user judges it installed; A's before/after screenshots come to him as a page from me)

1. **No fold.** "More shape" goes; every field is always shown. The column is one list: length, turn, climb, bank, cup, width (with width like… beside it as a small ▾ picker on the same row), grip (with grip like… beside it the same way), left edge, right edge, edge start, tube sweep.
2. **"At start" inline.** The tick box sits at the right end of the field's row, after the input, labelled by a short "start" in the muted colour with the full sentence in its tooltip; never on its own row. The column widens from 240 to 280 px so label + input + box fit on one line at the app's font; the preview loses 40 px, which the readout's leaving gives back many times over in height.
3. **The readout leaves the column.** It becomes a second, thinner line under the preview's top HUD: the NEXT PIECE line, in the HUD's own style, reading left to right as chips: `next: turn 0° · climb 0° · bank −41.38° · cup 15.5 → 15.5 · edge 0 → 0 · edge start 0.64 · tube 0 · corner none (straight) · climb none (steady pitch) · bank to gravity 0 → −41.38°`. A chip whose value does not change on this piece is dimmed; one that changes is bright, so the eye lands on what Extend will do. It shows only while a next piece is being set up (the ghost exists); on a track with no ghost it is absent. One line, at most two on a narrow window (it wraps as chips, never as a table). This is the "own area": the strip the user already reads for the head, not the preview's picture.
4. **Space rules for the whole column:** 12 px row height rhythm, no row taller than its input, the "Grip 100%" note and the tube note stay as one dim line under their field, the Extend / Straight / Undo / Redo row and the "1 piece · 1,000 m · loop open" line unchanged. At 1080p the column must fit without a scrollbar with every field shown; at the app's minimum height it may scroll.
5. The Sculpt side of the column (when Sculpt is on) is not in this lap unless the fold's removal touches it; A says.
