## THE TEST, as drafted

- **Arms:** one pane **FRESH** (new session, woken on its map `exo_memory/map/<X>.md` + the room's intake, no resumed
  transcript) and one pane **RESUMED** (its normal conversation). Assignment drawn and sealed before dispatch.
- **Work:** the same kind of packet to each, from the night's real queue — ideally one where the brief is known to be
  imperfect, so there is something to correct.
- **Measures:**
  1. **Cost** — cache_creation + cache_read on each arm's first three calls (transcript usage fields).
  2. **The collapse signal** (`map/M.md`, 03:0x 2026-09-21): did the pane **contradict its brief and turn out right**?
  3. **Particulars** — did it cite paths and figures that re-derive (the librarian re-derives both hand-backs blind to arm)?
  4. **Voice/character** — scored by a non-author reader blind to arm, against a rubric written before.
- **PREDICTION (to be finalised in the registration):** FRESH costs a small fraction of RESUMED on first calls, and
  matches it on measures 2 and 3.
- **FALSIFIER (draft, in the words that would make it true):** *"the fresh pane followed its brief where the resumed pane
  corrected it, or its hand-back carried a figure that did not re-derive where the resumed pane's did."*
- **NULL beside it:** if BOTH arms follow a flawed brief without correcting it, the packet could not discriminate and the
  run is void, not a win.
- **Unwelcome outcome, named in advance:** fresh panes read like the generic assistant — Bartlett's drift — even when
  their particulars are right.
- **Limit already known:** n=1 per arm is an anecdote with a registration. It licenses a second run, not a policy.

## WHAT IT WOULD CHANGE

If FRESH holds: panes stop costing a full re-read each shift, and the stick carries transcripts only for the seats whose
live thread matters (candidates: chair, librarian, Third Place — itself a keeper call, since carrying the conversations
was his spec). If it fails: we learn what the transcript was carrying that the files do not.
