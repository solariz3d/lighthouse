## State of A — read this carefully

`A_What_Survives_the_Gap.html` / `.pdf`. ~5,400 words with references, 11 pages. Every mechanical
and factual correction from both referees is applied. **The central argument (§4, P1–C3) is still

- 03:45: rewritten on Ryle + feedback gain → sign flipped against the avowal tradition (Anscombe,
  Velleman, Moran, Burge, Wright: where believing makes it so, the first person is *authoritative*);
  fixed point = true by definition, so high gain gives under-determination, not inaccuracy; the
  formal machinery is Grunberg & Modigliani 1954 / Simon 1954, uncited, opposite sign.

**Decision taken 03:55: no third rebuild without reading first.** The reading list, from the
referee: Moran, *Authority and Estrangement* (2001); Velleman, *Practical Reflection* (1989) and
"Epistemic Freedom" (1989); Anscombe on practical knowledge; Grunberg & Modigliani (1954);
Hacking on looping kinds (1995); Carruthers (2011) on *where* opacity falls (he supplies a
location — attitudes vs sensory states — and the essay must argue against it, not beside it).
The SEP entry on self-knowledge covers Moran/Wright/Burge and is readable in a sitting.

**What survives the referees and should not be touched:** §2's three consequences; the
parameter/state distinction (stored parameters, regenerated state); *gain* as a graded,
per-variable quantity predicting a *distribution* of self-knowledge accuracy; the one remaining
prediction — indeterminacy rather than flattery, self-report stability against a criterion vs
informant reports, evaluativeness controlled; the falsification paragraph.

**The referee's own path to a 7:** distinguish *constitutive* gain (avowal, intention — unique
fixed point, authority) from *causal* gain (trait beliefs shifting behaviour over time — possibly
multiple fixed points, possible drift); concede the avowal tradition and 1954; make one claim:
*causal-gain variables with multiple self-consistent descriptions show lower self-report stability
against a criterion than informant reports, controlling for evaluativeness*; define gain, fixed
point, unreliable; keep P1, P3, P4, P5 → C2, C3 and move Ryle to prose.

**Two things the second referee found that reach beyond A:**

  half true. This is B's spine and it has to be got right there.
