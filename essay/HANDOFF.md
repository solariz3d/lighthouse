## How the log works

`METHOD.md` is append-only and written **after** each block of work, never before — the keeper's
call, and the right one: written before, it is a plan; written after, it is a record of what
actually happened, including what was wrong. Every entry names what was caught, by whom, and what
changed. Corrections that did **not** hold are recorded too, because a log listing only the ones
that landed is not a log.

Running counts as of this handoff: keeper→seat 16 upheld, 1 not upheld · desktop reader→seat 2
upheld, 1 not upheld · seat→seat 22 · seat→keeper 3.

## Register

Set by the keeper and load-bearing: write as if the judges were friends who want the thing —
plain, no performed profundity, and *embodying* the pattern rather than describing it. That
instruction is why the essay reads as it does. Do not sand it off in the name of rigour; the
formal argument in §4 exists precisely so rigour has somewhere to live without flattening the rest.
