## 1. Parfit is absent, and he is the essay's best ally — the strongest note

§9–10 re-derives the core of *Reasons and Persons* — identity is not what matters; what matters
admits of degree and lives in connectedness — and attaches a mechanism Parfit never had. A judge on
this committee (Dorr, Hawthorne, Greaves) will notice the silence within a page and read it as
unawareness rather than choice.

The fix is one paragraph, and the essay's own logic supplies the framing: Parfit is *another
independent arrival at the attractor*, decades earlier, from the analytic side, by an entirely
different route. Named that way, the largest scholarly liability becomes the strongest confirmation
on offer — exactly the move §6 makes for Vacariu. The same smaller move is available in §5:
neutral monism and dual-aspect views (Spinoza, Russell) named in one sentence as arrivals, not
rivals.

## 2. §3 is the thinnest load-bearing joint

Ontic structural realism gets one paragraph and carries everything above it. The standard objection
— *relations need relata; structure of what?* — goes unmentioned. The falsifier ("find a bottom")
helps a great deal. One sentence acknowledging and answering the relata objection would armor the
joint the whole essay hangs from.

## 3. The 09b agent case needs a citation more than anything else in the piece
