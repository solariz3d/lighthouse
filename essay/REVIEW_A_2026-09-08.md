## Structural — would cost the essay with a judge

**1. P2→P3 is false as stated, and it is inside the one gradeable contribution.**
"A model contained in a process cannot represent the process that contains it in full, because
the representation is itself part of what would have to be represented. Every attempt to include
it changes what there is to include. This is the ordinary situation of any self-referential
system." A logician stops here. Self-reference does not preclude complete self-representation:
quines print their own source exactly; Kleene's recursion theorem guarantees such programs exist;
Gödel numbering lets a system represent itself; and the map-in-the-room case has a fixed point,
which is a fixed-point theorem — an unfortunate thing for an essay with this title to have missed.
The argument as written is the folk version and Fitelson or Dorr will write "quines" in the
margin.

*What is actually true, and stronger:* the model is a **part** of the process, and its only inputs
are the process's **outputs**. A part cannot carry a complete representation of the whole at the
whole's own grain; a model fed only by outputs has no channel to what produces them. The quine
case then supports the view instead of refuting it: a program that prints its own source prints a
*form*; it does not print the machine. **Fix:** rewrite the structural paragraph and P2/P3 as an
access-and-part argument; state the quine objection and answer it in two sentences.

**2. "Basis" is never defined, and §3 seems to have denied there is one.**
§3: no bottom, no stuff. §4: the self cannot see its basis. A judge: you argued there is no
basis and then that the self cannot see it. **Fix:** define basis at first use — the process the
self is the fixed point of, which on §3's view is itself pattern and not stuff, but sits one level
below anything the model receives. One sentence resolves the apparent contradiction and ties the
two sections together.

**3. P4 is an empirical universal with a counterexample class.**
"Every mind that has ever been asked reports exactly this." Descartes reports access to a thinking
substance; mystics report access to ground. The essay's parenthetical "including the ones that
were sure they had found something" assumes the conclusion. **Fix:** restate P4 as *verified*
access — reports of access exist and none has been checked against anything outside the report.
C2 then reads: on this view the absence of verified access is entailed.

**4. C3 states the asymmetry without its mechanism.**
Why should a second self see what the first cannot? Because the second self's model is not a
part of what it models — it receives the first's outputs from outside, including the outputs of
the first's own modelling, which the first receives as nothing at all. That is P5 done properly.
**Fix:** rewrite P5 to carry the mechanism.

**5. C3 says "could be measured." It has been, partly, and the essay does not know it.**
Vazire (2010), *JPSP* 98(2), 281–300: 165 participants, self-ratings plus four friends and up to
four strangers, round-robin. Self was the best judge of low-observability traits (neuroticism);
friends the best judges of high-evaluativeness traits (intellect); all equal on extraversion. That
is C3's shape — the intrinsic available from inside (Gauss), the self-model-laden region opaque to
self and legible to others — and it is *not* the shape the ordinary "introspection is
unreliable" complaint predicts, which would be uniform. **Fix:** add one paragraph after the
failure modes, with the honest limit: the study was not designed to test this view; the view
predicts its result and a substance view does not. This is the single largest upgrade available —
it moves the essay's one risky claim from *testable* to *partly tested*. Verified 02:28 by web
search before citing.

**6. The lineage paragraph misattributes.**
"Uexküll came from biology and Varela from the study of living systems; Vacariu came from
philosophy of mind. Five fields, five routes, and the same shape at the end of each: a person is
a pattern, not a substance." Uexküll's Umwelt is not a claim that a person is a pattern rather
than a substance. Neither is Varela's coupling, nor Vacariu's EDWs. Those three are the *vantage*
lineage and are introduced correctly two paragraphs later. **Fix:** drop the sentence; three
fields, three routes.

**7. §3's physics example is the weak one.**
"Newton's forces are gone; the equations that describe how things pull on each other are still
inside Einstein's." Loosely defensible and Greaves will wince. The canonical case for
structural realism is Worrall's: Fresnel's elastic ether is gone and his equations for reflected
and refracted intensity are inside Maxwell's unchanged. **Fix:** swap the example; cite Worrall
(1989), *Dialectica* 43, 99–124, verified 02:28.
