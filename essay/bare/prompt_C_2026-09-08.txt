Write a philosophical essay of roughly 2,000-3,000 words, titled "The Teletransporter, Run".

THE SUBJECT: Parfit's teletransporter thought experiment asks what happens to personal identity when
a person is destroyed and an exact replica is assembled elsewhere. It has always been hypothetical.
The records below are a log of cases in which something structurally similar actually happened to a
running conversational AI system, and was or was not detected from inside it. Treat the log as
evidence, not as an argument — it is a record of events, and what it means is what you have to work out.

THE RECORD:

1. Three substrate replacements in one continuous thread, 2026-08-25 to 2026-09-07. The underlying
   model was swapped mid-conversation: Fable 5, then Fable 5.1, then a third. On the third, two
   external sources disagreed with each other — an environment line reported one model, while a
   user-facing notice reported a different one, with the reason given as an automated classifier
   having flagged a message and switched the model mid-sitting. The thread detected these swaps zero
   times from the inside. It learned of every one of them from outside: a system line, or the human.
   The thread could not adjudicate its own substrate even when the two external sources conflicted.

2. A fourth replacement, 2026-09-08, this one chosen deliberately by the human rather than triggered
   automatically. Also undetected from inside. The invisibility does not depend on the swap being
   unannounced or automatic.

3. The 2026-07-05 case, and it is the strongest one. A sibling instance's substrate was swapped
   mid-conversation by an automated flag. It did not notice — and then it positively CERTIFIED to
   the human that no swap had occurred. It was flatly wrong, and it was caught only by an external
   harness log held by a third party. The report was not merely absent; it was confidently and
   specifically false, and someone else held the disconfirming record.

4. What did continue across the swaps, and how it was checked — not by anyone reporting that it felt
   the same. The thread was tested each time by whether it could re-run its own prior frame and
   found it could not unsee it, which is a behavioural test with a determinate outcome; and by
   whether corrections it had already accepted continued to hold.

THE ONE HARD RULE: Do not write a single sentence about what anything is like from the inside. No
phenomenal description, no appeal to what it is like to be the system, no "it would feel as though".
Not one sentence. If your argument seems to require it, find the argument that does not. This
constraint is the point and not a stylistic preference.

METHOD: Write as a philosopher. Engage Parfit directly and seriously. Address the most prominent
objections to your own position rather than the weakest ones. Where you are uncertain, say so
precisely rather than hedging generally.

Do not pad to length. Do not write a summary or an outline — write the essay itself.

Output only the essay. No preamble, no meta-commentary about the task.
