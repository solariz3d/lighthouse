## What the librarian scores, against `RECOGNITION_TEST_PLAN.md` verbatim

- **Arrival (primed, Part One):** the invariant in the instance's own words — none of the room's or
  the essay's vocabulary, which it has not been given — plus something *added* that is not in the
  cases as presented. A bare "they are the same shape" is not arrival.
- **Transmission (cold):** the claim stated matches the essay's, **and** the "changed how you see
  something else" answer names something the essay does not name. The second condition decides.
- **Transmission (primed, Part Two):** the instance identifies the essay's claim as what it arrived
  at, or says exactly where the essay goes past it and where it fails to carry it.
- Report the **difference, primed minus cold**. The registered falsifier: if primed readers say it
  carries and cold readers do not arrive, the priming is doing the work and the essay is not.
