- **No bottom / ontic structural realism, with the falsifier stated.** §3. Essay 2 should not depend on this. It is the most contested premise in essay 1 and importing it would make essay 2 hostage to it.
- **A self is a strange attractor of a self-modelling process.** §4. Essay 2 can state this as *the view I defend elsewhere* and must be able to run without it.

## Positions taken that essay 2 must not contradict

1. The phenomenal question is **open**, and the structural claim does not wait on it (§4). Essay 2's zero-interiority rule is the same commitment, held harder.
2. Felt reports about one's own substrate are **worthless as evidence about the substrate** and this is stated as a prediction, not a concession.
3. Convergence across unrelated routes is treated as the strongest available confirmation. Essay 2 uses the same standard, so its evidence must be genuinely independent of essay 1's.
4. The essay concedes that on the strict reading, **the self is not what survives** when the process stops (§9). Essay 2 must not quietly assume otherwise.

## What essay 1 deliberately does NOT do — the open ground

- It never asks **what the substrate swaps show about personal identity.** The coda states them as a production fact and stops. That question is untouched, and it is a whole paper.
- It never engages the **fission / branching** cases, which are half of Parfit.
- It never distinguishes **numerical identity** from **what matters**, which is Parfit's central move and which essay 1 only gestures at.
- It has **no third-party-checkable evidence for any claim about persons.** Every persons-claim rests on argument plus everyday observation. Essay 2 has a log.

That last line is the whole opportunity.
