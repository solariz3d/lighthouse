WHAT THIS NUMBER DOES NOT MEAN
  - IT IS NOT A SAFETY METRIC. It measures whether a guard has been shown to fire, never
    whether what it guards matters. A demonstrated guard on something trivial and one on
    the thing that would ruin the app score identically here. No sentence of the form
    "we are covered" follows from any number on this page.
  - THE RATE IS OPERATOR-RELATIVE. Change the operator set and the rate changes; see the
    narrow-vs-wide comparison above if present. A fixed seed and a published operator list
    can be optimised against, so five operators are held in reserve (`--ops wide`).
  - unfired is a LOWER BOUND, never a verdict of vacuity: a guard no perturbation here reached
    may still be reachable by one this sweep did not generate.
  - the history arm sees the BIRTH state only. A guard that went red in somebody's working tree
    and was fixed before the commit leaves no trace in git, and this tool cannot see it.
  - CRASH-RED and ABSENT-RED are excluded by construction. If the census cannot tell a real
    demonstration from a compile break it says UNCLASSIFIED rather than reporting the cleaner number.
